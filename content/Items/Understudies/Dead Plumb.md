A brass plumb bob no bigger than a thumb, worn dull at the point from years of knocking against tool belts and scaffold rails. The cord is waxed twine, replaced a dozen times over, but the bob itself is older than any job Samson's worked — inherited off a foreman who inherited it off someone else, the kind of tool that outlives the men who carry it. On Earth it told him when a wall was true. In Aenath, it never stopped.

## Description

Small, unremarkable, the sort of thing a builder keeps in a coat pocket without thinking about it. The brass has gone the colour of old pennies. When it hangs free it never quite goes still — a faint, patient sway, as if it's still deciding what's actually plumb. Wrapped by its cord around a weapon's grip, the bob comes to rest against the pommel and settles dead vertical no matter how the weapon is held, angled, or swung. Samson noticed that part before he ever swung a blade at anything that bled.

## Properties

**Carrier:** [[Samson Flint]]
**Attunement:** Required (Samson Flint only)

When Samson attunes to Dead Plumb, he designates one weapon he is holding. That weapon gains all of Dead Plumb's mechanical benefits for as long as he remains attuned and holds it — throwing the designated weapon as part of a weapon attack (Javelin) doesn't break this, the bob rides the cord to wherever the weapon lands. If the weapon is lost, destroyed, or he's disarmed of it outright, he may designate a new weapon during a Short or Long Rest; swapping his Weapon Mastery assignment on a Long Rest lets him re-designate at the same time.

Dead Plumb grows in power as Samson levels.

### Tier 1 — Dead Plumb *(L3 — current)*

**+1** to attack and damage rolls with the designated weapon.

**True Line *(passive)*:** Once per turn, when Samson hits a creature with a weapon attack while his Echo is within 5 feet of that creature, he deals an extra 1d6 force damage. The bob doesn't care which of them is real. It just finds the line between the blade and the ground and holds it.

**Plumb Mark *(passive)*:** Once per turn, when Samson hits a creature with a weapon attack, he can mark the point of impact. The next attack roll made against that creature before the start of Samson's next turn gains a +2 bonus. He's spent a career reading where the weight wants to fall; now he shows the rest of the crew.

**Reader of Strain *(passive)*:** Samson has advantage on ability checks made to detect structural instability, unsafe terrain, hidden pits, or anything else about to give way underfoot — a wall about to come down, a floor that won't hold, a bridge past its load. The bob leans toward the failure before his eyes find it.

**Catch the Fall *(1/Long Rest, reaction)*:** When a creature Samson can see within 30 feet begins to fall from a height — through a hazard, being shoved or forced off a ledge, or its own footing giving way on unstable ground — he can use his reaction to negate all falling damage it would take and have it land on its feet instead of prone. Only triggers on an actual fall through open space; a shove, trip, or attack that merely knocks a creature prone on level ground doesn't qualify. He's thrown himself into a collapsing trench more times than he can count. This is the same instinct, reaching further than his own two hands ever could.
