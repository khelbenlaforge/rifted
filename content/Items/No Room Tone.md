A small velocity-sensitive pad controller, four rows of rubber squares gone shiny at the corners from a decade of the same fingers finding the same positions in the dark. It was mid-master when the pull took him — plugged in, powered, one pad already lit and waiting for a hit that never landed. It came through anyway. It still reads the same input it always did: how hard you meant it.

## Description

Scuffed matte black, a cracked corner mended with the same gaffer tape every piece of his old rig wore eventually. The pads used to light for sound. Now they answer to something closer to intention, a faint ember-warm glow that matches the heat under Jack's own skin. These days he mostly just triggers it, the same practiced motion he used to pull one clean take out of forty ruined ones.

### Tier 1 — Clean Signal *(L3 — current)*

**One Frequency *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Jack hits with a spell attack, or a creature fails its saving throw against his Vicious Mockery or Dissonant Whispers, that creature takes an extra 1d6 psychic damage. The pad reads how hard he means it and answers in kind.

**Nothing Competing *(passive)*:** When a creature fails its saving throw against Jack's Vicious Mockery, its speed is also reduced by 10 feet until the start of Jack's next turn. He finds the one frequency that matters and drowns out everything competing with it — including the ground under whoever he's talking to.

**Pulled From the Noise *(passive)*:** Jack has advantage on Perception checks made to hear. Ten years pulling one whispered sentence out of a wall of static didn't stop mattering just because the wall changed.

**Clear the Mix *(1/Short Rest, Bonus Action)*:** For the next minute, Jack has advantage on Perception and Insight checks made to detect invisible creatures, see through illusions, or spot a disguise, as long as he's actively listening for it. Sound doesn't lie the way sight does. He's spent a career proving that.
