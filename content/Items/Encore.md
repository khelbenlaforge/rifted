It never once handled real magic before the crossing — just held between two fingers, worried smooth over four years of covers played to rooms of strangers in costume who'd wandered over for the novelty and stayed because four chords, played loud enough, don't care whose convention badge you're wearing. He still remembers exactly which song was playing the night he vanished mid-chorus.

## Description

A single guitar pick, tortoiseshell-pattern plastic gone glassy-smooth at the point from thousands of downstrokes, a faded band logo worn past legibility. He turns it between two fingers before he speaks in front of more than a few people — the same tic a frontman uses before counting off a song, now doing the same work for an oath.

### Tier 1 — Opening Act *(L3 — current)*

**Power Chord *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Sam hits with an attack, he deals an extra 1d6 thunder damage. The first note is always the loudest.

**Feedback *(passive)*:** The first time each turn a creature under Sam's Champion Challenge or Compelled Duel hits a creature other than Sam with an attack, it takes 1d6 thunder damage — a resonant snap of feedback for breaking focus.

**Play to the Back Row *(passive)*:** Sam has advantage on Performance checks, and on Persuasion checks made while addressing two or more creatures at once. A room this size never listens unless you make it.

**One More Song *(1/Short Rest, Bonus Action)*:** Up to three allies within 30 feet who can hear him gain advantage on the next saving throw they make before the start of Sam's next turn. He never once left a stage without playing an encore. He isn't about to start.
