A plain gold band, bought on a chemist's salary. Nobody chose it for how it looked. It came through with her the way the rest of her did — quietly, without asking, still on her hand. Somewhere in the years since, a thread of the same faintly luminous fungus that rings her in a fight took root in the band itself, so gradually she couldn't say when it happened. It hasn't dulled the gold. If anything, it's the only part of her that still shines.

## Description

Worn thin at the inside curve, the way a ring gets when it's never once come off. A hairline of pale fungal thread traces the band now, barely visible unless the light catches it. It doesn't look like rot. It looks deliberate, like something chose exactly where to grow. She never takes it off to check — she already knows exactly what's there.

### Tier 1 — The Steady Room *(L3 — current)*

**Grown In *(passive)*:** The fungal thread has worked its way into whatever Ruan wears, stiffening it where it needs to hold. Her worn armor functions as a Breastplate (AC 14 + Dexterity modifier, maximum 2) instead of its normal AC. This is not a magical enhancement — it's mundane-equivalent protection she could buy outright with enough coin; the ring just means she's never without it. (A genuine magical bonus begins at Tier 2.)

**Reagent and Remedy *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Ruan deals necrotic damage with a spell, or restores hit points with a spell, that effect is increased by 1d6. The same precision that once meant knowing exactly how much a body could survive now runs the other direction, toward keeping things alive.

**What It Does To a Body *(passive)*:** Ruan has advantage on Medicine or Nature checks made to determine what a toxin, fungus, or plant will do to a living thing. Old instincts, same calculation, different stakes.

**The Measure of It *(1/Short Rest, no action)*:** Ruan can touch the ring and know exactly how long a wound, poison, or affliction has before it turns irreversible. Not a cure. Just the number she used to know without having to ask.
