A heavy curved blade mounted on a long pole — a weapon that asks for two hands, and for a lifetime of training neither Rachel's mind nor her old body ever had. It was already in her grip the first time she looked down at it — a mercenary company's mark somewhere in the memory of muscles that had apparently been swinging one since before she got here. She picked it up before she thought to ask anyone if she was allowed to. Nobody's told her no since.

## Description

The blade is broad and single-edged, built to carry through a sweeping cut — stabbing was never really the idea. Swung right, it turns into more momentum than a body her size should be able to produce alone. There's a worn groove where her grip always falls, like the wood remembers a hand that isn't quite hers yet — or is, now, whether she meant it to be or not.

### Tier 1 — Before Asking *(L3 — current)*

**+1** to attack and damage rolls.

**Through the Line *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Rachel's Cleave weapon mastery triggers its free attack, that attack deals an extra 1d6 damage. It's never once stopped at the first target.

**Weight Behind It *(passive)*:** Once per turn, on a hit while Raging, Rachel can push the target 5 feet in a direction of her choice, no save. More force than her own strength alone should produce — the same weight a guandao has always carried, moving through her now.

**Not Her Voice *(passive)*:** Rachel has advantage on Insight checks made to tell when someone is being controlled, pressured, or told what to think by someone else. Eighteen years on the receiving end of it means she recognizes exactly what it looks like from someone else's side.

**Didn't Ask Permission *(triggered, no action)*:** Immediately after Rachel fails a Strength check or saving throw, she can add the Rage damage bonus for her Barbarian level to the roll after seeing the result — whether she's currently Raging or not. She's faintly startled every time it works. Nobody's ever told her no in the middle of a swing.
