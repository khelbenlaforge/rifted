The watch was on his wrist when the office vanished and still there when he woke. It is as much a part of his look as the suit. It is heavy and staggeringly expensive, though it does not announce it. The hands kept moving through the crossing. He has not reset them.

## Description

A wristwatch with a plain dark face, no logo worth the name, and a case that sits cool against the skin. It is a little too good for any room in [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Lore/Aenath|Aenath]]. The second hand sweeps instead of ticking. Beau glances at it before anything that costs something, whether that is a deal or a spell. It still keeps New York time.

### Tier 1—Principal *(L3, current)*

**Compounding *(passive)*:** Once per turn, when a spell Beau casts damages one or more creatures he has already damaged earlier in this combat, including earlier on the same turn, one of those creatures, of Beau's choice, takes an extra 1d6 psychic damage. Every spell that lands is a deposit against the last one, and he has never once lost money on an investment.

**Second Hand *(1/Short Rest, Reaction)*:** When a creature Beau can see hits him with an attack roll, he can force the attacker to reroll the d20 it used for that attack and take the lower of the two results. The attack uses that lower result, which can negate a critical hit or turn the hit into a miss. The second hand stutters backward, and whatever was about to happen unfolds slightly differently.

**Clock Speed *(passive)*:** When Beau casts a spell as a Ritual, it takes only 1 minute longer to cast than normal, instead of 10 minutes longer. It still expends no spell slot. Nine minutes was a long time in his old work, and he never had that long to spare.

**Quick Change *(passive)*:** Beau can use his Changeling Shape-Shifter trait as a Bonus Action instead of an action, both to take a new shape and to return to his true form. He is always on time, and he changes before anyone notices there was a seam.
