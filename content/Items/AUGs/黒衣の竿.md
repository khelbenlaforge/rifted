The pole was in his hand when he came to. He carries it the way a stagehand carries something he has been told not to drop.

## Description

The staff is headed with a cluster of three rough crystals, held in a collar of ringed cord. The shaft below is bound in more cord, in bands that crowd together where a hand would grip it and spread out toward the lower end.

Makoto calls it 黒衣の竿 (Kuroko no Sao), the kuroko's pole. A kuroko is a stagehand in traditional Japanese theatre who dresses in black from hood to feet and moves scenery and props in full view of the audience, who politely agree not to see them. Whatever he calls it, it is no stagehand's tool.

## Properties

**Carrier:** [[Sumida Makoto]]
**Attunement:** Required (Sumida Makoto only)

While attuned, Makoto can use the pole as an Arcane Focus for his Sorcerer spells.

黒衣の竿 grows in power as Makoto levels.

### Tier 1—Standing By *(L3, current)*

**Follow-Through *(passive)*:** The first time each turn a creature takes damage from a spell Makoto casts, that creature takes an extra 1d6 damage of the same type. If a spell damages several creatures at once, Makoto chooses which one takes the extra damage. A clean move carries through to the end.

**Step Aside *(passive)*:** Once per turn, after Makoto casts a spell, he can move up to 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. The move costs no action, and he can't make it while his Speed is 0 (for example, while Grappled). The pole's weight swings him a pace aside as the spell leaves it.

**Well Rehearsed *(passive)*:** Makoto has advantage on Charisma (Performance) checks. This adds to his other bonuses to that check. The cord is wound where his hand lands on its own, so he can give his whole attention to the room.

**Board Up *(1/Short Rest, Bonus Action)*:** Makoto raises the pole, and a flat black board stands up on a solid surface in an unoccupied space he can see within 30 feet of him. The board is 5 feet wide and 8 feet tall. When it appears, he chooses up to three creatures within 5 feet of it. Until the start of his next turn, each chosen creature has half cover (+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws), no matter which side an attack comes from or where the creature moves afterward. The board is not a creature. It does not block movement or sight, it can't be targeted or damaged, and it folds away when the effect ends. Half cover does not stack with other cover; a creature uses only the strongest degree it has. Once used, Board Up is available again after a Short or Long Rest. A kuroko puts the right thing in the right place and lets the audience assume it was always there.
