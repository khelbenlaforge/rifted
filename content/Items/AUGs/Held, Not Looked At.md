Nothing about the crossing explains how it ended up around his neck. It was just there — already looped, already worn — the same morning he woke up in a body that wasn't quite his either. A small cross on a plain chain, the cross-piece cracked clean through and reset with careful, amateur solder. He fixed it himself and never told anyone it needed fixing in the first place. MAS never saw it. Neither did whoever signed his name into House Kundarak's ledger. It's the one thing in his life nobody assigned him.

## Description

Small, plain, unremarkable at a glance. A hand finds it without the eyes ever needing to look down. The solder crack across the cross-piece is old, done in a hurry, done alone. Gregory doesn't look at it. He touches it, a thumb pressed briefly to the crack, in the half-second before he commits to a spell — the same unconscious check a man makes on a tool he trusts more than he trusts most people.

### Tier 1 — Muscle Memory *(L3 — current)*

**Steady Hands *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Gregory hits with a spell attack, or lands an attack made as part of casting a spell (such as True Strike), he deals an extra 1d6 force damage. His hands go still exactly when it matters — the same stillness he once used to fit a housing or calibrate a rune-line, learned so early he can't say when it was taught to him.

**Held Ground *(passive)*:** Gregory has advantage on Concentration saving throws while concentrating on a spell. The rest of him can be composed on purpose. This part isn't performance.

**Practiced Composure *(passive)*:** Gregory has advantage on Insight checks made to tell when someone is being performative rather than genuine. He's spent a lifetime being told charm is a competency, tracked and graded — he knows exactly what that looks like from the outside now.

**Thumb to the Crack *(1/Short Rest, Reaction)*:** When Gregory is about to make a saving throw, he can touch the pendant as a Reaction and gain advantage on that save. He doesn't know why it helps. It's just the thing his hand does before anything else does.
