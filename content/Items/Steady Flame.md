It lit more deals than cigarettes — the flick of a lighter across a table being as much a signal as a light, the thing you did with your hands so nobody watched your face instead. He's flicked it open over deals that could've gone either way, and the flame never once gave away which way he was betting.

## Description

A heavy brass lighter, the hinge worn loose, a crude engraving on one side — initials that aren't his, inherited off a man he doesn't talk about. The flame catches on the first strike, every time, rain or nerves notwithstanding. He doesn't smoke. He just likes having something in his hand that always, always works.

### Tier 1 — Never Blinked *(L3 — current)*

**Bad Batch *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Melvin hits with an attack, he deals an extra 1d6 poison damage. Not everything he ever sold was cut clean.

**Close It Out *(passive)*:** Once per turn, when Melvin reduces a creature to 0 hit points, he gains temporary hit points equal to his Barbarian level until the start of his next turn. Built to end things fast — never let one fight run long enough to think about.

**Never Flinch *(passive)*:** Melvin has advantage on Intimidation checks when he holds the upper hand or is calling a bluff. He never once let anyone see the math.

**One Warning *(1/Short Rest, Bonus Action)*:** Melvin flicks the lighter alight. One creature that can see him must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened of him until the end of Melvin's next turn. Know your supplier, know your buyer, never let either one see you sweat.
