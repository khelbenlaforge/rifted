> # Hollowstone

A naturally holed piece of granite from the deepest level of the Cerro Gordo Mines. [[Terra]] has carried it for years on a braided cord — worn smooth where a thumb has worried it ten thousand times. In the practices, you held it up and listened through the hole to find the resonant points in stone, the places where the membrane between physical and adjacent was thin enough to hear through. She understood this as technique. In Aenath, where the adjacent is not a metaphor, the stone started working properly.

## Description

Dark grey granite, roughly egg-sized, with a natural hole bored through it by long-dead water. The cord through it is plain — re-knotted several times over the years, the original ends impossible to identify. There is nothing about it that announces itself. That is, in retrospect, the point.

When held up to the eye and looked through, the view on the other side is slightly wrong. Not distorted — just off by half a heartbeat, as if the light arriving through the hole left from a different moment than the light arriving everywhere else.

*It does not glow. It does not hum. It listens.*

### Tier 1 — The Thin Place *(L4)*

**Listening *(passive)*:** Terra cannot be surprised while the stone is worn. She has Advantage on Perception checks that rely on hearing. The stone reads the room before she consciously does.

**The Gap *(passive)*:** Terra ignores half cover and three-quarters cover on all ranged weapon attacks. The arrow finds the thin place. Distance and obstacle both have gaps in them; she simply sees them.

**Threshold *(1/Long Rest)*:** As a Bonus Action, Terra partially slips through the membrane — teleporting up to 15 ft to an unoccupied space she can see. She does not pass through obstacles. She simply is not where she was.

---

### Tier 2 — The Adjacent *(L5 — current)*

**Fey Echo *(passive, 1/turn)*:** After Terra deals Psychic damage to a creature (from Dreadful Strikes or any source), that creature is Rattled until the start of her next turn — they have Disadvantage on their next attack roll. The wound in the mind lingers a half-moment longer than the wound in the flesh.

**Fault Finding *(passive, 1/turn)*:** When Terra hits a creature with a ranged weapon attack, the creature must make a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Wisdom modifier) or be pushed 5 ft in any direction Terra chooses. The shot finds unstable footing. Not force — fault lines.

**First Signal *(1/day)*:** The stone vibrates once, faintly. A voice answers through it — measured, certain, dressed in a register she recognizes. The Unbound, or something wearing his pattern. It answers one question. It always knows more than it says.

---

### Tier 3 — The Seventh Level *(L9)*

**Threshold *(upgraded)*:** Threshold now recharges on a Short or Long Rest and the range increases to 30 ft. After arriving, Terra has Advantage on her first attack roll before the end of that turn. When she uses Threshold, she may designate the arrival point as an **anchor** — once before her next Long Rest, she may return to that anchor point as a Reaction.

**Unveiled *(passive)*:** A creature hit by Terra's ranged weapon attack cannot benefit from being invisible or Hidden against her until the start of her next turn. The stone reads where they are even when her eyes cannot.

**The Knock *(1/day)*:** Terra holds the stone up and listens through it. One of the following effects:

- *Locate:* Terra senses the exact position of every invisible or hidden creature within 60 ft for 1 round.
- *Unravel:* One creature Terra can see must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Wisdom modifier) or be Incapacitated until the end of their next turn. The membrane thins too fast for their mind.
- *Breach:* A 5-ft opening appears in one non-magical solid barrier for 1 minute.
