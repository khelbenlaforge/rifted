The one CCR record he still owned. Side A worn soft with plays, the label half rubbed to blank. He never packed the record. There wasn't time for that, and there wasn't really a decision to make — it was simply already in his hands, the same as it was always in his hands on the harder nights. The runout groove past the last track is scarred deeper than the pressing should allow, decades of a needle sitting in it long after the song ended and nobody got up to lift the arm.

## Description

A plain black disc, unsleeved, kept close, though "safe" was never really the word for it. The scratch in the runout groove catches the light differently depending on how long it's been since Mike last held still. He doesn't play it. He doesn't need to. The groove already knows the shape of the silence that follows the last note.

### Tier 1 — Runout Groove *(L3 — current)*

**Second Pass *(passive)*:** When Flurry of Blows lands a second hit on the same target this turn, that hit deals an extra 1d6 necrotic damage. The needle catches the groove again, harder than the first pass.

**Already Gone *(passive)*:** When Mike uses Step of the Wind, he can also move through the space of any creature his size or smaller, treating it as difficult terrain rather than an obstacle. He isn't just faster than the fight. He's already somewhere it hasn't caught up to yet.

**The Unfinished Panel *(passive)*:** Mike has advantage on Investigation checks made to notice when something in view has been deliberately altered, arranged, or tampered with. Twenty years of composing a frame means he can spot the instant a scene has been arranged to read a certain way.

**Cut to Black *(1/Short Rest, Reaction)*:** When Mike drops below half his hit point maximum for the first time in a fight, he can use his Reaction to become Invisible until the start of his next turn. Getting caught losing was never on the table. He vanishes before anyone can watch him go down — an old habit, older than Aenath, back when he'd close the laptop before anyone saw an unfinished draft.
