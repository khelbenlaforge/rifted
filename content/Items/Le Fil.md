> # Le Fil

The package arrived when he was eleven — delivered to his father's house by a carrier who didn't wait to be thanked. Inside was something practical, already spent, already forgotten. Around it was a cord.

Fine silk, the technique somewhere between French and something older he never identified. Not useful. Not worth taking. His father's new wife put it on the windowsill and forgot it. Regius took it the same week, tied it around his wrist, and kept it there for years — through school, through the farmwork, through the visa application and the flight to Saint Barthélemy.

At the resort he started tying it around whatever he kept close. The knife he used to open wine crates. The long pen he kept in his breast pocket. Not for any reason he could articulate. Just the habit of keeping his hands near the only physical proof that someone had bothered to send something.

In Aenath, the cord began to pulse.

## Description

A length of braided silk cord, the colour of old ivory, neither fraying nor dirty despite decades of handling. The braiding pattern is fine and consistent — skilled work. At one end, a small loop is formed, the tail tucked back through itself with a technique that has no obvious knot and cannot be untied. At the other end, a clean cut, sealed against unravelling.

Wrapped around a weapon's grip, it seats itself. The loop cinches without being cinched. It becomes the grip — the weapon becomes his.

*Le fil* — French for the thread, the edge of a blade, the line of descent. He didn't know this until Aenath. He still isn't sure which meaning applies.

## Recent Discoveries

Regius tried speaking directly to Le Fil and got nothing back. But when he brought it to [[Blackstaff Mansion]], the unnamed [[Blackstaff Brother]] handled it and immediately sensed that the cord was soul matter rather than ordinary physical matter. He asked Regius how he had materialized something like that out of his own body, implying that Le Fil may originate from within Regius himself instead of coming purely from his mother's side of the family.

## Mechanics

**Carrier:** [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Regius]]
**Attunement:** Required (Regius only)

When Regius attunes to Le Fil, he designates one weapon he is holding. That weapon gains all of Le Fil's mechanical benefits for as long as he remains attuned and holds it. If the weapon is lost or destroyed, Regius can designate a new weapon during a Short or Long Rest.

Le Fil grows in power as Regius levels.

### Tier 1 — What Was Kept *(L4)*

**+1** to attack and damage rolls with the designated weapon.

**Echo's Grip *(passive)*:** When Regius makes an attack from his Manifest Echo's position (Unleash Incarnation), that attack deals an extra 1d6 force damage. The echo holds the same weapon. Le Fil does not distinguish between the hands.

**Present Absence *(passive)*:** While Regius's Manifest Echo is active within 30 ft, he gains a +1 bonus to AC. Enemies cannot determine which silhouette is the real threat. The cord is on the weapon; the echo holds the copy. Neither answer is wrong.

**One Opening *(1/Long Rest)*:** When Regius rolls a 1 on an attack roll, he may reroll and must use the new result.

---

### Tier 2 — The Distance *(L5, current)*

**+2** to attack and damage rolls.

**Split Strike *(passive)*:** When Regius swaps places with his Manifest Echo, his first attack with the designated weapon before the end of his turn deals an extra 1d8 damage. The real one arrives. The echo was already there.

**E's Direction *(1/day)*:** Regius holds the free end of Le Fil and focuses. The cord tightens fractionally, then tilts — pulled toward something he cannot yet name. He learns whether anyone connected to his mother's bloodline is currently within 1 mile (tightens sharply), 10 miles (tightens slightly), or farther (barely moves). He also senses a direction. The information is always accurate. He does not know how.

---

### Tier 3 — Both Sides *(L9)*

**+3** to attack and damage rolls.

**Both Hands *(passive)*:** Once per turn, when Regius has his Manifest Echo active and within 30 ft, he may make one attack from the echo's position without spending an Unleash Incarnation charge. This is in addition to any Unleash Incarnation attacks he makes.

**The Bearing *(1/day)*:** When Regius would be reduced to 0 HP, he can use his Reaction. His echo absorbs the blow instead — it takes the damage and is immediately destroyed, and Regius drops to 1 HP. If his echo is not currently active, Le Fil manifests one for a single instant to receive the impact (it does not persist afterward). The thread holds.
