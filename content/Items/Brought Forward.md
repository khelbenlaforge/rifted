They were the last thing she put on every morning before the mirror stopped agreeing with her — narrow, rectangular, a prescription strong enough that the world without them was a smear of shapes she'd long since stopped trying to name. Twenty-two years of ledgers read through this exact correction, the same frame reglazed rather than replaced, because Risma did not believe in changing what already worked. The light coming through the lenses the Monday everything rearranged itself was not Singapore light. The lenses did not seem to mind.

## Description

Narrow and rectangular, thin wire frames gone a little dull with age, the yellow cord still doing the job it always did — keeping them from disappearing into a bag or a desk drawer, now keeping them from disappearing into feathers instead. The prescription hasn't changed and neither has the frame; only the cord's drape has, looped down over plumage where it used to sit against a collarbone. When Risma looks at something and means to see it — really see it, not just glance past it — the lenses catch a thin, precise line of pale gold light along the inside edge, gone before anyone else is sure they saw it. It looks, for exactly that instant, like a column underlined and totaled.

### Tier 1 — Opening Balance *(L3 — current)*

**Line Item *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Risma deals damage to a creature with a spell, that attack deals an extra 1d6 psychic damage. Small, precise, and always exactly where it's supposed to be.

**Doesn't Balance *(passive)*:** The first time each turn a creature fails a saving throw against a spell Risma casts, it has disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the start of Risma's next turn. The lenses find the exact place a stance doesn't balance, the same way she used to find the line in a ledger that didn't.

**A Second Set of Books *(passive)*:** Risma has advantage on Intelligence checks made to accurately appraise the value of goods, or to uncover hidden financial or logistical connections in ledgers, contracts, and correspondence. She is proficient with a Forgery Kit if she isn't already — reading a false entry and writing one have always drawn on the same skill.

**Reconciled *(1/Long Rest, no action required)*:** Once during a Long Rest, Risma recovers one expended spell slot of 1st level. She always finds a way to make the books balance by morning.
