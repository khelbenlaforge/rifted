It clipped onto everything — a laptop, a bathroom mirror, a tripod bought used off Carousell — and never once cared whether what it lit was true. Twenty-three thousand followers learned to trust a face lit exactly like this one. She still isn't sure if crossing over made the trust more deserved, or just louder.

## Description

A ring-shaped halo of cold LED bulbs clipped to a battered accordion arm, small enough to fit in a coat pocket. The bulbs have never run on ordinary current, not even before the crossing — a good ring light runs on batteries and a prayer. It switches on now at a thought, and the light it throws makes everyone standing in it look a little better — herself especially.

### Tier 1 — Golden Hour *(L3 — current)*

**Filtered *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Sabina forces a saving throw with a spell, that spell deals an extra 1d6 psychic damage. Nothing gets through unedited, including the hit itself.

**Second Take *(passive)*:** The first time each turn a creature fails a saving throw against a spell Sabina casts, its AC is reduced by 2 until the start of her next turn. The angle was never flattering to begin with.

**On Brand *(passive)*:** Sabina has advantage on checks made to maintain a curated appearance, a disguise, or a cover story. She's been staying in character since long before any of this was literal.

**Going Live *(1/Short Rest, Bonus Action)*:** The ring light flares. Up to three creatures of Sabina's choice within 30 feet that can see her must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be unable to take Reactions until the start of her next turn. Look at her. Not them.
