> # Nyala

A family keris that crossed with [[Erwina Lesmana]] from Balikpapan Bay. In Indonesia it sat in a cloth-wrapped box at the top of a grandmother's wardrobe — worn at ceremonies, never drawn in anger. When Layla's light took Erwina, it came with them.

In Aenath, the *pamor* awakened.

## Description

Short and wavy-bladed, the iron dark with an iridescent sheen that moves across the surface in strong light — the way oil sits on dark water. The hilt is wrapped in reddish-brown cord, the grip worn down at one spot where a hand has rested on it, generation after generation. There is no maker's mark. There doesn't need to be.

When Erwina commits fully — when the conviction is clean and real — the pamor ignites with blue-white flame along the blade's waves. The fire doesn't char allies. It doesn't ask permission.

*"It's not my weapon. It's my grandmother's. I just kept it safe."*

## Mechanics

**Carrier:** [[Erwina Lesmana]]
**Attunement:** Required (Erwina only)
**Type:** Keris (scimitar — 1d6 slashing, finesse, light; serves as spellcasting focus)

The keris grows in power as Erwina levels. The *pamor* within it responds to conviction — its flame burns hotter when Erwina means what they're doing.

### Tier 1 — The Pamor Wakes *(current)*

**+1** to attack and damage rolls.

**Undying Flame *(1/Short Rest)*:** Erwina may make one Blade Flourish without expending a Bardic Inspiration die. The pamor provides the charge. The flame doesn't ask if there's enough left to fight — it goes.

**Pamor Brand *(passive)*:** When Erwina hits a creature with Nyala, the impact leaves a brand of blue-white fire. That creature has disadvantage on the next Concentration saving throw it makes before the start of Erwina's next turn. The flame doesn't always burn flesh — it disrupts focus, distracts, unsettles.

**Conviction's Edge *(passive)*:** While Erwina is at or below half their maximum HP, they add their Charisma modifier to the damage roll of each hit with Nyala, even if they don't normally add it to the roll. The keris burns hotter when things are desperate.

---

### Tier 2 — The Rising Tide *(L5)*

**+2** to attack and damage rolls.

**Rising Current:** When Erwina uses Mobile Flourish and moves to a new position, each hostile creature she passed adjacent to during that movement takes fire damage equal to the Bardic Inspiration die result. The same roll that pushes the target burns the path behind her.

**Standing Wave:** When Erwina uses Defensive Flourish, the AC bonus applies to Erwina AND one creature within 5 ft she can see. She can spend herself into a shield for someone else.

---

### Tier 3 — Burning Wake *(L9)*

**+3** to attack and damage rolls.

**Blinding Flame:** Pamor Brand upgrades — in addition to disadvantage on Concentration saves, the branded creature also has disadvantage on its next attack roll against Erwina before the start of her next turn. The fire is in their eyes as well as their focus.

**Grandmother's Knowing *(1/Long Rest)*:** Erwina holds the keris in both hands and asks it a question. The pamor answers in sensation rather than words: warmth for *yes, pursue this* — cold for *no, turn back* — a vibration like a plucked string for *the answer is already in you*. The answer is honest, drawing on whatever the pamor has witnessed and understood. The keris won't answer tactical questions, but it will answer questions of trust, direction, and conviction. The answer doesn't guarantee outcome — it guarantees that whatever the pamor has seen points that way.
