A cheap folding knife, the kind sold in a Moscow street market for cash and no questions. Nanaya carried it the whole assignment — not for show, the way some men carry a blade to be seen carrying it, but because he'd used it before and would use it again. It came through the Rift with him. Nothing else from that assignment did. He named it *ura no kao* — 裏の顔, the face behind the face. He's had more names than he can count. The knife's the one thing that was never any of them.

## Description

A worn steel folding knife, the handle wrapped in tape gone soft and grey with handling. No flash, no ceremony, which is exactly why it's still in his hand after everything else got left behind. When Nanaya opens it in a fight, the blade doesn't gleam; it goes matte, like something that's already decided not to be noticed.

### Tier 1 — Unopened Case *(L3 — current)*

**+1** to attack and damage rolls.

**The Mark Never Sees It Coming *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Nanaya hits a creature with a weapon attack roll made with advantage, he deals an extra 1d6 poison damage. Every face he wears buys him one clean opening. He's never needed more than that.

**Vanishing Point *(passive)*:** Once per turn, when a creature misses Nanaya with an attack, he can immediately move up to 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. He's spent a career not being where the last person who looked expects him to still be.

**Cold Read *(1/Short Rest, action)*:** Nanaya studies one creature he can see and knows, with certainty, whether it is lying to him right now — no detail on what the truth is, just whether he's being played. He read marks before he read reports; the instinct never left.

**No File On Record *(passive, narrative only)*:** A creature that has never met Nanaya before struggles to recall specific details about him afterward — his face, his voice, what he actually said. He's a man built to be remembered as no one, and the knife just makes sure of it.
