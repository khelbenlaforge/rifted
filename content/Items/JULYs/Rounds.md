## Description

A small tin, dented on one corner from a decade riding in a coat pocket, its lid gone soft at the hinge from being worked one-handed in the dark. It still smells faintly of things it shouldn't—dry biscuit, a scrap of fish, whatever he'd scraped together that week—long after whatever was in it is gone. He never named the cats he fed from it. Naming them would have made it a thing he did on purpose, instead of just a thing he did, the same as everything else he was ever quietly good at. It came through the crossing in his pocket, same as it always had, and he still can't say why it, of all things, made the trip.

### Tier 1—Last House on the Round *(L3—current)*

**Second Helping *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Ruairí hits a creature with a spell attack, he deals an extra 1d6 damage of the same type as the attack. There was always a bit more to go around, if you knew where to look for it.

**Steady Hands *(passive)*:** Ruairí has advantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration. He spent years not letting go of what needed holding. The habit didn't leave him just because the job did.

**A Nose For It *(passive)*:** Ruairí has advantage on Animal Handling checks. Something about him has always read as safe to the things that can't ask.

**Called In *(1/Short Rest, no action required)*:** When Ruairí or a creature within 30 feet of him fails a saving throw, he can call in a favor from something that owes the Feywild one, forcing a reroll of that save—the new result is used, even if it's worse. He couldn't tell you who he owes, or who owes him. Somebody's keeping count regardless.
