General Ren gave it to him for one job: run ahead, find the safe road, and blow it twice when the way was clear. For years, Kai always blew it twice. The last time he was sure enough not to check the road himself, he didn't get the chance to blow it at all. *Chàng tōng wú zǔ* — 畅通无阻, the road ahead is clear, nothing blocking the way. It's the signal he was supposed to give, not a wish someone says over him.

## Description

A small tin whistle on a length of worn cord, dented in one spot from a fall that should have cracked it and didn't. Kai still carries it, though he hasn't tried to sound it since the ambush — some things you don't test twice.

### Tier 1 — Ahead of the Column *(L3 — current)*

**First Move *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Kai hits a creature with an attack roll made with advantage, he deals an extra 1d6 damage of the same type as the attack. The first strike was always the one that mattered — everything after it was cleanup.

**Reading the Ground *(passive)*:** Kai treats difficult terrain as normal terrain for himself, and automatically senses (without a check, though he still needs to search to disarm or avoid) the presence of traps and natural hazards within 30 feet. He was the man who found the safe road. That part of him never stopped working.

**Second-Guess *(1/Short Rest)*:** Before making an Investigation, Perception, or Survival check to judge whether a route, plan, or position is safe, Kai can choose to have advantage on that check. He doesn't trust his first read anymore, so the whistle gives him a second one.

**Ren's Silence *(passive, narrative only)*:** No matter how hard Kai blows it, the whistle makes no sound — hasn't since the ambush, though he's never once considered leaving it behind.
