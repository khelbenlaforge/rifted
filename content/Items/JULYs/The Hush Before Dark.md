Coral gave it to her the year the brand consultants decided pearls photographed better than silver — plain, unbranded, nothing a stylist would have chosen. Odette wore it under her sleeve for every shoot after that, the one thing on her body nobody else had picked. She still doesn't know how it ended up on her wrist when the Rift took her instead of staying in a drawer in London.

## Description

A thin silver bracelet, the kind of plain that reads as deliberate once you notice how carefully it's kept. It holds light a beat longer than it should, the way the sky does in the last grey minute before full dark. When Odette steps between danger and someone else, the metal cools against her skin — a small, steady pressure, like a hand on her wrist confirming the instinct wasn't imagined.

### Tier 1 — First Watch *(L3 — current)*

**Standing Between *(passive)*:** The first time each turn a creature within 5 feet of Odette is hit by an attack, the next healing spell she casts before the end of her next turn restores an extra 1d6 hit points. She's spent her whole life noticing danger a half-second before anyone else does. Now it means something.

**Step In Front *(passive)*:** The first attack roll each round made against a creature within 5 feet of Odette, by an attacker who is also within 5 feet of her, has disadvantage. Nobody taught her this one — it's instinct, the one thing on her body nobody scripted.

**Reading the Room *(1/Short Rest, action)*:** Odette studies one creature she can see and knows, and learns with certainty whether it is being sincere or performing in this exact moment — nothing more specific than that. A decade of practiced smiles taught her exactly what a performance looks like from the inside.

**Coral's Thread *(passive, narrative only)*:** The bracelet warms, faintly, whenever Odette makes a choice that's genuinely her own rather than what was expected of her. She's noticed, and hasn't found a way to bring it up yet.
