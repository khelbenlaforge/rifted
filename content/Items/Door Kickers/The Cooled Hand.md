## Description

A palm-sized piece of dark glass still shaped like a hand, fingers pressed flat. It stays faintly warm to the touch and tugs toward something in the southwest, as if a tether ran from it.

## Properties

**Calm Ground.** Once per dawn, as a Bonus Action, you press the hand to the earth. Until the end of your next turn, allies within 10 feet of you ignore Difficult Terrain from rock, rubble and ash, and can't be knocked Prone.

**Heat Sink.** Once per dawn, as a Reaction when you take fire damage, you gain Resistance to that instance of fire damage, and the glass warms.

An Identify cast on it shows the same energy signature as the claw-shaped cord on the dead druid's belt, from which five threads of energy run to five other things.

## Significance

The right hand of the barefoot druid [[Nanuk]] killed at dawn on 7 Nightal, which cooled to dark glass and stayed fused to the bone at the wrist. Session 32: the party remembered that [[Ember]] had said some Lavaliths tattoo a flame on the underarm, and the body confirmed the dead man was one. [[Terra]] won it with a natural 20 on Investigation while searching the body.

[[Min Ji-ah]]'s Identify tied it to the cord that bears [[Malar]]'s symbol. At the colourless clearing north of [[The Road North|the Road]], the hand kept the party on their feet when a druid's push of rock hit them.
