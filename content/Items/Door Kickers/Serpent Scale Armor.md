## Description

A suit of scale mail that catches torchlight the wrong way, every plate a little too smooth and a little too iridescent to have come off any snake Nanuk's ever skinned. It moves like it isn't there. The leather backing underneath is stiff enough to hold a shape, but the scales themselves give with the body wearing them instead of against it.

## Properties

While wearing this armor, the wearer can apply their full Dexterity modifier when determining their Armor Class, instead of the usual +2 maximum for medium armor. It imposes no disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks, unusual for anything this heavy.

## Significance

Rolled out of [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Characters/NPCs/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Citrine District/Istar Silverlock|Istar Silverlock]]'s back-pay for the Guild's cleared debts, the same reward table that handed [[Min Ji-ah]] her [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Items/Door Kickers/Piwafwi of Fire Resistance|Piwafwi]] and [[Gill Bates]] his [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Items/Door Kickers/Spell-Refueling Ring|Spell-Refueling Ring]]. [[Nanuk]] took the better draw of the four, and the pull forced a correction the table had needed for a while: his sheet had been under-calculating his Armor Class, base 14 rather than the 17 it had listed. Corrected, the armor puts him at 19.
