## Description

A cloak of spider-silk, dark and close-woven, sticky under the fingers the way old webbing gets when it's been left too long in a corner. Its color shifts to match whatever stands behind it, not quickly, the way a chameleon moves, but the slow drift of something that was never in a hurry to be seen. A history check at the reward table placed the weave right away: drow work, made by hands that haven't needed sunlight in longer than the cloak itself has existed.

## Properties

While worn, the wearer has resistance to fire damage. While worn with the hood up, Wisdom (Perception) checks made to see the wearer have disadvantage, and the wearer has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide, the cloak's color shift doing the work. Raising or lowering the hood takes an action.

The cloak loses its magic after one hour of uninterrupted exposure to sunlight. **At this table:** a single minute back in darkness resets that clock, the table's own reading of the cloak's wording, not a rule printed on the item itself, settled after an argument over whether the reset needed the full hour undone or just a break in the exposure.

## Significance

Rolled out of Istar Silverlock's back-pay for the Guild's cleared debts, alongside armor for [[Regius]] and [[Nanuk]] and three thousand gold for the party. [[Min Ji-ah]] took the better end of that draw, a cloak built for people who don't see daylight, worn by a party about to leave the city and head into a wall that changes what sound does behind it.
