## Description

A fossil fragment the size of a thumb, pale grey with a faint amber vein running through it. [[Shen]] had it in his jacket pocket during the crossing — a Kunming field sample, unlabeled, from a stratum that shouldn't have produced anything. It has been warm since the moment he arrived in Aenath. Not hot. Not feverish. Warm, the way a held stone eventually becomes warm — except it has not cooled once in all the time since.

His field notes now have margin annotations he did not write. They are in his own handwriting.

He has checked the dates. Some of the annotations precede observations he has not yet made.

He has not mentioned this to anyone.

### Passives (All Tiers)

**Deep Time.** Shen senses the approximate age of any object or surface he touches. This is instinct, always present, never requested. The patron has not acknowledged it as a gift.

**Ancient Repository.** Advantage on Intelligence (History) and Intelligence (Arcana) checks relating to ancient, extinct, or undead creatures.

---

### Tier 1 — The Sample *(L4)*

**Active (1/Long Rest — choose one).**

Shen may consult the patron once per long rest. The patron answers. It does not explain how.

> **Speak with Dead.** As the 3rd-level spell. The patron provides the conduit. The voice that answers sounds older than the corpse — not just the individual, but the species.

> **Classify.** Shen observes a creature for one round. He learns: its creature type, whether its CR is lower, equal, or higher than his level (within 2), and one immunity or resistance of the DM's choice. He may relay this to all allies within 30 ft via Awakened Mind as a free action. Until the start of his next turn, attack rolls against that creature score a critical hit on a roll of 19–20 — the patron annotated the fault lines. The patron delivers its findings in dry academic notation. It has opinions about the taxonomy.

> **Strata.** Shen touches any object or surface and receives a 6-second psychic flash of the most significant event that ever occurred at that location — not the most recent, not the most violent: the most significant by the patron's reckoning, which may not match Shen's. He must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of his next turn — the sensation simply overwhelms him.

---

### Tier 2 — The Annotations Lengthen *(L5 — current)*

All Tier 1 abilities, plus:

**Active uses increase to 2/Long Rest.**

**Classify (upgraded).** Shen now learns: creature type, CR bracket, one immunity or resistance, and one behavioral fact ("it prefers isolated targets," "it is not where it seems to be," "this one is afraid"). The crit window on 19–20 extends until the start of Shen's *second* next turn. The patron chooses which behavioral fact to share. It is always accurate. It is not always the most immediately useful one.

**New active — Excavation.** Shen spends 1 minute in contact with a surface or object. He may ask the patron three yes/no questions about what occurred at this location. The patron answers in marginalia that appear in Shen's notes. Answers are accurate. They are not always immediately legible.

---

### Tier 3 — It Knows You're Looking *(L9)*

The fossil has changed. Hairline cracks radiate from the amber vein. The vein pulses, faintly, when Shen is not watching it directly.

All Tier 2 abilities, plus:

**Active uses increase to 3/Long Rest.**

**Initiates Contact *(passive, 1/day)*.** Without consuming an active use, the patron leaves an unsolicited annotation in Shen's notes. It is always accurate. It is never comfortable. The DM delivers this as a written note to Shen's player — not announced aloud.

**New active — Taxonomy.** Shen meditates for 10 minutes on a creature type or specific individual he has directly observed at any point. No remains required. The patron cross-references its record. What it provides depends on what it knows best.

*Non-humanoid creature types (beasts, undead, fiends, aberrations, monstrosities, and similar):* The patron provides a complete analytical profile — resistances, immunities, condition immunities, damage vulnerabilities, key abilities, approximate HP range, and worst saving throw. The DM reads the relevant sections of the stat block aloud. For 24 hours, Shen and any ally he briefs (via Awakened Mind or 1 minute of spoken explanation) have advantage on saving throws against that creature type's abilities.

*Humanoids (by type or specific individual Shen has directly observed):* The patron's record on humanoids is behavioral, not biological. It provides a psychological profile — what this creature or type fears, what motivates it, who it serves, and one piece of information it is actively concealing. The DM delivers this as a patron annotation in character. For 24 hours, the first time each humanoid target of that type makes a saving throw against one of Shen's enchantment or illusion spells, they do so with disadvantage.

The patron finds incompleteness in a taxonomy unacceptable.

**Strata: Outward Projection *(new use)*.** Shen can direct Strata at a creature he can see within 60 ft as an Action (consuming one active use). Rather than receiving the psychic flash himself, he directs it outward — flooding the target with the weight of the most significant moment in its existence. The target makes a Wisdom saving throw (DC = Shen's spell save DC). On a failed save: 4d10 psychic damage and Incapacitated until the end of its next turn. On a successful save: 2d10 psychic damage. Shen receives no information from this use. The Strata self-targeting use retains its original mechanics, now with a DC 15 Constitution saving throw; on a failed save, Shen is paralyzed rather than stunned until the end of his next turn.

### Final Form — Classification Complete *(L13+)*

The specimen is no longer grey. It is amber throughout. It generates its own heat. On careful examination, it is breathing.

**The Name *(1/Long Rest)*.** Once Shen knows the patron's name — the DM determines when it surfaces — he may invoke it directly. When he does, one expended active use is restored. The patron responds to its name. It does not explain what this costs it. Once Shen knows the name, he cannot unknow it.

**Deep Record *(1/Long Rest, 1-minute meditation)*.** Shen may ask the patron one question about any person, creature, location, object, faction, or event — past or present — in Aenath. There is no restriction on subject matter. The restriction is on what the patron has actually observed, which is not the same as omniscience, but is extensive: it has been present in Aenath longer than any living thing Shen has encountered. The answer arrives as marginalia, formatted as a field report, in third person, always "the observer." It has never replied "I don't know." Shen has not yet determined whether this is because it always knows, or because it always answers *something*.

**Temporal Anchor *(Reaction, 1/Long Rest — independent of Deep Record)*.** When Shen or any ally he can see would be reduced to 0 HP, Shen may use his reaction to invoke the patron. The patron removes that creature from the timestream for 1 minute (10 rounds) — they vanish from initiative, suspended, unreachable. At the end of that minute, they return to their exact position with 1 HP. They have no memory of the interval. The patron does not explain what happened during it.

**The Specimen *(passive)*.** The specimen is now a living arcane focus. Shen's spell save DC increases by 1 while attuned. Additionally, once per long rest, when Shen fails a concentration saving throw, he may treat it as a success instead — the specimen sustains the connection when his mind cannot. It is no longer a fossil. No stratum produced it. No taxonomy contains it. Whatever it is, it has been alive since Kunming — Shen simply did not have the instruments to detect it.

## Significance

The specimen has no classification in any published taxonomy. Shen submitted it to two colleagues before the crossing and received no response — not a negative response. No response at all. Whatever it is, it is not from any stratum that should have been inhabited.

The patron chose [[Shen]] for reasons it has not shared — and has not been asked, because the question feels like the wrong question. A palaeontologist who studies what was, trained to sit with things that take millions of years to matter, more comfortable with the dead than with the living — this is not a coincidence the patron would leave to chance.
