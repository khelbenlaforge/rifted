## Description

A whistle carved from transparent crystal, resembling a tiny dragon curled up like a snail. The name Night Caller is etched into it in Dwarvish runes.

## Properties

Blown in darkness or under the night sky, the whistle casts *Animate Dead* on a single target, which can be affected through up to ten feet of soft earth or similar material. If it is, the animated creature takes a minute to claw its way to the surface. Once the whistle has animated a creature this way, it can't do so again for seven days. Once every 24 hours, the wielder can also blow it to reassert control over one or two creatures already animated with it.

A DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana or History) check recalls that duergar craftsmen made several whistles like this one, long ago, for various groups whose purposes are no longer recorded.

## Significance

Carried for some time by [[Jonathan Jordan]] before he traded it to [[Min Ji-ah]] in Crystal City, just before the party left the city in Session 29, leaving [[Augustus Kennedy|Augustus]]'s old blade, [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Items/Door Kickers/Toothpick|Toothpick]], in his keeping in exchange. Where JJ found it in the first place isn't recorded anywhere the party's own notes reach.
