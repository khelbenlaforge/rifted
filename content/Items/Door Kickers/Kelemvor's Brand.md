## Description

It appeared the morning after [[Augustus Kennedy]] returned from the prequel vision — etched into the warforged plating along his left shoulder and down his forearm in lines too deliberate to be damage, too old to be anything Aenath made. The mark resembles a panther mid-leap, rendered in the style of something pre-heraldic: crude and alive. It does not glow. In darkness, it is warm.

When activated, the lines deepen. The metal around them hums faintly. Something behind Augie's eyes goes very still, as if a much older thing has noticed it is being asked to wake.

The brand vanishes the instant he kills, all at once, with no fade to it. Whatever lives in it does not countenance death. It will return after a long rest, traced back into the plating like it was always there.

## Properties

**Activation (Bonus Action):** Augie awakens the brand. Fur pushes through gaps in the warforged plating. Claws extend. Fangs drop. He is recognizably Augie, and recognizably wrong.

**Duration:** The brand stays active until the extinction clause fires. Augie cannot voluntarily deactivate it at Tiers 1–2. If combat ends without a kill, he remains partially transformed — loss of control saves still apply — until the clause fires or he takes a long rest. At Tier 3, he may spend an Action to force deactivation.

**Loss of Control:** At the start of each turn while the brand is active, Augie makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, he must attack the nearest visible enemy — he cannot pick his target freely. He never targets allies; the resonance recognizes them. DC scales by tier.

**Extinction Clause:** The brand deactivates the instant Augie reduces a creature to 0 HP. All benefits end immediately. Each deactivation spends one extinction charge (see per-tier limits). When all charges are spent, the brand is locked out until his next long rest. The curse does not permit a kill without cost — if he wants to preserve his charges, he must knock out, restrain, or pass enemies to his party.

### Tier 1 — The First Pull *(L4)*

**Extinction Charges:** 1 per long rest.

**Speed** +10 ft.

**Keen Smell:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on scent or hearing.

**Claw Strike:** Melee attacks deal an additional 1d6 slashing damage.

**Loss of Control:** DC 12 Wisdom save at the start of each turn.

**Drawbacks:**
- Cannot cast spells of 2nd level or higher while the brand is active.
- Disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks.

**Extinction Backlash:** Speed reduced to 0 until the end of Augie's next turn.

**Armor:** Non-magical armor strains but holds.

---

### Tier 2 — The Pounce *(L5 — current)*

**Extinction Charges:** 2 per long rest.

All Tier 1 abilities, plus:

**Claw Strike:** Damage increases to 1d8 slashing.

**Pounce:** If Augie moves at least 20 ft straight toward a target and hits it with a melee attack on the same turn, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Strength modifier) or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, Augie can make one Bite attack against it as a bonus action (1d6 + Strength modifier piercing).

**Loss of Control:** DC 12 (unchanged).

**Extinction Backlash:** Disadvantage on all attack rolls and saving throws until the end of Augie's next turn.

**Armor:** Non-magical armor strains but holds.

---

### Tier 3 — The Brand Resists *(L9)*

**Extinction Charges:** 3 per long rest.

All Tier 2 abilities, plus:

**Speed** +20 ft (replaces +10 ft).

**Claw Strike:** Damage increases to 1d10 slashing.

**Darkvision** 60 ft while the brand is active.

**Climbing Speed** 40 ft while the brand is active.

**Blood Frenzy:** Advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that is below half its hit point maximum.

**Loss of Control:** DC 14. On a failure, Augie must also spend his full movement toward the nearest enemy — the body commits before the mind does.

**Drawbacks (additional):**
- Disadvantage on Intelligence checks while the brand is active.
- Brand resists deactivation — the only voluntary exit is spending an Action to wrench himself out (no extinction charge spent; no backlash).

**Extinction Backlash:** Disadvantage on all attack rolls and saving throws until the end of Augie's next turn, plus 1 level of Exhaustion lasting until his next long rest.

**Armor:** Non-magical external armor splits open and is non-functional for the duration. Requires approximately one hour of repair afterward.

### Final Form — The Panther *(L13+)*

*1/Long Rest. Activation requires an Action. Concentration, up to 1 minute.*

The warforged plating doesn't break — it flows. Augie becomes a Large metal panther. His mass redistributes in seconds: black iron where black fur should be, claws that ring against stone. The brand is gone from his shoulder because it is no longer a mark. It is him.

**Loss of Control:** None. He surrenders fully and paradoxically has complete control. The resonance is finally whole.

**Extinction Clause:** Suspended. The panther form has no prohibition on killing.

**Hit Points:** The panther has a separate hit point pool equal to Augie's hit point maximum at the time of transformation. When the panther form's HP reaches 0, Augie reverts to his warforged form with whatever HP he had before transforming.

**Statistics:** Use the [[Lyonsbane Panther]] stat block as the base, with the following adjustments:
- Proficiency bonus +5 (L13)
- HP replaced as above
- Multiattack matches Augie's normal attack count
- **Drops** Blood Hunger and Trapped Soul — Augie chose this; he is not compelled
- **Retains:** Pounce, Bite (with grapple), Claw, Rend (Recharge 5–6), Speed 50 ft, Climb 40 ft, Darkvision 60 ft, Keen Smell

**Ending the Form:** Action to end, or concentration breaks. Augie returns to warforged form immediately — no unconsciousness, no eruption. That was Kelemvor's version.

**After Transformation:** Augie suffers one level of Exhaustion lasting until his next long rest.

**Armor:** All non-magical armor is destroyed in the transformation.

## Significance

[[Kelemvor Lyonsbane]]'s hereditary curse was never a weapon. His bloodline carried it as punishment for selfishness given flesh. Augie did not inherit it. He inhabited the man who bore it, felt the predatory instinct flood a body that was not his, watched himself pursue an enemy he could not stop himself from hunting. He understood something in that courtyard that Kelemvor had only just learned: that the curse does not care about intent. It cares about the act.

The brand chose him anyway. It does not explain itself.
