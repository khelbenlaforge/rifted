A dented steel hip flask, a duty-belt clip-mark worn pale into one side from years riding a pocket on a job that didn't ask him to bring it home. It crossed over with him mid-pour, and it hasn't poured what he expects since.

## Description

Worn matte from a decade of bad nights it's outlasted, dents mapping every one of them. It still smells faintly of whatever he used to keep in it. When Roberto opens it now, reaching for it on purpose instead of out of habit, what comes out isn't liquor. It's warm, moves like it's alive, and for the length of a swallow tastes like the first clean breath after a bad scene.

### Tier 1 — First Pour *(L3 — current)*

**Chaser *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Roberto deals damage with a spell, that attack deals an extra 1d6 radiant damage. The first time each turn he restores hit points with a spell, that healing is boosted by an extra 1d6. One pour does the work of two.

**Steadying Pull *(passive)*:** Once per round, when Roberto is Bloodied (at or below half his hit point maximum), he can take a pull from the flask as part of another action to gain temporary hit points equal to his cleric level (3). The habit that used to just get him through the night now actually holds a line.

**Working the Scene *(passive)*:** Roberto has advantage on Medicine checks made to assess an injury, a cause of death, or what actually happened to someone. The clinical eye never left; it just has better things to look at now.

**One Real Pull *(1/Short Rest, action)*:** Roberto takes a real drink — not the muscle-memory kind — and picks one creature he can see. For the next minute, any time he looks at that creature, he knows what it's actually feeling underneath whatever it's showing him, no save, no roll. It costs him something to actually look instead of assess and move on, which is rather the point.
