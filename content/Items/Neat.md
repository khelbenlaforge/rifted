A dented steel hip flask, a duty-belt clip-mark worn pale into one side from years riding a pocket on a job that didn't ask her to bring it home. It crossed over with her mid-pour, and it hasn't poured what she expects since.

## Description

Worn matte from a decade of bad nights it's outlasted, dents mapping every one of them. It still smells faintly of whatever she used to keep in it. When Roberto opens it now, reaching for it on purpose instead of out of habit, what comes out isn't liquor. It's warm, moves like it's alive, and for the length of a swallow tastes like the first clean breath after a bad scene.

### Tier 1 — First Pour *(L3 — current)*

**Chaser *(passive)*:** The first time each turn Roberto deals damage with a spell, that attack deals an extra 1d6 radiant damage. The first time each turn she restores hit points with a spell, that healing is boosted by an extra 1d6. One pour does the work of two.

**Steadying Pull *(passive)*:** Once per round, when Roberto is Bloodied (at or below half her hit point maximum), she can take a pull from the flask as part of another action to gain temporary hit points equal to her cleric level (3). The habit that used to just get her through the night now actually holds a line.

**Working the Scene *(passive)*:** Roberto has advantage on Medicine checks made to assess an injury, a cause of death, or what actually happened to someone. The clinical eye never left; it just has better things to look at now.

**One Real Pull *(1/Short Rest, action)*:** Roberto takes a real drink — not the muscle-memory kind — and picks one creature she can see. For the next minute, any time she looks at that creature, she knows what it's actually feeling underneath whatever it's showing her, no save, no roll. It costs her something to actually look instead of assess and move on, which is rather the point.
