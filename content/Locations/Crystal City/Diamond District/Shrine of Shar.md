# Shrine of Shar

> [!infobox|right wikipedia]+
> # Shrine of Shar

The Shrine of Shar is a hidden one-room shrine off Cistern Street in the [[Diamond District]], [[Crystal City]], with a low ceiling and one patch of wall painted flat black — the kind of room nobody looks at twice; it was the site of the failed Rising of the Dark ritual and Mother Ilyra Duskveil's death in Session 27.

Region:: [[Diamond District]]
Controlled By:: Shar cult (unnamed leadership above [[Mother Ilyra Duskveil]])
Status:: active

## Description

Nothing about the alley off Cistern Street suggests a door worth finding — deliberately so. The shrine itself is a single low-ceilinged room, unremarkable, the kind nobody looks at twice. No idol was ever necessary; Shar's faithful don't carve a face for what they worship. One patch of wall, floor to shoulder-height, is painted a matte, light-swallowing black, the kind that reads as absence even with a torch inches from it. Before Session 27, that patch carried a Darkness spell layered over it, thick enough that the room read as bottomless from the doorway. Ember's Dispel Magic broke the spell, and it hasn't been recast since.

## Features

- **The Altar** — a raised stone slab at the room's back, unremarkable stonework, the only thing in the shrine built to hold a person down. This is where the Gravelord was bound during the Rising of the Dark.
- **The Black Wall** — the unlit, matte-black patch anchoring the room's magic; whatever it's for beyond atmosphere and cover for Ilyra's Darkness spell is unconfirmed.
- **The Braziers** — three, snuffed out mid-fight during Session 27, not yet relit.

## Residents

None confirmed living. Mother Ilyra Duskveil ran the shrine as its officiant until her death here in Session 27; whether she lived on-site or simply worked from it is unknown. Four cultists stood with her when the fight began. What happened to them, where they went, and whether any of them answer to someone above Ilyra all remain unconfirmed.

## Current Events

As of Session 27's end: the shrine sits exposed, its ritual interrupted, its officiant dead, and its darkness spell broken. Nobody has secured or investigated it further yet. Whether the cult attempts to reclaim or abandon it is an open DM question.

## Scene Features

**Atmosphere:** Total, unnatural dark under a low ceiling — even relit, the braziers throw light that never quite reaches the black wall at the back. The room holds sound oddly; a whisper carries, a shout doesn't.

**Ambient Effects:** Before Session 27, the Black Wall's Darkness spell filled the room with magical dim-to-total darkness depending on the braziers' state — broken this session by Dispel Magic, not yet restored.
