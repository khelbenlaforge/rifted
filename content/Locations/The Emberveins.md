## Description

A deep, lava-lit cavern strung with black-rock platforms and stone arches, threaded with old quartz veins that still hold a trace of residual light-magic. [[Layla]] has been using it as a repeat arrival point for newcomers pulled to [[Aenath]] — by her own account, one of many she's run through here. Shadow creatures nest in the dark between the platforms.
