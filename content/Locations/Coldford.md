> # Coldford

Coldford is a city on the eastern coast of Corranor, east-northeast of [[Crystal City]], within the [[Azure Tamers]] patrol network — per the vault's Corranor map (`zzz_Attachments/Corranor.png`), it sits further east and slightly south of [[Stormberg]], which is the more directly-north coastal settlement. One of several regional cities in Corranor territory.

A working port-and-freight hub rather than a showpiece — the kind of city that processes people quickly and asks questions later, which makes it a plausible drop point for anyone Layla wants moved fast and forgotten.
