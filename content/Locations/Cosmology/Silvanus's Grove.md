## Description

Legend says [[Silvanus]] calls a worthy soul to his grove, a place that sits behind interplanar gates rather than at any fixed spot on the map—gates that keep shifting open and closed, clustered generally north of [[Stormberg]]. Passage may require some form of sacrifice once a gate is actually found open.

The [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Corranor/Ancients of Lava|Ancients of Lava]] have searched for it for some time; Silvanus has never welcomed any of them.

## Role in the Story

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk|Nanuk]] feels a steady pull toward the Grove, sitting north and north-west since Session 29—confirmed real by the DM, not a red herring. Still open as of Session 30.
