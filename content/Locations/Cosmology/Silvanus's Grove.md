## Description

Legend says [[Silvanus]] calls a worthy soul to his grove.

The [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Corranor/Ancients of Lava|Ancients of Lava]] have searched for it for some time; Silvanus has never welcomed any of them.

## Session 29 Update (The Door Kickers)

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk|Nanuk]] feels a steady pull toward the Grove, sitting north and north-west since Session 29. Still open as of Session 30.

a place that sits behind interplanar gates rather than at any fixed spot on the map—gates that keep shifting open and closed, clustered generally north of [[Stormberg]]. Passage may require some form of sacrifice once a gate is actually found open.

## Session 31 Update (The Door Kickers)

- No gate found yet. After the owlbear's spirit seeped into Nanuk his pull toward the grove is stronger (checks that lean on it are made with advantage). Silvanus told Nanuk to find his way to him. The dawn druid told the party to take him to the grove or die.
