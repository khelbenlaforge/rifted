## Description

One of the Lower Planes, sitting inside the astral barrier alongside the [[Nine Hells]] and Hades. [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]] judges and forwards the dead rather than keeping them, and chaotic evil souls are routed here.
