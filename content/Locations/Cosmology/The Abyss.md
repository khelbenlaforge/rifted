## Description

One of the Lower Planes, an Outer Plane beyond the astral barrier alongside the [[Nine Hells]] and Hades; the [[Temple of Kelemvor]]'s Threshold Gates are the one sanctioned way across. [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]] judges and forwards the dead rather than keeping them, and chaotic evil souls are routed here.
