## Description

One of the Lower Planes, ruled by [[Asmodeus]]. [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]] judges and forwards the dead rather than keeping them, and lawful evil souls are routed here. The [[Temple of Kelemvor]] keeps one of its three Threshold Gates trained on it, and [[The Gravelords]]—the temple's priesthood—hold library accounts of clerics who plane-shifted there in past centuries to police soul trafficking, though whether that enforcement capacity still exists is something the Gravelords don't confirm publicly.
