## Description

One of the Lower Planes, an Outer Plane beyond the astral barrier, ruled by [[Asmodeus]]. [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]] judges and forwards the dead rather than keeping them, and lawful evil souls are routed here. The [[Temple of Kelemvor]] keeps one of its three Threshold Gates trained on it, and [[The Gravelords]]—the temple's priesthood—hold library accounts of clerics who crossed the Gates into it in past centuries to police soul trafficking, though whether that enforcement capacity still exists is something the Gravelords don't confirm publicly.
