## Description

The gods' own realms—everything the astral barrier shuts out. Where the Ethereal Plane, the Shadowfell, and the four elemental planes all sit inside the barrier alongside Aenath itself, the Outer Planes lie entirely beyond it, out of reach for anyone or anything still inside. [[Arborea]] and the [[Astral Sea]] are the two confirmed instances built out so far—Arborea home to [[Corellon]]'s Crescent Grove, the Astral Sea a vast incorporeal realm where over 10,000 souls were seen trailing a colossal entity during the cataclysm that preceded [[The Awakening]].

Neutral and godless souls are destined here under [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]]'s judgment—see [[The Soul Economy]] for the full afterlife routing. Nothing sends them here directly. They wait behind the barrier until their name is read aloud at the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Lore/Feast of the Moon|Feast of the Moon]]'s Deeds of the Dead vigil; an unread name stays stuck.
