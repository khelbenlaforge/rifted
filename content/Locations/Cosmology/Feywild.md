## Description

One of Aenath's echo planes, alongside the Shadowfell—sitting inside the astral barrier, not beyond it. [[Veyris Thantorn]]'s cosmology lesson to [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Min Ji-ah|Min Ji-ah]] names both as echoes of the material world, part of the same lesson that placed the astral barrier as currently stuck in place.

The membrane between the Feywild and the material world is a physical, not metaphorical, thing. [[Terra]]'s [[Hollowstone]]—an Earth object, carried across before anyone understood what it did—works because it was always built for exactly this: listening through the thin place between the physical and the adjacent. In Aenath, where the adjacent is real, the stone started working properly.

## Crossings

At least one Rifted arrival passed through the Feywild on the way in. [[The Exodus/The JULYs/PCs/Ruairí|Ruairí]]'s own crossing snagged on something there first—a detour he still can't fully account for, long enough for the Feywild's own rules to leave a mark on him before it let him go. He came out the other side a changeling, carrying an unsettled bargain with an archfey patron he hasn't identified.
