## Description

One of Aenath's echo planes, alongside the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Cosmology/Shadowfell|Shadowfell]]—sitting inside the astral barrier, not beyond it.

The membrane between the Feywild and the material world is a physical, not metaphorical, thing.

## Session 21 Update (The Door Kickers)

[[Veyris Thantorn]]'s cosmology lesson to [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Min Ji-ah|Min Ji-ah]] names both as echoes of the material world, part of the same lesson that placed the astral barrier as currently stuck in place.

[[Terra]]'s [[Hollowstone]]—an Earth object, carried across before anyone understood what it did—works because it was always built for exactly this: listening through the thin place between the physical and the adjacent. In Aenath, where the adjacent is real, the stone started working properly.

## Session 3 Update (The JULYs)

At least one Rifted arrival passed through the Feywild on the way in. [[The Exodus/The JULYs/PCs/Ruairí|Ruairí]]'s own crossing snagged on something there first—a detour he still can't fully account for, long enough for the Feywild's own rules to leave a mark on him before it let him go. He came out the other side a changeling, carrying an unsettled bargain with an archfey patron he hasn't identified.
