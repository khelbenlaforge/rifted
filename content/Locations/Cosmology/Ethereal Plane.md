## Description

The plane that short-range transit magic actually reaches. [[Veyris Thantorn]]'s reading, out of the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Corranor/Alchemist's Guild|Alchemist's Guild]], is that Misty Step and similar effects only skim the Ethereal—which is why they still work in a world with no long-distance teleportation. The Ethereal sits inside the astral barrier alongside the Shadowfell and the four elemental planes; nothing that reaches past it has anywhere left to reach.
