## Description

The plane that short-range transit magic actually reaches. [[Veyris Thantorn]]'s reading, out of the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Corranor/Alchemist's Guild|Alchemist's Guild]], is that Misty Step and similar effects only skim the Ethereal—which is why they still work in a world with no long-distance teleportation. The Ethereal sits inside the astral barrier alongside the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Cosmology/Shadowfell|Shadowfell]] and the four elemental planes; nothing that reaches past it has anywhere left to reach.

## Session 32 Update (The Door Kickers)

- The party found a gate to the Ethereal Plane: a black pool 10 feet across in a silent hollow, about 70 feet from the tree line, ringed by a perfect circle of frost; sound dies beyond 5 feet; a leaf hung in the air mid-fall; Detect Magic reads only within 30 feet.
- A stone thrown in vanished with no ripple; an arrow on a rope went slack and came back heavy.
- [[Terra]] went in on a rope, froze in fog moving at hurricane speed, and freed herself by casting Misty Step; the rope stayed inside.
- [[Min Ji-ah]] (Arcana 21): it is raw primordial energy that becomes fire, water, life or entropy; it touches the Material Plane, which is why Misty Step slips through it; it is almost unnavigable because direction, sound and space are lost.
- Without Misty Step, escape would have taken about four hours of clawing.
- Nothing about it pointed to the grove of [[Silvanus]].
