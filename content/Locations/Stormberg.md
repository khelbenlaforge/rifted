## Description

A northern port settlement on the [[Corranor]] mainland, sheltered from the region's debilitating storms only by the **Stormwall** — a colossal stone-and-magic barrier the [[Magocracy of Corranor]] has raised and maintained over the last 222 years. When [[Corranor]]'s main island rises airborne, the Stormwall is the only thing standing between the town and violent ethereal and physical storms sweeping down from the north; when the island descends back to the ocean, massive gates in the wall open and the harbor resumes normal traffic. [[Stormberg]] serves as a maritime hub for trade and travel, and as the launch point for arctic research expeditions.

- Confirmed regional city north of [[Crystal City]]
- [[Azure Tamers]] operate in the region
- Mentioned alongside [[Coldford]] in the Session 21 geographic survey
