## Description

Once the greatest center of magic in Faerûn, now a ruin haunted by demons—the elves overreached, and the city became a den of something older and worse. Nobody in the party has set foot there; everything on record comes secondhand.

## Role in the Campaign

Myth Drannor's fall is what gives the [[Knights of Myth Drannor]] their name and their mandate: defend the Dalelands, keep the roads open, work toward the city's eventual restoration. [[Torm of the Dales|Torm]] is one of the Knights; [[Illistyl Elventree]] has personal ties to the community that still lives in the ruin's shadow. The city itself never appears on-screen.
