## Description

A temple in Shadowdale, unremarkable until the night it became the site of a battle between mortals, a fallen god, and a dying goddess's last fragment. Beneath its floor: the **Celestial Stairway**, invisible to most—a road out of Faerûn for gods who can find it.

## Current Events

**Session 15's climax.** Retreating from an elite Zhentarim force with something that was once a god among them, the party—inhabiting the bodies of Torm, Adon, and Midnight, with Cyric and Kelemvor alongside—took shelter inside the Temple. [[Elminster]] was already there, and barred the doors. The Stairway, invisible until then, began glowing through the stone floor, and Midnight's pendant responded with warmth—something in it recognizing something in the Stairway.

Six infantry and a Battlelord broke through the shattered doors first, a diversion: the Battlelord's Spirit Guardians filled the temple with hostile radiance while his soldiers pinned the party down, buying time for the door behind him to open wider. Then the temperature dropped in a way no thermometer would explain, and [[Bane]] arrived: eight feet tall, black plate, red eyes with no business in a mortal face. He looked at the Stairway, then at Midnight, then at Elminster, and said nothing—the silence of a man who has already decided.

What followed was a duel fought mostly through Time Stop, spell after spell exchanged between Elminster and Bane while the Stairway grew brighter with every round, each man's working falling further behind the other's. Midnight was pulled toward it, not by anything she could see—the pendant knew where it came from, and the Stairway was calling it home. Four of the party held her by the arms and shoulders and coat, anchoring her the way you anchor a door in a storm.

By the ninth round, Elminster dropped to one knee, smoke rising from his coat. The pendant in Midnight's hand ignited, and she was not Midnight anymore, or was entirely Midnight, more than she had ever been—wild-haired, made of blue-white starmotes, speaking with a voice that was not only hers: *"You killed me once. I thought you should know—I remember."* She bound herself to Bane, not with a spell but with something older than spells, and pulled him toward the Stairway, smiling, all the way to the door out of the world.

The east wall blew outward in the last moment. A masonry shard caught Adon across the face—a scar that will never fade, on a cleric of the goddess of beauty. When the light cleared, Elminster, Bane, and Mystra's fragment were gone through the open sky. The pendant in Midnight's hand was cold. Empty. For the first time since a goddess charged her with it, it was just a pendant.
