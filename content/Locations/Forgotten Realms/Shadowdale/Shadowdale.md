## Description

A small, quietly legendary settlement in the Dalelands. Shadowdale is not grand — it is a dale like many others, with farms and hills and a tower that does not look like much from the outside. What makes it significant is who lives there.

[[Elminster]] has made his home in Shadowdale for longer than most people can remember. The town has, as a result, become a place that powerful things happen near. Not always comfortably.

## Role in the Campaign

[[Mourngrym Amcathra]] is lord of the town.

## Mourngrym's Hall

The lord's seat: black stone scarred by old arrows in the mortar, standing with a slight lean toward the river. It doesn't look like a lord's hall. It looks like something that survived.

## Session 13 Update (The Door Kickers)

The destination of the party's prequel journey. Following [[Mystra]]'s destruction at the hands of [[Helm]] and Helm's cryptic directive to *find the Tablets of Fate*, the group—inhabiting the bodies of [[Torm of the Dales]], [[Adon]], and [[Midnight]], with [[Cyric]] and [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]] in company—turned toward Shadowdale to find [[Elminster]].

## Session 14 Update (The Door Kickers)

The party arrived in Session 14. [[Selmavra Elsree]] met them at the guard post; [[Jhaele Silvermane]] received them at the [[Old Skull Inn]]. **[[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Forgotten Realms/Zhentarim|Zhentarim]] soldiers are already inside Shadowdale**—pre-positioned, confirmed by a nat 20 perception check in the inn—and ambushed the party in the inn courtyard. The attack was repelled.

## Session 15 Update (The Door Kickers)

The Elminster meeting is deferred to Session 15, which climaxes at the [[Temple of Lathander]]—the party's last stand against [[Bane]], and the site of the Celestial Stairway beneath it.

[[Mourngrym Amcathra]] ran his war council here over a feast—a man who understands that people think more clearly fed. Three colored stones marked the map: the Bloody Doom pressing north on Tower Farm, the Claw of Voonlar contested at the crossroads by the Harpers, and the West Fang moving on [[Ashaba Bridge]]. [[Manshoon]], the plan's author, stayed at [[Zhentil Keep]] and sent battle lords in his place.

Mourngrym offered the party the rank of Sword—above his own guardscaptains—at twenty gold a day. They accepted. For [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]], the distinction mattered past the coin: fighting under official orders, in service to a lord, wasn't the same as taking pay as a sword for hire. The curse let it pass unpunished.
