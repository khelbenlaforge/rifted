## Description

Warm light in an upper window, a pond visible through the trees, the smell of pipeweed on the air. Not an impressive structure for the power it holds—the kind of building that suggests its owner has better things to think about than architecture.

## Role in the Campaign

The party found [[Elminster]] at the pond's edge, feet in the water, pipe lit, hat at a familiar angle—[[Storm Silverhand]] watching the treeline, [[Rathan Thentraver]] eating an apple on the garden wall. Rathan's reunion with [[Torm of the Dales|Torm]] ("Two silver, Torm") and a silent, grief-laden look between Storm and [[Midnight]] both landed here before the briefing began.

Elminster laid out what the party needed to know: Ao the Overgod stripped the gods of their power rather than merely weakening them, and they now walk mortal flesh. The [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Lore/Tablets of Fate|Tablets of Fate]] were stolen; one sits in [[Tantras]], on the Dragon Reach. A Celestial Stairway runs beneath the [[Temple of Lathander]], and [[Bane]] is coming for it—specifically for the shard of [[Mystra]] carried in Midnight's pendant. Storm raised her hand and showed a thread of silver fire of her own: more than one person now carries a piece of the dead goddess. Elminster's plan was to neutralize Bane, not kill him—another dead god would only deepen the Weave's instability.
