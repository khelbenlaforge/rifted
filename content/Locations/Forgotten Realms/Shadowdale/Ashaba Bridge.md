## Description

The bridge over the Ashaba, on Shadowdale's western approach—the crossing point Mourngrym's war council watched for the West Fang's advance.

## Role in the Campaign

[[Mourngrym Amcathra]]'s war council named the bridge as the likely crossing point for the West Fang, one prong of a three-pronged [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Forgotten Realms/Zhentarim|Zhentarim]] advance on Shadowdale (the Bloody Doom pressing Tower Farm, the Claw of Voonlar contested at the crossroads). [[Selmavra Elsree]]'s [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Forgotten Realms/The Harpers|Harpers]] had eyes on the approach, though the intelligence stayed thin.

The party—inhabiting [[Torm of the Dales|Torm]], [[Adon]], [[Midnight]], [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]], and [[Selmavra Elsree|Selmavra]]—deployed to hold the bridge alongside [[Storm Silverhand]] and [[Rathan Thentraver]]. The West Fang crossing turned out to be some fifty infantry and a Battlelord. [[Midnight]]'s Tasha's Mind Whip caught the Chaos table on the way down and scattered its damage into targets she never aimed for; a Fog Cloud blinded the enemy rear at the moment that mattered most. The bridge held.

A second force reached the far bank before the first was cleared—the Ninth, the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Forgotten Realms/Knights of Myth Drannor|Knights of Myth Drannor]]'s own regiment, converging on the sound of battle rather than any summons. [[Illistyl Elventree]] found Torm in the press: *"You're alive."*
