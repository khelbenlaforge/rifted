## Description

A fortress northeast of [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Forgotten Realms/Arabel|Arabel]] that wore its malice openly: a fog-thick, blood-smelling courtyard, and a magic circle worked in four layers into the second-floor landing—one layer for the fog, one for the hallucinations it fed each visitor, one for a seal, one a trap on the door beyond. Nothing about it survived the day the party came for it.

## Role in the Campaign

[[Caitlan Moonsong]] led the party here during the Avatar Crisis prequel vision, asking them to rescue her mistress. The courtyard's fog split the group into private hallucinations built from each person's own desire; everyone made it through, and Caitlan followed soon after, pointing out an electrum door that hadn't been visible a moment before. The party dismantled the landing's four-layer circle and opened the door beyond, marked with [[Bane]]'s symbol. The trap on it turned out to be real: [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]] and [[Torm of the Dales|Torm]] each rolled a saving throw, and one of them took necromantic damage for it.

Beyond the door, in the Chamber of Chains: red mist, crawling skeletal hands, and [[Mystra]]'s divine form chained by Bane. The party fought through the shadow creatures guarding it and won. Caitlan stepped forward and touched the captive form—Mystra, freed, occupied the girl's body, completing an apotheosis that had, it turned out, been Caitlan's intention from the start. Bane withdrew. The castle's supernatural effects came apart, leaving only an old fort's bare ruins.

Mystra, now towering and lit from within, opened a gate through the ruins and led the party to [[Helm]] at a celestial staircase—and lost. Everything within half a mile of the castle was leveled in the fight that followed.
