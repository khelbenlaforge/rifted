## Description

The oldest and quietest quarter of [[Crystal City]], where the families who built this city before the [[Corranor Council]] existed still reside behind high garden walls and iron-fitted gates. The architecture here predates the crystalline aesthetic of the newer districts — deep blue-veined stone, private courtyards, estate facades worn smooth by two hundred years of rain. Commerce does not happen in the streets. It happens in drawing rooms and salons, over tea, by appointment. The air smells like clipped hedgerows and something older than the city itself.

The [[Magocracy of Corranor]] governs this district nominally. In practice, the noble families who have called it home for generations govern themselves.

## Features

- **[[Sapphire Terrace]]** — A quiet salon run by [[Soral Vayne]], former household steward to the [[Hucrele Family]]. Old money gathers here to exchange information, settle scores, and remember what Crystal City was before the Council. The best introduction service in the city — and the most careful.
- **[[Hucrele Manor]]** — The Hucrele family's Crystal City estate, now largely vacant since the family's relocation to [[Sunhold]]. Maintained by a skeleton staff. [[Soral Vayne]] holds a key.
- **Cassaveth House** — One of the oldest family estates in the district. Reclusive. Rarely holds public functions but will receive visitors who arrive with the right name.
- **Irenmoor Walk** — A private promenade along the district's inner wall where families take their evening exercise. More business is conducted here than in any Council chamber.

## Residents

- **[[Soral Vayne]]** — Former Hucrele retainer; now the district's foremost social broker
- **[[Hucrele Family]]** — Crystal City origin; estate maintained but family primarily based in [[Sunhold]]

## Current Events

The district is quieter than it has been in a generation. Several families relocated from Crystal City during the city's descent from the Floating Lands — some to [[Sunhold]], some farther east. The estates they left behind are watched over by retainers and caretakers, but the warmth has gone out of them. Those who remain notice it. Soral Vayne, who has watched this neighborhood for forty years, says it feels like a held breath.

[[Lea]] arrived in the district seeking connections. What [[Soral Vayne]] saw when she walked through his door is not something he has shared.

## Scene Features

**Atmosphere:** *A place that remembers everything and volunteers nothing — every introduction carries a cost the visitor doesn't learn until later.*

### Triggered Effects
- *Old Favor.* When the party invokes a noble family name, the first NPC they encounter will know that name — and react before they say anything else. The reaction reveals something about the name's current standing.
- *The Wrong Question.* Asking directly about money, political allegiance, or the Council in polite company causes an immediate social freeze. The party loses advantage on all Charisma checks in the district until they make good — a sincere apology, a gift, or a clever redirect.

> *Which noble house in this district has the most to gain from the party's situation — and hasn't introduced themselves yet?*
