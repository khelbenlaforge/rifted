> # House Verenthal

House Verenthal is a minor noble estate in the Sapphire District of Crystal City, carrying the posture of old coin long after the security behind it has started to thin.

The estate still knows how to present itself. Its gate is freshly painted, the manners are polished, and visitors are met with the kind of correctness meant to end questions before they begin. But the cracks show everywhere else: half the household staff wear livery a shade too faded to match the gate, the hedges have gone too long without trimming, and the whole place feels too quiet for a manor of its size. It is the silence of a house listening for something.

Guests meant for discreet business are received at a side gate rather than the front. A servant keeps the exchange brief and professional, with the grand manor visible behind them like an inheritance being maintained by willpower alone. The estate is quietly bleeding money to someone or something, and the family is hiring in secret because they cannot weather a public scandal on top of whatever private pressure is already tightening around them.

*A name matching "Verenthal" turned up in one of [[Corvin Adder]]'s older ledgers inside [[The Marrow Ledger]] (Session 25) — a plausible thread back to whatever's draining this house, not yet confirmed as the same Verenthal.*
