## Description

The [[Hucrele Family]]'s ancestral estate in the [[Sapphire District]] — a wide stone manor of two stories, its deep-blue facade darkened by age, its shutters drawn on every window but the kitchen. The front garden is well-kept; someone still comes to trim the hedges. But the gateposts are streaked with rain and the family crest above the door has not been repainted in years.

The Hucreles relocated south to [[Sunhold]] after a deterioration in their Crystal City political standing. The manor was not sold — families like the Hucreles do not sell estates — but it has not been truly occupied since. A small staff of three maintains the property and reports to [[Keldara Hucrele]] by post.

## Features

- **Front Drawing Room** — Formally presented but faded. Furniture under dust covers. The family portraits on the north wall cover several generations, ending abruptly with [[Talgen Hucrele Sr.]] and [[Keldara Hucrele]] in their younger years.
- **Family Archive** — A locked room on the upper floor containing correspondence, ledgers, and documents going back to the family's founding. Only [[Soral Vayne]] and [[Keldara Hucrele]] hold keys. The most sensitive documents were removed before the family left.
- **East Wing** — Sealed. The staff has been told not to enter and don't ask why.
- **Kitchen and Servants' Quarters** — The only rooms still in active use. The three remaining staff are loyal, discreet, and old enough to remember when the manor was full.

## Residents

- **Maintenance Staff** — Three retainers; unknown to the party. Report to [[Keldara Hucrele]].
- **[[Soral Vayne]]** — Holds a key; visits on the first of each month; considers himself informally responsible for the property.

## Current Events

The estate has been untouched since news arrived from [[Sunhold]] about [[Talgen Hucrele Jr.]]'s death and [[Sharwyn Hucrele]]'s rescue. The staff received a letter from [[Keldara Hucrele]] but no further instructions. The east wing has been sealed for longer than any of them have worked here.

## Scene Features

**Atmosphere:** *A house that was abandoned before it was left — the question isn't who lived here, but what happened.*

### Triggered Effects
- *Portrait Gallery.* Any PC who studies the family portraits and succeeds on a DC 12 History or Insight check notices either a pattern in the faces — or a gap where a portrait should be.
- *The East Wing.* The sealed door has no lock on the outside. It has been sealed from the inside.
