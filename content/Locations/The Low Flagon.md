## Description

A wide, low-ceilinged tavern that has been in the same building on the same corner of the [[Garnet District]] for longer than anyone can remember. The floors are worn smooth, half the tables are sticky, and the sign outside is missing a letter — no one has fixed it in twenty years. It serves the cheapest ale in [[Crystal City]] and, inexplicably, the best fried fish. Off-shift dockworkers, miners, and locals fill it every night. The crowd is not dangerous. The back room is not public.

[[The Claret]] has operated out of the Low Flagon for at least three generations. The owner — a silent partner who has never been identified — is Claret-affiliated. The staff knows what the back room is for and has been well compensated to forget.

## Features

- **The Public Bar** — Long wooden bar, mismatched stools, a fireplace that draws smoke the wrong way on cold nights. The barkeep is a Claret associate and the first filter: if you come asking the wrong thing, you leave without an answer.
- **The Reserved Tables** — By unspoken agreement, four tables along the east wall are never sat at by random patrons. Regulars know. New faces learn fast.
- **The Back Room** — Accessed through an unmarked door behind the bar that does not face the common room. Small, no windows. A table, four chairs, and a single lamp. Where [[The Claret]] conducts business. It does not officially exist.

## Residents

- **[[Tavia Orn]]** — Uses the back room for collections meetings and initial contact with new parties. Not always present — she has three blocks to cover.
- **Barkeep** — Claret associate. Name unknown to the party.

## Current Events

Business as usual. The Low Flagon has been busier since the [[Citrine District]] docks reopened — more off-shift workers moving through the neighbourhood, more strangers asking questions. The barkeep has been more selective about who gets pointed toward the back. [[Tavia Orn]] has asked for a second table in the reserved section; she hasn't said why.

Daily [[The Claret]] handoffs are confirmed: bosses collect stolen goods from street-level associates each afternoon. The code phrase for walking in is four litres of blood to sell. [[Tinny]] is the street-level Claret contact who directed the party here.

## Scene Features

**Atmosphere:** *A place where everyone is minding their own business — including the people who aren't.*

### Triggered Effects
- *Wrong Question.* If a PC asks openly about [[The Claret]], criminal activity, or the back room without being vouched in, the barkeep goes pleasant and blank and no one at the bar makes eye contact.
- *The Reserved Tables.* A PC who sits at one of the reserved tables without authorisation will be quietly approached within one minute by someone who offers them a seat elsewhere. It is not a threat. It is very firm.
- *Vouched In.* When a PC is visibly vouched by a Claret contact (like [[Tavia Orn]]), the atmosphere shifts — the barkeep fills glasses faster, patrons nod without speaking, the back room door is unlocked.
