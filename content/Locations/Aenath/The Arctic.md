## Description

Past Stormberg and its Wall, the world stops behaving like a continent should. The Arctic is not a circle here—it's a line drawn from one end of the world to the other, and Stormberg is said to know it better than Crystal City ever will.

## Current Events

The Arctic's rhythm follows Corranor's own gate cycle rather than any season. Grounded, the Stormgate opens and supply and survey runs head out past the Wall; airborne, with the gates shut, anyone already out there stays out until the next grounding. Runs rarely finish inside a single window—most are gone for the length of an airborne cycle or longer, which is normal rather than alarming, if it does sometimes make the harbor nervous when a party runs late.
