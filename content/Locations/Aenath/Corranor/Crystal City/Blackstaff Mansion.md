> # Blackstaff Mansion

Blackstaff Mansion is the huge, heavily warded Crystal City estate of the local Blackstaff branch, built as much like an arcane stronghold as a noble residence.

Its towers evoke old wizard keeps more than conventional manor architecture, rising with the deliberate arrogance of a family that has expected power to gather around it for generations. The approach announces that same confidence in stranger form: there is no visible front door at all. Visitors stand before a blank wall until someone trusted speaks, a point of light traces a line across the stone, and a concealed doorway opens where there seemed to be none. Even indoors the estate never feels unguarded. Its throne room is protected by scores of magically armored guards, the sort of force that makes clear this house expects enemies worthy of wards rather than locks.
