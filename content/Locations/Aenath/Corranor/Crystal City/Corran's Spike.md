## Description

A colossal formation of rock and light that pierces the heavens east of [[Crystal City]]. A brilliant column of radiance rises from its core, surrounded by a swirling helix of debris and stone that never settles. The landscape surrounding the Spike is shattered — magma jets, floating rock fragments, and scarred earth mark the site of the cataclysm that nearly destroyed the world.

## Significance

During [[The Awakening]], the archmage [[Corran Blackstaff|Corran]] channeled immense power to contain a catastrophic force that threatened to tear the land apart. In a final act of sacrifice, he entombed himself and four constructs within this massive rock formation, stabilizing the chaotic terrain and allowing civilization to take root. The Spike stands as both a geographical anchor and a memorial — a reminder of what was paid so that [[Corranor]] might exist.

The [[Jade Spire]] in [[Crystal City]] was erected in his honor, but it is here, at the Spike itself, that [[Corran Blackstaff|Corran]]'s sacrifice is easiest to feel. The party experienced visions of this moment during their early days in [[Corranor]], witnessing the cataclysm and Corran's final ritual firsthand.

## Current Events

[[Layla]] has run a second measured arrival circle at the Spike's base—chalk markings extending twenty feet outward from the runic circle in every direction, read by a compass instrument of three concentric silver rings spinning independently, tracking the dissipating arrival energy the way a physician reads a pulse. Three newcomers landed here together on their crossing, two of whom (Terra, Sadiah) arrived out of a shared vision of this exact place: a flat world cracking apart, a man pressing himself into the earth until it stopped moving—Corran's own sacrifice, seen from someone else's memory rather than their own.

[[Tidecaller Marin Deepcurrent]] has separately offered [[Gill Bates]] a future task: sense the ocean again at the next landfall, choosing between Corran's Spike and [[Stormberg]]. Still open, unresolved.
