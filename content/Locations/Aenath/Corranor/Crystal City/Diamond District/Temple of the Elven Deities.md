## Description

A multi-deity temple complex for the Elven deities, distinct from the separate complex that houses [[Mystra]], [[Azuth]], and [[Savras]] elsewhere in the [[Diamond District]]—two different complexes, not one. Only Sehanine's shrine within it has seen actual play; which other elven deities keep a presence here isn't established.

## Session 26 Update (The Door Kickers)

- **Sehanine's shrine**—site of [[Terra]]'s Feast-rite dance across Sessions 26-27

[[Terra]] danced [[Sehanine Moonbow|Sehanine]]'s Feast rite here through the night of Session 26 into Session 27—her wings glowing silver, her [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Items/Door Kickers/Hollowstone|Hollowstone]] pendant heating and melting its cord twice mid-dance.
