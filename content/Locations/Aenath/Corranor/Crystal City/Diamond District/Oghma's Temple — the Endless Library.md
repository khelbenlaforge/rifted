## Description

A towering repository of knowledge dedicated to [[Oghma]], the Binder of Books. The interior stretches upward in seemingly endless shelves of books, scrolls, and manuscripts, illuminated by ethereal light that needs no flame. The architecture blends ancient design with arcane enhancements, creating an atmosphere of reverence where whispers carry farther than shouts and every footstep feels like an intrusion upon centuries of accumulated wisdom.

## Notable Features

- **The Stacks** — Towering shelves of knowledge spanning centuries, organized by magical cataloguing systems
- **Mentor Assignment Hall** — Where [[High Lorekeeper Miriam Brightquill]] uses magical detection to identify divine callings and match individuals with mentors

## Notable Residents

- **[[High Lorekeeper Miriam Brightquill]]** — Oversees the temple, identifies divine callings, and assigns mentors

The party visited the temple after joining the [[Adamantine Guild]]. [[High Lorekeeper Miriam Brightquill]] assessed each member's affinity: she placed [[Min Ji-ah]] with [[Elmindaria L'Thorian]], [[Gill Bates]] with [[Tidecaller Marin Deepcurrent]].
