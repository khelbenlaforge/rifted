## Description

A single temple complex housing three deities of magic and fate together rather than three separate shrines—Mystra at its center, Azuth and Savras attending her as much as worshipped in their own right. None of the three keep clergy in the traditional sense. Mystra gives magic freely enough that no priesthood is needed for her; Azuth's Magistrati and Savras's own thin, scattered ranks are informal followings of devoted magic-users rather than an ordained temple faith on the scale of the Diamond District's other houses of worship.

## Current Events

Min Ji-ah picked up the complex's existence secondhand, her own instinct for "divine gossip" catching what nobody in the room said aloud: Azuth, once one of the most powerful mages alive, now serves as Mystra's devoted—and by some accounts amorous—advisor on magic's advancement and preservation. Which Mystra that refers to is left deliberately unresolved in play.
