## Description

Westward runs two seasons, and anyone who stays past a full cycle learns to tell them apart. At landfall, the river mouth fills with hulls. The [[The Ferry-Masters|ferry-masters]] work the crossing to [[Sunhold]]'s island while the strait still holds water, and the quays run loud enough to drown out the forge-hammers. Then the island lifts clear of the sea. What was harbor drains out from under the docks, and the river that fed it keeps flowing anyway, off the cliff and into open sky. No one has ever followed it down to see where it goes. The ferries sit beached on mud through the weeks between landfalls. The town doesn't stop; it just changes hands, foundries and charcoal smoke carrying the work the harbor can't.

Come at it from the forest and the smoke finds you before the buildings do—charcoal camps working the Wildwood's edge, feeding foundries built up on slag heaps that predate anyone's grandparents. Westward is one of [[Corranor]]'s five named cities, by [[Layla]]'s own count, roughly a day's ride from the open scrubland to the south, with an [[Adamantine Guild]] branch run by [[Dessa Kestrel]].

## Features

- **The Ferry Quays**—the river-mouth docks where the [[Sunhold]] crossing loads and unloads. [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Lore/Aenath Landmass Types|Landfall]] turns them into the loudest place in town; the rest of the cycle, they're mudflats with boats resting on their sides.
- **Slagrow**—the foundry district, built up over generations on slag heaps nobody remembers piling. The smiths here swear by [[Sunhold]] ore over anything a local vein could give, though nobody's ever traced exactly why the difference holds so true.
- **The Coalings**—burn camps scattered through the Wildwood, hauling cut fuel in by cart for every forge in Slagrow. The burners go a little further into the trees every year, and not all of them come back with a story anyone quite believes.
- **The Cliffwatch**—the [[Adamantine Guild]]'s Westward branch, a squat stone post at the top of the western cliffs. Thinly staffed, like every Guild post these days, but it watches the stair below more closely than it watches the ledgers.
- **The West Stair**—a switchback stair cut into the cliff face below the Cliffwatch, roped off and guarded, usable only while the island rides aloft, and the Guild has never said what's down there.
- **The Deep Index**—the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]]'s westernmost branch, a cramped antiquities annex built into the cliff near the Cliffwatch. Not yet named to any party at the table; grounds Dessa Kestrel's own vague reference to "a couple of researchers in the area" (Session 3). Separate charter from the Guild, same small town, so word travels between them regardless.

## Residents

- **[[Dessa Kestrel]]**—Cleric of [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Oghma|Oghma]], the Cliffwatch's branch leader. Runs the post alone most days.
- **Osric Ferrowe**—House Ferrowe's man in Westward, minding the family's affairs in town.

### Off-Cycle (Current)

Westward is off-cycle right now, between landfalls. The strait's long drained, and the Ferry Quays stand empty, keel-scars still drying in the mud. Slagrow and the Coalings carry the town instead—char thick enough to taste, forge-heat leaking through every open doorway, a hammer-beat that never quite lines up with itself. That's the noise the AUGs walked into first, delivered to the Cliffwatch's door by a guide Layla arranged in advance. Dessa Kestrel met them alone, same as most days, and opened a Guild evaluation on the Proving Floor. It still hasn't closed.

## Scene Features

**Atmosphere:** Westward wants you to take the work at face value—two honest trades, ferry and forge, with nothing underneath either one worth asking about.

### Triggered Effects
- *Lift-off drain.* When the island pulls clear of the sea, the strait empties within hours—a visible, town-wide signal everyone in Westward reads the same way.

> *Who in town still stops to watch, even after a dozen drains, and who doesn't look up from their work at all?*

- *A Coalings crew doesn't come back on schedule.* When a burn-camp crew misses its return to Slagrow, the forges start rationing charcoal within a day, and talk in town turns fast—no name gets spoken as fact, just guesses.

> *What does the town believe happened, and how far from the truth is that guess?*
