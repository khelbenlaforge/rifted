## Description

Westward runs two seasons, and anyone who stays past a full cycle learns to tell them apart. At landfall, the river mouth fills with hulls. Ferry-masters work the crossing to [[Sunhold]]'s island while the strait still holds water, and the quays run loud enough to drown out the forge-hammers. Then the island lifts clear of the sea. What was harbor drains out from under the docks, and the river that fed it keeps flowing anyway, off the cliff and into open sky. No one has ever followed it down to see where it goes. The ferries sit beached on mud through the weeks between landfalls. The town doesn't stop; it just changes hands, foundries and charcoal smoke carrying the work the harbor can't.

Come at it from the forest and the smoke finds you before the buildings do—charcoal camps working the Wildwood's edge, feeding foundries built up on slag heaps that predate anyone's grandparents. Westward is one of [[Corranor]]'s five named cities, by [[Layla]]'s own count, roughly a day's ride from [[The Drowned Dawn]]'s surface exit, with an [[Adamantine Guild]] branch run by [[Dessa Kestrel]].

## Features

- **The Ferry Quays**—the river-mouth docks where the [[Sunhold]] crossing loads and unloads. Landfall turns them into the loudest place in town; the rest of the cycle, they're mudflats with boats resting on their sides.
- **Slagrow**—the foundry district, built up over generations on slag heaps nobody remembers piling. The smiths here swear by [[Sunhold]] ore over anything a local vein could give, though nobody's ever traced exactly why the difference holds so true.
- **The Coalings**—burn camps scattered through the Wildwood, hauling cut fuel in by cart for every forge in Slagrow. The burners go a little further into the trees every year, and not all of them come back with a story anyone quite believes.
- **The Cliffwatch**—the [[Adamantine Guild]]'s Westward branch, a squat stone post at the top of the western cliffs. Thinly staffed, like every Guild post these days, but it watches the stair below more closely than it watches the ledgers.
- **The West Stair**—a switchback stair cut into the cliff face below the Cliffwatch, roped off and guarded, usable only while the island rides aloft, and the Guild has never said what's down there.
