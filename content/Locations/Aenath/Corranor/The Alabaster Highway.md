## Description

A road unlike any other in Corranor: brand new pale stone that never touches the ground, floating slightly above the earth its whole length, running east from Crystal City toward an unnamed point on the peninsula below Coldford. Seen from the air it resolves into something almost readable—a crack healing wrong across the land, farming communities strung along it like knots in a rope.

## Current Events

Magical-creature blood flows in along the highway from the peninsula, bound for the Ether Scryer's Academy and the Alchemist's Guild once it reaches Crystal City. [[Torem]] knows the supply chain that far and trusts the Academy with what happens to it after arrival—he doesn't track who actually buys it once it's inside the city. The [[Azure Tamers]] patrol the length of the road and watch for creature attacks on the settlements along it.
