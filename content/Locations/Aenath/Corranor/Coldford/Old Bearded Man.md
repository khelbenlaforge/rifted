A pub nearer to Coldford's back roads, but still fine enough for Manifest House patrons, where the man in the good hat has been seen.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- [[Tamsin Vell]] named it when [[Odette Langford]] asked about the man in the good hat; it is nearer to the back roads but still fancy enough for the Manifest Houses.
- Tamsin has not been inside.
- [[Odette Langford]] cast Augury about seeking out the man there and flirting with him: good and bad, the good stronger.
- The party chose it for the night of 8 Nightal and the session ended before they reached it.

## Session 7 Update (The JULYs)
- The sign showed an old man's cotton-wool beard pasted to the board and trailing down the post; the door knob was warm. Inside, the long room was hot, loud and golden, with a fiddler, a dice table, a bar, benches, a curtained alcove and a narrow stair.
- The tall, lanky, dark-skinned bartender with pointed ears and violet eyes said the local specialty was rum, with ale and lager but no wine. A green-skinned bouncer with two tusks stood in the corner in good clothes, carrying brass knuckles and a club.
- At the back, under the stair, an empty table had a lit lamp, one chair and a fresh cup; every other table was taken. A barmaid changed the cup every five minutes, bringing mulled wine heated in the kitchen.
- The man in the good hat arrived with two armed men, one with a bow and one with a crossbow. They sat by the door near the windows, while he took the back table.
- [[Ruairí]] stirred up a quarrel with the two men at the door that became a brawl while [[Odette Langford]] slipped out. About six people and one black cat were thrown out; the two guards stayed inside with their boss.
