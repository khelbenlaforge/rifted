Coldford's dockside district, where the Manifest Houses keep their intake desks side by side: open ledgers, tally slates and a bell rung for every closed count. The [[Backrows]] lie behind it, on the side away from the harbour.

## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- [[Hobb Abbe]] rings the tally-bells for a run of desks from a raised platform here. [[Ruairí]] worked beside him, and the Manifest Houses were curious why Hobb had let him help.
- Ruairí saw some clerks quietly let a tally pass and usher the next person in instead of signalling Hobb.
- [[Marl Dunning]] keeps a stall at the east end. From the shadow of an awning, Ruairí watched a man in a good hat with two studs stop there three times in one day.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- At the debrief, someone in the Tallyrows was counted among those working with [[The Claret]].
- On the morning of 8 Nightal [[Ruairí]] worked the Tallyrows with Guidance behind his read of the room. Marl's stall sits near the far end of a row of about twenty counters, as far from Hobb's platform as it can be.
- The man in the good hat came by again that morning. [[Tamsin Vell]] later called him a regular around the Tallyrows.
- Ruairí's Detect Magic found memory alteration and suggestion, cast more than once in that place. Red chalk check marks keep returning to one leg of Marl's counter.
- [[Sister Margaret]] asked to have Marl brought to her, since the memory work would take greater restoration to undo.
