## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- [[Ruairí]] named it to [[Petra Hale]] as the quarter where the building he followed the man in the good hat to stands.
- Petra said even the city guard does not walk there.
- Coldford's pubs lie in this area, and the nicer part of it is where the Manifest Houses stand.
- The party planned a night there on 8 Nightal.
