The [[Adamantine Guild]]'s Coldford branch, run by [[Petra Hale]]: a common room, a contracts board out front, and a way into Petra's pocket dimension, where she tests newcomers and lodges them. The building is shielded, so no one outside can hear what is said within.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
