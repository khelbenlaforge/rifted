A pier in Coldford's harbour where the bell pilots work a row of bells, among them [[Dagny Rusk]], who tightens the bells and hammers out their dents.

## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- [[Dagny Rusk]] was found working down the row of bells here. She is a bell pilot, one of the few who know every code that guides a ship to its pier.
- [[Torun]] had explained that each pier has a bell, and that when a ship is coming in only that pier's bell may ring, so ships can steer by sound through the fog.
- Dock talk tells of a night bell with a pitch no pilot's bell has. The first time someone answered it by accident, four crew vanished. Dagny thinks it a prank and says it rings only once every few weeks.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- On the morning of 8 Nightal Dagny was practising along the row of bells though no ship was due.
- She taught [[Kai]] how the piers talk to each other. When a ship is coming in, a pier signals the others at once, and each pier has its own come-in code. Kai learned the hold-off warning, which turns a pilot away in bad tide or water, and the night set for the first sixty feet.
- She described emergency codes too: if a ship ignores the piers and keeps coming, boats go out to block it. A further night set covers the stretch beyond the first sixty feet.
- Kai learned that a pilot has to know about seven or eight come-in codes; that day bells are simpler because a boat can be seen for the first sixty feet in fog, while at night boats are guided in from about two hundred feet out; and, as Dagny put it, that if a ship arrives that does not know the codes, Kai must signal every pier at once.
- Ruairí knows the harbour has emergency piers: boats about to collide are sent to them, and they serve the fishermen and the coast guard rather than cargo.
- A pier nobody knew about would need its own variation of every code, especially come-in and hold-off. Whether an emergency code exists for a false pier was left open.
- Dagny asked whether Kai was very sure the night bell was not a prank. When he said he did not think so, she said they would need evidence, or one of them would have to hear it.
- At the debrief the extra bell, and someone tied to the bell codes working with [[The Claret]], were counted among what is still open.
