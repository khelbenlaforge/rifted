## Description

Ordinary temperate forest, mostly — old growth, dense canopy, the kind of quiet that makes footsteps sound louder than they are. It sits in the wild country of northern Corranor, inland and slightly south of the direct line between [[The Sundered Choir]]'s drifting position and [[Stormberg]], close enough that something crossing that line by air passes near enough to notice trouble on the ground below.

The one thing that marks it as more than just trees: a single clearing near its center never quite catches daylight properly. Every other patch of the Hollowreach gets ordinary dappled sun through the canopy — but that one clearing sits in a dusk-dim gloom no time of day explains, ringed by trees that all lean very slightly away from its center, like something asked them to a long time ago and they never stopped listening.

## Features

- **The Shadow Font** — the anomalous dim clearing at the forest's heart; the one patch of the Hollowreach that behaves like the underside of the Sundered Choir rather than an ordinary wood.
- **The Long Deadfall** — a wide swath of storm-toppled trees, half-rotted, that forces anyone moving through it to pick a slow path or risk a bad step.
- **The Drywash** — a dry streambed cutting a straight, mostly-clear lane through the trees; fast footing if you don't mind being seen.
- **The Antler Trail** — a game path worn by generations of deer, the only clean sprint line through the deep woods.
- **The Weeping Rise** — a low rocky outcrop with just enough elevation to see over the canopy toward the coast — the nearest thing to a landmark for anyone trying to get their bearings.
- **The Doorway** — a portal standing where no door should be, cut into the middle of the tree line, its edges not quite sitting right in a way nobody has been able to articulate. It opens onto **[[The Hexad]]**. A Shadow Creature dragged [[Samson Flint]] through it in Session 2–3; [[Layla]] followed and was stopped dead by a barrier on the far side that did *not* stop the three PCs who tried the same threshold. Still standing, still unexplained.

## Current Events

On a normal day, nothing here is stranger than a forest gets — birdsong, deer on the Antler Trail, the odd hunting party from Stormberg giving the Shadow Font a wide, superstitious berth without being able to say exactly why. That changes the moment something crosses through the Font from somewhere else entirely.

- 29th Uktar, Year 222 PA: [[Layla]] arrives in person, rattled, warns them shadows are coming and not to fight, and misty-steps away. Her sending-stone call pulls two goblin-riders and their giant bats out of the Stormberg-bound formation—bringing [[!D'bleh|Gaslight]] and [[Fern]] down here mid-fight.

The four break contact and cross the Shadow Font's edge into ordinary daylight, where the shadows stop following entirely.

## Scene Features

**Atmosphere:** the Hollowreach doesn't want to be crossed quietly — it wants whatever's moving through it seen, chased, and run to ground before it reaches the treeline.

### Ambient Effects
- **Ordinary daylight throughout the forest, except the Shadow Font**, which sits in permanent dusk-dim gloom regardless of the hour — the inverse of [[The Sundered Choir]]'s uniform dimness, and the only patch of ground here where a [[Shadow Creatures|Shadow Creature]] can stand without penalty.
- **The Long Deadfall is difficult terrain** — a failed DEX save or check crossing it costs momentum, not just a bad step.
- **The Drywash and the Antler Trail are both clear, fast lanes** — full speed available, but nothing about them offers cover.
- **Canopy blocks sightlines** beyond short range anywhere outside the Drywash, the Antler Trail, or the Weeping Rise.

### Lair-style Features *(the location's own toolkit — no boss creature here, so these are DM-optional environmental tools, not a mandatory initiative-20 action)*

- **Forcing a Shadow Creature out of the Shadow Font and into open canopy-broken daylight** applies Sunlight Weakness in full — to the true Shadow as well as any Spawn, unlike the Choir's asymmetric trick. A party that works out the Font is the creatures' only safe ground here has found the actual solution to this fight, not just a tactic.
- **The Weeping Rise** gives whoever climbs it (an action, or a DEX/Athletics check if pressed) a clear look at the whole chase below for one round — enough to call out the fastest lane, a hazard ahead, or which direction help is actually coming from.

> *Who notices the trees around the Font leaning away first — and do they say it out loud, or just start walking faster?*

### Triggered Effects
- *Condition.* Anything driven off the Antler Trail or the Drywash and into the Long Deadfall loses the speed advantage those lanes offer until it fights or scrambles its way back to open ground.

> *What does the Hollowreach sound like in the last few seconds before something starts hunting you in it?*
