The Ether Scryer's Academy's westernmost branch, in the town of Westward, behind columns at its entrance; its cool, dry, lamp-lit stone passage holds crates stencilled with dates and numbered drawers. Its clerk is [[Tavian Wick]], and reports go to the [[Director of Antiquities]] in [[Crystal City]] every ten days.

## Session 8 Update (The AUGs)
- Building with two columns at its entrance beside the river; cool, dry air inside; an unbroken row of crates from the main room out the door, stencilled with dates, drawer numbers, materials (clay, obsidian, unknown) and words such as [[The Old Forge|Old Forge]], [[The Keel|Keel]] and forest; a planar studies shelf; the Chronicle Library lies further in.
