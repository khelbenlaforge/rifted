## Description

[[Westward]]'s foundry district, built up over generations on slag heaps that nobody remembers piling. The forges are stacked around a huge pit at its centre. Hammers ring from every side and never land together, and char sits at the back of the throat. The streets run hot. Slag spilled from the furnaces has cooled into rivulets across them, and the bricks stay warm with no fire in sight. Frost that lands on the slag melts into small puffs of steam.

## Features

- **[[Cinderstay]]**—the master smith [[Ansel Krane]]'s smithy, with a shaft down to [[The Old Forge]] in the back bay.
- **The pit**—the forges stand in order around it, at the middle of the district.
- **The slag streets**—the cooled slag is slippery, but pockets of brick remain where a boot can find footing, and people used to walking the streets cope with them.

## Residents

- [[The Foundry-Owners]], the forge-owning families who run the furnaces.
- [[Ansel Krane]], master smith of Cinderstay.
