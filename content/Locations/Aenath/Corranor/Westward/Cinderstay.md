A smithy cut into the slope in [[Slagrow]], where the floor bricks stay hot with no fire lit and its hammer beat does not match the shops next door; a stone-lined shaft in the back bay by the quench troughs runs down to [[The Old Forge]].

## Session 8 Update (The AUGs)
- Slagrow's streets run hot around it: cooled slag in rivulets, frost melting to steam; the forge inside burns with no fuel; the shaft is a metal ring a foot off the floor, five to ten feet across.
- [[Gregory Yang|Gregory]] worked there; [[Mike Kanbei|Mike]], [[Tobias Atticus Rockefeller|Beau]] and [[Sumida Makoto|Makoto]] visited with him.

## Session 09 Update (The AUGs)
- The party used the shaft behind [[Ansel Krane|Ansel]]'s forge for its descent into the [[The Old Forge|Old Forge]]: [[Gregory Yang|Gregory]] built a rope line of about 220 feet with a block and tackle hung from the frame over the shaft, and held the rope while [[Mike Kanbei|Mike]], [[Tobias Atticus Rockefeller|Beau]] and [[Sumida Makoto|Makoto]] went down.
- Ansel's furnace brightened before each exhale of the Old Forge, which gave a few minutes of warning topside.
- A steward of the foundry-owners walked in wearing a clean leather apron with a ring of keys at his belt, and remarked that the bench stood cold that day.
