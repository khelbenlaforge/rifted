## Description

The [[Adamantine Guild]]'s Westward branch is a tall house of pale plaster on the cliffs above the harbour. It is unfinished, like a lighthouse that stopped partway up. Inside it is warm: wood, candlelight, a fire in the hearth, long tables under the windows and one long room that has nothing in common with the noise and char of the town outside. The branch is thinly staffed, and [[Dessa Kestrel]] runs it alone most days.

## Features

- **The front room**—long tables and benches, paper squared into neat drifts, a counter where Dessa does her paperwork, and a board of postings. The Guild's crest of a scroll, a gauntlet and a coin sits above the mantel.
- **The back corridor**—it runs from the front room to the entrance of the Guild's pocket dimension.
- **The pocket dimension**—a space locked to the building. A floating table produces any food or drink its user pictures, and the personal rooms around it take whatever look their occupant wants. Nothing made inside can be carried out.
- **The Proving Floor**—an arena of worn stone under low mist, reached through a portal behind the pocket dimension's door. Dessa runs the Guild's evaluation there.
- **The ridge road and the stone lip**—the road outside is packed earth and slag, with hammers ringing from the forges. A path runs west along the cliff to a stone lip with an iron rail, and on to the stair-head gate, where a stair goes down the cliff face to [[The Keel]].

## Residents

- [[Dessa Kestrel]]—the branch leader.
