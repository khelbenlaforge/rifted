## Description

The road running north out of [[Crystal City]], heading north, in [[Stormberg]]'s general direction—wide enough for carriage traffic, the forest close on both shoulders where it isn't kept clear. The [[The Door Kickers|Door Kickers]] left Crystal City on it the third of Nightal, Year 222 PA, Nanuk's pull toward [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Silvanus|Silvanus]]'s grove drawing them on. An unnamed watering hole breaks the trip partway along; half a day past it, the road widens into a turnaround at [[The Straggler's Rest]].

## Features

- **The watering hole**—an unnamed rest stop along the road, no note of its own. Flying low past it on [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Corranor/Azure Tamers|Bella]]'s back, the party spotted a deer, dead at the road's edge, clean-killed, antlers gone, no other sign of a struggle.
- **[[The Straggler's Rest]]**—a roadside tavern and waystation where the road widens into a turnaround, run by an innkeeper named Ossa.

## Current Events

Two things are moving through this stretch of road, both still open. Wildebeest herds are running south early and heavy, predators trailing the column—the migration mystery [[Torem]] hired [[Nanuk]] to look into, seen firsthand from griffon-back. And something killed the deer near the watering hole clean enough to leave no fight sign, its antlers sheared off with a blade; the trail afterward was scraped clean of tracks and wheel-ruts, leading west into the forest on a trace of magic nobody at the table recognized. A High Religion check named [[Malar]] as a possible hand in it, though the killing itself read as too controlled and unhurried for his usual creed—the theory sits unconfirmed, not ruled out.

## History

A pack of six starving wolves and two sabertooth tigers broke from the treeline here, fleeing something further north that even they seemed afraid of. [[Nanuk]] talked the pack down instead of fighting—an offer, not a command—and the whole business ended without a single death on either side. [[Erky]] arrived separately mid-encounter, disguised as a [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Bestiary/Fey/Velvet Vulper|Velvet Vulper]], to head off a second, unnamed group of rangers converging on the same starving animals; he revealed himself once they'd been driven off, and rejoined the party that night.
