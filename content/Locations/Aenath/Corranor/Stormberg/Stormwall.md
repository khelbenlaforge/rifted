## Description

The Stormwall is stone and working magic climbing high enough to blot out the horizon, running along the northern coast until it's out of sight in both directions. Its earthen core was raised by the [[Ancients of Lava]] roughly two centuries ago; the [[Magocracy of Corranor]] later sheathed it in Stormberg obsidian and built out its magical defenses over the years since, and publicly takes the credit for the Wall. It's sized to block the northern storms that hit the continent whenever [[Corranor]]'s main island is airborne (see [[Aenath Landmass Types]]; Corranor's main island is Transient). When the island rises airborne, the Stormwall is the only thing standing between [[Stormberg]] and violent ethereal and physical storms sweeping down from the north; when the island descends back to the ocean, massive gates in the wall open and the harbor resumes normal traffic. Gate openings track the same rise/fall cycle as Crystal City's, since it's the same landmass. This is known, celebrated civic engineering.

## Features

- **The Blind Reaches**—sealed breach sections inside the Stormwall's own mass, cordoned off and Guild-only wherever wall-pests have chewed in too deep. The actual culling work happens out of sight, inside the Wall itself, off-limits to everyone else, for good reason.
- **The Stormgate**—twin ironbound gates through the Wall, opening only on the same rise-fall cycle as the island. Departure point for arctic expeditions and any trade ship timed tight enough to catch it.

## Current Events

Right now, with the gates still shut, two parties are out past the Wall and can't come home until the next grounding: a routine relief run resupplying an existing arctic camp, and a Magocracy-sponsored survey team chasing rumors of etheric-crystal shards further out than anyone's charted recently. Neither's cause for alarm, it's just gone quiet a beat longer than usual.
