## Description

A two-storey hall with no ornament, set inside the city's main mansion, a set of wings and buildings. The Magocracy's crest hangs from pennants along the wall and again over the door. The long room on the second floor is where the 15 Nightal meeting was held.

## Features

- **The long room**—upstairs, plain, with one table down the middle: three bigger chairs and a fourth on one side, a row of smaller ones on the other, and a clerk's corner. The windows are clear and look down on the street.
- **The silence**—on 15 Nightal, at the threshold of the long room, every sound stopped, including the drone and the wind against the Wall that the whole city lives with. Whether it is always so is not recorded.
