## Description

- Polished stone floors and hushed conversation, more wine list than tap list—the noble district's overflow room when their own parlors get boring.
- A private mezzanine reached by a spiral stair sits above the main floor, reserved by standing arrangement for Guild officers and anyone who tips the right way.
- Regular traffic runs to House Verenthal servants buying food and drink to carry back to the fortress or the manors, [[Ether Scryer's Academy]] members between shifts, and well-to-do citizens generally.
