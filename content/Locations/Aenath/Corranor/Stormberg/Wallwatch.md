## Description

The [[Adamantine Guild]]'s Stormberg branch: a street-level office with a contracts board, three streets in from the outer gate, run by [[Rhona Voss]], who usually covers the night desk alone. Its dormitory is a pocket dimension off her own hallway, the same shape as the [[Crystal City]] branch's.

## Features

- **The contracts board**—postings and contracts on the wall; the branch holds the standing contract to cull the creatures that nest inside the [[Stormwall]].
- **The pocket dimension**—the branch's dormitory, five doors to rooms and a common room with its own floating disk, off Rhona's own hallway.
- **The party's tavern**—a tavern the party opened inside the Wallwatch on 14 Nightal.

## Residents

- [[Rhona Voss]], the branch leader.

## Session 11 Update (The Understudies)

- 14 Nightal, morning: the party came out of their pocket dimension and sat down with [[Rhona Voss]], who asked them to see, if they could, that nothing spread outside. Nobody wanted a panic, and nobody wanted the people at the coming meeting to walk in already told.

## Session 12 Update (The Understudies)

- 14 Nightal, evening: the tavern was running, with [[Kili]] and [[Roberto]] serving and [[Keith Poe]] cooking in the kitchen. By about half past nine few people were left and the rest were drifting home. Around 9:35 five newcomers walked in: [[Zarantha Verenthal]], [[Terendar]] and [[Jarr]], and two armed guards who made no secret of what they were. Kili and Roberto served them. About fifteen minutes later, Rhona came out of the corridor from the party's rooms, tired and swaying a little, and walked across to Zarantha's table.
- 15 Nightal, noon: [[Vestra Coln]] told the clerk at the meeting that she had attended the planning session at the Wallwatch on the evening of the 13th of her own initiative, uninvited, and that she took no position.
