## Description

Eleven stories of timber and rope stand against the south western stretch of Stormberg's city wall, and nothing inside the city rises higher except the [[Stormwall]] far to the north. The bottom three stories are old wall stone, the remains of a watchtower the city wall once had there. Everything above is ladders, platforms and tarred planking, with wings, beaks and tails showing through the frame at every landing.

Griffins sit on the top landing, each one bigger than a horse, with an eagle's head and chest and a lion's or tiger's back half. Every one of them faces north toward the Wall, whichever way the wind blows.

The smell reaches the street before the tower does: wet feathers, hot straw and raw meat.

## Features

- **The yard and booking desk.** A paved square at the foot of the tower, with a counter under a slate roof where flights are priced and booked. Flights between cities are costly, and the desk says so before it quotes a figure.
- **The platform lift.** A winch-driven platform runs up the middle of the frame for riders, harness and feed. The Westward Aerie has the same arrangement, and its winch seized there in an earlier season.
- **The feed floors.** The fourth and fifth stories hold the meat store, the straw and the weighing tables. With the city eating through its winter stores, these floors are where the Aerie's worries are counted.
- **The mid landings.** Tack, saddles, cleaning racks and the sleeping loft for the handlers. Ladders run between every landing, and the handlers move on them without looking.
- **The top landing.** The griffin perches, open on all sides, with the best view of the Stormwall anywhere in the city.

## Residents

- [[Jorund Fenn]], the Aerie's keeper and its senior ranger.
- [[Linnet Crowe]], who runs the booking desk and reads the sky.
- [[Wren Dace]], a young handler on the mid landings.
- Other Tamers come and go on patrol. The Westward Aerie had many out on patrol on the day the party visited, and the same is likely here.

## Current Events

With the gates shut and the island airborne, a low drone off the Wall carries through the whole tower. The griffins do not settle. The handlers have learned to work through it, and a few of them have stopped hearing it at all.

Winter is the larger problem. Hunting has nearly stopped and the fields outside the city lie fallow, so meat for the birds is scarce and dear.

## Scene Features

**Atmosphere:** A tower of working animals and wind, watched over the whole day by a wall nobody can stop listening to.

### Ambient Effects
- The Wall's drone runs through the timber and the planking, loud on the upper landings and a tremor in the rungs.
- Wind rises with each story. The top landing is open, loud and cold, and a handler there has to shout.
- The smell of raw meat and wet feathers fills the lower floors.

### Triggered Effects
- *Breach alarm.* When a wall-pest incursion is called, every griffin on the top landing turns its head to the same stretch of the Wall at once. The tower goes silent first, then the handlers start shouting.
- *Overdue return.* When a Tamers flight runs late past its expected window, Linnet chalks it on the booking desk's board, and the yard goes quiet around the board.
