## Description

The Hollow announces itself before it's visible: green mist sitting low between the buildings, a smell of crushed leaves and old honey that has no business existing this far from anywhere fey. Stormberg is otherwise a city of grey, weather-scoured stone, built to hold a wall up and not much else—the Hollow is the one stretch of it where the color got away from whoever was in charge. Hawthorn trees grow straight through the cobblestones in a rough ring, too old for the district around them, and stepping inside the ring changes the way sound carries: the Wall's constant subsonic drone, audible everywhere else in the city, goes distant and thin, like someone finally turned the volume down. *What was here before Stormberg's own wall-building crews ever broke ground on this stretch of coast?* Nobody living can say for certain, but the Hollow itself has never once needed to ask permission to keep existing.

## Features

- **The Hawthorn Ring**—a courtyard whose cobblestones have been split and reset around the trees rather than the other way around, city planning conceding to something older than the city. It's where most of the Hollow's own foot traffic actually gathers.
- **The Traders' Row**—a loose scatter of stalls and standing deals with no fixed hours, no fixed prices, and no fixed idea of what counts as currency. [[Hazelwit]] works this stretch.

## Residents

A fey community lives here, tucked inside the city rather than hiding from it—satyrs, pixies, and stranger things that mortal Stormberg mostly leaves alone rather than mostly ignores. [[Hazelwit]], a satyr trader, is the one resident named so far.

## Current Events

Life in the Hollow runs on its own clock, unbothered by the Wall's winter or the Guild's own downtime blocks. A normal day here looks like trade: small deals, odd currencies, favors banked instead of spent. What's different right now is only that a new face has started showing up—[[Fern]], who found the district on [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Characters/NPCs/Aenath/Corranor/Stormberg/Rhona Voss|Rhona Voss]]'s own referral during her first week back in the city, and left a piece of herself behind in one of those deals.
