## Description

Confirmed to exist and reachable—Aenath is flat with no horizon, so distant landmasses are visible to anyone who climbs high enough. The real barrier has always been the storms, and the lack of a vessel built to cross that far.

## Current Events

A Magocracy Guild expedition sailed for it around Marpenoth, Year 222 PA, aboard one of the Magocracy's only two crystal-powered airships. [[Rhona Voss]] shared this as general world lore during a Stormberg briefing—she had never seen the continent herself, and neither had anyone present.
