**Azuth** (pronounced: / [ɑː](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/A_as_in_%22bard%22 "A as in \"bard\"") ˈ [z](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Z_as_in_%22wizard%22 "Z as in \"wizard\"") [uː](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Oo_as_in_%22noon%22 "Oo as in \"noon\"") [θ](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Th_as_in_%22strength%22 "Th as in \"strength\"") / *ah-ZOOTH* [^6] or: /ˈ [ɑː](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/A_as_in_%22bard%22 "A as in \"bard\"") [z](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Z_as_in_%22wizard%22 "Z as in \"wizard\"") [uː](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Oo_as_in_%22noon%22 "Oo as in \"noon\"") [θ](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Th_as_in_%22strength%22 "Th as in \"strength\"") / *AH-zooth* [^2] [listen](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/7/7d/Azuth.ogg/revision/latest?cb=20201010003201 "Azuth.ogg")) was the [Faerûnian](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Faer%C3%BBnian_pantheon "Faerûnian pantheon") [lesser deity](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lesser_deity "Lesser deity") of [arcane magic](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arcane_magic "Arcane magic") whose concerns included the perpetuation of the [magical](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Magic "Magic") arts as a craft. **The Lord of Spells** was a servant of [Mystra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystra "Mystra") and worshiped by all manner of [arcane](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arcane "Arcane") spellcasters, earning particular veneration from [wizards](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Wizard "Wizard").[^6]

“

There was once a wizard who wanted power beyond all mortal reach. Such stories always end poorly. But luckily for the wizard, the Lady of the Mysteries took a shine to him and became his queen. She granted him powers—such powers—until he was no longer a mere wizard but a god in truth. A god dedicated to his lady and all who wore her crown.

”

— Azuth telling his story.[^16]

## Description

Azuth's [avatar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Avatar "Avatar") was usually 10 feet (3 meters) tall, and he was generally depicted as a white-haired, bearded, vigorous old man garbed in gray silk robes (and never wearing a hat).[^5]

He could, however, *[shapechange](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shapechange "Shapechange")* at will and often appeared as a 20 feet (6.1 meters) tall pyramid shape with no apparent eyes or mouths covered in glittering, pulsing lights. Despite this lack of sensory features or apparent means of mobility, he was perfectly capable of sight, speech, spellcasting, and gliding through the air.[^5]

### Possessions

Azuth wielded the [Old Staff](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Staff "Old Staff"), an ancient, divine artifact taking the form of a long, [wooden](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Wood "Wood"), [iron](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Iron "Iron") -shod [staff](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Staff "Staff") about one and a half times taller than himself. It was inscribed with all manner of sigils and runes and topped with a large gemstone known as the Spirit Stone or Donjon of Facets atop it, varyingly noted as being a cabochon-cut [topaz](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Topaz "Topaz") or a blue [sapphire](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sapphire "Sapphire"). The Old Staff had a host of arcane powers, including but by no means limited to: acting as a *[staff of striking](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Staff_of_striking "Staff of striking")*, *[staff of power](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Staff_of_power "Staff of power")*, and *[staff of the magi](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Staff_of_the_magi "Staff of the magi")*, reflecting all spells cast at it or Azuth on the same [plane](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Plane "Plane"), increasing magical resistance (useless for Azuth himself but beneficial for those without a certain level of resistance already), and [imprisoning](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Imprisonment "Imprisonment") targets in the Spirit Stone.[^5] [^17]

## Activities

![The Hand of Azuth directing adventurers to .](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/7/7d/Hand_of_Azuth_S-SotL.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20241214080224)

The Hand of Azuth directing adventurers to Watcher's Knoll.

As the Lord of Spells, a major duty of Azuth's was to monitor the casting of [Epic Magic](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Magic "Epic Magic"), and curb its usage amidst non-mortals & extraplanars, especially when this was done heedlessly.[^18]

## Personality

Azuth was a somewhat paternal deity, sober yet not humorless or mean-spirited (although when he was upset his wrath was terrible). Indeed, when he was in good humor, he enjoyed giving small gifts to those who beseeched him, such as edible delicacies, unusually colored flowers, or even elegant magical fabrics. However, his was a sardonic humor with an appreciation for wordplay and subtlety, and he had so perfected the art of straight-faced delivery that it was often unclear whether or not he was joking.[^5]

Even other deities found Azuth crotchety, not understanding the crafty god's sense of dry wit. Besides that, he was also misunderstood for his unending devotion to the accumulation of magical knowledge, concerning himself with the preservation and advancement of the magical arts.[^6]

## Divine Realm

Azuth's [divine realm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_realm "Divine realm"), located on [Buxenus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Buxenus "Buxenus"), the second layer of [Arcadia](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arcadia "Arcadia") in the [Great Wheel cosmology](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Wheel_cosmology "Great Wheel cosmology"), was also called [Azuth](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Azuth_\(realm\) "Azuth (realm)").[^5]

Mystra's failsafe 'echoes' resided here as well, within a royal castle and surrounded by blue mists.[^19]

## Relationships

Azuth was served by the deities [Velsharoon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Velsharoon "Velsharoon") and [Savras](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Savras "Savras"), the latter with which he maintained a complex relationship, having kept the *Lord of Divination* as prisoner for many years.[^6] He also counted [Deneir](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Deneir "Deneir"), [Oghma](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Oghma "Oghma"), and [Leira](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Leira "Leira") as allies of his,[^5] and he watched closely over [Anastra Syluné Silverhand](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Anastra_Sylun%C3%A9_Silverhand "Anastra Syluné Silverhand").[^20]

In life, and beyond, Azuth maintained a dynamic relationship with his [divine](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Divine "Divine") superior [Mystra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystra "Mystra"); having served as her [Chosen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chosen "Chosen"),[^21] her subordinate, and her lover.[^6] Azuth was subservient to both Mystra and later [Midnight](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystra_\(Midnight\) "Mystra (Midnight)"), having sat as an adviser for and acted as a favored student of the goddess of magic.[^6]

While his relationship with the first Mystra was one of deep mutual affection, he took a more paternal stance with her later incarnation, guiding her through a difficult time of acclimatization to her role.[^5] Although he was one of Mystra's lovers,[^6] he was in love with one of her [Chosen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chosen_of_Mystra "Chosen of Mystra"), [Alvaerele Tasundrym](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Alvaerele_Tasundrym "Alvaerele Tasundrym"), having protected her from danger.[^22]

## Worshipers

![The Symbol of Azuth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/0/0e/Azuth_symbol.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20060320173710)

The Symbol of Azuth

The clergy of Azuth were collectively known as the [Magistrati](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Magistrati "Magistrati") and included wizards, [monks](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Monk "Monk"), and [clerics](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cleric "Cleric") within their ranks. They preferred to maintain their temples within large cities, align themselves with powerful magical guilds, and act as a sort of magical administration to facilitate communication between spellcasters and enforce the Magebond.[^23]

Among the highest order of Azuth's followers were a group of arcane paragons known as the Favored. These heralds of magical development furthered the channeling of the [Weave](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Weave "Weave") by formulating arcane processes, a process viewed as the apex of magical science among the faithful.[^23]

The church of Azuth were widely viewed as archaic in their tradition, pedantic in their debates, and oblivious to the real-world problems within the Realms. They often possessed a moral neutrality that garnered an unfavorable opinion by the average layperson.[^23]

[Mellomir](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mellomir "Mellomir"), also known as the "Kind Wizard", was an ardent worshiper of Azuth. Azuth helped him craft Mellomir’s Mantle - a particularly potent abjuration spell. Mellomir travelled the world to see its magics after meeting a manifestation of Azuth who inspired him to do so.[^24]

### Orders

- Loomwarden: A stern sect of the church of Azuth that was distrusting of [sorcerers](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerer "Sorcerer") and the more chaotic nature of Mystra's power. They were particularly influential in cities like [Halarahh](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Halarahh "Halarahh") and [Bezantur](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bezantur "Bezantur").[^23]
- Spellsavants: This tolerant order of mages urged for cooperation between all practitioners of the arcane and idealized the cumulative efforts of humanity as a whole to push the boundaries of magical knowledge.[^23]

## History

Records of Azuth's life as a mortal were virtually non-existent and Azuth himself did not deign to answer questions regarding the topic; therefore, most of what was known of Azuth's life was legend.[^25]

As a mortal man, Azuth was hungry for power, and in his search for it he studied several lost empires in the hope of finding forgotten magical secrets. Eventually, his dedication led to him being granted the title of [Magister](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Magister "Magister"), the first of his kind, recognized by Mystra as her most promising mortal pupil.[^21] At some point, Azuth believed himself no longer worthy of being called Magister, and so Mystra turned him instead to one of her [Chosen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chosen_of_Mystra "Chosen of Mystra").[^26]

### Ascension

During or shortly after the [Dawn Cataclysm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dawn_Cataclysm "Dawn Cataclysm"), in his bid for divinity, Azuth sought to steal a portion of the demigod [Savras](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Savras "Savras") ' power in a gambit that ultimately failed. This led to a protracted series of battles between Azuth and Savras, culminating in a duel that shattered a mountain and created a deep lake.[^21] Azuth was successful and managed to imprison the deity Savras within a staff he had created, the [Scepter of Savras](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Scepter_of_Savras "Scepter of Savras"). And thus, with the help of Mystra who had now become his lover, Azuth finally ascended to divine status.

Azuth lost the staff, but Syluné Silverhand found it and returned it to him [^21] [^27], whereupon Azuth began to feel guilty for imprisoning Savras and, after the [Time of Troubles](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Time_of_Troubles "Time of Troubles"), freed him from his imprisonment in exchange for a pledge of fealty.

### Spellplague

“

He forgot what it was to be human. What it was to not wear the god's mantle. He forgot what it was to crave power. And so he was not there when her rivals came for his queen. Down, down, the wizard fell, from heights of the heavens to the depths of the Hells, the fabric of the very planes tearing as he passed. And perhaps, then, it was a fitting punishment, for the wizard who forgot what it was to want, that he landed broken at the feet of one who was nothing but want incarnate. He remembers now, too late, what it means, the perils and the potentials.

”

— **Azuth** ending his tale.[^16]

The destruction of [Dweomerheart](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dweomerheart "Dweomerheart") sent the dying Azuth into [Asmodeus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Asmodeus "Asmodeus") 's domain of [Nessus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nessus "Nessus"). Although many people believed the [archdevil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Archdevil "Archdevil") killed Azuth and consumed his divine essence to attain godhood,[^9] in fact Asmodeus fused with Azuth, and both gods coexisted since then in the same body.[^28] Most of the time Asmodeus overpowered Azuth, using his divine powers while the God of Wizards was in a dormant state.[^29] But in a rare few occasions Azuth was able to gain dominion of their shared body, and many denizens of the [Nine Hells](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nine_Hells "Nine Hells") believed Asmodeus had become mad due to his strange behavior.[^30]

### The Second Sundering

In [1486 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1486_DR "1486 DR"), Azuth had regained most of his strength and was able to choose Ilstan Nyaril as his Chosen.[^31] Asmodeus and Azuth began to struggle for dominion over their shared body and the hierarchy of the Nine Hells was jeopardized because of that.[^28] [^30] Ilstan and [Farideh](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Farideh "Farideh"), one of the Chosen of Asmodeus, devised a plan to separate both gods and avoid a potential [devil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Devil "Devil") invasion to all the [multiverse](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Multiverse "Multiverse").[^29]

“

Sometimes the only choice is a sacrifice

”

— Azuth.[^32]

They contacted the god [Enlil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Enlil "Enlil") through his Chosen, [Kepeshkmolik Dumuzi](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kepeshkmolik_Dumuzi "Kepeshkmolik Dumuzi"), and Asmodeus agreed to release Azuth from his body and to resurrect the [Untherite](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Unther "Unther") god [Nanna-Sin](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nanna-Sin "Nanna-Sin") as a non-god immortal and in exchange Enlil allowed Asmodeus to consume Nanna-Sin's divine spark to become a god unto himself.[^33] In a ritual performed in [Djerad Thymar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Djerad_Thymar "Djerad Thymar") amid the [First Tymanther-Unther War](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/First_Tymanther-Unther_War "First Tymanther-Unther War"), on [Hammer 10](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Hammer_10 "Hammer 10") of [1487 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1487_DR "1487 DR"), Ilstan sacrificed his life to allow Azuth become an individual god once more.[^34]

After the [Second Sundering](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Sundering "Second Sundering") ended, Azuth returned to the [Faerûnian pantheon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Faer%C3%BBnian_pantheon "Faerûnian pantheon").[^1] [^7]
