The **Feast of the Moon**, or **Moonfest**, was an annual festival in the [Calendar of Harptos](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Calendar_of_Harptos "Calendar of Harptos"), occurring between the final night of [Uktar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uktar_30 "Uktar 30") and the first day of [Nightal](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nightal_1 "Nightal 1"). It was the last great festival of the calendar year.[^2] [^3] [^4]

The day traditionally marked the onset of winter. It was also a time to celebrate and honor the ancestors and the respected dead. On this day, folk blessed their ancestors' graves and performed the Ritual of Remembrance.[^2] [^3] People also gathered to tell stories of the deeds of their ancestors and of the gods until deep into the night, until they merged and became legend. This was a time to hear of past heroes, great treasures, and lost cities.[^4]

In [Faerûn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Faer%C3%BBn "Faerûn"), battles were typically fought between harvest-time and the coming of winter. This meant that most of the fighting usually occurred in the month of Uktar. The timing of the Feast of the Moon—after recently slain soldiers had joined the ranks of the dead—was thus practical, if sadly ironic.[^2] [^3]

The [Heralds of Faerûn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Heralds_of_Faer%C3%BBn "Heralds of Faerûn") had a number of special duties on the Feast of the Moon. Prime among these was to perform the [Bloodsong](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodsong "Bloodsong") ceremony, at which a Herald publicly recited the genealogies of each noble family in the area. In this way, the Heralds reaffirmed a noble family's traditional authority and status, as well as the respect accorded to them.[^5]

Priests of a number of deities of various pantheons held rites, ceremonies, and festivals on the Feast of the Moon. Many, though not all, focused on remembering the dead in one way or another.

- Worshipers of the [halfling](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Halfling "Halfling") deity [Arvoreen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arvoreen "Arvoreen") perform the Ceremony of Remembrance to honor those who died defending their community.[^6]
- Priests of [Bhaal](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bhaal "Bhaal") recount tales of heroic slayings.[^7]
- Guardians of [Gorm Gulthyn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gorm_Gulthyn "Gorm Gulthyn") gather for ritual salutes, martial weapons displays, chanted prayers, and the offering of weapons used in a defensive action.[^8] [^9]
- Worshipers of the [dwarven](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dwarven "Dwarven") deity [Haela Brightaxe](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Haela_Brightaxe "Haela Brightaxe") call this day the Commemoration of the Fallen and is spent recounting the battles and deeds of those who died defending the Stout Folk and dedicating new weapons and armor to their memory.[^10] [^9]
- Priests of [Kelemvor](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kelemvor "Kelemvor") tell the Deeds of the Dead to preserve their memory.[^11] [^12] [^13]
- Worshipers of [Lliira](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lliira "Lliira") open a holy festival with the Swords Cast Down ritual in which weapons are thrown on the ground with chanting and covered with fresh flowers.[^14] [^15]
- Worshipers of [Lurue](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lurue "Lurue") observe a quiet ceremony ushering in winter and remembering the dead. It is the anniversary of the founding of [Silverymoon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Silverymoon "Silverymoon") and is celebrated by the unveiling of works of art and song.[^16] [^17]
- Followers of [Marthammor Duin](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Marthammor_Duin "Marthammor Duin") call this day the Beacon and celebrate the path shown them by Finder-of-Trails.[^18]
- This day is known as the Day the Dead are Most With Us by worshipers of [Myrkul](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Myrkul "Myrkul"). They believed the essence of the dead would roam Toril as ghosts.[^19]
- This day is known as the Feast of the Silver Coin to worshipers of [Nephthys](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nephthys "Nephthys") and is a time of tithing.[^20] [^21]
- Worshipers of [Savras](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Savras "Savras") the All-Seeing call this day the Vision and spend 24 hours meditating in a steam bath or a haze of incense.[^22] [^23]
- Worshipers of [Sehanine Moonbow](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sehanine_Moonbow "Sehanine Moonbow") gather this night for the [Mystic Rites of the Luminous Cloud](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystic_Rites_of_the_Luminous_Cloud "Mystic Rites of the Luminous Cloud") and are often transformed into scintillating streaks of light that fly across the starry heavens.[^24] [^25]
- Worshipers of [Shar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar "Shar") call this day the Rising of the Dark and plot their nefarious deeds for the next year after a live sacrifice.[^26] [^27] [^28]
- Worshipers of [Shiallia](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shiallia "Shiallia") invite friendly creatures to join in feasting, dancing, and singing.[^29] [^30]
- Worshipers of [Talos](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Talos "Talos") call for storms and lightning.[^31] [^32]
- Worshipers of [Tempus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tempus "Tempus") honor the dead.[^33] [^34]
- Worshipers of the [orc](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Orc "Orc") deity [Luthic](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Luthic "Luthic") know this day as the Coming of the Winter Cave, a festival in which many young are conceived.[^35]
- [Heralds](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Heralds_of_Faer%C3%BBn "Heralds of Faerûn") perform the [Bloodsong](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodsong "Bloodsong"), reciting the genealogies of noble families and reaffirming their traditional authority and status.[^5]
- [Menzoberranzan](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Menzoberranzan "Menzoberranzan") holds the Ceremony of Graduation for graduating students from [Tier Breche](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tier_Breche "Tier Breche"). Visitors are barred from entering the city and conflict between houses is absolutely forbidden, as is any and all trade. Houses with graduating students are expected to celebrate by hosting feasts.[^36]
- In [275 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/275_DR "275 DR"), the holy sword *[Dornavver](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dornavver "Dornavver")* was enchanted at [Allyn's Anvil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Allyn%27s_Anvil "Allyn's Anvil").[^37]
- In [449 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/449_DR "449 DR"), King of [Tethyr](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tethyr "Tethyr") Silvyr Ithal was slain by treachery in single combat with Emperor [Amahl Shoon VII](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Amahl_Shoon_VII "Amahl Shoon VII") in [Shoonach](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shoonach "Shoonach").[^38] [^1]
- In [464 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/464_DR "464 DR"), [Arun Maerdrym](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arun_Maerdrym "Arun Maerdrym") and his [nameless son](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Khelben_Arunsun "Khelben Arunsun") sacrificed their lives to prevent the [vampire](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Vampire "Vampire") [Palron Kaeth](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Palron_Kaeth "Palron Kaeth") from destroying the [Sharn Wall](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sharn_Wall "Sharn Wall").[^39]
- In [711 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/711_DR "711 DR"), the [Weeping War](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Weeping_War "Weeping War") began between the [Army of Darkness](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Army_of_Darkness "Army of Darkness") and the [Akh'Velahr](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Akh%27Velahr "Akh'Velahr") of [Cormanthor](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cormanthor "Cormanthor").[^40]
- In [1369 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1369_DR "1369 DR"), [Tyr](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tyr "Tyr") declared that his clergy would thereafter be [specialty priests](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Specialty_priest "Specialty priest") with additional powers to fight for justice.[^41]
- In [1372 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1372_DR "1372 DR"), the [Deep Imaskari](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Deep_Imaskari "Deep Imaskari") broke the Great Seal which had kept them isolated from the rest of [Toril](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Toril "Toril") for millennia, and tentatively began exploring with secret agents.[^42]
- In [1372 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1372_DR "1372 DR"), the [Citadel of Black Ash](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Citadel_of_Black_Ash "Citadel of Black Ash") in the eastern [Smoking Mountains](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Smoking_Mountains "Smoking Mountains") was covered by lava when normally dormant [^43] volcanoes erupted.[^42]
- In [1374 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1374_DR "1374 DR"), [Esvele Greycastle](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Esvele_Greycastle "Esvele Greycastle") brought together the [Church of Shar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_Shar "Church of Shar"), the [Zhentarim](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Zhentarim "Zhentarim"), and the [Lolthites](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lolth "Lolth") of [House Dhuurniv](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/House_Dhuurniv "House Dhuurniv") in an unholy alliance [^44] with an eye toward the subjugation of [Shadowdale](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shadowdale "Shadowdale").[^45]
- In [1374 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1374_DR "1374 DR"), a large gathering of spellcasting elites joined in the raising of the [Hidden City of Hope](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Rhymanthiin "Rhymanthiin") out of the swamps of the [High Moor](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/High_Moor "High Moor"). Among the notables were five [Chosen of Mystra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chosen_of_Mystra "Chosen of Mystra") including [Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Khelben_%22Blackstaff%22_Arunsun "Khelben \"Blackstaff\" Arunsun") who gave his life to make the [High magic](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/High_magic "High magic") ritual permanent.[^44] [^46]
