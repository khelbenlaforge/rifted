**Sehanine Moonbow** (pronounced: /ˈ [s](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/S_as_in_%22sorcerer%22 "S as in \"sorcerer\"") [ɛ](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/E_as_in_%22elf%22 "E as in \"elf\"") [h](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/H_as_in_%22halfling%22 "H as in \"halfling\"") [ɑː](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/A_as_in_%22bard%22 "A as in \"bard\"") [n](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/N_as_in_%22dragon%22 "N as in \"dragon\"") [i](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ee_as_in_%22fee%22 "Ee as in \"fee\"") [n](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/N_as_in_%22dragon%22 "N as in \"dragon\"") ˈ [m](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/M_as_in_%22mage%22 "M as in \"mage\"") [uː](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Oo_as_in_%22noon%22 "Oo as in \"noon\"") [n](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/N_as_in_%22dragon%22 "N as in \"dragon\"") [b](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/B_as_in_%22bard%22 "B as in \"bard\"") [oʊ](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/O_as_in_%22gnome%22 "O as in \"gnome\"") / *SEH-ha-neen MOON-bo* [^14]), also called **Daughter of the Night Skies**, **Goddess of Moonlight**, **the Lunar Lady**, **Moonlit Mystery**, **the Mystic Seer**, **the Luminous Cloud**, and **Lady of Dreams** was an [elven](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Elf "Elf") [goddess](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess "Goddess") of death, dreams, heavens, journeys, moon (full moon to be precise), [moon elves](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_elf "Moon elf"), stars, and transcendence as well as the most powerful female member of the [Seldarine](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Seldarine "Seldarine").[^15]

Around the time of the [Spellplague](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Spellplague "Spellplague"), Sehanine Moonbow was the [fey](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fey "Fey") [aspect](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Aspect "Aspect") of the [Faerûnian goddess](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Faer%C3%BBnian_pantheon "Faerûnian pantheon"), [Selûne](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sel%C3%BBne "Selûne").[^7] However, with the coming of the [Second Sundering](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Sundering "Second Sundering"), she was once again an independent goddess.[^4]

## Description

![ of Sehanine Moonbow.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/3/36/Sehanine_Moonbow_symbol.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20070210080010)

Holy symbol of Sehanine Moonbow.

In her [avatar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Avatar "Avatar") form, Sehanine Moonbow looked like a 5 feet (1.5 meters) tall youthful, ageless, female elf clad in a diaphanous gown, that looked like it was made of moonbeam and dropped moonbeam drops every 10 minutes, that could be bottled as [*potions of invisibility*](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Potion_of_invisibility "Potion of invisibility").[^16]

Another depiction of Sehanine was as a male elf. This depiction portrayed the [Protector](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Corellon "Corellon") and Sehanine as lovers. Characteristic about Sehanine's willowy male form were his eyes. They showed a sense of melancholy and tenderness when he was depicted with Corellon.[^17]

## Personality

![A   of Sehanine Moonbow.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/2/2c/Sehanine_Moonbow_priestess.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220807145333)

A moon elf priest of Sehanine Moonbow.

The Lunar Lady was a very protective deity towards elves. She sent omens to keep her people from danger, crafted illusions around elven hideouts like [Evermeet](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Evermeet "Evermeet"), [Myth Dyraalis](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Myth_Dyraalis "Myth Dyraalis"), [Rucien-Xan](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Rucien-Xan "Rucien-Xan"), and [Synnoria](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Synnoria "Synnoria"), and organized the journeys for elves to these lands.[^15]

She hated [undead](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Undead "Undead") and her followers made it their duty to destroy these beings.[^14] She was intolerant towards [necromancy](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Necromancy "Necromancy") in general, with the sole exceptions being the practice of white necromancy (only tolerated) and the creation of [baelnorn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Baelnorn "Baelnorn") [liches](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lich "Lich"), which she wouldn't tolerate if it was not necessary.[^18] [^14]

Nonetheless, she was quite an aloof deity and apart from the aforementioned deeds, she kept herself apart from the happenings of [Toril](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Toril "Toril").[^15]

As a general rule, she did not speak in a direct manner. Instead, she relied on dreams, visions, etc. to relay information.[^15] This did not mean that she was incapable of speaking in a clear manner as she was seen doing that too.[^19]

## Abilities

Sehanine Moonbow was noted as the most powerful of the female elven [deities](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Deity "Deity") but her power was not stable. To be more precise, it fluctuated with the phase of the moon. She was at her most powerful under full moon,[^9] weak under new moon or fully eclipsed moon,[^20] and practically powerless during daytime.[^21]

She could cast spells from all spheres and schools but favored those of the [all](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/All_sphere "All sphere"), [astral](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_sphere "Astral sphere"), [charm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Charm_sphere "Charm sphere"), [divination](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_sphere "Divination sphere"), [guardian](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian_sphere "Guardian sphere"), [healing](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Healing_sphere "Healing sphere"), [necromantic](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Necromantic_sphere "Necromantic sphere"), [protection](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Protection_sphere "Protection sphere"), [summoning](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_sphere "Summoning sphere"), [sun](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_sphere "Sun sphere"), and travel.[^16]

[Illusion](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Illusion "Illusion") spells cast by the Lunar Lady were hard to resist against and just looking at her could cause a person to sleep for ten to forty minutes. She could cast *[sleep](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sleep "Sleep")* and *[false seeing](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/False_seeing "False seeing")*, the former three times per day, the latter once every minute against one creature within 120 ft. (36.58 m) and was particulary hard to resist. While walking, she created a trail of *[motes of moonlight](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Motes_of_moonlight "Motes of moonlight")* and shot it anywhere she wanted to shoot with it. Once every minute, she could create a *[moonbow](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Moonbow "Moonbow")* and *[moonbridge](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Moonbridge_\(spell\) "Moonbridge (spell)")*. When casting *[dream](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dream "Dream")*, she did not need to enter a trance.[^16]

The Daughter of the Night Skies was completely immune to illusions and any effect directed at altering her mind. It was impossible to hurt her with a weapon that was less enchanted than a *+2 weapon*.[^16]

## Possessions

Sehanine Moonbow owned the *Moonshaft*, a *+2 quarterstaff* with the abilities of *[staff of the moonglow](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Staff_of_the_moonglow "Staff of the moonglow")* and *[staff of night](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Staff_of_night "Staff of night")*.[^16] This weapon was her favored weapon,[^14] but was not used as often as other items and abilities such as wands, spells, and her innate abilities. Other items of hers were her gown, a *[cloak of displacement](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cloak_of_displacement "Cloak of displacement")*, a *[wand of polymorphing](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Wand_of_polymorph "Wand of polymorph")*, and a *[wand of paralyzation](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Wand_of_paralyzation "Wand of paralyzation")*.[^16]

## Divine Realm

Sehanine Moonbow had her realm in [Arvandor](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arvandor "Arvandor"), which was found on the [layer of the same name](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arvandor_\(layer\) "Arvandor (layer)") within the plane of [Arborea](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arborea "Arborea").[^15]

## Activities

As mentioned under [Personality](#Personality), Sehanine Moonbow protected her people’s hideouts via illusions.[^15] She directed her followers to use illusions to hide elven settlements, [^9] guide elves to elven hideouts like [Evermeet](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Evermeet "Evermeet") in the wake of the [Retreat](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Retreat "Retreat"), kill undead,[^22] and seek out/excavate magical lore, especially ones concerning [divination](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Divination "Divination") and illusion magic.[^9]

Elves believed in reincarnation and the Mystic Seer, alongside [Corellon Larethian](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Corellon_Larethian "Corellon Larethian"), were believed the ones to guide the elves towards an end point of perfection. [^15]

## Relationships

### Seldarine

Sehanine Moonbow was a member of the Seldarine and had good relationships with the other members. She was either the daughter or wife of Corellon Larethian [^15] and had at least one son, [Tethrin Veraldé](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tethrin_Verald%C3%A9 "Tethrin Veraldé") with him.[^23]

Her personality trait of kindness was such, that she could bring [Fenmarel Mestarine](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fenmarel_Mestarine "Fenmarel Mestarine"), who isolated himself after an affair with Lolth, to Arvandor on occasion, as well as to calm down [Shevarash](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shevarash "Shevarash") during his fits of rage.[^15]

She was an aspect of [Angharradh](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Angharradh "Angharradh") alongside [Aerdrie Faenya](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Aerdrie_Faenya "Aerdrie Faenya") and [Hanali Celanil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Hanali_Celanil "Hanali Celanil"). However, she was the primary aspect and the others merely secondary ones.[^15]

### Dark Seldarine

Sehanine Moonbow was an enemy of the Dark Seldarine. She opposed Lolth and was specifically hated by [Vhaeraun](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Vhaeraun "Vhaeraun") for escaping his prison. The one exception to this rule was [Eilistraee](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Eilistraee "Eilistraee"), whom she considered an adopted daughter, but had a strained and distant relationship with.[^24]

### Allies

Sehanine Moonbow was an ally of [Baravar Cloakshadow](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Baravar_Cloakshadow "Baravar Cloakshadow"), [Cyrrollalee](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cyrrollalee "Cyrrollalee"), [Dumathoin](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dumathoin "Dumathoin"), [Kelemvor](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kelemvor "Kelemvor"), [Leira](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Leira "Leira"), [Lurue](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lurue "Lurue"), [Milil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Milil "Milil"), [Mystra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystra "Mystra"), [Oberon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Oberon "Oberon"), [Savras](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Savras "Savras"), [Segojan Earthcaller](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Segojan_Earthcaller "Segojan Earthcaller"), [Shaundakul](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shaundakul "Shaundakul"), [Selûne](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sel%C3%BBne "Selûne"), [Titania](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Titania "Titania"), and [Urogalan](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Urogalan "Urogalan").[^15] [^25]

### Enemies

Sehanine Moonbow was an enemy of [Cyric](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cyric "Cyric"), [Gruumsh](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gruumsh "Gruumsh"), the [Gods of Fury](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gods_of_Fury "Gods of Fury"), [Myrkul](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Myrkul "Myrkul"), the [Queen of Air and Darkness](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Queen_of_Air_and_Darkness "Queen of Air and Darkness"), [Shar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar "Shar"), and [Velsharoon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Velsharoon "Velsharoon").[^15] [^25]

## History

How Sehanine Moonbow came to be was unknown, but one theory posited, that she was Corellon’s daughter.[^15] It was believed that the elves originated from a mixture of Corellon’s blood and Sehanine’s tears.[^14] She and Araushnee (later known as Lolth) were rivals over the favor of the Protector. She was one of those who participated at the creation of *[Sahandrian](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sahandrian "Sahandrian")*, Corellon's sword.[^26]

When Araushnee tampered with the sheath of Corellon, so it would break during Corellon's fight against Gruumsh, she did so under a full moon, not only when Sehanine was at her strongest, but also in plain sight of her. When the sword broke, Sehanine came in to help Corellon prevail against Gruumsh.[^27]

Sehanine confronted Araushnee during daytime, and got trapped and imprisoned by Vhaeraun. Around the time, when the [War of the Seldarine](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/War_of_the_Seldarine "War of the Seldarine") ended, she came free under heavy but undefined sacrifices and kept Araushnee from poisoning Corellon. She then created Angharradh with Hanali and Aerdrie.[^28]
