**Savras** (pronounced: /ˈ [s](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/S_as_in_%22sorcerer%22 "S as in \"sorcerer\"") [ɑː](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/A_as_in_%22bard%22 "A as in \"bard\"") [v](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/V_as_in_%22vision%22 "V as in \"vision\"") [r](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/R_as_in_%22ranger%22 "R as in \"ranger\"") [ɑː](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/A_as_in_%22bard%22 "A as in \"bard\"") [s](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/S_as_in_%22sorcerer%22 "S as in \"sorcerer\"") / *SAV-ras* [^2]) was the [deity](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Deity "Deity") of wizards, divination, fate, and truth in the [Faerûnian pantheon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Faer%C3%BBnian_pantheon "Faerûnian pantheon").[^2] [^8] **The Lord of Divination** lost his place as the god of spellcasters to [Azuth](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Azuth "Azuth") and swore an oath of fealty to him, becoming the specific deity of his [school of magic](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/School_of_magic "School of magic"). While some believed **He of the Third Eye** guided the forces of destiny, others thought he knew all history but was unable to affect it,[^7] his emotionless demeanor a mask to hide his deep concern for [Faerun](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Faerun "Faerun") 's fate despite his relative inability to change it.[^2]

“

Ever since Savras's loss, sages have speculated that the All-Seeing One must have foreseen his own defeat. If this is true, perhaps Savras planned his own defeat as a feint in a long-term ploy for victory. Only he can say, and he refuses to speak on it.

”

— Foresight Forsaken [^7]

## Description

He was portrayed as a man with a crystalline face, clear to all who saw him. His statements were clear, concise, and rarely what the listener wished to hear. Savras' symbol was a monstrous eye, or a crystal ball filled with eyes.[^7] [^8]

## Activities

Savras' main duty was to monitor the casting of [Epic Magic](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Magic "Epic Magic"), and curb its usage amidst non-mortals & extraplanars, especially when this was done heedlessly.[^9]

## Relationships

Savras, along with [Velsharoon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Velsharoon "Velsharoon"), served [Azuth](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Azuth "Azuth"), who was his superior, and thus served Mystra indirectly.[^7] [^2] He resented his service to Azuth,[^2] but the two were steadily, if cautiously, establishing a formal division of portfolios and responsibilities, and a more amiable relationship.[^7]

Savras was worshiped in both the north and south as a god of diviners and truth-speakers. His clergy was still sparse in the late 14 <sup>th</sup> century [DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/DR "DR"), as Savras had just been released from his centuries-old prison.[^7] [^2] Savras's only famous servant was [Alaundo](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Alaundo "Alaundo") of [Candlekeep](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Candlekeep "Candlekeep"), the one true prophet of the [Realms](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Realms "Realms").[^7]

Savras was particularly beloved in the nations of the [Chultan Peninsula](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chultan_Peninsula "Chultan Peninsula") ([Tashalar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tashalar "Tashalar"), [Samarach](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Samarach "Samarach"), [Nimbral](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nimbral "Nimbral"), and so on), where [scrying](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Scrying "Scrying") and spying were practically the national pastimes because the inhabitants' fear of the [yuan-ti](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Yuan-ti "Yuan-ti").[^10]

The [Gurs](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gur "Gur") were known to worship Savras, which may explain the survival of his faith during his long period of imprisonment.[^11]

The most devout worshipers of Savras carried around elaborate [staves](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Stave "Stave") with them at all times. This was in the hope that their god might make their staff his temporary residence.[^12]

### Orders

Benign Order of the Third Eye

A secret cult would seem to be anathema for a god devoted to finding the truth, but in [1371 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1371_DR "1371 DR") such a cult did form. Consisting mainly of [archivists](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Archivist "Archivist") (and incidentally containing the highest concentration of archivists of any group in the Realms) the Benign Order of the Third Eye 's members were all granted a vision from Savras where he impressed upon them the need to collect magic and lore in preparation for an upcoming battle with [Cyric](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cyric "Cyric"). Unfortunately, the Order was overzealous in its search and became known for graverobbing.[^13]

## History

Once Savras was a mortal wizard of [Halruaa](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Halruaa "Halruaa").[^14]

Savras, a demigod, and Azuth, a wizard with god-like abilities, once dueled when Azuth contended for Savras's place in the [Year of the Hangman's Noose, 161 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/161_DR "161 DR"). Savras fell, with Azuth becoming the one god of wizards.[^7] [^2] [^15]

Savras was imprisoned in the *[Scepter of Savras](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Scepter_of_Savras "Scepter of Savras")* by Azuth, where he remained until the [Time of Troubles](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Time_of_Troubles "Time of Troubles") in the [Year of Shadows, 1358 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1358_DR "1358 DR"). Following this, Azuth agreed to release Savras in exchange for a pledge of fealty.[^7] [^2]

But in the [Year of Blue Fire, 1385 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1385_DR "1385 DR"), [Cyric](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cyric "Cyric") entered [Dweomerheart](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dweomerheart "Dweomerheart") with [Shar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar "Shar") 's aid and there murdered [Mystra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystra_\(Midnight\) "Mystra (Midnight)"). The plane broke apart, hurling Azuth and Velsharoon deep into the [Astral Plane](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Plane "Astral Plane") and killing Savras outright.[^4]

Yet he got better, returning to godhood by the 1480s DR.[^1] [^3] By [1490 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1490_DR "1490 DR"), priests were still actively worshiping Savras in temples across [Chult](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chult "Chult").[^8]
