# 5e 2014 vs 2024: What Changed

A one-stop map of how the 2024 core books (PHB/DMG/MM, sometimes called "5.5e" or "5.24") differ from the 2014 originals. Built from web sources collated 2026-10-24, then spot-checked against the local 5etools install.

> [!info] How to read this note
> - ✅ = checked against the 2024/2014 book data on disk (5etools `XPHB`/`XDMG`/`XMM` vs `PHB`/`DMG`/`MM`).
> - Unmarked = from web sources only. Probably right, but look it up in 5etools before ruling on it at the table.
> - **Rules text always wins over this note.** It summarizes changes; it doesn't reproduce them.
> - **Web claims I found to be wrong** are listed at the bottom so they don't sneak back in.

---

## Top 15 Changes That Hit the Table Hardest

1. **Ability score bonuses come from your background, not your species.** ✅ (+2/+1 or +1/+1/+1, max 20, but only among the **three abilities that background lists**; e.g. Acolyte = Int/Wis/Cha.)
2. **Every character gets an Origin feat at level 1**, via their background. ✅
3. **All subclasses start at level 3**, including Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard, and Druid. ✅
4. **Weapon Mastery:** every weapon has a mastery property; Barbarian, Fighter, Paladin, Ranger, and Rogue unlock them (not Monk). ✅
5. **Surprise no longer skips a turn.** Surprised creatures roll Initiative with Disadvantage instead. ✅
6. **Exhaustion is a flat penalty:** −2 per level to every D20 Test (checks, attacks, saves), −5 ft Speed per level, death at 6. ✅
7. **One spell slot per turn for casting.** You can't cast two slot spells in one turn (e.g. a Bonus Action spell plus an action spell). Cantrips are fine, and non-casting slot uses (e.g. Sorcerer Font of Magic) don't count. ✅
8. **Potions take a Bonus Action** to drink or administer. ✅
9. **Long Rest restores ALL spent Hit Dice** (was half), and you need at least 1 HP to start a rest. ✅
10. **Grapple and Shove are Unarmed Strike options** that force a save (target picks Str or Dex) instead of contested Athletics checks. ✅
11. **Hide is a DC 15 Stealth check** that grants the Invisible condition until you make noise, attack, cast a Verbal spell, or get found. ✅
12. **Heroic Inspiration** (renamed from Inspiration) rerolls **any die** (damage dice included) **after** you see it, and you must keep the new roll. 2014 Inspiration gave Advantage instead. ✅
13. **Every class gets a level 19 feat**: an Epic Boon or any other feat you qualify for. ✅
14. **Divine Smite is now a spell**, so it costs a Bonus Action and a slot competes with the one-slot rule. The once-per-long-rest free cast from Paladin's Smite uses no slot. It also works with Unarmed Strikes. ✅
15. **Counterspell forces a Con save** instead of an ability check, and a countered caster keeps their slot. Don't let a 2014-trained caster assume the old version. ✅

## 1. Core Rules & Terminology

**New vocabulary**

| 2014 | 2024 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Race | **Species** | ✅ |
| Inspiration | **Heroic Inspiration** | ✅ Reroll after rolling |
| "Attack roll / save / check" listed separately | **D20 Test** | ✅ Umbrella term for all three |
| Cast a Spell action | **Magic** action | ✅ Also covers activating magic items/features |
| Use an Object | **Utilize** action | ✅ |
| (Persuasion/Deception/Intimidation, freeform) | **Influence** action | ✅ New formal action |
| (Recall lore, freeform) | **Study** action | ✅ Int check |
| Grapple / Shove actions | Folded into **Unarmed Strike** | ✅ |
| Aura-style areas | **Emanation** | New area shape that moves with you |
| — | **Bloodied** | Formal term: at or below half HP |

A **Rules Glossary** appendix now defines every rules term in one place. ✅

**Rule changes**

- **Surprise:** no lost turn; Disadvantage on Initiative instead. Being Invisible when rolling Initiative gives Advantage. ✅
- **Exhaustion:** −2 × level to D20 Tests, −5 ft × level to Speed, die at level 6, Long Rest removes 1 level. The 2014 version was a six-step ladder of different penalties. ✅
- **Resting:** Long Rest returns all HP **and all Hit Point Dice**. Both rest types need at least 1 HP to start. You must wait 16 hours between Long Rests. An interrupted Long Rest can be resumed, at +1 hour per interruption. ✅
- **Hiding:** DC 15 Dex (Stealth) while Heavily Obscured or behind Three-Quarters/Total Cover and out of enemies' line of sight. Success grants the Invisible condition, and your check total becomes the DC to find you. You stop being hidden if you make a sound louder than a whisper, make an attack roll, cast a spell with a Verbal component, or an enemy finds you. ✅
- **Heroic Inspiration:** if you gain it while already holding it, you either give it to a PC who has none or lose it. ✅
- **Drawing weapons:** you can draw or stow one weapon as part of each attack in the Attack action, in addition to your free object interaction.
- **Moving between attacks** in Extra Attack is now stated explicitly.
- **Flanking** (an optional DMG rule in 2014) doesn't appear in the 2024 DMG. ✅

## 2. Combat

- **Unarmed Strike has three modes:** Damage, Grapple, or Shove. ✅ Each one replaces one attack, so you can punch, grapple, and shove in a single Extra Attack sequence.
- **Grapple/Shove DC:** 8 + Str mod + proficiency. The target makes a Str or Dex save (its choice). ✅ Escaping a grapple uses the Escape a Grapple action. ✅
- **Grappled condition:** Speed 0, Disadvantage on attacks against anyone except the grappler, and the grappler can drag you (extra movement cost). ✅
- **Two-Weapon Fighting:** the extra attack still comes from the Light property. The **Nick** mastery moves it into the Attack action, freeing up your Bonus Action. ✅ The Dual Wielder feat adds another.
- **Potions:** Bonus Action. ✅

> [!note]- Weapon Mastery properties (8) ✅
> | Mastery | Gist |
> |---|---|
> | **Cleave** | Hit with a melee attack → free attack on a second adjacent creature (once per turn) |
> | **Graze** | Miss → still deal damage equal to your ability modifier |
> | **Nick** | Light-weapon extra attack happens inside the Attack action, not as a Bonus Action |
> | **Push** | Hit → push a Large or smaller target 10 ft |
> | **Sap** | Hit → target has Disadvantage on its next attack |
> | **Slow** | Hit and damage → −10 ft Speed |
> | **Topple** | Hit → Con save or knocked Prone |
> | **Vex** | Hit and damage → Advantage on your next attack against that target |
>
> Barbarian, Fighter, Paladin, Ranger, and Rogue get Weapon Mastery at level 1 ✅. Anyone else can get it from the **Weapon Master** feat.

## 3. Character Creation

**Order of operations changed:** pick a class, then a **background** (which supplies ability scores, an Origin feat, skills, a tool, and equipment), then a **species** (traits only, no ASIs). The "no ASIs from species" part is ✅; the step order comes from web sources.

### Species ✅

| 2014 PHB | 2024 PHB |
|---|---|
| Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Human, Tiefling | All return, reworked (subraces folded into "lineage" choices) |
| **Half-Elf, Half-Orc** | **Removed** |
| — | **Aasimar, Goliath, Orc** (new to the PHB) |

- Species no longer give ability score increases. ✅
- Human gets a bonus Origin feat (the 2014 "variant human" idea made core).

### Backgrounds ✅

Sixteen backgrounds, each granting **set ability score options plus a specific Origin feat**:

> [!note]- 2024 backgrounds and their Origin feats ✅
> | Background | Origin feat | | Background | Origin feat |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Acolyte | Magic Initiate (Cleric) | | Hermit | Healer |
> | Artisan | Crafter | | Merchant | Lucky |
> | Charlatan | Skilled | | Noble | Skilled |
> | Criminal | Alert | | Sage | Magic Initiate (Wizard) |
> | Entertainer | Musician | | Sailor | Tavern Brawler |
> | Farmer | Tough | | Scribe | Skilled |
> | Guard | Alert | | Soldier | Savage Attacker |
> | Guide | Magic Initiate (Druid) | | Wayfarer | Lucky |
>
> **New:** Artisan, Farmer, Guard, Guide, Merchant, Scribe, Wayfarer.
> **2014-only (dropped):** Folk Hero, Guild Artisan (≈ Artisan), Outlander (≈ Guide), Urchin (≈ Wayfarer), plus all the "variant" backgrounds and the Custom Background rule. The "≈" pairs are my thematic matches, not official ones.

### Feats ✅

Feats are now core and come in **four categories**:

| Category | When you get them | Count (2024 PHB) |
|---|---|---|
| **Origin** | Level 1 (from background) | 10 |
| **General** | Level 4+ (in place of ASI; ASI is itself a General feat) | 43 incl. ASI |
| **Fighting Style** | Through the Fighting Style class feature | 10 (+ Blessed Warrior / Druidic Warrior, class-only) |
| **Epic Boon** | Level 19 | 12 |

- **Most General feats now include +1 to an ability score** (half-feats became the norm).
- **2014 PHB feats not reprinted:** Dungeon Delver, Linguist, Martial Adept, Mobile. ✅ (Speedy covers some of Mobile's ground.)
- **New feats pulled from other 2014-era books:** Chef, Crusher, Piercer, Slasher, Fey-Touched, Shadow-Touched, Telekinetic, Telepathic, Poisoner, Skill Expert. ✅
- **Brand-new:** Crafter, Musician, Speedy, Martial Weapon Training, plus all Epic Boons. ✅
- **Big reworks:** Great Weapon Master and Sharpshooter lose the −5/+10 trade (GWM now adds proficiency bonus to damage). Lucky is tied to proficiency bonus. Alert lets you swap initiative with an ally.

## 4. Classes

**Shared changes across every class** ✅
- Subclass at level 3.
- Epic Boon at level 19.
- A lot of **Tasha's Cauldron optional features became core**, e.g. Steady Aim (Rogue), Deft Explorer (Ranger), Wild Companion (Druid), Primal Knowledge (Barbarian). ✅
- **Spell preparation was unified:** most casters now prepare spells from a table, and nearly every class can swap prepared spells.
- **Ritual casting** for any caster, as long as the spell is prepared. ✅ Wizards (Ritual Adept) can ritual-cast straight from the spellbook without preparing it. ✅
- Paladin and Ranger get **Spellcasting at level 1** (was level 2). ✅

> [!note]- Class-by-class deltas
> *A ✅ here means the feature names and levels match the disk. Mechanical detail inside each entry comes from the web unless it's separately confirmed. Confirmed details so far: Countercharm reaction, Stunning Strike once per turn, Divine Order, Primal Order, Second Wind two uses, Wild Shape as a Bonus Action with temp HP equal to druid level and a fixed set of known forms, Magical Cunning.*
> **Barbarian** ✅ Weapon Mastery (L1). Brutal Strike (L9) replaces Brutal Critical: trade Reckless Attack's Advantage for extra damage plus a rider. Rage stays up if you attack, force a save, or spend a Bonus Action to extend it (taking damage no longer counts), regains one use on a Short Rest from L1 ✅, and at L15 regains all uses on initiative, once per long rest. ✅ Primal Knowledge (L3) lets you use Strength for some skills while raging. New subclass: **World Tree**. Totem Warrior became **Wild Heart**. **Zealot** joins the PHB.
>
> **Bard** ✅ Expertise moves to **L2** (was L3), and a second Expertise moves to **L9** (was L10). Countercharm at L7 is now a reaction. Font of Inspiration at L5 stays. Song of Rest is gone. Magical Secrets (L10) becomes "pick from the Bard, Cleric, Druid, or Wizard list" for all future spell choices. L20 = **Words of Creation**. New subclasses: **Dance**, plus Glamour from XGtE.
>
> **Cleric** ✅ The subclass moves from L1 to **L3**. **Divine Order** (L1): choose Protector (martial weapons + heavy armor) or Thaumaturge (extra cantrip + Arcana/Religion bonus). Channel Divinity adds **Divine Spark** (heal or damage). **Sear Undead** replaces Destroy Undead. **Blessed Strikes** (L7) replaces domain-specific Divine Strike / Potent Spellcasting. Greater Divine Intervention at L20. **Knowledge, Nature, and Tempest dropped** from the PHB.
>
> **Druid** ✅ The subclass moves to **L3**. **Primal Order** (L1): Magician or Warden (martial weapons + medium armor). Wild Shape is a Bonus Action, has a fixed list of "known forms", and gives **temp HP = druid level** (Circle of the Moon: 3 × druid level) instead of the beast's HP pool. ✅ Wild Resurgence (L5). **Elemental Fury** (L7). New subclasses: **Sea** (new), **Stars** (from TCE).
>
> **Fighter** ✅ Weapon Mastery (L1). **Tactical Mind** (L2): spend Second Wind to boost a failed ability check. **Tactical Shift** (L5): move when you use Second Wind. **Tactical Master** (L9): swap a weapon's mastery for Push, Sap, or Slow on an attack. ✅ **Studied Attacks** (L13). Second Wind gets more uses. New subclass: **Psi Warrior** (from TCE).
>
> **Monk** ✅ Ki → **Focus Points**. Martial Arts die starts at d6. **Uncanny Metabolism** (L2) regains Focus on initiative once per long rest. Patient Defense and Step of the Wind have a no-cost version (Disengage or Dash as a Bonus Action); spending Focus adds more. Flurry of Blows still costs Focus. (Web-sourced; check the Monk entry.) **Deflect Attacks** replaces Deflect Missiles and covers melee hits too. Stunning Strike is limited to once per turn. Four Elements is rebuilt as **Warrior of the Elements**. **Mercy** (from TCE) is added.
>
> **Paladin** ✅ Spellcasting and Weapon Mastery at **L1**. Lay on Hands is a **Bonus Action** (was an action) ✅. Divine Sense moves into **Channel Divinity** (L3). Smite is now the *Divine Smite* spell (Bonus Action, one free cast per long rest via Paladin's Smite). **Faithful Steed** (L5) gives a free *Find Steed*. **Abjure Foes** (L9). Improved Divine Smite → **Radiant Strikes**. Cleansing Touch → **Restoring Touch**, which feeds off Lay on Hands. **Glory** (from TCE) is added.
>
> **Ranger** ✅ Spellcasting and Weapon Mastery at **L1**. Favored Enemy becomes free *Hunter's Mark* casts. Deft Explorer (L2), **Roving** (L6), Expertise (L9), **Tireless** (L10), **Relentless Hunter** (L13, damage can't break Concentration on Hunter's Mark), **Precise Hunter** (L17). Natural Explorer, Primeval Awareness, and Hide in Plain Sight are gone. **Fey Wanderer** and **Gloom Stalker** join the PHB.
>
> **Rogue** ✅ Weapon Mastery (L1). **Cunning Strike** (L5): spend Sneak Attack dice on riders such as Poison, Trip, or Withdraw. Reliable Talent moves to **L7** (was L11). **Improved Cunning Strike** (L11), **Devious Strikes** (L14). Blindsense is gone. **Soulknife** (from TCE) is added. Expertise stays at L1 and L6, same as 2014.
>
> **Sorcerer** ✅ **Innate Sorcery** (L1): a short buff to spell DC and attacks. Metamagic moves to **L2** (was L3). Sorcerous Restoration moves to **L5** (was L20). **Sorcery Incarnate** (L7). L20 = **Arcane Apotheosis**. The subclass moves to **L3**. **Aberrant** and **Clockwork** (from TCE) are added.
>
> **Warlock** ✅ Eldritch Invocations at **L1** (was L2). **Pact Boons are now invocations**, so you can take more than one. **Magical Cunning** (L2): a 1-minute rite that restores up to half your Pact slots, once per long rest. ✅ **Contact Patron** (L9). The subclass moves to **L3**. **Celestial** (from XGtE) is added.
>
> **Wizard** ✅ **Ritual Adept** (L1). **Scholar** (L2): Expertise in one academic skill. **Memorize Spell** (L5): swap a prepared spell on a short rest. Cantrip Formulas (a TCE option) is folded into Spellcasting: swap one cantrip per long rest. ✅ The subclass moves to **L3**, and only **Abjurer, Diviner, Evoker, and Illusionist** remain in the PHB. **Conjuration, Enchantment, Necromancy, and Transmutation are dropped.**

> [!note]- Subclass roster: 2014 PHB → 2024 PHB ✅
> | Class | 2014 PHB | 2024 PHB |
> |---|---|---|
> | Barbarian | Berserker, Totem Warrior | Berserker, Wild Heart, World Tree, Zealot |
> | Bard | Lore, Valor | Dance, Glamour, Lore, Valor |
> | Cleric | Knowledge, Life, Light, Nature, Tempest, Trickery, War | Life, Light, Trickery, War |
> | Druid | Land, Moon | Land, Moon, Sea, Stars |
> | Fighter | Battle Master, Champion, Eldritch Knight | + Psi Warrior |
> | Monk | Open Hand, Shadow, Four Elements | Mercy, Open Hand, Shadow, Elements |
> | Paladin | Devotion, Ancients, Vengeance | + Glory |
> | Ranger | Beast Master, Hunter | + Fey Wanderer, Gloom Stalker |
> | Rogue | Arcane Trickster, Assassin, Thief | + Soulknife |
> | Sorcerer | Draconic, Wild Magic | + Aberrant, Clockwork |
> | Warlock | Archfey, Fiend, Great Old One | + Celestial |
> | Wizard | 8 schools | Abjurer, Diviner, Evoker, Illusionist |

## 5. Spells

**System-level changes**
- **One spell slot per turn**, for casting spells only. ✅
- Any caster can ritual-cast a prepared spell; Wizards can also ritual-cast unprepared spells from their spellbook. ✅
- **Emanation** is the new area shape for self-centered auras (Spirit Guardians, etc.).
- Summon spells include their own stat blocks in the PHB.
- Smite spells trigger **after** a hit, as a Bonus Action. **Most dropped Concentration**, but Banishing Smite and Shining Smite still need it, and Blinding, Searing, and Wrathful Smite still last 1 minute. ✅
- **Most healing spells roughly doubled:** Cure Wounds 2d8 ✅, Healing Word 2d4 ✅.
- 2024 PHB has 391 spells. ✅ About 10 are brand-new; the rest come from the 2014 PHB, XGtE, and TCE.

> [!note]- Brand-new spells (10)
> Starry Wisp, Sorcerous Burst, Elementalism (cantrips) · Divine Smite (1st) · Arcane Vigor (2nd) · Fount of Moonlight (4th) · Yolande's Regal Presence, Jallarzi's Storm of Radiance (5th) · Tasha's Bubbling Cauldron (6th) · Power Word Fortify (7th).
> Pulled in from other books: Toll the Dead, Mind Sliver, Ice Knife, Synaptic Static, Vitriolic Sphere, Word of Radiance, and others.

> [!note]- Big individual spell changes
> **Cantrips**
> - **Guidance:** choose one skill; the 1d4 applies to every check with that skill for the duration. ✅
> - **True Strike:** now makes a weapon attack using your spellcasting modifier, with damage that scales. ✅ Went from a joke to a staple.
> - **Blade Ward:** Concentration; attackers subtract 1d4 instead of you getting resistance.
> - **Resistance:** reduces damage of one type by 1d4 instead of boosting saves.
> - **Poison Spray:** spell attack, 30 ft.
> - **Shillelagh:** force damage option, and the damage die scales.
> - **Friends:** a failed save means Charmed.
> - **Spare the Dying:** ranged.
>
> **1st–3rd**
> - **Sleep:** no more HP pool. Wis save or Incapacitated, then a second failed save means Unconscious. Concentration, smaller radius. ✅
> - **Hunter's Mark:** the extra damage is now **force** and triggers on **any attack roll** (2014: weapon attacks only). The Advantage on Perception/Survival to find the target was already in 2014. ✅ The class gets free casts.
> - **Hex:** still Bonus Action + Concentration, necrotic. ✅
> - **Barkskin:** Bonus Action, no Concentration, AC 17. ✅
> - **Spiritual Weapon:** **now requires Concentration.** ✅
> - **Inflict Wounds:** Con save, 2d10 necrotic, half damage on a success. ✅
> - **Chromatic Orb:** bounces on doubles.
> - **Color Spray:** Con save or Blinded; no HP pool.
> - **Find Steed:** cast in an action, one otherworldly steed stat block.
> - **Lesser Restoration:** Bonus Action.
> - **Prayer of Healing:** also grants short-rest benefits.
> - **Hail of Thorns / Lightning Arrow:** Bonus Action, no Concentration. Hail of Thorns is cast after a ranged hit; Lightning Arrow after a hit **or a miss**. Lightning Arrow **replaces** the attack's damage with 4d8 lightning (half on a miss). ✅
> - **Conjure Animals:** now a damaging spectral pack, not a summon.
> - **Spirit Guardians / Cloud of Daggers:** damage at end of turn and more consistent triggers.
> - **Counterspell:** target makes a **Con save**. On a failed save the spell is countered, but the caster **keeps the slot**. ✅
>
> **4th+**
> - **Polymorph / True Polymorph / Shapechange / Animal Shapes:** the new form gives **temp HP** instead of a fresh HP pool. Only **Polymorph** ends early when those temp HP run out; the others keep going. ✅
> - **Conjure Minor Elementals / Woodland Beings / Elemental / Fey:** reworked into damage auras or spirits, not multi-creature summons.
> - **Giant Insect:** summons a stat block.
> - **Animate Objects:** number of objects based on your spellcasting modifier.
> - **Contagion:** Con save, 11d8.
> - **Feeblemind → Befuddlement:** heavy psychic damage and blocks casting, no more Int 1.
> - **Plane Shift:** can't banish an enemy anymore.
> - **Earthquake:** caster chooses effects.
> - **Power Word Heal:** ranged.

Spells also moved between class lists. Check the class list on the spell itself (XPHB spell entries list their classes).

## 6. Equipment

- Every weapon has a **mastery property** (see §2). ✅
- **Tools are spelled out:** ability, uses, and what you can craft with each one.
- **Crafting** rules moved into the PHB and became usable.
- Adventuring gear has clearer mechanical effects (fewer "the DM decides" items).
- Armor and weapon stats are largely unchanged.

## 7. Dungeon Master's Guide

- **Encounter building:** XP budget per character × party size, with three tiers: **Low / Moderate / High**. ✅ The 2014 Easy/Medium/Hard/Deadly scale and its group-size multipliers are gone.
- **Bastions** (new chapter): player-owned strongholds with facilities and hirelings that generate benefits during downtime.
- **Magic items:** ~204 items, 21 new, 88 redesigned, 95 revised (figures approximate, verify). Adamantine and Silvered Weapon are now formal items.
- **Expanded DM's Toolbox:** chases, mobs, curses, fear and mental stress, hazards, traps, supernatural gifts, siege equipment, and more.
- **Reorganized** from "running a session" up to "running a campaign," with a Greyhawk example setting and a lore glossary.
- **Flanking** isn't in the 2024 DMG. ✅

## 8. Monster Manual

**Stat block format**
- **Gear** line: what equipment the monster carries. ✅
- **Initiative modifier** printed in the stat block.
- **Bonus Actions** get their own section (e.g. the Goblin Warrior has a Bonus Action block the 2014 Goblin lacked). ✅
- Saves and ability modifiers combined in one table.
- Spellcasting rewritten as at-will and per-day lists, not spell-slot casters. ✅ (e.g. the Mage)

**Content**
- **Most 2014 monsters kept their CR** or have a similar-CR replacement, but **not all**. The Mind Flayer Arcanist went from CR 8 to CR 11. ✅ Check CR per creature before running an old adventure.
- **85+ new monsters**, more apex creatures, and dragons' spellcasting built into their stat blocks.
- **Many humanoid "race" monsters were generalized**, for example:
  - Orc → Tough
  - Drow → Priest Acolyte
  - Thug → Tough
  - Veteran → Warrior Veteran
  - Cult Fanatic → Cultist Fanatic
- **Renames:**
  - Goblin → Goblin Warrior
  - Androsphinx → Sphinx of Valor
  - Gynosphinx → Sphinx of Lore
  - Poisonous Snake → Venomous Snake
  - Quipper → Piranha
- Lore entries are shorter. Some fiend lore moved to the DMG.

## 9. Mixing Editions (Our Table Allows 2014 Options)

The 2024 PHB itself gives conversion rules ✅:

- **Older background** (no ASI) → add +2/+1 or +1/+1/+1 yourself, max 20. If it has no feat, take an **Origin feat** of your choice.
- **Older species** → **ignore its ability score increases**. Your ASIs come only from your background.

**Where 2014 picks can underperform or collide** (my reading, not official):
- A **2014 subclass** on a 2024 class may expect features at the old level (e.g. a 2014 Cleric domain's L1 features now arrive at L3).
- **2014 Great Weapon Master/Sharpshooter** wording doesn't fit the 2024 base classes. Pick one version per character.
- **2014 spells** that were reworked in 2024 (Sleep, Counterspell, Spiritual Weapon, etc.): which version applies is the DM's call. Settle it per character before play.
- **2014 monsters** run fine, but they lack Initiative scores and Bonus Action lines.

## Web Claims That Turned Out Wrong

These showed up in sources and **contradict the disk**:
- "Rogue Expertise moved to level 1": no, it was already level 1 in 2014. ✅
- "Sleep: Wis save for unconsciousness": the first failure gives Incapacitated; the repeat failure gives Unconscious. ✅
- "Exhaustion reduces d20 tests by 2": it's 2 **per level**. ✅
- "Inflict Wounds deals 3d10": it's 2d10. ✅
- "Every 2014 monster kept its CR": the Mind Flayer Arcanist went from 8 to 11. ✅
- "Smites no longer need Concentration": Banishing and Shining Smite still do. ✅
