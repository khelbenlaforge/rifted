# Avatar Crisis Chaos Table

## The Rule

During the party's shared prequel vision of the Time of Troubles—the Avatar Crisis—any spell cast in combat rolls d100 against the source **FRE1 Magical Chaos Table** (its own front-cover chart, not reproduced here—see Sourcing below). The roll is modified: +1 per caster level, +12 for a transformative spell, +4 for a small or simple cantrip. Avatars and [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Characters/NPCs/Forgotten Realms/Shadowdale/Elminster|Elminster]] are exempt from the roll entirely, left to the DM's discretion instead.

The rule only applies inside these Time of Troubles-era scenes, when Mystra's death has "doubled the chaos magic roll frequency for all casters in Faerûn" (see [[Mystra]] § The Fragment Carriers). Every other combat in the campaign runs unmodified 5e spellcasting.

## Sourcing

The percentile table itself is the **FRE1 Magical Chaos Table** (inside front cover)—a physical TSR product, not something reproduced or reconstructed in this vault. Only the modifier rule and the outcomes actually rolled at the table are recorded here. If a full transcription is ever wanted, it needs to come from the source book directly, never invented to fill the gap.
