## Description

The Time of Troubles — the Avatar Crisis — is the cataclysm at the center of the party's shared prequel vision: the moment [[AO]], the Overgod, discovered the Tablets of Fate had been stolen and, holding every god equally responsible for the negligence, stripped them all of their divine power and cast their essences into mortal avatars. Struck from the heavens without warning, the gods walked the world in borrowed flesh, vulnerable in ways they had never been.

The disruption reached everywhere at once. [[Helm]] alone stood guard at the celestial stairways, barring even fellow gods from passage. [[Oghma]]'s vast network of temples and scholars fragmented under the strain. [[Storm Silverhand]] describes trying to sense the Weave's disruptions during this period as "like trying to breathe smoke." Political alliances that had held for generations — the kind [[Mourngrym Amcathra]] now works to hold together in [[Shadowdale]] — came under a strain that has not fully lifted.

Beyond the mortal world, the disruption tore at the boundaries between planes. Vast numbers of souls were severed from their proper afterlives and scattered across the multiverse, unclaimed and unreachable by the divine ledgers that should have received them. The scope of that scattering, and who if anyone is still trying to undo it, is a thread very much still unfolding.

## Role in the Story

The party didn't hear about this event secondhand — they lived it, in a shared prequel vision alongside Forgotten Realms figures like [[Elminster]], [[Midnight]], [[Storm Silverhand]], [[Torm of the Dales]], and [[Selmavra Elsree]]. Its exact relationship to the party's arrival in [[Aenath]] has not been made clear to them.
