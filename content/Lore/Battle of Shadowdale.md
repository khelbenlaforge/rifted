# Battle of Shadowdale

## Key Participants

## Session 15 Update (The Door Kickers)

The defense of [[Shadowdale]] against a [[Zhentarim]] assault during the Avatar Crisis, circa 1358 DR. The battle ended with the intervention of [[Elminster]], the [[Knights of Myth Drannor]], and the manifestation of [[Mystra]] through [[Midnight]]'s pendant—pulling [[Bane]] through the Celestial Stairway and displacing Elminster in the process.

- [[Elminster]]—held off Bane long enough for Mystra to manifest
- [[Midnight]]—vessel for Mystra's manifestation
- [[Bane]]—Zhentarim patron; pulled through the Celestial Stairway
- [[Storm Silverhand]], [[Rathan Thentraver]]—present at Elminster's Tower
- [[Knights of Myth Drannor]]—arrived in the final phase
- [[Selmavra Elsree]], [[Torm of the Dales]]—Harper operatives

The party witnessed this event firsthand during the prequel vision in Session 15.

- [[Sharantyr]]—charged the party with Elminster's murder as the vision ended
