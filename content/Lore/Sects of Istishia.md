# Sects of Istishia

The four sects of [[Istishia]]'s faith, as taught by [[Tidecaller Marin Deepcurrent]] at the [[Tidecaller's Sanctuary]]. Each sect emphasises a different aspect of water's nature. Marin himself calls them four doors, and gives each the same shorthand he teaches students by: "Sacred Sea, where you learn to stop controlling the water and let it carry you; Paths of Water, where you direct without forcing; Storm, chaos as teacher; and Eternal Transformation"—which he leaves for whenever a student has earned the handle, and won't elaborate on early.

## Sacred Sea

The Sacred Sea emphasises reverence for water in its vast, unchanging form — the ocean as the origin and destiny of all things. Its practitioners tend toward contemplation and awe, finding the divine in stillness and depth.

[[Gill Bates]] is the first PC to walk this door. Marin compressed the first level of the trial to a single downtime day, then ran it to completion the following session: one failed attempt (Gill tried to hold the goddess's fading presence by force rather than let it go, and lost two days to it) and one successful attempt, reached by prayer rather than force, with Marin casting Sending mid-vision to remind him to breathe. Gill came out of it with Create or Destroy Water as an always-prepared spell and a smooth grey stone pressed into his hand from the pool's own bed. Marin's task for him afterward: try to feel the ocean again at the next [[Aenath Landmass Types#Landfall|landfall]], or at any underground spring along the way.

## Watery Paths

The Watery Paths sect follows the river's logic — going where the current leads, trusting that water always finds its way. Its core teaching is surrender to flow rather than resistance. Marin warned [[Gill Bates]] that this path can make a person seem detached to others; the practitioner may appear unanchored when they are simply moving with forces others cannot perceive.

## Magnificent Storm

The Magnificent Storm sect centres on water as force — the ocean in fury, the storm surge, the flood. Its practitioners embrace [[Istishia]]'s destructive and overwhelming aspect, understanding that water does not ask permission when it moves.

## Eternal Transformation

The Eternal Transformation sect holds that water's defining quality is its capacity to change state — liquid, ice, vapour — while remaining essentially itself. Its teachings centre on cycles and transition — change as continuity taking a different shape, rather than as loss.
