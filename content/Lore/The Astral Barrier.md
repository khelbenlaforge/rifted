## Description

The astral barrier encloses far more than Aenath itself. The Ethereal Plane, the Shadowfell, and all four elemental planes sit inside it alongside the material world—it shuts out only what lies beyond them, which is where the gods are meant to live. That is why the Church of [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]], whose charge is policing souls out to the afterlives, cannot do the job directly, and has fallen back on a ritual instead, hoping the ritual works.

Nothing that punches a hole through space and time functions here. Short hops still work—[[Veyris Thantorn]]'s reading, out of the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Factions/Corranor/Alchemist's Guild|Alchemist's Guild]], is that Misty Step and similar effects only skim the Ethereal, which the barrier still encloses. Anything that would reach further has nowhere to reach.

The party worked out, unprompted, during their Session 28 Guild debrief that the barrier was raised to hide the new world as much as to protect it. It went up while the world itself was still being made, and whatever hired the entity backing [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Shen|Shen]]'s patron to help hold the line while it rose, it was hired mercenary-fashion—a job, not a calling.

## The Stuck State

Veyris Thantorn's own read on the barrier, from her Session 21 lesson to [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Min Ji-ah|Min Ji-ah]]: it is "stuck in a position no one has caused and no one knew how to undo." Working theories on what might shift it circulate among scholars who study the question—tiefling blood, aasimar blood, and other substances tied to the outer planes generally are all floated as possibilities, along with planar items carried by living magical creatures. None of these is confirmed to do anything. PC growth items are specifically ruled out as counting toward this.

## How the Party Arrived

This world has no native teleportation magic, which makes the party's arrival not merely unusual but impossible by Aenath's own rules—they did not come from another plane. They came from another universe. Nobody in Aenath, player character or otherwise, has an explanation for the mechanism. **This is deliberate and stays that way**: record only what's been observed (runic circles, blood/bone/consciousness components, anchors, a soul described as "ripped in half") and the theories characters float about them, and never state or resolve how the crossing actually works.
