# The Weave in Aenath

> [!infobox|right wikipedia]+
> # The Weave in Aenath
> ###### Quick Reference
> | | |
> | ---- | --- |

During Session 19 downtime, [[Veyris Thantorn]] explained the Weave to [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Min Ji-ah|Min Ji-ah]] as a three-dimensional tapestry whose threads can be pulled and retied. In principle, teleportation could be recreated through it at fixed sites on the land—the theoretical basis behind [[Perrin Quickquill]] and [[Layla]]'s [[Transit Magic in Aenath|Jade Spire circle project]], which never got further than a stone vanishing mid-transit.

[[Mystra]] cannot reach Aenath directly; she sits outside the astral barrier. [[The Moons of Aenath]] carry the Weave to every caster in the world instead, working through the three demigod moons rather than through her.
