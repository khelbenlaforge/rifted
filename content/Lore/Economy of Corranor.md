## Description

Corranor moves goods around a coastline that periodically leaves the ocean entirely and comes back to it. Every artery below answers to the same fact: the roads are the only constant, and the harbors, ferries, and sea lanes all run on the [[Aenath Landmass Types|landfall]] cycle instead.

### The Sky Roads

[[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Characters/NPCs/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Merrit|Merrit]], Keeper of the Sky Roads, holds the Council's portfolio over established routes, trade, cargo, and supply—but only the *eastern* lanes are on his books. Nothing west of [[Crystal City]] appears in his ledgers.

### The Alabaster Highway

Running east out of Crystal City to an unnamed point on the peninsula below [[Coldford]], the alabaster highway is the route magical-creature blood travels to reach the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]] and the [[Alchemist's Guild]]. What doesn't come through legitimate channels moves through [[The Claret]] instead.

### Sunhold, Westward, and the Iron Seam

[[Sunhold]]'s copper, silver, and iron have never had a named buyer inside the capital's own trade circuit, because none of it ever reaches the capital. At landfall, ore crosses the strait to [[Westward]], where river power and forest charcoal smelt it at Slagrow before it ever sees a Crystal City dock. From there it travels north by road, through the fork above the capital, straight to [[Stormberg]]'s [[Iron Seam Guild]], bypassing Merrit's lanes and tolls entirely.

### Coldford's Blind Manifests

Whatever half of Corranor would rather not have tracked moves through [[Coldford]] instead, on blind manifests—cargo and passage logged by count and fee only, no questions asked. No named western suppliers appear on them yet—one more gap in a trade map that has more holes than roads.

### The Merchant Guild

The one Sunhold–Crystal City link that *does* run legitimately, and inbound only. See [[Merchant Guild]] for the organization itself and its named routes; this note only tracks where Corranor's goods move as a whole.
