# The Forgotten Age

Nobody in [[Corranor]] remembers anything from before [[The Awakening]]. Not vaguely—not at all. Even [[Elmindaria L'Thorian]], one of the oldest living elves and old enough to know exactly how many years have passed since, draws a hard blank past that count. She has the number. She doesn't have anything behind it.

## The Standing Question

The [[Ether Scryer's Academy]] keeps a whole department on this alone. Its [[Director of Antiquities|Director]] runs the Antiquities division for one purpose: figure out why no one in Aenath remembers anything from more than two hundred years back. It's not a side project—it's the department's entire reason to exist, staffed, funded, and taken seriously by an institution that doesn't waste resources on idle curiosity.

Beyond the settled districts, the evidence keeps surfacing on its own. Ancient ruins, strange lights in distant mountains, worked stone carrying symbols nobody can read—all of it predates the Awakening, all of it unexplained, and none of it has ever been traced back to whoever or whatever built it first.
