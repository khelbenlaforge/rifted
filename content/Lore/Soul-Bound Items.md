# Soul-Bound Items

A growth item doesn't get left behind. Not lost, not stolen, not misplaced in a hurry—it follows its owner, every time, because it isn't really a separate object. The party worked this out for themselves: their growth items are pieces of their own souls, and a piece of a soul doesn't wander off on its own. [[Inukshuk]], [[Nanuk]]'s own spirit-guide companion, confirmed the same binding applies to him too—told Nanuk directly that he's bound to Aenath the same way Nanuk's growth item is, and cannot be left behind either.

## Independently Noticed, Twice

Nobody announced this. Two separate Guild veterans, on two separate ends of Corranor, noticed it on their own and reached the same conclusion without comparing notes. [[Rhona Voss]] clocked it in [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Stormberg/Stormberg|Stormberg]]. [[Luks Borg]] flagged the same thing from Crystal City, relaying word through the Guild's own channels. When the two compared what they'd seen, neither was surprised the other had noticed too.
