# Peoples of Aenath

Long life buys nothing here. A seven-hundred-year-old elf remembers exactly as much about pre-Awakening Corranor as someone born the week the ground stopped shaking.

## Who Lives Here

Humans, elves, dwarves, orcs, and gnomes all woke into Corranor together at the Awakening, and all five sit represented at the highest levels of the Magocracy today: [[Garm]], an orc wizard, holds the Jade Spire's own presiding seat on the [[Corranor Council]]; [[Doran]], a dwarf abjurer, commands the city's wards as Keeper of the Bulwark; a gnome Keeper sits the council too, unnamed to the party so far; [[Elmindaria L'Thorian|Elmindaria]], a high elf, has taught at the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]] and served the [[Adamantine Guild]] for centuries. Nothing in the vault establishes which of these peoples is more numerous than another, or where any of them lived before Year 0, only that all of them were there for it.

## The Shared Ceiling

Elmindaria has walked Corranor for over seven hundred years, longer than any human family tree could hope to trace, and she still can't place anything before the Awakening. She knows the number, 222 years, the same figure anyone could recite. Beyond that number, there is nothing to recall, not a childhood, not a home, not a single year of the world before it rearranged itself. Nobody in Aenath remembers further back than that, of any people; Elmindaria is simply the sharpest test of the claim, since seven hundred years should have been more than enough time for something older to surface if it existed.

## The Rifted, Separately

The five strangers now arriving from a place nobody's mapped aren't a sixth people of Aenath in the way the others are; they didn't wake here at Year 0, and they didn't lose a shared history along with everyone else. See [[The Rifted]] for what's actually known about them.
