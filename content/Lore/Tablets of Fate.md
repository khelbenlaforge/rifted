# Tablets of Fate

Someone stole the terms every god in the Realms answers to, and the gods themselves have been walking the earth in borrowed flesh ever since.

## What They Are

`AO.md` made the Tablets as the divine contracts binding each god to a portfolio and a set of responsibilities, the terms of the whole pantheon written down in one place. The party's own prequel vision describes them as stone slabs, each as long as a man's arm, inscribed with the names of every god and the duties owed, plain enough for any mortal race to read if one ever found them. At least one more exists beyond the one traced to Tantras below; the exact total the party has heard named is two. [[AO]] does not answer prayers or accept worship; the Tablets are the closest thing to his voice that exists in the world.

## The Theft

[[Bane]], Myrkul, and Bhaal stole the Tablets between them. AO held every god equally responsible for letting it happen, and stripped the entire pantheon of its power in response, casting each god's essence into a mortal avatar to walk the Realms without it. That stripping is [[Time of Troubles]]. Without the Tablets restored, the divine compact they once enforced cannot be either.

## Where the Trail Leads

One tablet has been traced to **Tantras**, in Dragon Reach, to the east. The party's own prequel journey ended before that road was walked; it remains the confirmed, unfinished thread waiting on the far side of everything since.
