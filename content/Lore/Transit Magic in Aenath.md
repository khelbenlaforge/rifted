# Transit Magic in Aenath

Long-distance teleportation does not exist in Corranor. Not "is rare"—doesn't work. [[Rhona Voss]] has told at least one party outright that it "has no business working in this world at all." The cosmological reason why sits in the Astral Barrier lore (own note pending); this note covers what's actually been attempted, and by whom.

## The Jade Spire Project

[[Perrin Quickquill]] and [[Layla]] spent months building a circle meant to link cities—a real engineering effort, not a rumor. They tested it once: a stone sent through the circle appeared on the other end for three seconds, then vanished without arriving anywhere. They never sent a person through it, and had no plans to.

## What the Reagents Showed

When [[Perrin Quickquill]] redrew a version of that circle from Layla's own working stock, it opened and pulled someone through—an accident, not a working attempt at a person. [[Elmindaria L'Thorian|Daria]], reading the reagents left behind, called the working a seventh-level spell requiring two things: a physical anchor attuned to a target plane, and the matter of a living mind. Not creature material. Human blood, human bone, and—by her read—whatever in a person actually thinks.

That's Daria's own reading of one sample. It hasn't been tested against a second, and nobody has claimed it explains how the Rifted's own crossing worked.

## Short Hops Still Work

The wholesale ban is on distance, not on magic touching the Ethereal at all. [[Veyris Thantorn]]'s explanation: spells like Misty Step only skim the Ethereal Plane, and the Ethereal—along with the Shadowfell and the four elemental planes—sits inside the same barrier Corranor itself is behind. A short hop never crosses that barrier; a long-distance teleport would have to, which is exactly the working nothing in Aenath has ever managed. Full detail on the barrier itself: the Astral Barrier lore (own note pending).

## What Actually Brought the Rifted Here

Unsolved. Whatever happened to the Rifted did not use any spell anyone in Corranor recognizes, and nobody—not [[Layla]], not the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]], not the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Diamond District/Temple of Kelemvor|Church of Kelemvor]]—has produced a working theory that accounts for it. Treat every explanation below as exactly that: an explanation someone in-world believes, not a confirmed answer.

- **The Academy's line:** newcomers land with only half a soul, which regrows over time—a real, measurable effect, but a description, not a mechanism.
- **Veyris Thantorn's theory:** tieflings and aasimar carry planar echoes in their blood, and the crossing may have run along a current already present in at least one of the Rifted rather than punching through fresh.
