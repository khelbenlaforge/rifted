# Crossing Visions

Not every vision a Rifted experiences happens at the moment of arrival. Some come later, once they've already been living in [[Corranor]] a while—and when they do, they don't read like the arrival itself. They read like memory.

## The Three Dreams

One night in [[Stormberg]], after the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Lore/Feast of the Moon|Feast of the Moon]] had already come and gone, sleep took [[Kili]], [[Roberto]], and [[Keith Poe]] the same way and let go of none of them cleanly. Each dreamed something different, and each dream converged on the same place by the end.

Kili found herself somewhere with no ground at all—a fey court mid-collapse, two colossal shapes clashing hard enough to knock her flat, elves stepping forward one at a time to feed their own light into a golden-haired figure's rising bow. Roberto stood in a temple full of clerics losing their god mid-prayer, watched that god climb a celestial staircase toward home, only for an unnamed voice to shatter the stairs from above and take his god down with it—*"I will take your children,"* the only thing the voice needed to say, and Roberto's own soul lifted out of him before the dream let him go. Keith woke inside someone else's burned body, on crutches, marking maps of where the old powers had scattered while the world above them fell into open war.

The dream drew all three of them together for its close, at a second stairway none had seen before. A fallen god argued his own case to return; a figure carrying two shields blocked him outright—*"I will not let you return. My business is to protect every single staircase."* Word reached them, distantly, that a new goddess of magic had been reborn, and that it changed nothing: souls were still being siphoned to other worlds even as they watched. Keith caught himself mid-prayer to her and felt his own soul pulled loose the same way Roberto's already had. Kili's dream simply went black instead.

None of the three know whether what they saw was memory, prophecy, or something else with no name yet. They woke with the images intact and nothing else.
