# History of Corranor

Two hundred and twenty-two years of recorded history, and not one page of it before that. Everyone who lives in [[Corranor]] shares the same starting line.

## Year 0—The Awakening

The first peoples of Corranor opened their eyes amid violent land formation: earth heaving, magma erupting, rock twisting into shapes no mason had cut. Whatever homes and families they'd had were gone from memory entirely. Only their own names came through intact. The archmage [[Corran Blackstaff|Corran]] sacrificed himself to stabilize the chaos, and where he stood the terrain calmed into [[Corran's Spike]]. Everything since has been built in the shadow of that one act.

## The Long Rebuild

Civilization did not come back all at once. The **Sapphire families** were the first to claw any real power together, trading, organizing, holding the frightened and the leaderless into something that could function day to day. But it was the wizards among the survivors who went on to form the ruling body of the [[Magocracy of Corranor]], and it's technically their council, not the Sapphire houses, that led the rebuilding.

Roughly two centuries ago, a wilderness druid circle, calling themselves the Lavaliths at the time and known today as the [[Ancients of Lava]], spent months mass-casting Shape Earth to raise the [[Stormwall]]'s earthen core along the northern coast. The Magocracy came later, sheathing that raw earth in Stormberg obsidian and building out its wards and defenses over the years since, and it publicly takes the credit for the Wall. Both hands built it. Only one of them still gets thanked for it.

## The Present (222 PA)

Corranor counts its years from the Awakening: **PA**, Post-Awakening, puts it at 222 years old this year. The isolation is only recently over, the Magocracy's two crystal-powered airships have opened routes to distant continents nobody in living memory had reached before. Behind that progress, older troubles persist. [[Shadow Creatures]] test the Wall and hunt the newly arrived. Frontier settlements like [[Sunhold]] still need protecting more than they can protect themselves. And five strangers who call themselves Earthers have started arriving from somewhere no scryer has ever mapped, a mystery the Magocracy has only begun to take seriously.
