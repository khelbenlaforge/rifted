Aenath's sky carries four moons, not one. [[Selûne]]'s own moon hangs there every night, the way it does over any world she watches—pale, familiar, the one the party would recognize from Earth. The other three are a different matter entirely.

They show themselves only once a year: on the [[Feast of the Moon]], alongside Selûne's own full moon, all four hanging in the sky together. The rest of the year the sky reads as one ordinary moon, and nothing in it suggests the other three exist at all.

## The Three Moons

The three are named [[Solinari]], [[Lunitari]] and [[Nuitari]], and on the Feast night each hangs in the sky beside Selûne's own.

## Session 28 Update (The Door Kickers)

[[Mystra]] is shut outside Aenath's astral barrier and cannot reach the world directly. Three Dragonlance-derived demigods—Solinari, Lunitari, and Nuitari—knelt to her and gave themselves up willingly to become moons in her stead, anchoring the Weave so that spellcasters in Aenath can still draw on it. Mystra knelt back to them, weeping, before she let it happen. The three carry the Weave to every caster in the world, working through them rather than through her, since she cannot work through the barrier herself.

[[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Characters/NPCs/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Citrine District/Elmindaria L'Thorian|Elmindaria L'Thorian]]'s Session 26 account of this mechanism was in-fiction testimony until [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Min Ji-ah|Min Ji-ah]]'s [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Characters/NPCs/Forgotten Realms/Shadowdale/Elminster|Elminster]]'s Inquiry independently confirmed it and named the three moons directly, during the party's Session 28 Guild debrief.

Each demigod has its own short entry: [[Solinari]], [[Lunitari]], [[Nuitari]].
