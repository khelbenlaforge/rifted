# Session 05—Word from Crystal

## Three Places at Once

The three began the day apart. [[Ruairí]] was down at the docks, working the count. [[Odette Langford]] had stopped at the [[Temple of Selûne]] and then gone on to [[Hester Crane]]'s bakery. Kai was at the other harbour, learning the bell codes.

Then Ruairí saw the man with two garnet studs in his ear, the same well-dressed man in the good hat who had stopped at [[Marl Dunning]]'s stall.

## Over the Rooftops

Ruairí did not wait for the others. He went straight after the man and tailed him alone across the rooftops, and the fog of Coldford helped him stay hidden.

On roof after roof he found lookouts belonging to the man's organisation. Ruairí took them for the Claret's people.

## Lunch at the Guild

Kai and Odette set out to reach Ruairí, but bad luck steered them back to the Adamantine Guild instead, and they ended up having lunch with [[Petra Hale]]. They kept her up to date on what they were doing. Ruairí, who had gone straight to tailing, had told no one. Only over lunch did they realise something might have happened, because Ruairí was usually on time.

The walk to the place where Ruairí worked cost them more time. From there they picked up his trail.

## The Channel

The three worked out a distraction between them, and in the end it worked. It kept the lookouts' attention elsewhere and let Ruairí stay hidden. He changed into a smaller humanoid, a gnome, small enough to squeeze into a sewer channel under the building where the man had gone.

From the channel he overheard two men talking. They spoke of word from Corvin in Crystal City and of tiefling and aasimar blood, and of how they should start procuring it. They planned to begin the next day, looking for isolated tieflings and aasimar in Coldford.

## A Rat for a Messenger

Ruairí was not alone down there. With Speak with Animals and a careful hand, he won over a rat. It agreed to bring him news if the building saw a lot of activity, such as a crowd of people leaving.

## Bread and a Husband

Up on the street, Kai and Odette were nearly made. Odette handed out bread from her bakery as though that were why she was there, and she called Kai her husband. Kai half-lied that he was looking for a black cat. Persuasion and a little trickery got the two of them clear, and they headed back to the Guild.

## Home by Separate Roads

Ruairí had what he needed. He left the sewers well away from the building and led his new pet rat to the Guild, so that it would know where to find him. The session closed that same day with the three in different parts of the city, all heading for the Guild.

## Still Unfinished

Ruairí had yet to tell the others what he had overheard. The hunt for isolated tieflings and aasimar was due to start the next day.
