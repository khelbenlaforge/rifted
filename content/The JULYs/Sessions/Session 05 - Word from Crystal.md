# Session 05—Word from Crystal

## Three Places at Once

The three began the day apart. [[Ruairí]] was down at the docks, working the count. [[Kai]] was at the other harbour, learning the bell codes. [[Odette Langford]] had stopped at the [[Temple of Selûne]] and then gone on to [[Hester Crane]]'s bakery. After the lunch rush, Hester noticed her staring out the window as though she were working out directions.

Odette flipped a coin to choose between finding Kai at the harbour and finding Ruairí at the [[Tallyrows]]. She went for Ruairí, since Kai was busy with his lessons. She carried the party's sending stone, which [[Petra Hale]] had already given them. At the Tallyrows she saw [[Hobb Abbe|Hobb]] up on his platform ringing bells, but no Ruairí. She walked back to the [[The Tallhouse|Tallhouse]], thinking he must have gone off to lunch by himself.

## The Tail

Ruairí had spotted the man with two garnet studs in his ear, the same well-dressed man in the good hat who had stopped at [[Marl Dunning]]'s stall. He did not wait for the others. He went after the man alone and tried to hex him. The man felt the spell being cast and turned around in confusion. Ruairí managed to sneak out of sight, helped by the fog of Coldford. The man shrugged and walked into an alley, and Ruairí followed him through the alleys.

## Over the Rooftops

The alleys led toward a district of abandoned and half-converted warehouses. The party had an unspoken rule to stay out of Claret territory, because of the possible danger. The man started up a set of stairs into a tall building. To keep him from vanishing into the fog, Ruairí used Misty Step to reach a nearby rooftop.

He tried to follow across the roofs, missed a jump and fell. The fall cost him four points of bludgeoning damage, but he stayed unseen. When he looked the man over as he climbed, an Insight check told him something: the man was only trying to look relaxed. He was alert and watching everything around him.

Ruairí kept after him, and miraculously nobody noticed. The man took a running start and jumped to another roof, and Ruairí jumped after him. The man stretched, turned his head, gave a thumbs-up and picked up a stick. Then he set off across the rooftops.

There were lookouts on roof after roof. Ruairí noticed three others on nearby buildings, all heading the same way, as though the whole thing were coordinated. They belonged to the man's organisation, and he took them for the [[The Claret|Claret]]'s people. He dropped into the street, landing clumsily in the cover of the fog. He had already worked out where they were going. From the ground he followed their shadows and the sound of the man landing on the roof where an archer stood. Behind that building he went up the back alley and suspected still more lookouts, though he could not see where they were.

The hat man and the archer came together at the edge of the next building and talked, but their voices were too muffled for him to make out. Ruairí went down for a closer look at the building they seemed to be closing on.

## Lunch at the Guild

Odette ended up having lunch with Kai and [[Petra Hale]] at the [[Adamantine Guild]], and the two of them kept Petra up to date on what they were doing. Ruairí, who had gone straight to tailing, had told no one. Over lunch they began to suspect something had happened, because Ruairí was usually on time.

They walked to the place where he worked, which cost them more time, and from there picked up his trail.

## The Channel

Ruairí changed into a gnome, small enough to slip under the floorboards and into a sewer channel beneath the building where the man had gone. Two men were talking above him, and their voices came down through the planks. They spoke of word from Corvin in Crystal City, and of tiefling and aasimar blood and how they should start procuring it. It was said that if Corvin had his head screwed on straight they could come into a lot of money. They said someone wanted the goods very badly, and that nobody here had ever asked for tiefling blood. They planned to begin the next day, looking for tieflings and aasimar in Coldford who were isolated, with no friends or family. The voices also turned to a couple who had wandered into the area looking for their cat, and wondered why they had not heard that it was off limits.

## A Rat for a Messenger

Ruairí was not alone down there. With Speak with Animals and a careful hand, he questioned the sewer rats about the men above. The two-legs gave them lots of food, they said, better food than anywhere else in the city, and sometimes the rats squealed. Asked what the men looked like, the rats said one of them was always dressed darkly and smelled nice.

He had cheese rations on him and used them to strike a deal. The rats would update him whenever the two-legged creatures became more active, such as a crowd leaving the building. One rat, the one he made the bargain with, came along with him so that it would know how to find him at the Tallhouse.

## A Couple Looking for Their Cat

The distraction was made up on the spot, and it kept the lookouts' attention elsewhere for a while. Kai and Odette stepped away from the building for a little while, a couple looking for their cat. Odette handed bread out as though that were her errand and called Kai her husband, and Kai half-lied that he was looking for a black cat. A little persuasion and trickery got the two clear for the moment.

Odette cast Detect Magic and found abjuration and divination on the building's windows. She also saw divination coming up from the sewers.

## The Patron Is Pleased

All the sneaking and spying had stirred up Ruairí's mischievous side, and he reached level 4. His patron's voice came to him, delighted: finally, some whimsy, some mischief. It spoke of him being fae and embracing his nature, and he felt his power grow. He gained the Arcane Artist feat, the Infestation cantrip and the Invisibility spell.

## Bread and a Scream

Kai and Odette crept round to the side where the sewer opening was. Odette wanted Ruairí to know they were close, and she tossed a piece of bread into the opening to tell him. She missed. The bread landed outside, and the rats smelled it and swarmed out. Odette screamed, and every head in the area turned. People started closing in on her and Kai.

Ruairí had little room in the tunnel, and no way to come out quietly. He heard the scream, recognised it and sighed. He pointed toward the sound and cast Message to tell Odette he would take another way out and meet them back at the Tallhouse. He crawled on, took his rat along to train it, went around a corner and deeper in, and found an opening leading down.

Outside, Kai turned, grabbed Odette, picked her up and told her to run. The two got away and regrouped at the Tallhouse.

## Home by Separate Roads

Ruairí left the sewers well away from the building and led his new pet rat to the Guild, so that it would know where to find him. The session closed that same day with Kai and Odette already at the Tallhouse and Ruairí still on his way.

## Still Unfinished

Ruairí had yet to tell the others what he had overheard. The hunt for isolated tieflings and aasimar was due to start the next day.
