# Campaign Timeline

## The Awakening (Year 0 PA)

Two hundred and twenty-two years before the present, the people of [[Corranor]] awoke amid violent land formation with no memories but their names. The archmage [[Corran]] sacrificed himself to contain a catastrophic force, creating [[Corran's Spike]] and stabilizing the chaotic terrain. Civilization began its slow rebuild from nothing. See [[The Awakening]] for details.
