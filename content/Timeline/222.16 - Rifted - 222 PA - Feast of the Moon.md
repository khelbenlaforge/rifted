# Rifted — 222 PA: Feast of the Moon

## Year 222 PA — Feast of the Moon (Session 26 — The Weight of the Moon)

*(Processed from a player's notes, not yet a DM-verified account — a reprocess is expected. Treat specifics as strong testimony, not settled fact.)*

### Three of Four Moons

The night after Corvin Adder's deal closed, [[Terra|Terra]] and [[Gill Bates|Gill]] each fell into a vision. Terra's, per the DM's direct account rather than the original notes: a choice to merge with something far larger than herself, a body left behind, a being made stronger for the sacrifice. Hearing it, [[Nanuk|Nanuk]] recognized the same shape — a vision of his own from days before (Session 24), only now put into words. Gill's put him somewhere else entirely, a mote of light adrift in vast space, watching a humanoid figure work an anvil with a hammer. [[Inukshuk]] told Nanuk the Great Spirit itself was hurt and needed him. Four spirits of the cardinal directions, converging to build a new world. Gill's spear carried a different voice: [[Adon]], warning that [[Istishia]] and the unnamed primordials beside her are weakening, with an apology reaching past Gill to someone named Midnight.

[[Istar Silverlock|Istar]] brought the party outside to a city gone fully dark — no moon, only three glowing orbs where it should have been. [[Elmindaria L'Thorian|Daria]]'s account, unconfirmed: Aenath has four moons total, and its spellcasters draw on the Weave by proxy through three of them, visible together only on the Feast of the Moon. [[Selûne]]'s own moon, the one that looks like Earth's, takes no part in the casting — the Weave runs through the other three alone.

### What the Dance Remembered

[[Sadiah Ispahani|Sadiah]] reached into the moon-threads with Mage Hand and saw a dark figure reading from a book, shadow creatures pouring out of its own cast shadow to search the world. [[Terra]] danced Sehanine's rite without ever having learned it — silver-winged, moving beside elves, dwarves, fae, and humans who somehow knew the same steps, [[Hollowstone]] (worn as her pendant) growing hot enough to melt its own cord twice, falling, and returning both times. The dance carried her through to noon the next day.

### The Grove and the Temple

Come morning, [[Sylvara Oakwhisper|Sylvara]] told Nanuk at [[Mielikki's Grove|the Grove of Mielikki]] what no one had ever named for him before: he is a child of [[Silvanus]], the Oak Father — locked in an opposition with [[Malar]] that neither can end without collapsing something larger, with the [[Belak|Ancients of Lava]] as its extremists. She gestured toward Stormberg, vague and unhurried — a direction to start in, not a destination she'd name.

Gill and Min Ji-ah walked to the [[Temple of Kelemvor]]. Seven of its own paladins burst out screaming that they'd lost the Gravelord. Initiative rolled with the party split three ways — Terra still at Sehanine's shrine, Nanuk on the road back from the Grove, Gill and Min Ji-ah at the Temple steps.

The archway door at the back hummed with the Guild's own vault-feeling. Sadiah stayed at her window. Terra was already gone. JJ, Shen, Regius, Gil, Nanuk, and Min Ji-ah crossed through together, and every line back to the street went dead the moment it closed behind them.

## Year 222 PA — Feast of the Moon (The Understudies — Session 04 — Half a Soul)

### Guild-Marked

The same midnight the Door Kickers watched three moons rise over Crystal City, five Understudies — [[Kili]], [[Keith Poe]], [[Roberto]], [[Samson Flint]] (recovered and walking again), and [[Esca]] (rejoining the group with five brass [[Adamantine Guild]] bracers, sender unknown) — watched the same three moons and the same southern dance of gold motes from the road into [[Stormberg]]. They reached the outer gate at midnight, mid-lockdown for the holiday; the bracers waved them through to the Wallwatch.

### What Rhona Knew

**[[Rhona Voss]]**, the Wallwatch's Branch Leader, inducted all five under standard Guild terms: a cot and coin against future work. Then came the wider picture — [[Corranor]], the Blackstaff founder-family, the [[Corranor Council]], and the fact that the Understudies aren't the only Rifted group. An earlier group, in [[Crystal City]], is conducting its own investigation. From that group, by way of the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]], came the finding now reaching Stormberg as common knowledge: the Rifted carry only half a soul each, growing toward whole the longer they remain in Aenath.

### Settling In

Rhona walked the five to the Guild's Stormberg pocket-dimension dormitory — five rooms, a common room, the same shape as the Crystal City branch's own. Five of the seven Understudies are now together in one place, housed and safe. [[!D'bleh|Gaslight]] and [[Fern]], last confirmed separately en route to Stormberg after Session 02, did not arrive and went unmentioned all session — their status is now unaccounted for, not merely delayed.

## Year 222 PA — Feast of the Moon (Session 27 — Rising of the Dark)

### Six Ways In

The scream that ended Session 26 kept going the instant it started again: seven Knights spilling from the Temple of Kelemvor with no Gravelord and no idea where he'd gone. Gill and Min Ji-ah were already at the steps when it broke; Min Ji-ah's Sleep and Terra's blade, Terra arriving just in time, put down the two disguised cultists guarding the door. Inside, [[Augustus Kennedy|Augustus]] shook the truth out of the loudest fleeing paladin, [[Ser Tamsin Rook]] — no body seen, only an empty, sealed chamber. [[Hollis Dawnwarden|Hollis]] rode in from the Hall of Justice on an undead Shadowfell steed he calls Holland; [[Ember]] and [[Nanuk|Nanuk]] felt the wrongness pull them back from Mielikki's Grove; [[Terra]], mid-dance at [[Sehanine Moonbow|Sehanine]]'s shrine, sent the night's other dancers fanning out across the city to warn it, and went to the temple herself.

### What Rook Gave Up

More cultists surfaced in the crowd — one shot a monk mid-chant before the Knights closed ranks around the vigil — and a captured cultist under Hollis's Zone of Truth offered nothing but a taunt about a decoy. Augustus ran the real trail down alone, his Brand's panther-energy pinning three figures at the end of a bent alley; Ember and Vesper pulled two more hidden cultists out of the crowd by their fake panic — Vesper dragging the halfling toward Nanuk, Ember taking the elf herself. It was Terra who broke Rook open: the Sharites had found his dead sister's soul, still stranded on the material plane by what he called Kelemvor's failure, and offered to set it right if he looked away twice. He named Cistern Street and the boulevard, and called the temple fight a distraction. A decoy Gravelord went down to Augustus's fists soon after, confirming it.

### The Shrine of Shar

By night the party followed the boulevard to a hidden, unremarkable shrine off Cistern Street — one patch of wall painted flat black, total darkness inside that Ember dispelled to find [[Mother Ilyra Duskveil]] chanting over the bound Gravelord, four cultists between her and the door. Augustus opened with Guiding Bolt; Gill tore the Gravelord clear of the altar with his own spear; Min Ji-ah's Tasha's Mind Whip ended Ilyra before the rite could finish. Then something with bat wings rose out of nowhere, folded her body into itself, smiled, and said there'd always be another day for darkness before it vanished — not a devil, not a demon, closer by every account to a fallen angel. How it crossed the barrier at all, nobody in the shrine could say. The Gravelord survived; the vigil held.

## Year 222 PA — Feast of the Moon (The AUGs — Session 01 — Five of Six)

### Five New Arrivals

Five more were pulled through the same night, an ocean and more apart on Earth a moment before: [[Mike Kanbei|Mike Kanbei]] mid-call with his mother in Shimokitazawa, [[Ruan Myrt Zaltana|Ruan Myrt Zaltana]] walking toward a burning doorway in a Glendale dream, [[Rachel Tan|Rachel Tan]] caught reaching for her father's wallet in Singapore, [[Gregory Yang|Gregory Yang]] mid-honest-answer to his girlfriend in another Singapore flat, and [[Rismawati Bayu|Rismawati Bayu]] with one hand on her own front door on an ordinary Monday. All five shared a vision of the Awakening cataclysm en route, independently corroborating the event (see [[The Awakening]]), before resolving onto [[The Drowned Dawn]] — a previously undiscovered, abandoned temple to [[Lathander]], buried underground rather than adrift like [[The Sundered Choir]].

[[Layla]] watched from the tower above, miscounted them against an expected sixth, and left them with nothing but a place to climb toward and a reason to run if the dark moved. Shadow Creatures rose to meet the five of them; the party fought clear and lit several of the temple's dead crystal sconces in the process, restoring enough light that the last of the creatures froze rather than fled. The session closed on the party climbing the temple's tower toward the promised transport, narrated as a montage rather than played out — whether they reached it is unconfirmed.

## Year 222 PA—Feast of the Moon into 1 Nightal (Session 28—A Name I Cannot Read)

### Seven on the Steps

Half an hour short of midnight, with the Cistern Street fight still on their clothes, the party stood below the [[Jade Spire]] and watched the [[Corranor Council]] come out to face the city as a body of seven for the first time anyone could remember. [[Garm]] wore the purple robes of his office instead of his usual brown, said he had been asked to speak first and thought that a mistake, and told the city the First Gravelord was alive and would close the Feast himself.

**[[Doran]]**, Keeper of the Bulwark, an old dwarf in robes with no armour on him anywhere, gave the city an account of the night that was true in every particular except the one that mattered. There had been a summoning, he said; it was bound at the site and dispersed there, and nothing entered Crystal City from outside it. He looked at the party while he said it. They had all watched the thing leave under its own power.

**[[Oriel Blackstaff]]**, Keeper of the Writ, declared the shrine unlawful assembly, took its property for the city, and then opened an inquiry into the [[Temple of Kelemvor]] and the [[Knights of the Eternal Order]]'s admissions during a vigil. A magocrat has no standing in religious law. The square heard him take it anyway. [[Ser Tamsin Rook]] was remanded to his own Order, its findings owed to the Council rather than the public, and the matter declared closed to discussion.

### A Name He Could Not Read

**[[Cadmus Tallow]]**, thirty-one years First Gravelord, stood above the Council on his own threshold with his voice in ruins and reopened what the Writ had shut. Eleven hundred and forty names had come to the temple since sundown, and all eleven hundred and forty were spoken before dawn took the list—spoken, he was careful to say, while he was not standing in it. The church is the ledger, and a ledger must be precise.

Then the name he could not read. **[[Alys Rook]]** died in this city four years ago, was never met and never counted, and is still here. Her brother had known for four years, and opened a door for people who promised to fix it. *I am expected to say that he betrayed us. I have read the book. The book says we failed her first.* He took the failure onto the office and collapsed into [[Gill Bates|Gill]]'s arms.

The monks burned the names off their papers into a line of script across the sky. [[Nanuk]] and Gill watched the souls come up to meet them, half-blind until the chant turned their heads: gold closing around each one as it found its own name, blue and red and black light taken without distinction. [[Terra]] reached for the same sight and got nothing. Down in the crowd, some never rose at all.

### The Threads Laid Bare

At the [[Adamantine Guild]] afterward the party laid every open thread on one table, and the shape underneath them finally surfaced. Aenath has no teleportation magic at all; the astral barrier encloses the Ethereal, the Shadowfell and every elemental plane, and shuts out only what lies past them, which is where the gods are meant to live. Kelemvor's church cannot police souls out because there is nowhere to send them, so it recites instead and hopes.

The four spirits [[Inukshuk]] has spoken of for months were named at last: [[Istishia]], [[Grumbar]], [[Kossuth]] and [[Akadi]], primordials of water, earth, fire and air, all four weakening, all four sealed inside with everyone else. [[Silvanus]] is not among them but stands in the same condition, here and in pain and still able to speak. [[Mystra]] is not: she is beyond the barrier, and magic reaches Aenath only because three demigods, [[Solinari]], [[Lunitari]] and [[Nuitari]], knelt to her and then gave themselves up to become moons. She knelt back, weeping, before she let them.

Older than all of it, [[Selûne]] and Shar were a single light, and the first Mystra came out of their splitting. And on the ledger side of the same cosmology: gods take their power from worship, the Lower Planes take theirs from souls in circulation, and that soul economy is what makes a name like [[Asmodeus]]'s worth writing beside [[Layla]]'s.

[[Sadiah Ispahani|Sadiah]], who has had no vision, found the piece anyway in her own breathing—she came loose from her body on the way here and felt relief, and speaks Common without having learned it. It is the party's own reading, not anything confirmed: if Layla is carrying souls *into* this world, and those souls began here, then the party were never stolen from Earth so much as returned from it. Nobody in the room could prove a word of it.

### The River, and the Florist's

[[Regius]]'s absence resolved into two things. The first was a dream on the night of the Claret meeting: a black river, memory dissolving the longer he floated in it, four people beside him he knew mattered and could not name. Something winged drove off the scavengers and was handed a scroll by the only lucid one among them. *As we agreed. Three souls for mine.* The rest of them folded down into coins. She picked up three and looked at him last.

The second was work. Across two nights tailing [[Sellas Verenthal]] for [[Ostrin Feld]], he watched her feign drunkenness outside a shuttered florist's that was still taking deliveries, and then, on the second night, kill a Claret man inside it with a spell scroll and walk home unhurried. He was seen. He told Ostrin, badly, and was paid fifty-two gold for it.

### North, and the Brotherhood

The party chose Silvanus over the Blackstaffs: a god who is here and can answer beats a family two days' travel away. The grove is not a physical place, no one has ever been welcomed into it, and the [[Belak|Ancients of Lava]] have been hunting it for years without success. [[Nanuk]] can feel the pull. It points north, past [[Stormberg]] and its two-hundred-foot sea wall, toward an Arctic that runs as a line from one edge of the world to the other.

On the first day of Nightal, [[Tidecaller Marin Deepcurrent|Marin]] told [[Gill Bates|Gill]] what the four elemental clergies have sworn never to admit: that they can hear their gods, that the voices have been fading for thirty years, and that their public rivalries are staged to hide a single shared purpose. Then he named the making of the world. The four elemental deities let [[Gond]] hammer their own divine bodies into its fabric, and a fifth gave himself the same way to bring life. Marin does not know who the fifth was. Gill did not need to be told.

## Year 222 PA—Feast of the Moon into 1 Nightal (The Understudies—Session 05—What Didn't Burn)

### The Blessing

In [[Stormberg]], the same midnight synchronized to a different square. [[Kili]] approached the young priest first, but it was [[Esca]] who tore his own paper in half. He named no one from his old life. **[[Rhona Voss]]** caught his collar, her hand closing over it more to steady him than to hold him. Kili skipped mourning entirely; she turned and charmed the priest. [[Keith Poe|Keith]] wrote a name and told no one it was his own. [[Roberto]] gave one name, not himself. Two papers among the whole square's worth never caught. They stayed in the monks' hands, untouched, long after everything else had gone up. Roberto alone watched the claiming happen. Translucent figures rose out of the crowd, blind and drifting, each one finding its name and going up in gold or flame or a color he had no word for. A handful of them stayed standing after the last name rose, unclaimed, gone only when the chant itself stopped.

### A City in Winter

The turn into Nightal split the four of them across Stormberg. [[Esca]] toured the [[Ward Works]] under **[[Ostrun Kell]]**'s supervision and came away with a library full of abjuration theory and a wall under constant, active maintenance against the storm. Kili worked a morning shift at **[[Yenna Marrow|Yenna]]**'s noodle stall and used it to stir the city's own quiet resentment toward the noble houses funding it. Roberto split his day between Stormberg's chapel of Lathander, where **[[Sister Priya Dawnwell]]** confirmed he'd been called by [[Helm]] and [[Sune]] alongside Lathander, and the Temple of Kelemvor, where **[[Brother Silas]]** laid out the old doctrine of the Wailing Wall and the newer promise that every soul, worshipper or not, is sent somewhere it belongs. Keith stayed at breakfast with Rhona and learned Stormberg's economy runs on three legs: the Academy, House Verenthal's mills, and Blackstaff money. Artificers of the **[[Iron Seam Guild]]** run a fourth leg alongside them, keeping the wall's non-magical machinery alive.

### Word from Crystal

As the day closed, Rhona relayed what little the Guild in Crystal City could tell her, secondhand and imperfectly recalled: Rifted attacked by the Cult of [[Shar]], and the First Gravelord himself apparently nearly lost on an altar to her during the same rite the party had just watched from Stormberg's own square. She also named [[Garm]] of the Council of Seven, Layla's employer. That was the word Rhona used, and the only one. His research is what Layla's been chasing: a teleportation that by every account Corranor has should not exist at all. The more concrete lead was reagents cut from planar blood, and a Crystal City black market trading in them. Then she led them through a shimmer into a training ground, drew a greatsword one-handed off her back, and called for initiative. A test cut short before the first blow landed, held open for Session 6.
