# Rifted — 000 PA: The Awakening

## Year 0 PA — The Awakening

Two hundred and twenty-two years before the present, the people of [[Corranor]] awoke amid violent land formation with no memories but their names. The archmage [[Corran]] sacrificed himself to contain a catastrophic force, creating [[Corran's Spike]] and stabilizing the chaotic terrain. Civilization began its slow rebuild from nothing.
