# Rifted — 222 PA: Uktar

## Year 222 PA — 19th Marpenoth to 3rd Uktar (Session 19 — Seven Doors)

### Return

The party came back to [[Crystal City]] two weeks after the [[Sunless Citadel]], with [[Balsag]] and [[Gork]] gone south into the underground and a crate of marsh snappers in the wagon. The [[Adamantine Guild]]'s common room was as they'd left it, except for two things: a floating disc producing beverages on demand, and two new doors at the far end of the curve — [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Shen]] and [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk]] had rooms now, quietly, without ceremony.

[[Istar Silverlock]] was somewhere in the building cleaning things. [[Elmindaria L'Thorian|Daria]], on learning that [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Shen]]'s patron had been present during the Sunless Citadel's events, asked [[Erky]] to attempt Banishment. The spell produced a brief blue light, then did not. The patron was not removable. She said it was not supposed to exist in this universe, and then stopped saying things about it.

### The Council's Report

The [[Corranor Council]] had found what it could. [[Layla]] — [[Garm]]'s own apprentice — had left her magical signature on the transit event; she had cast something powerful enough to move six people between worlds, and Garm was working to understand how to undo it. Wards now protected the [[Jade Spire]] from the [[Shadow Creatures]]' attacks; the attackers had not withdrawn, but the wards had closed the avenue. A separate Corranor contingent was returning with news of found land. The party was promised a bastion in the city.

On the matter of their souls: the missing half was growing back. They had noticed — probably — that they were thinking in Common more than English. Two weeks, the Council added, and the [[Gulthias Tree|Gulthias]] island would resurface. [[Belak]]'s own notes had suggested a week for a potential vampiric resurrection if the conditions were right.

### A Month in the City

Six people, six threads, one month.

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk]] found the [[Azure Tamers]] in the western wall towers — a ranger collective responsible for transportation and communications, with griffins and eagles that were comfortable with him before introductions were finished. A senior member named [[Torem]] ran the network and knew something useful: [[Layla]] had been close, very close, with her co-apprentice [[Perrin Quickquill]]. Nanuk joined the Tamers and brought [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Jonathan Jordan]] with him when he went to find Perrin. [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Ember]] came too, via Torem's eagle, to a wilderness clearing where the [[Ancients of Lava]] lived. Elder [[Jess]] and the circle spoke freely until they didn't — Belak's name closed the conversation. *He was one of our best.* [[Sylvara Oakwhisper]] at the Temple of [[Mielikki]] filled the rest in: forty years since Belak had left the Lavaliths — the old name for the Ancients of Lava.

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Jonathan Jordan]] followed threads to the [[The Low Flagon|Low Flagon]] and [[Tavia Orn]] with a Claret ring. Thirty-five gold got him Layla's location (the [[Garnet District]], buying illegal spell components, a valued [[The Claret|Claret]] client) and a meeting with someone above Tavia's station — who would come to him when ready. He took bar shifts at the [[Griffin's Hat]] and, from [[Istar Silverlock]] still scrubbing guild stone, received his next task: steal the mithral scales from the [[Hall of Justice - Temple of Tyr|Temple of Tyr]]'s great statue, forty feet up.

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Lea]] went to the Alchemist Guild and a tattooed elf named [[Veyris Thantorn]], who explained the Weave as threads running in three dimensions and offered a theory: Layla was tiefling, and tieflings carry planar echoes, and the shifting spell may have run along a current she was born with. Planar Mechanics and Alchemy track now open. The [[Hucrele Manor]] came after — [[Sorelle]] at the door, [[Soral Vayne]] at the table — and Soral spoke about the city's first family. The Blackstaffs. A scion on the Council of Seven. A family of wizards, careful and durable.

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Gill Bates]] sat with death for a month: [[Tidecaller Marin Deepcurrent]]'s water philosophy by day, the Temple of [[Kelemvor Lyonsbane|Kelemvor]] by evening. Three factions inside — Monks of the Long Death, Priesthood, Knights (who guard cross-planar exits) — and a question he couldn't stop turning over: the god had been a man first, the man had felt things, and somewhere in between, the feeling had gone. The Church of Kelemvor held a feud with [[Mystra]]'s and [[Oghma]]'s temples. In the [[Diamond District]], a shrine to [[Cyric]] stood alone, tended by city workers who would not say they attended it.

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Augustus Kennedy]] went to see [[Sir Gareth Ironhand]] and left belonging somewhere he hadn't expected. Gareth detected the Lyonsbane panther energies in the Brand — not Tyr's, clearly not Tyr's — and brought him to the [[Temple of Kelemvor]]. The clerics and paladins didn't need long. Augustus left the Church of Tyr with the grace of someone who has genuinely loved a thing and found it pointed elsewhere, and then — because he was still himself — went back to the [[Hall of Justice - Temple of Tyr|Hall of Justice]] and signed up with Tyr's law enforcement arm as a junior officer. The city now has him filed in two temples. It appears unconcerned.

## Year 222 PA — 3rd-12th Uktar (Session 20 — Strange Peace)

### The God Who Was a Man

[[Sir Gareth Ironhand]] had been waiting for [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Augustus Kennedy|Augie]] to come back and understand it. He walked him to the [[Temple of Kelemvor]]. [[The Gravelords]] assessed the signature in [[Kelemvor's Brand|the Brand]], said *adherent*, gave him library access, and left him with one of their quieter number. The Brand settled. The panther energy — which had always been a hum — went home.

The library holds accounts of Gravelord clerics who have plane-shifted into the Nine Hells to police soul trafficking. Most of it is beyond Augie's current vocabulary. He read it anyway. He also met the [[Monks of the Long Death]], who occupy the eastern ambulatory with the specific stillness of people who have looked at death enough times that the dread stopped preceding the observation. They are against resurrection, with the considered grief of people watching a structural error be repeated rather than fury. To them death isn't a thing to fear or reverse; it's a thing to understand.

Augie left an adherent, with an extra Turn Undead once a day as if the god had answered while the paperwork was still being processed. Gareth gave him eighteen gold and opened the city's enforcement network to him. The [[Knights of the Eternal Order]] were already expecting him — a paladin with that Brand does not need to argue his affiliation.

### The Jade Spire, Two Weeks On

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Jonathan Jordan|JJ]] and [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk]] arrived at a tower with fresh rune-work and barely-dry paint. [[Perrin Quickquill]] explained: while the party was in [[Sunhold]], the [[Shadow Creatures]] had found the Spire. The assault halved the city guard. The mages had rebuilt the wards. [[Garm]] believed the creatures were extraplanar — not from any recognised plane. The problem had been contained. The problem had not been solved.

Perrin also explained the teleportation project: he and [[Layla]] had spent months building a circle to link cities, sent a test stone through, watched it appear for three seconds and vanish without arriving anywhere. They had not sent people through it. They would not. And on the day the party arrived — High Harvestide — Layla had been supposed to be in the Sanctum below the Spire's top floor, adjusting runic circle angles. Not casting a transit spell. Adjusting angles.

JJ implied Layla had done something criminal. Perrin's face went somewhere it did not fully come back from. She had been his only friend. They had cast Locate Person; she was not within a thousand feet. The party left with an open invitation to bring planar questions back, extended by a man who was still cooperating out of something that had stopped being goodwill.

### Blood in the Garnet District

[[Tinny]] was running the same move that had gotten him caught the first time. Augie recognised him and the purse went back to its owner without ceremony. Tinny had four hours until his bosses came to collect at the [[The Low Flagon|Low Flagon]]. The [[The Claret|Claret]]'s supply chain came from outside the city. The code phrase for walking in was four litres of blood to sell.

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Lea]] messaged [[Luks Borg]] about whether the Blackstaff family was involved in illegal trade. Luks said no. The Low Flagon was next.

## Year 222 PA — 12th-21st Uktar (Session 21 — Three More)

### The Circle at the Spike

Three strangers arrived at [[Corran's Spike]] in the failing light of 12th Uktar. [[Layla]] was already there — crouched over chalk measurement grids extending twenty feet from a runic circle she had built independent of the [[Jade Spire]]'s project, her compass instrument reading the circle's residual energy with the focus of someone checking results, not maintaining infrastructure. She counted three. She had three [[Adamantine Guild]] bracers already prepared. She told them to find [[Perrin Quickquill]], and she told them the [[The Claret|Claret]] code phrase as if she'd been carrying it for a long time, and she stayed to finish her readings while [[Shadow Creatures]] gathered at the far edge of the clearing — ignoring her, tracking the newcomers. The arrivals: [[Erwina Lesmana]] (tiefling bard, Indonesia), [[Terra]] (aasimar ranger, California), [[Sadiah Ispahani]] (wood elf rogue, Pakistan). All three had seen the same vision on arrival: Corran pressing his hands into the earth, the Spike rising where he had stood. All three made it to [[Crystal City]] a week later without fully understanding what they'd witnessed.

The shadow creatures followed to the city walls and stopped. They did not leave.

### Two Weeks in the City

While the newcomers traveled the alabaster highway east-to-west, the city's threads pulled tighter. [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk]] took an aerial survey with [[Torem]] on griffin-back and saw the regional supply chain from above: magical creature blood flowing in from eastern settlements, disappearing somewhere between the road and the [[Ether Scryer's Academy|Academy]]. [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Gill Bates]] received his water mastery path from his [[Temple of Istishia]] mentor — four levels beginning with Sacred Sea — and instructions to spend two downtime tracks in meditation before the next chapter opens. [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Lea]] found the gap: a week before her own arrival in Crystal City, a massive shipment of magical creature blood had been bought out entirely, leaving the city low on healing potions. [[Veyris Thantorn]] mapped the cosmological structure underneath the problem — echoes, a stuck astral barrier, wolf bone from [[Arborea]] carrying a through-line to a wild plane — and confirmed that something was systematically consuming the material the Alchemists needed to keep people alive.

### Three More

The newcomers arrived at the [[Adamantine Guild]] with Layla's metal and Perrin's name and no context for either. [[Istar Silverlock]], still serving punishment in an apron, pulled [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk]] aside and argued that the Claret was a better path to Layla than a chase east — a position that landed on a man who didn't trust him and was convincing anyway. [[Erky]] ran the testing: three pairs, a pocket arena, a standing order not to kill each other. [[Terra]] flew. [[Sadiah Ispahani]]'s sneak attack opened Nanuk's neck for twenty damage on the first round. [[Erwina Lesmana]] healed the people she was supposed to be fighting, because it was the only mode she had. Everyone was restored to full by the end. Everyone leveled to five.

By session's end, Erwina was a silhouette in direct light. Her keris — [[Nyala]] — did not fade with her. The guild had a stranger's blade on the table and no instructions for what to do with it.

## Year 222 PA — 21st-30th Uktar (Session 22 — Blood and Bone)

### The Echo at the Top of the Tower

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Regius]] arrived on the top of the [[Jade Spire]] tower in a waiter's uniform, because [[Perrin Quickquill]] had found [[Layla]]'s reagent drawer and tried to reconstruct her work. The circle had opened. It had found Regius the way a signal finds the receiver built for it — his echo had been flickering in [[Crystal City]]'s Garnet District for weeks before his body arrived, a faceless silhouette the locals had learned to pretend not to see.

The [[Shadow Creatures]] came through the ceiling and did what they always do: straight for the Earthers, ignoring everything else. Perrin threw torches from the upper floors and screamed at the party to evacuate. [[Terra]] stepped into the center of the room and let her inner radiance burn at the cost of her own body — paralyzing two shadow masses, setting a third apart in light — until [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk]]'s bear could carry her out. [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Shen]] dragged Regius down the stairwell. The party extracted floor by floor, and by the time [[Luks Borg]]'s mages had sealed the tower in ritual-cast glyphs, everyone was outside and breathing.

### The Shape of a Circle

At the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]], [[Elmindaria L'Thorian]] put [[Perrin Quickquill]] in front of the assembled party and told him to explain himself. He did. The reagents in Layla's drawer — a handful of metal scraps, dark red powder, fragments of bone — had been enough to redraw the circle and open a path. He had not directed it at anyone. The circle had found Regius.

Daria named what was in the pouch. Metal: the splintered remnants of a magic item attuned to another plane, the anchor that gave the transit spell its direction. Powder: humanoid blood, dried. Bone: humanoid, small fragments. The transit spell — seventh level — required two things: a planar-attuned anchor and the matter of living consciousness. Blood and bone and the mind behind it. The city's reagent shortage was not coincidence. Something was consuming the material of people in order to move between worlds.

The [[Church of Kelemvor]] tracks cross-planar soul movement by divine mandate. [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Gill Bates]] and [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Augustus Kennedy]] had paths into that institution. Those paths had just become relevant in a new way.

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Shen]], in the two weeks since his arrival, had embedded himself in the Academy's herbalist society and its antiquities department, earning sixty-four gold in the process and giving the department's tall gray-skinned director a full description of the [[Sunless Citadel]]'s Sanctum murals. The department exists to answer one question: why does no one in Aenath remember anything from before the Awakening? They now have a collaborator who was there.

### The Maimed God's Scales

With the transit spell's components understood and the [[The Low Flagon|Low Flagon]] meeting ten days away, [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Jonathan Jordan]] laid out his open obligation to Istar. The scales of justice held in the [[Hall of Justice - Temple of Tyr|Temple of Tyr]]'s great statue — the actual scales, in the main hall of the church that ran the city's entire law enforcement apparatus. [[Istar Silverlock]] heard the party's version of this proposal, let the silence sit, and smiled. He offered proficiency training to anyone who helped.

### Open Sesame

The party formed their approach to the Low Flagon: JJ, [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Shen]], and [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Regius]] as sellers — *I have four litres of blood to sell* — with [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk]] on the roof and Inukshuk within eyeline as cavalry. The goal was to pull down a mid-level [[The Claret|Claret]] contact, learn who was buying and why, and avoid becoming the product themselves.

The plan was complete. The meeting had not happened yet.

## Year 222 PA — 21st-28th Uktar (Session 23 — What the Stone Remembers)

### What the Stone Remembers

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Shen]] dreamed of an older Aenath — a burning red plane surrounded by lava, millions of scintillating lights, people casting spells that failed to coalesce, dark shadows cracking a barrier meant to keep them out. A presence beside him, unafraid-inducing, answered an impatient god with a scream: *"If you need my help, come talk to me one on one."* Three formless lights smelling of mortal souls told him he'd arrived just in time to help reinforce the barrier, and named someone he couldn't catch.

Awake, the party laid their magic items out together for the first time. [[Elminster's Spellbook]] proved translucent, not fully of this world — a fragment of [[Mystra]] herself, Lea's personal anchor. [[Terra]] confirmed the same shape in her own [[Hollowstone]]: not the Unbound himself inside it, but a fragment of his consciousness, present enough to scold her — *"Seek the light, especially in darkness"* — when she struck the stone in frustration. [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Regius]]'s [[Le Fil]] stayed silent when he spoke to it directly. Stacking all three items produced only a faint resonance, nothing more. [[Sadiah Ispahani|Sadiah]] remained in her room, still traumatized. The party noticed, without quite deciding when it started, that they're speaking more Common than English now.

### Errands and Discretion

Lea and Regius sought fixer work at the Hucrele House; Soral ruled out the Blackstaffs as clients and sent them to [[Ostrin Feld]], steward of [[House Verenthal]], on her introduction. The job: trail [[Sellas Verenthal]], the house's wayward young lady, discreetly through the city at night — fifty-one gold, and ten crystal glyphs to break if she found trouble. Regius took a second job at the docks, earning eighteen gold and a foothold with the [[Azure Tamers]] — sky tower access, use of one airship.

[[Istar Silverlock]] sent [[Terra]] to formally serve [[Mielikki's Grove]] under [[Sylvara Oakwhisper]] — protect the outskirts from overharvesting, protect people from the wild without killing it, guild first. Touching the grove's earth, Terra sensed a unicorn: one of Mielikki's own aspects, briefly present.

### Behind the Wall

Garm secured Lea, Nanuk, Terra, and Regius an audience with the Blackstaff family — not the head, but [[Lady Blackstaff]] and an unnamed brother, met behind a warded, doorless wall at the family's [[Blackstaff Mansion|Crystal City mansion]] — the head of house was absent, and it was Lady Blackstaff and an unnamed brother who received them. Lea shared her landing vision of [[Corran Blackstaff]]; Lady Blackstaff, who had never known what he looked like, was moved to find the account matched recorded history. She knew nothing of the [[Sunless Citadel]] beyond what Daria and Garm had already shared, but examining Elminster's Spellbook convinced her the expedition mattered more than she'd thought — the Blackstaffs will travel there with the Director of Antiquities after the Feast of the Moon, two days after the party's own meeting with the Claret. The brother, shown Le Fil, identified it at once as soul matter, materialized, he said, out of Regius's own body.

The 28th of Uktar closed as the Feast of the Moon. The Claret meeting is two days out.

## Year 222 PA — 29th Uktar (The Understudies — Session 01 — The Sundered Temple)

### Four New Arrivals

Four more were pulled through in the same instant, an ocean and a continent apart on Earth a moment before: [[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/!D'bleh|!D'bleh]] mid-goodbye to his aunt in Stellenbosch, [[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/Esca|Esca]] walking his mother home along a Mokpo harbor, [[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/Fern|Fern]] mid-punch as her sanctuary project was torn away outside Ürgüp, [[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/Kili|Kili]] stepping out of a Singapore lift lobby. All four resolved onto [[The Sundered Choir]] at once — a ruin adrift over the main Corranor continent to the northeast, nearer to [[Stormberg]] than [[Crystal City]], now confirmed, after this session, to be a previously undiscovered, abandoned temple to [[Lathander]].

[[Layla]], watching from above, warned the four to run and dove off the platform into the clouds before the fight even began — never engaging directly. Shadow Creatures met them there next — more solid and humanoid than any previous encounter, resistant to everything but the light of the ruin's own braziers once the etheric lines feeding the central circle were severed. A couple of rounds in, [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Regius|Regius]] and Lea arrived by griffon to hold the line.

## Year 222 PA — 30th Uktar (Session 24 — Four Litres of Blood)

### The Vision

[[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk]] dreamed as a polar bear, fighting alongside wolves, raptors, and eagles under a hundred-foot antlerless protector, against a wave of dire wolves and dark panthers led by an equally huge, many-antlered shape whose face drifted between antelope, tiger, and lion without settling. Both huge figures died in the same breath — his protector cutting down the enemy as its own killing blow landed. No god's name attached to either. Everyone who checked the emotional weight of it against their own instincts agreed: whatever Nanuk saw, it happened somewhere real.

### Two Reports, One Meeting

Morning of the 30th — Feast of the Moon a day off, the Claret meeting hours away. [[Sadiah Ispahani|Sadiah]] came down to breakfast for the first time since Session 23. [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Regius|Regius]] and [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Lea|Lea]] carried a story from the day before: a floating ruin cracked open above the Corranor mainland to the northeast, nearer Stormberg than Crystal City, four strangers fighting shadow creatures more solid and resistant than any prior encounter, three radiant-charged figures scattering the shadows outright. Regius and Lea helped, then had to leave for the Claret clock — the four strangers were routed toward Stormberg, not home. Whoever is pulling people through has gotten better at it: four this time, not one.

A group investigation check confirmed what the party suspected — the Claret already has six of their faces clocked, JJ's first among them, tied to the party since before anyone realized they were being watched.

### The Low Flagon, By Day and By Night

[[Sadiah Ispahani|Sadiah]] and [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Nanuk|Nanuk]] scouted [[The Low Flagon]] by morning, Regius and [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Gill Bates|Gil]] holding back two streets as backup, and found more than expected: a side window that opened onto the same hollow, pressureless dark as the Guild's own pocket-dimension door, a second window trapped with a needle device (disarmed), and an unexplained corridor running from the tavern into the city wall itself. [[Tavia Orn]] sat alone inside with a ledger, in no rush at all. The afternoon went to leverage — blood vials from [[Terra]] and Regius, rare reagents talked out of an [[Alchemist's Guild]] contact who's now owed a favor.

By night, the plan reshuffled once more — no disguises up front, [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Jonathan Jordan|JJ]], [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Shen|Shen]], and Regius as sellers, Gil, Nanuk, and Lea at a table with drinks as cover, Sadiah keeping still near the wall, Terra on the roof. Terra's stealth failed on a natural 1; Tavia noticed her outright, and the party sent her home to the Guild. Sadiah, by contrast, went the whole night unremarked. In the warded back booth, Tavia weighed the reagents and then the vials — and it was the vials that moved her. JJ dropped the act and told her plainly: they want to go home, and they think [[Layla]] is why they can't. Tavia knows the name. She's a client. She offered JJ a future place under her and walked the group — JJ, Shen, Regius, Gil, Nanuk, and Lea — toward whoever runs the Garnet and Sunstone Districts for the Claret, a name she didn't give them.

## Year 222 PA — 30th Uktar (Session 25 — The Adder's Hold)

### The Floor Test

The archway sealed behind Tavia the instant the last of them crossed through, and the six found themselves inside [[Corvin's Hold]] — the private pocket-dimension gauntlet belonging to the Claret lieutenant who runs Garnet and Sunstone. No framing: ten of Corvin's own people waited in a bare stone room, and a flat, level voice from nowhere told them to show what they actually were before anyone wasted tea on them.

Lea's Sleep dropped two before the fight was a minute old. Shen's Suggestion took a Warden out of the fight entirely, and his Keen Mind read the room fast enough to catch what mattered: nobody here was hunting them, something else was meant to trigger the violence. Partway through, Lea's Elminster's Inquiry asked the only question that counted — *how do we get through these people unharmed* — and the Weave answered in three words: **"Don't kill me."** The party pulled back hard from lethal intent: Gil's Thunderwave threw five guards into a wall instead of cutting them down, Shen's Hold Person took two more out without killing them. It wasn't clean — two guards still died to the party's blades before the shift took hold — and when a third broke and told JJ to just go, the voice in the corner killed him for it with a lightning bolt: *"I pay good money for loyalty."* Mercy, it turned out, wasn't free in this house either.

### The Hub and Its Doors

The party spilled into a wide chamber with six doors and an open archway, the same voice delighted now, calling them "the new celebrities of Coronel." A stone antechamber held three basins — coin, blood, truth — and blood, offered, opened a hidden panel onto **[[The Marrow Ledger]]**: shelves of ledgers recording huge money transfers, one open on a stand with a major client's name scraped out and re-inked into code. Lea's Elminster's Inquiry asked the Weave who the client was. The answer came back in two words: **"Layla and Asmodeus."** No one at the table fully understood what that meant. Everyone understood it mattered.

Elsewhere in the Hub: a curtained room held a young Claret associate, **[[Fillis]]**, mid-threat against a debtor, visibly relieved when the party turned out to be the backup he'd been promised — playing along got the debtor pressured into admitting he had cash and promising payment within the week, and got the party a favor owed by Fillis himself. A plain door opened onto two clerks calmly taking inventory, slipped past without a fight. A cell door and a sealed wax door were flagged as a trap and a fake, respectively, by Nanuk's instincts, and left alone.

### Corvin Adder, In Person

The last door opened on Corvin Adder himself — cane he didn't need, tea already poured for exactly the right number of guests. He introduced himself by name, thanked the party for the evening's entertainment, and confirmed what they'd suspected: [[Layla]] is a longtime, reliable client of his. He also named, without much hesitation, the fault line inside his own organization — another lieutenant, **[[Vashti Corel]]**, pushing her people into territory that isn't hers.

In exchange for a lead on Layla — and, pressed, a sending stone he could use to reach her directly — the party negotiated a condition: he won't make that call until they've returned from following the Blackstaff family to the Sunless Citadel. He agreed, and named his own price: a future favor, unspecified, no questions asked. Somewhere in the middle of it, genuinely curious, he asked what it was like to come from a world with no magic at all, and got an explanation of television and advertising for his trouble.

The session closed on that agreement — no blood between the party and Corvin Adder, a new name in their problems (Vashti Corel), a lead on Layla gated behind a trip they haven't taken yet, and a debt they haven't been asked to pay.

The archway door at the back hummed with the Guild's own vault-feeling. Sadiah stayed at her window. Terra was already gone. JJ, Shen, Regius, Gil, Nanuk, and Lea crossed through together, and every line back to the street went dead the moment it closed behind them.
