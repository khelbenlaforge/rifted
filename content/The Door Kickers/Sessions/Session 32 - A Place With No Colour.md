# Session 32 - A Place With No Colour

**In-world date:** 7 Nightal, Year 222 PA, from dawn into the night, in the forest north of [[The Road North|the Road]] and about 70 to 80 miles from [[Crystal City]].

**Present:** [[Nanuk]] · [[Terra]] · [[Gill Bates|Gill]] · [[Min Ji-ah]]
**Away:** [[Regius]] · [[Shen]] · [[Sadiah Ispahani|Sadiah]] (gone with [[Erky]])
**Off-page:** [[Ember]] · [[Jonathan Jordan|JJ]] · [[Augustus Kennedy|Augustus]] (none of the three appeared in play)

## What Happened

### The Morning After

The sun came up slowly. It was winter, frost lay on everything, and the party's breath hung in the air a beat too long. The forest around the kill-site was still flattened and frozen. The tang of blood had faded but not gone.

Before play began, the DM adjusted one thing from the last session. When Erky turned away from the dead druid, he took Regius, Shen and Sadiah with him, and all four left together. That left four of them, with the belt in Nanuk's hands and the pull toward [[Silvanus]] still steady and warm in his chest, north and a little west.

Ten paces off, the dead man lay where he had fallen. The gold had gone from his eyes. His right hand, the one that had run like lava, had cooled to something dark and glassy, almost obsidian, with frost ringed around it. Gray dust still sat in the creases of his face. The cord on the belt still carried an owl's hooked beak, a set of claws and a pair of deer antlers.

The party wanted a long rest. The DM was plain about it: they had only just fought, so resting meant waiting for night, and in winter night comes early. Eight hours were theirs if they wanted them. The rangers read the signs and the DM spelled out the rest. Herds had been driven south by starving predators, and one man could not have done that. There were more of them.

They hid the cart, the horses, Bella and themselves in the tree line. A Survival check of 31 was enough, and the DM asked for no stealth roll. Predators would not willingly walk into an owlbear's territory, and nothing else was likely to either.

### The Glass Hand

The dead man had very little on him. No pack, no lamp. His knife was serrated, jagged and a little chipped: a poacher's knife, made for taking trophies, nothing magical. The thought of what that blade had done to a living animal made it worse.

Terra rolled a natural 20 on Investigation. Before the result, the party remembered something Ember had once told them. Some Lavaliths tattoo a flame on the underarm, as a point of pride for the work they did to stabilize the world during the cataclysm. The body confirmed it: the dead man was a Lavalith. The glass stopped clean at the wrist, fused to the bone, not a coating. The soles of his feet were unmarked, with no cuts and no bruises, the feet of a man who could cross a forest without trouble.

For the natural 20, the DM gave Terra the glass hand. It does two things, each once per dawn. Pressed to the earth as a bonus action, it lets allies within 10 feet ignore difficult terrain from rock, rubble and ash and keeps them from being knocked prone until the end of their next turn. As a reaction when she takes fire damage, it gives her resistance to that instance of fire damage. It stayed faintly warm in her hand. The DM said it was a big clue: the hand was not following something. It was pulling something, tethered to a thing out to the southwest.

Min Ji-ah ritual-cast Identify, ten minutes, on the hand and then on the belt, with advantage on her Arcana check. The cord knotted into the shape of a claw is the symbol of [[Malar]], and five threads of energy ran out of it, each tied to something else. Cord and hand carried the same energy signature. One thread ran southwest, one west by northwest, one south, one northeast and one north. They pointed like the hands of a clock, and some drifted slightly, as if what they were tied to was moving. The DM called them bridges, not a spell: the cord was linked to five other things. The beak, claws and antlers carried no magic, though the DM called them very high-quality materials.

### Eight Pings

The party woke from the long rest in the middle of the night. The DM asked for a round of checks. Terra looked through the bore of the [[Hollowstone]] and, on a poor roll, saw eight flashes of energy to the northwest, each a wash from a huge area. One was familiar. It felt like the ethereal touch of Misty Step, and Terra and Min Ji-ah, the two who cast it, both knew it.

Gill held the stone he had taken from the Sacred Sea, and it pulled along the groundwater toward one specific direction, also northwest.

Someone flew up on Bella for a bird's-eye look and, on a 29, saw two things that did not belong. One was a column of smoke that was no campfire. It was brimstone. The other was a font of pure life energy, shooting up like a geyser from inside the trees, about a mile from the first. He also saw the owlbear's spirit, still drifting over the forest as mist.

From all of it the DM could place five of the eight pings: a font of life, a column of brimstone, a place where energy was being sucked into nothing, a strong pull of elemental water, and the ethereal one. None of the five matched the five threads. Each time slot, a night or a morning or an afternoon, would allow one site, and hunting for a ping they had not found would cost a slot too.

They chose the ethereal one first. It was the closest thing they had to planar travel, and the nothingness sat near it.

### The Black Pool

Following the direction of the pings and about an hour on foot, they came upon a hollow with a pool in it. The wind was gone. No animal called. A light snowfall had followed them in until the moment they entered, and then everything stood still. The black water had not a ripple, a perfect circle of frost ringed it, and their voices went flat and died unless they stood within five feet of each other. The last leaf of a tree that was losing its leaves hung in the air where it had been falling.

It was about 70 feet from the tree line to the pool, and the pool was 10 feet across. Detect Magic read nothing until they were within 30 feet of the water. Gill walked in and stopped about 20 feet short. His Arcana check of 23 told him what the water was: a gate, a way into another plane. The stillness was being forced on this one spot, like the vacuum behind a car moving fast.

They tested it carefully. A stone thrown in broke the surface and vanished with no ripple. The water was black and reflective, like an obsidian mirror. An arrow tied to a rope went in, and the rope went slack. When the party pulled, the arrow came back suddenly heavy and almost flew into the face of whoever held the rope. It was still a perfectly good arrow. Inukshuk lost his sense of direction as soon as he came near.

Min Ji-ah's Arcana check of 21 gave the lesson from her Academy days. The Ethereal Plane is raw primordial energy that can become fire, water, life or entropy, the stuff the elements are made from. It is a kind of reflection of this world, and it touches the Material Plane, which is why Misty Step can slip through it and come out again. It is not exactly dangerous, but it is almost unnavigable: sense of direction, sound and space all go. Nothing about it said it would lead them to Silvanus.

Terra offered to go in. They tied a rope to her, agreed to pull her out after five seconds, and she went in face first. She felt the air pulled from her lungs and slid in fast, into fog moving at hurricane speed. When her last foot crossed, she froze in place, pulled back and drawn forward at once, and as far as she could tell she was paralyzed. The rope went slack on the outside. The strength rolls to haul her out failed. Terra rolled Perception, then switched to Religion with the same roll. The result, a 20, told her what she needed to know: to cast Misty Step and ease her way out. She did, and she reappeared on the outside, unhurt. The rope stayed inside. Had she not managed it, the DM said, she would have spent about four hours clawing out.

### The Camp, Again

They went back toward the old campsite, on the way to the next ping, and reached it late that night. The party cleaned up the camp as they arrived.

An Insight check of 28 brought a sudden sense of hatred, the residue of someone's hatred sitting in the air. Survival and Perception checks of 26 and 30 found barefoot humanoid tracks, a single set, leading north.

The dead druid had been propped upright against the fir, set there with care, as if someone had brushed him off and leaned him against the trunk. An Investigation check of 17 turned up nothing else done to him: no trap, no tracking device. Whoever it was had done it respectfully, almost lovingly, and had not buried him. The party decided to take the body with them anyway.

The five threads on the cord had moved. Two now pointed northeast, one northwest and two back toward the road, and one of those five ran exactly where the footprints went. Min Ji-ah sent a message with her Sending Stone to [[Luks Borg]], in 25 words: the party had been attacked by a Lavalith, north of Crystal City. The answer, such as it was, said the Lavaliths keep to themselves and that even the Grove knew little more. Ember was with them and could not be reached by message, because she was underground. An Investigation check, with advantage, found that the black pool had erased the party's own traces, so whoever had come to the camp had guessed.

They weighed calling Erky. There were only four of them, and the druid they had killed had needed eight, with Erky, to bring down, and then only because a chimera had tied up most of them. They decided to go on and see for themselves, and nobody pressed the glyph. Pass without Trace would have helped, but neither ranger had it prepared, so they ran a group Stealth check, and the median was 16.

### A Place With No Colour

They went on to the place where energy was being swallowed. The way was easier than the way to the pool, with fewer trees and enough light from the waning moon. As they came near, they saw a ring of trees with white bark around a clearing that didn't feel like a clearing.

One Perception roll of 12 saw an ordinary forest. A Nature check, also a 12, gave more: birch is not that white. Detect Magic showed blanket energy: entropy, the opposite of life. It was not rot. It was an absence of substance, and the party could taste it. Inside the ring the trees were bare, with no crowns, their roots broken off mid-way, and everything was ashen and colourless. It was 60 or 70 feet from the edge to the middle.

Min Ji-ah sent a branch in on her Mage Hand. At 30 feet it was gray, and it came back brown. She failed an Intelligence save with a 12 and felt irritated beyond reason: the place was devoid of meaning, as if the rest of the world were a poem and this were a page where the words had been scrubbed out. It had no mechanical effect, and it touched only her.

Gill, with Min Ji-ah's help, rolled a 20 on Perception and saw movement on the far side of the trees. A Survival check of 25 found two sets of barefoot human prints and four sets of canine paws. They had stopped about 120 feet off, pressing deeper where they stood, and then wove around the edge. Someone messaged the others: two druids, four wolves, and keep quiet. It was too late for a stealth plan. Min Ji-ah's irritation had already cost them much of it.

### The Ambush

Nobody rolled above a 10 for initiative. Before anyone could act, a hiss came across the trees, and four wolves covered 120 feet in a single turn. They were emaciated, with red-glowing eyes. From the other side a cheerful voice called out, "Ahoy, friends! What are you doing out here on this lovely moonlit night?"

Terra tried Animal Handling on the wolves and rolled a natural 20. Two of them did not become friends, but they hesitated. She flew up a tree. Nanuk told the wolves they were being misled and used as weapons. He rolled a 16, which was not enough, but Terra's 20 held. The voice came again: "It's an interesting place, ain't it? What place has no colour? Why are you here?"

Min Ji-ah's Chromatic Orb in poison hit the wolf nearest Gill for 16, and she Misty Stepped behind cover. She wanted to use the whistle and the body in the cart, but it takes a minute to cast. Gill raised his Spirit Guardians as fish with teeth, 15 feet around him. Across the clearing a Fog Cloud 20 feet wide bloomed, and the party lost sight of the two druids. One wolf died to Gill's Spirit Guardians with no scream, only a sense of something dissipating.

Then one of the druids walked out from behind a tree in the open, called "Ahoy, friends," stepped to stand behind the wolves and compelled them in a guttural, hissing voice. He brought down a column of lightning on all four of them. Everyone took 12, and Terra and Nanuk used Absorb Elements to gain resistance. Both wolves in front missed.

Terra answered with arrows, one with lightning from her Absorb Elements and a Hail of Thorns, and the wolves began to fall. Nanuk's arrow for 16 finished one. A small voice asked why they had to go and kill it, and the wolves dissolved into mist. Soon all four were dead.

Min Ji-ah cast Tasha's Mind Whip on a druid and then, when a druid began a summoning of spectral animals, Counterspelled it with an Intelligence check of 19 against a high-level spell. The action was wasted.

The other druid kept just outside Gill's Spirit Guardians, close enough to have all four of them in range, and answered with a push of rock that dealt 20 bludgeoning to the whole party and shoved them 15 feet. The glass hand kept them on their feet. Gill struck a druid for 19.

### Out of Initiative

On his turn the druid who had hidden in the fog looked Nanuk over and called to his partner that there was a Silvanus person in the house. He cast Tree Stride. A parting attack caught him as he moved, for 16 damage, and he vanished into a tree. Nanuk had put his Hunter's Mark on one of the druids, and through it he could feel one of them, about 500 feet away.

Min Ji-ah, furious, fireballed the other druid from 150 feet for 15. Gill dashed forward to bring his Spirit Guardians down on him. The druid looked around and said, "I was just beginning to have fun."

He did what no wolf had dared. He walked toward the centre of the clearing on purpose, taking two opportunity attacks, 17 and 15 damage, on the way. The colour drained out of his face and body and he winced, but he went on, and he turned and said, "Zadok's gonna say hi soon." Then he found a tree that was barely alive, stepped into it with Tree Stride and was gone.

The druid who had cast Tree Stride was still in the clearing. Terra's Entangling Strike restrained him on a failed Strength save. He spent his action to break free with a Strength check of 18, kept his concentration on Tree Stride, and stepped into a tree. Everyone was out of initiative, and both druids were gone.

After the fight the DM named the druid who had cast the fog and hidden behind the tree while the other stood in the open: Sweeper, the one who had swept their tracks.

## Key Takeaways

- **The dead druid was a Lavalith.** [[Ember]] had once said some Lavaliths tattoo a flame on the underarm, and the body confirmed it. See [[Ancients of Lava]].
- **Terra now holds the dead man's glass hand.** It lets allies ignore rough ground and not be knocked prone, and it gives resistance to fire for a moment. It stays warm and pulls toward the southwest, as if tied to something.
- **The cord carries five threads.** Each is tied to a different thing and has the same signature as the hand. They shifted in the hours the party was away, and one ran the way the footprints went.
- **The black pool is a gate to the Ethereal Plane.** It is not where Silvanus is, and Misty Step is the way out.
- **Zadok is coming.** The druid who walked into the colourless place said so.
- **Two druids and four wolves ambushed them, and both druids escaped by Tree Stride.** One of them is called Sweeper, and Nanuk can feel his Hunter's Mark link to one of them about 500 feet away.
- **Still open:**
  - Five of the eight pings are placed, and none matches the five threads.
  - Who set the body against the fir.
  - Where Erky went.
  - What the glass hand is tethered to.
