# Session 26 - The Weight of the Moon

**In-world date:** Late 30th Uktar rolling into the [[Feast of the Moon]] (the intercalary day between 30 Uktar and 1 Nightal), Year 222 PA — the same night the Corvin Adder deal closed in Session 25.

**Present:** [[Lea|Lea]] (Ming) · [[Nanuk|Nanuk]] (Francis) · [[Gill Bates|Gill]] (Chian) · [[Sadiah Ispahani|Sadiah]] (Veronica) · [[Terra]] (Simar) · [[Shen|Shen]] (Avinaash) — Shen split off early for offscreen research and doesn't appear again this session.
**Absent:** [[Jonathan Jordan|JJ]] (Jonathan) — narratively away with Tavia · [[Ember|Ember]] (Dora) · [[Augustus Kennedy|Augustus]] (Justin) — both confirmed returning Session 27 · [[Regius|Regius]] (Clarissa)

## What Happened

[[Tavia Orn|Tavia]] led the six of them out of [[The Low Flagon]] after three hours inside; half an hour later they were back at the [[Adamantine Guild]], eleven at night. JJ stayed behind for his new Claret earring stud. Shen peeled off for the Amethyst Quarter, still chasing down what's actually happening to the city. What was left of the party settled into the kind of quiet that comes after a deal that could have gone very differently — Nanuk frustrated that "white people" kept routing them through other people's errands instead of their own problems, [[Inukshuk]] working to calm him, Gill turning the shape of Corvin Adder over in his mind: *insane*, in the specific way of a man wealthy and secure enough to risk it all for entertainment.

Then the visions came. Terra found herself a guardian at the side of something massive under attack by dark-skinned humanoids. She tried to heal it, tried to light the dark around it; neither worked. She felt herself pulled toward it, felt herself merge. She understood, as it happened, exactly what was asked of her: give up her soul so the spirit could rise and empower the being it served. Her body was left dead the moment the merge completed. Hearing it, Nanuk recognized the shape at once — it echoed a vision of his own from days before, one he was only now putting into words: if he'd let himself be drawn in fully, his body would have died the same way, his soul rising to make the being he fought beside stronger — multiple animal spirits had already done exactly that. Gill's vision put him somewhere else entirely, a mote of light adrift in vast space, watching a humanoid figure work an anvil with a hammer.

[[Inukshuk]] told Nanuk plainly: the Great Spirit was hurt, but alive, and needed him. *"Stop being inward. Open your heart and your eyes a bit more. The spirit needs us. The four spirits of the North, South, East, and West are converging to form a new world."* Nanuk had tears in his eyes before Inukshuk finished. Gill, for his part, was told to pay closer attention to his studies with [[Tidecaller Marin Deepcurrent]].

Then Gill's spear started to vibrate. A voice surfaced in his head, unimpressed: *"That is so not beautiful."* Gill fired back — if you're going to criticize the fishing, say so to my face. The voice softened, almost sheepish: *"Ok, people have told me I'm hard to be around. I'm sorry, Midnight."* It was [[Adon]] — [[Fair Catch]]'s resident voice, reaching past Gill to someone from his own life. *"You are very connected, maybe more so than other people in this world. Can't say much because I'm under contract, but I need you to know that I love that you love what you do, but Istishia is weakening, and so are her siblings. Everyone needs to get to the bottom of this soon."*

[[Istar Silverlock|Istar]] walked in on the tail end of it, [[Luks Borg|Luks]] and [[Elmindaria L'Thorian|Daria]] with him. *"The Blackstaffs do not own this guild. If we choose not to go, we don't go."* Whatever the tension with the Blackstaffs is, Istar wasn't backing down from it, and he was plainly grateful to have this batch of newcomers around despite how unceremoniously they'd arrived. He brought them outside. The city was fully dark, no lamps, no candles, and where the moon should have been, three glowing orbs hung in the sky instead: pure white, dark red, and a black so complete it shouldn't have been visible at all. Everyone felt it at once, a stillness like every living thing in the city had stopped to watch the same moonrise. Inukshuk mourned, quietly, for things long gone.

Daria explained what she could: Aenath has four moons. [[Selûne]]'s looks like Earth's own; the other three — the ones hanging in the sky right now — are what spellcasting actually draws on, the Weave reaching casters by proxy through them, and nobody knows exactly why. **This is her account, not confirmed cosmology — treat it as strong testimony until independently verified.** The Feast of the Moon is the only night all three of those moons are visible together, each one a different aspect of the Weave. Gill, listening with senses tuned to water, felt [[Istishia]] faintly: a wave crashing into lava, steam rising into a cloud around a baby in a crib, a dark hand reaching for the child and finding only camouflage, grasping at nothing three times over.

Sadiah reached for the moons with Mage Hand, pulling at threads of light woven across the sky like a tapestry, and got a vision instead: a dark figure opening a book, black mist pouring from its own shadow as it tried to read, the mist resolving into shadowy creatures that peeled away to search for something. Terra felt the pull of Sehanine's rite building all night. By the next evening, the last day before the full moon, motes of light rose from the whole city in a line meant to cover the last dark sliver before the moon completed. Every mote was a dancing figure. Terra's wings started glowing silver, not their usual fairy-gold, and she rose into a dance she'd never learned, alongside elves, dwarves, fae, and humans who somehow already knew it too. [[Hollowstone]], worn as her pendant, grew hotter through the dance, hot enough to melt its own cord twice, fall, and reappear around her neck both times. Once, unbidden, it said: *"This is not the never after I was speaking of."*

Istar told them the Council of Seven would want to see all of them the next day, after a public address to the city; a priest of Kelemvor would close the Feast with a blessing for Crystal City. Everyone returned to the Guild for a long rest.

The next morning, now the Feast of the Moon itself, Nanuk went to the [[Mielikki's Grove|Grove of Mielikki]] in the Diamond District, restless since hearing from Adon. [[Sylvara Oakwhisper|Sylvara]] met him there and told him something he'd never felt named before: *"You're a child of [[Silvanus]], the Oak Father."* Silvanus keeps no grove in the city. Sylvara described him and [[Malar]] as locked in an opposition where either one's death would collapse the balance between them, and named the [[Belak|Ancients of Lava]] as extremists, eco-terrorists acting in Silvanus's name without his blessing. The Oak Father has no fight with civilization itself, only with the idea that anyone stands outside the tree. She gestured in the general direction of Stormberg — not a confirmed destination, just where to start looking.

Meanwhile Lea's radar for divine gossip caught something new: Mystra, [[Azuth]], and [[Savras]], all housed in the same temple complex — and none of them keep clergy in the traditional sense. Mystra gives magic freely enough that no priesthood is needed; Azuth and Savras are the same, each attended by devoted magic-users who worship them rather than serve as ordained clerics. Azuth, once one of the most powerful mages alive, became her devoted (and by some accounts amorous) advisor on magic's advancement. Midnight and Kelemvor's shared vision resurfaced unprompted too, the one where Bane's chains on Mystra broke and she died soon after, at Helm's hands. As far as anyone in the room understood, gods aren't supposed to be able to die at all, and nobody knows what actually happened after that. Something is looking for her. Even Asmodeus, Lord of the Nine Hells and part of the cosmic balance, works only through contracts and proxies. Devils can't cross the barrier into Aenath any more than anyone else's kind can, which is exactly why a proxy like Layla would need to exist at all.

Lea pushed further, casting [[Elminster's Spellbook|Elminster's Inquiry]] to ask the Weave directly: how do they get help from the gods? The answer came back in three words — **"Get past barrier."** Has Layla herself crossed that barrier? No. This casting was the third from the spellbook — it finally unlocked the book's locked cantrip, Elminster's Footnote.

Terra danced through to noon at [[Sehanine Moonbow|Sehanine]]'s shrine, exhausted and, for the first time in a long while, completely at peace. Gill and Lea walked to the [[Temple of Kelemvor]] together.

Seven of the Temple's own paladins burst out of it screaming. **"We lost the Gravelord!"**

Initiative rolled with the party split three ways — Terra still at the shrine, Nanuk walking back from the Grove toward the Jade Spire, Gill and Lea outside the Temple doors. The session closed there.

## Key Takeaways

- **Three of Aenath's four moons channel magic (unverified NPC claim):** Daria's account — Selûne's moon looks like Earth's own, but the other three drive spellcasting via the Weave by proxy, not directly — is strong in-fiction testimony, not confirmed cosmology. Worth independent corroboration before treating as settled fact.
- **Elminster's Footnote (cantrip) unlocked.** Lea's casting of Elminster's Inquiry for the "Get past barrier" answer was the book's third casting in play — the locked cantrip is now readable.
- **Terra's Cold Open, corrected by the DM directly (not in the raw source notes):** the celestial once bound to her made an active choice when its tether ended — it merged with a massive being, giving up its continuity to empower it, mirroring the mechanic Nanuk's own vision describes. This supersedes the earlier prepped Cold Open (a passive parting) and Lea's own notes, which cover Terra's vision but skip the sacrifice detail.
- **Adon's warning:** Istishia is weakening, "and so are her siblings" — the other elemental primordials, unnamed. Delivered through [[Fair Catch]], addressed in part to Midnight specifically. Urgent, not yet explained.
- **Nanuk is a child of Silvanus.** Confirmed by Sylvara at the Grove of Mielikki. Gestured in the general direction of Stormberg for whatever comes next — not a confirmed destination. Ties into the established Silvanus/Malar opposition and the Ancients of Lava's extremism.
- **Sadiah's vision may be the origin of the [[Shadow Creatures]]** — a dark figure reading a book that births shadowy searchers. Unconfirmed, unclaimed by anyone in play yet, worth flagging against the existing Shadow Creatures thread.
- **Cliffhanger:** "We lost the Gravelord" — the Temple of Kelemvor's own leadership title, not the god himself — screamed by seven fleeing paladins. Initiative rolled with the party split across three locations. Session 27 opens here.
