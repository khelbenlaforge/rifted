# Session 31 - The Oak Father's Answer

**In-world date:** 6 Nightal, Year 222 PA, into the dawn of 7 Nightal, on the [[The Road North|Road north]] out of Crystal City.

**Present:** [[Nanuk]] · [[Gill Bates|Gill]] · [[Min Ji-ah]] · [[Regius]]
**Voiced at the table:** [[Terra]] (her player was away; played alongside Nanuk) · [[Shen]] · [[Sadiah Ispahani|Sadiah]]
**Off-page:** [[Ember]] · [[Jonathan Jordan|JJ]] · [[Augustus Kennedy|Augustus]] (none of the three appeared in play)

## What Happened

### Cartoons and a Glyph

The party camped on the Road and woke to the kind of cold that gets into everything. The fire was down to its last coals, breath hung in the air a beat too long, and winter had started to claw its way into daily life. The bread was gone. Ahead the road kept climbing, and the forest closed in tighter on both sides than it had the day before.

Erky was up before anyone and noisier than all of them. Everyone woke with something rude drawn on their faces, and only Terra had really tried to stop it. Two attempts to pay him back missed, and the party gave up.

He hadn't spent the whole dawn playing. He had gone out early and come back with a report: whatever was spooking the wild animals and eating the kills was still close. He had turned it away once and never got a look at who it was. The table weighed chasing it against finding Silvanus and chose to keep following the pull.

Before he left, Erky picked Nanuk as the most responsible of them and drew a small glyph on his forearm with two fingers. If Nanuk concentrated on it, Erky would know where they were and could be called. He said he would not be far, and that he would come if anything went badly wrong. Then he split in two, and the halves went off in opposite directions to check that no other pack was being driven.

### The Smell of Iron

Nanuk took to the air. Terra rode with Bella, and Gill, Sadiah and the others went by carriage. The tree line made the forest hard to read from above. On the ground the smell came first: fresh, wet iron, with nothing rotten in it. From the air, those with a good eye saw a stretch of forest flattened and frozen, a long drag mark, and at the end of it something feathered and huge. The sharpest could tell it was bigger than a cart and lay among knocked-down trees with its head turned north, as if it had been watching something when it lay down.

### The Owlbear

Nanuk reached it first, ahead of the carriage by a quarter of an hour or so. It was an owlbear, the upper body of an owl on the lower half of a bear, the size of a car, and it was alive. Its beak had been cut off at the root, and every claw on all four feet ended in a bloody stump.

Nanuk knew the work. He knew the angle a blade needs to take a talon clean, and how deep to go for a beak without splitting the jaw, because back home he had done both on animals already dead for the butchering. He had never seen it done to anything this size unless it was dead. Someone had done it to a creature that was still breathing.

A thin, warm light rose off her like a stream of water. It lifted about a foot, then leaned north and a little west and began to drift. Only Nanuk could see it. Inukshuk read it for him: her essence, bleeding out and going home.

The others came up. Someone's Medicine check put the cuts at a day old at most. Wounds like that cannot heal themselves, and she had two or three days of painful dying ahead. The party could close the wounds, but nobody present could regrow a beak or claws, and without them she would die in the wild no matter what. Terra's Perception turned up a second set of tracks coming in from the west. They were barefoot, big, five-toed and human. Beak and claws from an owlbear were worth a great deal to the right buyer, as reagents or as crafting material.

Shen put a hand to the ground and took the six-second flash his Specimen gives him, the most significant thing that had happened there. Bare feet on frozen earth. Stone dust on the backs of two hands. A blade that was not hurried at all. A cord on a belt, knotted into the shape of a clawed paw. A Religion check, pushed with Guidance, put a name to the paw: Malar. Gill moved to end her suffering at once, and Nanuk asked him to wait.

### Asking Her

Nanuk would not decide for her without asking. From fifteen feet, near enough to be heard and far enough to stay clear of a lunge, he spoke to her through Beast Speech. He told her he meant her no harm and would find whoever had done this. Then he asked one thing: did she want to be healed and restored, or set free? She could look one way for the first and the other way for the second.

Her breathing slowed. She raised her stumps in front of her face and looked at them, and she seemed confused. Terra read her and said she wanted to live and had no idea how. Someone closed the wounds on her face with a Channel Divinity heal, and her pain eased. She tried to stand, cried out when her stumps met the ground, and Nanuk told her to stay down and rest.

Nanuk put a hand over the glyph, concentrated, and said, *Erky, I need you. We need you.* Inside ten seconds there was a flash, and Erky was standing among them.

He saw the owlbear and stopped smiling, and none of them had seen that before. A tear ran down his face. He swore, which none of them had heard from him either. He had seen a druid on his chase, working to stir rage and aggression in the predators of the area, and the druid had slipped him like an eel. He could not regrow a beak or claws. Only a cleric of Ilmater, god of endurance, suffering and martyrdom, or of Lathander, the god of the morning, might manage it, and there were none within reach. When someone asked what a druid would want the parts for, he said potions and spell reagents, and that owlbear beak and claws were among the hardest materials around, good for weapons and armor.

When he started west in a rage, the party stopped him and told him to give them facts before he gave them fury. He waved a hand, and divine energy pulsed out of him. He spoke to her in squawks, and then he said what none of them wanted to hear: they might have to put her down. It was not fair to her, and none of them could fix it. He called her little sister.

Then he turned to Nanuk and asked what Silvanus would do. He didn't think Silvanus would ever do this on purpose, but it would count toward Nanuk's worthiness, and it was Nanuk's call as someone of Silvanus and of this world's life force. As companions, the rest of them had to make it together. Erky's own choice was to put her down. He told them he was going to find whoever had done this, and he left, furious.

### Letting Her Go

Nanuk asked her one more thing, a yes or no: did she have cubs who needed anyone? She did, and they were old enough to fight for themselves. The party chose mercy, respectfully, and with what grace they had.

Nanuk went close and told her he was sorry they couldn't put her back together. He told her to go back to the spirit, and that he would see her later. Inukshuk came up beside him. One paw covered Nanuk's eyes, and the polar bear took her throat. The light came off her as a mist and rose into a great cloud. Inukshuk looked up and said that little sister was happy now, and was going home.

### What She Left

Detect Magic found the same magic here as at the first kill-site. Something else had changed. After her spirit left, a little of it seeped into Nanuk and the rest went its own way, as if in thanks. The pull toward Silvanus grew a great deal stronger, and from here on, any check that leaned on the pull would be made with advantage.

Some of the party wanted to hunt the druid down and make them pay. Nanuk, uncharacteristically, said that they could not beat Malar by acting like Malar, and that the answer was to keep the balance and find Silvanus. The pull ran north and west, and Erky's chase had run roughly west, so the two were not far apart, but a hunt would cost time.

It was coming on evening. The ride had taken most of a day, four hours out, a break, and four more. Min Ji-ah cast Elminster's Inquiry on the owlbear and asked who the people were and what they wanted. The answer came in three words: drawing out Silvanus. It also settled that the two kills were the same hands, beyond doubt. Nanuk took it to mean that Malar's followers wanted Silvanus lured into the open so they could kill him. Someone asked whether they might be trying to bring him back instead.

Traveling by night would mean more predators, hungry ones. They decided to camp.

### The Fir

Nanuk had been feeling Silvanus's connection to the world weaken, and he had not heard from him lately. The party urged him to reach out and tell Silvanus they were coming. He walked to the biggest tree near the kill-site, a huge old grandfather of a fir, knelt with Inukshuk beside him, put a hand on the trunk and closed his eyes. He felt for the part the tree played in the balance of things, and said, *stay hidden.*

It was a hard reach. The DM set a high difficulty and let the table shave it down. Inukshuk's presence took some off. Terra stayed beside him in prayer, which gave him advantage. Speech of Beast and Leaf took more. Gill prayed to Istishia and put Guidance on him. Someone talked Nanuk down from the worst of his rage, and it held. Sadiah drew a small flower out of the winter ground. A check of the Weave found it steady here, which closed off one idea but was worth knowing. The number came down far enough that a good roll with advantage could beat it. It did, by the skin of its teeth.

For the first time, the life force of the world was something Nanuk could sense instead of only see or hear. Some of [[Mielikki]]'s essence came to him and gathered round his thoughts. He reached into the tree's roots and heard the stories of frost, of spring and of fires. His awareness ran out along lines like a web, to a few deeper branches that held a deeper consciousness, and he heard a very deep voice.

*I can't hide, and I never will. This world needs my life force.* He said he loved and respected all that Nanuk had done. Nanuk had returned another child to him, and back into the cycle she went. He and Malar had danced this dance for millennia. He did not condone it. He would not be baited, and Malar had been trying for years. But every child who turned from him weakened him further, and animals counted. He would not kill Malar, and neither of them could kill the other; both were necessary for whatever was to come. Malar only wanted more of the world than he should have. There were bigger things than Malar out there, and if Nanuk chose to fight him he could, but Silvanus was looking at a larger picture. *I will not hide, my child. I will keep the heart of this world beating. Find your way to me.*

Nanuk asked how he could help restore balance if he did not fight. Silvanus said the life force of the world's souls also fuels the world, and that all the gods had lost their connection to all souls. His family was behind a barrier. He and four others were the only ones here, holding everything together: Kossuth, Grumbar, Istishia and Akadi. All five had given themselves freely to keep the world going until the barrier could one day be released. The barrier couldn't be removed. Nanuk asked who made it, and the answer was *we did, all of us.* His entire family had done it, to protect all of you. Even Asmodeus, whom he would not trust within an inch of his life, wanted to protect souls.

Then he paused. *You haven't been here long. How did you come back?* Nanuk told him they had been taken from a world called Earth and had simply appeared here. Silvanus answered, *No, child. You are of the souls of this world.* He had not spoken with his siblings for millennia and did not know what was happening beyond the barrier. The gods had to relink with the life force of the world, with souls to link to rather than souls to feed on, because a god is only as strong as those who revere and connect to them. Linking did not have to mean worship, though Nanuk could not do it yet.

Nanuk had one question left, and he asked what the barrier was protecting them from. The link gave out as Silvanus began to answer. Nanuk opened his eyes and told the others everything. They had all heard it.

### The Barefoot Man

The party took a long rest by the owlbear's body, and the rangers saw to the carcass. They set watches through the night. The early ones passed with nothing to report.

Nanuk had the last watch, with Inukshuk beside him. As the party slowly came awake in the dawn, someone walked out of the trees to the west. He was barefoot on frozen ground, with no pack and no lamp. His eyes were gold and glowing, slit like a cat's, and gray dust sat in every crease of his hands and face. A heavy cord hung from his belt. Tied to it were a hooked beak as long as a forearm, a set of claws, and a pair of deer antlers cut clean at the skull.

Terra and Nanuk were both awake at once. Whatever came off him was nature's own energy, and it smelled of blood and needless violence. He looked straight at Nanuk. *Looks like my little adventurer has brought something to the fold.* He said it was hard to get rid of that gnome, that he was probably off chasing one of his shadows. Then he said the Beastlord had his purposes, and that the Beastlord wanted Nanuk's father's purpose too. His handiwork spoke for itself, he said, because Malar was never one for the clean kill. They could take him to where the grove was, or he would kill every one of them now.

Shen cast Detect Thoughts. It gave no intelligible thoughts, only violence, and something large, a monstrosity, within thirty feet. Nanuk read the man's strength. He had taken down an owlbear alone and did not have a scratch on him, but a party could hold its own against him, if it was not easy. Nanuk said they were going to fight.

### The Fight

The man moved before most of them could. His hand turned the color of molten lava as he ran, and he slashed down on Inukshuk for fourteen fire damage and knocked the bear flat. He swung at Nanuk and Terra on the way and missed both.

Min Ji-ah opened with Chromatic Orb in lightning, twenty-five damage. The lightning ran down his arms to his fingertips and made a halo around him, and from then on elemental damage fed him temporary hit points, so Nanuk shouted at everyone to avoid it. Regius's echo cut chunks out of him in a flurry. Inukshuk got up and bit. Nanuk kept his bow in the man's face and put an arrow through the cord that held Malar's claw. The belt dropped with everything tied to it. His next arrow, a silencing one, went into the man's chest for twenty-one and silenced him. He only smiled, with blood in his mouth, and goaded Nanuk to *give into it.* Terra stayed in melee with her short sword and stepped away with Misty Step. Shen hexed the man for Wisdom and hit him with Eldritch Blast. Gill's spear struck true and his spiritual weapon, a glowing fish hook, missed. Sadiah's daggers drew blood before she cut away.

At the top of the next round, before his own turn, he pushed both hands into the ground and let out a feral cry that made no sound, because he was silenced. The earth split open behind him, and a lion's head rose out of the ravine, then a goat's, then a third over the top, scaled and dripping lava. When his turn came, he sank into the earth, popped up again where he stood, and an aura of fire flared out of him and burned everyone near him for eleven, with no save. The Chimera acted right after him. It went for the soft ones at the tree line with fire breath for sixteen, rammed Shen to the ground for nine, and bit at Sadiah, whose Shield turned it.

The party agreed to go after the druid and leave the Chimera alone unless they could hinder or trap it. Nanuk put his hand on the glyph and told Erky the man was here. Erky came running into the fight, and he was no longer cheerful. He stared at the druid and at the Chimera, then banished the Chimera, and it vanished. He healed Shen with Healing Word and told Nanuk to deal with the druid, because he would look after the rest.

The druid said it was all or nothing and used everything he had. Cinder Bolt hit Nanuk for twenty-one. Another burst of fire went out around him for nineteen, and Inukshuk went down. Nanuk touched him and he rose again. Min Ji-ah's Tasha's Mind Whip struck him for eight psychic, and Terra's Ensnaring Strike did not hold him. Nanuk's last poisoned arrows left the druid on eleven hit points.

Nanuk set the bow aside and drove an arrow through the man's skull by hand. *Tell Malar I say hello.* The light went out of the golden eyes, and he dropped.

Erky walked up to the body, kicked it, and spat on it. *Never do that to my people again.* Then he walked off, angry. What was left was the thing they had set out to do, which was to find Silvanus.

## Key Takeaways

- **[[Silvanus]] speaks with [[Nanuk]] at length.** He will not hide and will not kill [[Malar]], and the party's task is to find him. He says the barrier was made by all the gods together to protect everyone, that he and the four primordials hold the world, and that the gods are cut off from the world's souls. He also told Nanuk that the party are "of the souls of this world."
- **The two kills are one hand.** The druid wore Malar's claw, carried a hooked beak, claws and a pair of deer antlers on his belt, and is dead. Whether he acted alone is open.
- **[[Nanuk]]'s pull is stronger.** Checks that lean on it are made with advantage from here.
- **[[Erky]] came twice when called** and banished the Chimera. He left angry, and his whereabouts are unknown.
- **[[Torem]]'s migration job advances.** The druid who mutilated the owlbear is dead, and the herds' flight is still unexplained.
- **Still open:** the grove has not been found, what the barrier protects against was never answered, and the party has not decided what to do with the belt's trophies.
