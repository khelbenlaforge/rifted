# Session 06—Two of Five

## The Ledge

Mike came to on the ledge beside the Ribs, the platform that hangs over the ocean on dark pillars. The last thing he had held was the round room: statues around a map, a muscled figure with a hammer raised, a wall of lights streaming away from a dark shape, and his hand on the carving. What came back from the blackout was only fragments: a fiddle, cheering, and faces around a table. He remembered a burly man, a thin man with cat-like eyes, a second thin man who talked like a charmer, and two women, one with jet-black hair and a haunted look.

[[Dessa Kestrel|Dessa]] was standing where she had left them, with dark bags under her eyes and crumpled oiled paper around her from her meals. "Quick, you're back." She asked if he was hurt and whether all his limbs were there. He was fine. Dessa asked where [[Ruan Myrt Zaltana|Ruan]] was, and he had no idea. It was the second day since they had gone in: 49 hours, 20 minutes and 37 seconds. She had already sent Gregory, Rachel and Risma back up. Mike asked what had happened to him and what the room was. Dessa said the room she had seen on her own visit had smooth marble walls and a fountain in the middle, so something must have changed. If it had, she could not see it, and she thought that was probably down to who and what they were. She would wait for Ruan and told Mike to go up.

## The Climb

On the switchback he met [[Gregory Yang|Gregory]], who had come down to the stair gate on Rachel's behalf. The huge guard there had told Gregory not to go further. They traded news. Rachel had thrown Gregory up the Aerie's tower, he had fixed the lift, and he now had a weekly job there. He wanted the blacksmiths more, and he planned to join the Academy people who came every ten days. Dessa had told them earlier to keep their growth items out of sight, because anyone who saw something useful would want it. Mike's body felt the two days all at once on the climb. They went back to the Cliffwatch's pocket rooms to rest. While he rested Mike was asked about the life he had left behind, his work and what drawing meant to him.

## Downtime

They had a ten-day downtime ahead. It ran on three tracks, work, relationships and investigation, taken in any order at DC 5, then 10, then 15, with a fourth at DC 20 if they could justify it. Each 5 above the DC brought a better result, and a failure meant exhaustion. Every track would be played out as a scene.

Mike and Gregory took separate tracks. Mike wanted to know why they had been brought here and what the ruins were. Gregory wanted to get close to the people who ran the city.

## The Deep Index

Mike's first track was work, and he tried the Academy's library. He rolled Persuasion and got an 8, a thin pass against DC 5. The Deep Index was a lamp-lit stone passage behind columns at its entrance, cool and dry, with crates stencilled with dates and numbered drawers all along the walls. The clerk was Tavian Wick, young, with very pale skin, pink eyes, white hair and ink to his elbows. He took off his glasses and stared at Mike's bracer. He was swamped. He needed someone who could lift crates, read bad handwriting and help catalogue. He would pay a silver a day, as the Guild did, doubled if Mike did more. Mike also got the run of the library. Tavian said the academians would come at the end of the ten days.

## Cinderstay

Gregory's first track was work too. He had thought about courting the aristocracy, and then settled on the blacksmiths. He rolled Tinker's Tools for a 21 against DC 5. The smithy was Cinderstay, in Slagrow, where molten slag had run over the street and cooled in long rivulets. The bricks underfoot were hot with no fire in sight, and the hammer beat did not match the shops next door. Its master was Ansel Krane, a stoic human in his fifties, grizzled and scarred.

"What's brought this skinny man to my humble establishment?" Gregory said he was from the [[Adamantine Guild]], and Krane called him one of Dessa's boys. Gregory listed what he could do. Krane handed him a chipped dagger without a word, and Gregory fixed it with Mending, rolling 17 on Arcana. Krane judged it good. He needed repair work, and he paid well: a bench of his own and five silver a day.

The 21 got Gregory more. At the back of the smithy, by the quench troughs, was a stone-lined shaft, and Krane told him it ran all the way down to the Old Forge, the ruin under the city. He was the only one with direct access, and the Guild sometimes sent people down through it. The heat from below powered most of the forges in Slagrow. Dessa came by every now and then because she had broken her pen. Krane said he made things to last ten years, and Dessa appreciated that.

## Tavian

Mike's second track was a relationship with Tavian, and he played to him with jokes while they worked. The roll was strong enough that Tavian began to treat him as a friend. Tavian let slip that he sent a report to the [[Director of Antiquities]] in Crystal City every ten days, and he explained the Academy's directors. He also wrote down the name of whoever touched each item, so that anything could be traced back. Mike asked whether he could meet the Director. Tavian said the Director was keen for any news of the old places. Tavian had heard that others had found a ruin on an island the city was connected to.

Mike told Tavian a little of what they had seen near the Ribs, and that a friend had not come out. Tavian grew serious and said the Director would want to hear it. He asked whether he could write Mike's account up for the Director. He offered to bring them to meet the academians when they arrived. The Director's name, he said, was Yilde.

Tavian then began opening drawers. Gregory rolled 16 plus 7 on Investigation with advantage and Mike rolled a 9, and the total for both came to 23. There were dragon figurines from the Sunless Citadel, which Tavian said had been a place of dragon worship, and parchments with deity symbols copied from the door of a chamber there. A fragment of a door came next. Mike and Gregory could see the carving on it move, one symbol sliding off and another sliding on, though Tavian, pointing straight at it, saw nothing change. The symbol that returned most often was [[Kelemvor]]'s. Tavian's guess was that the muscled figure with the hammer in the Sunless Citadel's mural was [[Gond]]. A last drawer came from the ruin they had woken in, and he said it had been a temple of [[Lathander]]. He added that people who had lived long enough remembered the gods speaking to their clerics, and that since the Awakening none had.

Tavian also told them how Dessa had seen the Ribs. She had walked them and ended up in a room with only a fountain. She lost three days. She took a piece of rubble from the door and declared the place unsafe.

## What Tavian Told Gregory About Westward

Gregory's second track was investigation, and he asked Tavian how the city was run. His roll came to 25 against DC 10. Westward ran on two seasons. The [[The Ferry-Masters|ferry-masters]] ran the crossing to [[Sunhold]], a new mining settlement with rare ores, and the [[The Foundry-Owners|foundry-owners]], Krane among them, turned the ore into goods. Neither could claim to be the bigger power, because each needed the other. A third group, the charcoal camps, worked the [[The Wildwood|Wildwood]] outside the city. Above them stood [[House Ferrowe]], whose man in Westward was [[Osric Ferrowe|Osric]], its youngest son, who favoured neither side. Seven majors on the [[Corranor Council]] in Crystal City ruled the continent, and the Guild answered only to the Council. Tavian was not the person to ask about the underbelly.

## What Dessa Had Heard

On the fourth day Dessa met them as they came out for breakfast. She looked tired and still sad, and she had news from Crystal City. [[Garm]], the Council member, had given two assistants the task of working out teleportation: [[Perrin Quickquill|Perrin]], a halfling, and [[Layla]]. Perrin was away at the first experiment. Layla vanished just as the first Rifted arrived. Teleportation across planes did not work. Bringing someone across seemed to need something from another plane. By the researchers' reckoning, Dessa said, that meant tiefling or aasimar blood. There was a secret, illegal trade in blood, and Layla might now be closer to Westward than to any other city.

The one criminal group she knew of was [[The Claret]], run from Crystal City by a leader nobody had named or seen. They had a hand in everything from stolen artefacts to drugs. The Rifted in Crystal City had found them smuggling extraplanar blood for Layla, for the money and with no malice in it. It had been quiet in Westward, a town too busy working to buy much vice. She said the shadow creatures appeared every time Rifted were teleported in, which by her count was confirmed across four or five sources, and they went after nobody but Rifted. She had no memory of anything before 222 years ago, and had lost centuries of her own. She made a sign to Mystra and lit candles with flame from her hands. The goddess was still there, she said, even if silent. Rachel, she thought, was the one to watch, being a tiefling.

## The Shifting Symbols

Mike and Gregory went over what they had. The Ribs, the temple and the Sunless Citadel all seemed to predate the Awakening, and the murals felt like time capsules to Mike. Only Rifted seemed able to see them. Rachel could be in danger, since the Claret might hunt her to sell her blood to buyers like Layla. They agreed to go and find her.

First Gregory told Tavian that the symbols on the door fragment changed over time and that Mike and Gregory could see it while he could not. He rolled a 1 on Persuasion, then a 20 with advantage after Mike joined in. They spent a day describing the symbols as they moved, and Mike rolled a 20 on Perception. Tavian recited ten to fifteen deity names. He paid them a gold and said the Director must hear this when the academians came. Tavian, Mike gathered, saw the Director as a researcher who cared about information above anything else.

## The Hill

Their third track was the search for Rachel, taken together, on Survival. Mike rolled 19, which with his +2 came to 21 against DC 15, and Gregory rolled a 1. They took the higher. The city guards said Rachel's training party had gone north, and it was starting to snow. The 21 let Mike recognise her stride, heavy and one third longer than the men's. They followed the trail half a day up a hill about half the height of Fuji.

They made Constitution saves against the cold on the way. Mike's came to 14. Gregory's came to 8, so he spent his Heroic Inspiration on a reroll and passed.

They found Rachel midway up with a squad of the city militia, laughing, after she had thrown three men with a single swing of her glaive. Gregory told her about the Claret and the market in tiefling blood, and that she was a target. Rachel said she had felt eyes on her around the city, especially when she and Risma went to the baker. She gave them the address of [[Teira]], a tiefling friend from the city's small tiefling community, and asked them to keep Teira safe. A soldier remembered a case the week before of magical reagents smuggled out of the city and suggested asking at the Guild's headquarters.

Mike and Gregory told Rachel too about Layla: Layla had seemed to want to help them, and they wanted to help her. Rachel said word had come down from Crystal City that the whole continent was hunting for Layla. The Tamers and the militia had been told to watch for Layla, and soldiers were posted at the place where the party had first arrived. Rachel would stay with the squad until the next night and pass on what the militia learned.

## Teira's Door

They walked the four hours back in the cold, Gregory making a torch. Teira's house was in a poorer street of laborers and cooks, where hammers rang on the Slagrow side and the street itself was empty. It was a two-storey tenement with no windows and a single door, housing three or four families. Nobody answered. Gregory called out that Rachel had sent them. A neighbour behind them told them to shut up, and lights came on nearby, though not in Teira's building. Gregory cast a protective spell on himself. The door opened onto darkness. The smell that came out was metallic, like iron and blood. The session ended there.
