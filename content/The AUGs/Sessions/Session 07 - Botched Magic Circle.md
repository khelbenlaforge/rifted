# Session 07—Botched Magic Circle

## The Cold Opens

The session opened with the cold opens for Beau and Makoto, and then went back to the cliffhanger at Teira's door.

## Up the Stairs

[[Mike Kanbei|Mike]] and [[Gregory Yang|Gregory]] went in through [[Teira]]'s open door, looking for her. The house smelled of blood. They found blood and drag marks that led up the stairs of the tenement, and they followed them up.

The second floor held a teleportation circle, drawn in a hurry and badly, and two dead [[The Claret|Claret]]. Teira lay there unconscious. Another Claret had already jumped out of a third-floor window.

## The Top Floor

Above them, [[Tobias Atticus Rockefeller|Beau]] and [[Sumida Makoto|Makoto]] had landed on the top floor of the tenement after being teleported. Both were unconscious. The roll left Makoto out for one round and Beau out for three.

## The Shadow Creature

Initiative was rolled. A shadow creature attacked Makoto first. Mike and Gregory, elsewhere in the house, were slow to notice until Makoto ran downstairs to get away from it. Beau came to and joined the fight, and the four of them killed the creature.

## Catching Up

Mike and Gregory gave Beau and Makoto a quick download of the situation all the Rifted are in. They also made some investigation checks, and what those turned up will come in the next session. Teira was still unconscious when the session ended, and the party will carry her to the [[Adamantine Guild|Guild]] next session.
