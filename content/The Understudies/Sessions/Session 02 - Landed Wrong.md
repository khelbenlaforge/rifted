# Session 02 — Landed Wrong

## Two Doors, Same Night

Keith Poe was working a source at a noodle stall near the Rim Moei market, chasing a human trafficking pattern across the Myanmar border, when his patron's number lit up his phone — more money coming, a push to wrap the story fast, Naw Eh Paw watching him take the call with a look that said she'd seen this exact moment from three other journalists already. He stepped away to take it. He hadn't worked out how to answer the questions still waiting for him at the table when the walk down toward the riverbank stopped being a walk at all. The water went the wrong kind of still. The bank app on his screen turned blue instead of showing a balance. Then he was gone.

Roberto was on the tail end of a mob cleanup job in a Bensonhurst basement, walking his green trainee Nicky through what gets bagged and what gets bleached while Nicky heaved and vomited through most of it — an intimidation, more than a pep talk, was what actually got him steady again. Job finished clean, the two of them stepped outside, and the flask came out of Roberto's jacket pocket the way it always did after a night like that. He took the pull right as the D train's streetlight over 86th Street stopped flickering and started glowing a color streetlights don't make.

Both of them fell the same way down: a tower under a cloud that wouldn't stop turning, ground breaking open under running crowds, people crushed by falling stone before either PC was. Whatever this actually is, it seems to meet every arrival somewhere on the way through — this isn't the first landing to carry a vision with it, even if what each one sees isn't identical. Both of them died in it. Both of them woke up anyway, on the other side, in a forest neither of them recognized.

## Landed Wrong

Roberto came to first, senses sharper than they should have been, and turned his head a hundred and eighty degrees to find a green gremlin backing away behind a tree. *"What the fuck are you,"* he said. *"You're American??"* it answered, in an accent that didn't match the question. Neither of them had time to process the other before a third stranger arrived — a red-skinned, horned woman, breathless and visibly rattled.

**Layla** didn't offer much. Shadows were coming. Don't fight them. Transport was already arranged. She read as annoyed, embarrassed, frustrated — nothing that read like a con — and then she misty-stepped away before either of them could ask a second question.

## The Hollowreach

The shadows didn't wait long after that. Keith Poe lunged at Roberto in a panic that looked a lot like an attack before either of them understood what was happening, then broke for the treeline himself, shouting at him to run. Roberto disengaged and ran too, and the shadows caught him anyway — the pain got through even with a flask of Neat still working through his system.

Going down, he prayed. What came back wasn't just consciousness. A sunrise at the edge of his vision. A woman built out of light, ephemeral, beautiful past describing. A harp somewhere in the room playing itself. A fully armored figure behind it all, silent. Out of everything, one word arrived clear: *Lathander*. He grasped his holy symbol and stood back up at 11 hit points with Radiance of the Dawn already moving through him — two shadow creatures didn't survive it.

Gaslight and Fern reached the ground mid-fight, diverted off their own route toward Stormberg by the same correction that had sent Layla scrambling in the first place — her sending-stone call had pulled two goblin-riders and their giant bats down out of the Stormberg-bound formation and toward the Hollowreach instead. They'd already learned this lesson once, at the Sundered Choir: shadows keep coming out of dim ground no matter how many you drop, so standing and grinding wasn't the plan. Getting the newcomers moving was. Between the four of them, they cut down what they could and crossed the Font's edge into ordinary daylight, where the shadows stopped following entirely.

## Left Behind

There wasn't room for everyone on the bats. Gaslight and Fern lifted off north toward Stormberg with the goblin-riders, one more arrival from the same batch still unaccounted for and no time to look for them tonight. Kili's own rider — the one that had carried her since Session 1 — descended a second time and set her down with Keith Poe and Roberto before following the rest north, no scene played, just a passenger left where the flight ended early. Layla never came back. Two of the batch found, Samson Flint still missing somewhere in the wild, and nobody in that clearing with an answer for where to start looking.
