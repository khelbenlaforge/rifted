# Session 12—The Room Where the Sound Stops

## A Full House

Roberto and Kili opened the party's tavern in the [[Wallwatch]] that night, and Keith Poe worked the kitchen. It had been a full house, though by half past nine few people were left and the rest were drifting home. A player asked whether Oskar and a patron called Deb were still inside.

Around 9:35, five figures in fine dark satin or velvet came in with their hoods up. The first three sat down at a table. The other two wore armor and carried weapons, and they sat apart at a table with a good view of the room. The room took the first three in stride, as any tavern takes travelers, but the armed pair made people sit up: someone important had arrived. Then the hoods came down. The woman in the middle needed no introduction.

## The Best on the House

The tall elf at the front of the five bowed to [[Kili]] and asked for the best the house had. He looked about thirty. His long right ear was pierced from tip to base, and he spoke politely and without warmth. [[Keith Poe]] was in the kitchen, cooking, while Kili and Roberto worked the floor. Kili laid out burnt ends, cheese, jerky, pork-rind cracklings and sourdough, and Roberto brought his mead. Kili's presentation came to a 19, and Roberto's roll to put together a good menu was a 16.

The elf watched the plates arrive and said he appreciated the care. The woman beside him then lowered her hood. Her silver hair fell loose around a human face, and her right eyebrow was studded with piercings along its whole length. She looked about forty-five, and her necklace was plain and costly, with stones that caught the light from five feet away. Nobody had said her name. From her face and from everything [[Rhona Voss]] had told them, Kili and Roberto took her to be [[Zarantha Verenthal]]. They could have asked, but chose to pretend they had not realized.

She asked whether Lady Rhona was in. Kili said Rhona was busy. Zarantha said she had heard good things about the place and was sorry it had taken her so long to come, and she took the two of them for new recruits. Kili took the cue and gave a spiel: this was her dream, to serve and be useful, and she used to be a poor academy student herself. Zarantha looked up at that, interested. She told them she was grateful for the Guild's work, whatever form it took, and that without it many people would be lost with no champion. Kili rolled a 22, with Roberto's Guidance. Roberto rolled a 25 on Insight and found her very hard to read. He felt himself being won over by her smile.

The two armed guards at the other table looked at everyone in the room except her. Roberto and Kili took them for well trained, since they watched the room and not the woman they guarded.

## A Politician's Small Talk

She noticed the Morning Lord's symbol on Roberto's chest and asked after [[Sister Priya Dawnwell|Sister Priya]]. Roberto said Priya was very well. Zarantha said the city was grateful to the temple, which gives free healing to desperate cases now and then. Roberto knew the temple is also paid well for blessing weddings and births, and he answered with one of its strongest teachings, to be there to serve. The whole exchange had the sound of a script, and he could not read past it. He was quite taken with her.

About fifteen minutes later Rhona came out of the corridor from the party's rooms. She was tired and swaying a little, though not drunk, and she said it was late and the tavern was still going. Roberto went to tell her quietly who was at the table. Rhona looked over, saw whose table it was, and walked across. "My lady, I wasn't expecting you." Zarantha said, "Rona, my old friend," and Rhona said she could have sent word and been given a proper welcome. The elf said the place was amazing. The fare was not what they were used to, he said, but it was well made, and who could complain?

Rhona took a seat at the long table, which she rarely does, since she is usually behind the counter, and Kili served her some sourdough and dip. She introduced Kili and Roberto as new intakes and as Rifted. Zarantha said she had only known of the Rifted in Crystal, and called these two the rumored second round. It was said to be just as well that they had landed in Stormberg instead. Talk turned to Crystal and the Council of Seven, and Rhona said there were a few good ones, at least the ones who supported the Guild and gave it money.

The rest was small talk, and Kili could feel it was a contest. She rolled a natural 20 on Insight. The DM did not roll for Zarantha, because Rhona has no Deception to hide behind, so Kili read the whole exchange through Rhona. Zarantha was gauging Rhona's state of mind.

Roberto saw an opening and brought up [[Sir Verenthal|Quill]]: they had been doing good work, and one of the people they had been helping was her nephew. Zarantha looked up with polite curiosity and asked whether there was trouble. Kili watched her face. It did not change. Even on a natural 20 there was no flicker, and the DM told her the missing flicker said more than a flicker would have. It was as if she already knew.

Roberto said Quill had been a great help. Zarantha said she left the administration to her nephew, an amazing administrator, so that she could give her attention to other duties. Kili said perhaps she should keep a closer eye on the ledgers, and then moved to stand behind the Lady. The two of them had told her different things. Kili's glare at Roberto went unnoticed by the skin of her teeth.

Zarantha then told Rhona that Quill had come to her that very day and somehow knew about tomorrow's meeting, which she had thought was kept inside the council. It emerged that Priya had offered the temple's support, which the DM tied to Roberto's earlier talk with her about the workers and the meeting, though not the dragon. Rhona said she and Quill were not on the best of terms and she had told him nothing. Zarantha smiled and said all these little misunderstandings could be worked out. Rhona's eyes went to Roberto. The DM reminded him that he had gone straight to Rhona with everything when he came back from the Wall, and that she had gone to gather the council members.

The second manservant pushed back his hood and spoke. Two horns showed, dark violet skin and blood-red eyes. He said the servants had been saying that a stretch of the Wall was roped off as dangerous. Zarantha said anything to do with the Wall was her jurisdiction, and that she had heard talk of sending an extermination team, though she now understood the matter had been settled and the stretch was a cordoned safe zone. Her own men were already on the way to inspect it. Kili thought of what she had told [[Oskar Brell]] and felt sure it had spread. Roberto used Neat's One Real Pull on Zarantha: she was acting surprised, and in her own mind she knew everything, though whether she truly did was another matter.

Rhona gathered herself. She said the Lady surely had ears everywhere, as she should, and that she would not speculate before the meeting. The witnesses should speak for themselves, so the room began from a baseline of truth. [[Vestra Coln|Magister Vestra]] should be there, and Rhona would sit for the Academy, whose head was away. Zarantha agreed, smoothly.

Kili and Roberto worked out the timing. The Aerie's birds were all busy, so the inspection party was going on foot or by horse. It took a day to get out there, then the investigation, and then, if no one died, a day or two to get back.

## Sitting with the Lady

For about forty-five minutes Kili and Roberto joined the table. Roberto asked how much she handled of Quill's work with the laborers. Her horned manservant began to answer for her, and Zarantha cut in: no, [[Jarr]], she could answer for herself, but she appreciated it. She had heard that someone from Rhona's Guild was helping Quill with the talk among the workers. Roberto said that was him, that he did groundwork, talked to the laborers and worked with the office's administrators, and that he came at it wanting to understand the problem. Zarantha said the House of Verenthal was grateful and in his debt. She said every worker on the Wall mattered to her, from the patchers to the squad leaders, gang leaders and section leaders. It was plain she knew the hierarchy thoroughly, though not every worker in it. She added that handling so many people was a risk, and that no one could please them all. Rhona bristled and said Quill worked as best he could. Zarantha suggested the two of them sit down together, and then praised the mead and said The Gilded Loom might do well to stock some. The two checks Kili and Roberto rolled came to 11 and 23. Zarantha was confident on partial knowledge, and she did not hear everything from Quill.

Roberto asked about her. Zarantha said her uncle had been the esteemed overseer of the city. She had visited him often to learn, and she had come to love Stormberg and what it stands for. When her uncle stepped down and went back to the main house in Crystal, she was handed enormous shoes to fill. She said it was the people and their resilience that had made her job easy, and she thanked Rhona, Vestra and the work gangs, naming every group in the city and missing none.

Kili turned to the elf. He gave his name as [[Terendar]]. The twisted shapes in his ear were wool as well as gold, grown into ornaments, reminders of his family: wood elves, some of them druids. He was Lady Zarantha's seneschal, her right hand, answering only to her. Anyone who wanted an appointment went through him, and he ran a team of manservants under him. He said he had learned from the best, the head seneschal to a high lord in Crystal. The family was among the oldest, he said, though without the accolades of the Blackstaffs. He said Corranor is named for Blackstaff, who saved them all in the Awakening. The DM reminded the party of the visions they had seen: one man standing under a towering spire of light, calming the whole continent. He said the Verenthals were the first to see that the storms were a danger no society could live with, so they sent their best sorcerers. He said sorcerers, not wizards, which stood out, since the DM noted the whole continent is ruled by wizards. In his telling the Verenthals spearheaded the building of the Wall, with the Magocracy's backing.

Kili asked about the expedition. Terendar said the soldiers and investigators had left about two hours ago, and that their lady wanted to know what had happened. It was only an inspection, he said, since the House's soldiers were good in a fight but were not sorcerers. Workers had been hurt, and then word came that the stretch was safe. Kili asked whether he knew [[Yenna Marrow]]. He said she was retired now, and that she and her late husband were legends in the Guild who had saved many workers on the Wall. He turned to Rhona and said she had been in the same party as Yenna, and Rhona agreed. His eyes sparkled when he talked about Yenna. If time allowed, he said, he would like to taste her broth. The DM added, aside, that he is at least two hundred years old.

Roberto asked about her plans, and rolled a 21. Zarantha said that now the Wall had stood the test of time, the city was looking outward, toward the Arctic and the elemental stones the city earns so much from. They were thinking of maybe building lighthouses on separate islands and clearing some of the islands. Some of the Guild's own people were out there still, unable to get back until Stormberg's island landed. She spoke of a rest stop beyond the Arctic gates for ships, like the halfway stop on a long highway. Roberto wondered about dragons in the north. White dragons do like the cold, he knew, though nobody had yet told him that an expedition had run into trouble with dragons. He turned the thought over: [[Calcryx]] could be their ambassador.

The party took a long rest.

## Birds Gone South

The next morning Kili took one more face to memorize. She wanted a bodyguard's, and not one of the very important people, because she did not think she could pass as one of them. Which bodyguard it was is for the next session.

Kili and Roberto went up to the [[Stormberg Aerie]] and found [[Linnet Crowe]] pacing. The griffins were gone. She thanked Kili for the help with her scheduling earlier, said her griffins were all out scouting, and told them why. The animals of the north were migrating south, all at once, far too early. Even the new handler, [[Wren Dace|Wren]], was out. Zara, the roc, stayed with her eggs and would not leave them in winter.

She showed them a map with a pin in every city for its bird count. Stormberg had one big pin. The migration covered a vast stretch of the north. Kili rolled Nature on what could cause it and got a plain answer: nothing natural makes an animal migration like this. The rifts, said to be in the middle of the continent, would not have caused it either. Kili asked herself whether she had seen animals scurrying when she was Rifted, and answered that it had been bizarrely silent. The DM added that there had been no trouble with the animals as she got out, and that she had seen squirrels and rabbits.

Roberto wondered aloud about the Rifted who had left Crystal for the north, and Kili wondered whether they were causing trouble. The DM reminded them what Zarantha had actually said: she had meant the Council of Seven. A couple of bad eggs there had wanted to lock down the Rifted in Crystal, and the Rifted had refused, and she thought most of the Council were good eggs.

Linnet knew nothing about rifts. Kili's Insight on her face was an 11, and it told her what the pacing already said: the last time they met, Linnet had been cool as a cucumber, pinning things to her map. Kili copied the map down, with the lack of pins around Stormberg. It struck the party that the empty perches had bought them time. Otherwise the Verenthal soldiers would be flying to the Wall.

## The Procession

Roberto went to the Roll. Priya was not there. A group stood in front of the Wall: older women, women in their thirties and forties of different races, goblins among them, children, old men, fathers and grandfathers. They had stepped off the harbor pier onto ground that was dry now, and they placed their hands on the stone, exchanged old coins for new, and laid flowers on the ground. It lasted about an hour, and by about nine they climbed back onto the harbor. The harbor was full of onlookers who knew what the day was. Servants from Quill's office stood at the edge of the harbor, whispering to each other.

Roberto was easy to spot in his bright robes. He recognized some of the women as Sister Priya's people, and as they walked past they bowed to him and smiled. They carried work tools across their chests: hammers, nails, masonry scrapers. It was a procession in good order, and it worried Roberto.

He followed at the tail. His Perception came to a 10, enough to see Quill's face at an office window, sniffing furiously at a rose. The servants went inside as the line passed. The procession turned down a side street and made for the city square.

Priya was in the square, with volunteers, putting up awnings and booths. [[Yenna Marrow|Yenna]] had arrived with a small army of cooks and was setting up a booth of her own. It had the feel of a festival. Roberto reminded Priya that the day was for spreading Lathander's teaching of love and peace. She was surprised: she had thought they wanted to make a point. Yes, Roberto said, but subtly, and not like a protest. He asked her to make sure the tools did not look like weapons. Priya said they were only symbols of the work, and that she would have the marchers talk about their experiences in the city. The old couples set their hammers on the booth table. Roberto's Insight, boosted by Guidance, came out at 11, and he got a partial answer: it felt like a remembrance, the city looking on with support.

A column stood in the middle of the square with the emblems of the groups that built the Wall. Lathander's clerics, and monks of other temples, kept booths where marchers could ask for prayers for the dead. Kili bounded over, pleased with what she had learned, and told Roberto she had known she could count on him to start the revolution.

Rhona appeared in full ceremonial armor with her sword and went straight to Yenna's stall. Yenna called her a freeloader and, as Rhona stress-ate, patted her metal shoulder. Priya went from person to person like a bird, and clerics walked up to young couples. Kili teamed up with Yenna's noodles, which sat side by side with Roberto's mead in big kegs. Roberto took mead and noodles to the paladins of Lathander and other temples who were standing at the square's corners, and his roll was a 19, plus 2. They accepted politely and bowed, and seemed less afraid of him than before. The DM said they would roll later for how the tavern's business was going.

## The Hall of Gray Stone

Just as the hour came, Rhona wiped her mouth, stood up, and signaled Roberto, Kili, Keith and [[Samson Flint|Samson]]. She looked them over, brushed the dust off Samson's jerkin, and decided Keith was fine. The four of them followed her into the city's main mansion, a set of wings and buildings, and came to the [[Hall of Gray Stone]]: two stories, no ornament, with the Magocracy's crest hanging from pennants along the wall and again over the door. As they went in, the DM rolled a 16 for the fifty women and the rest listening to Priya. Nobody stood near the building making a scene, only children running around with hammers. Rhona told the clerk she represented the Adamantine Guild and the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]] for a party of five, and the clerk pointed them to the second floor.

They stepped over the threshold of the long room and every sound stopped. The street was loud behind them with boots and children, and then there was nothing. It did not fade out. The drone stopped too, and so did the sound of the wind against the Wall that the whole city lives with. The windows were clear, and below them the festival went on in silence.

The room was long and plain, with one table down the middle. Three bigger chairs and a fourth stood on one side, and a row of smaller ones on the other. Rhona sent the party to the smaller chairs, which felt like an interview or a tribunal. She went to the other side herself, took a chair there, unbuckled her claymore and slapped it down on the table. Samson and Keith sat quietly, running through the facts in their heads.

Vestra Coln came in first, thin and cold and very elegant. Her glasses were on, she carried a stack of papers, and she had brought her own quill and inkwell. She took the biggest chair, in the middle, and looked at them, counting: one, two, three, four. Then she told the clerk in the corner, for the record, that she had attended the planning session at the [[Wallwatch]] on the evening of the 13th of her own initiative, uninvited, and that she took no position. The clerk wrote it down.

Zarantha came in smiling, dressed in the gray and silver of her house instead of black. Those happen to be the city's colors too. Her piercings caught the light. Terendar and Jarr followed in livery. She held a hand out to every one of them as if they were guests in her house. "Good morning. I hope the kitchen forgives me for last night." Vestra and Rhona stood as she came in.

A fourth person arrived, a stocky dwarf with a big belly and a clean-shaven chin, holding a mug. He made an entrance that Vestra seemed to find vulgar, and he waved off her complaint: life was a little too short, my love. He sat in the last chair. His name was [[Jeezindra]], he said, and he was the Merchant Guild's representative. Everyone except Vestra and Rhona had a manservant standing behind their chair, and the clerk stood in the corner.

Rhona opened. The Guild had called the meeting, she said, and it was the Academy's opinion that the council needed to act at once, on reports from trusted Academy and Guild members. She introduced each of them with their record. Jeezindra said, "In the flesh," and Zarantha only smiled. Vestra winced whenever the dwarf opened his mouth. He told them the Merchant Guild would love to pick their brains about the worlds they came from.

The DM laid out how it would run. There were four voters, Zarantha, Vestra, Rhona and Jeezindra, and a decision needed three of the four. So either Jeezindra could be won, or Zarantha embarrassed into voting with them. The meeting would go in stages: the facts first, then cross-examination, then opinions. Any skill was allowed as long as nobody was caught. A spell with a somatic component risked it. A spell with only a verbal component, such as Guidance, was not a big problem, the DM thought. The session ended there, before the party had presented its facts. The DM expected the next session might take the whole sitting and be grueling, unless a natural 20 ended it in the first two rounds.
