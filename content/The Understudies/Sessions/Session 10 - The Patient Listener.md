# Session 10—The Patient Listener

*The recording of this session was lost, so this note is rebuilt from the DM's memory. Anything the DM could not recall is left blank.*

## Planning at the Wallwatch

On 13 Nightal, [[Roberto]], [[Kili]] and [[Keith Poe]] met [[Rhona Voss]] at the Wallwatch to plan for the meeting. They did a lot of planning. The details of what they settled on were not kept.

## An Unannounced Listener

While they planned, [[Vestra Coln]] arrived unannounced. She had come, ostensibly, because Rhona had not given her much detail when setting the meeting up.

Vestra listened politely and respectfully. She asked a few questions here and there and otherwise communicated by body language. The party's good points brought her round slowly, a little at a time. By the end she was on their side as far as she could be: she would never say so openly, and she had to stay neutral.

## Roberto and Priya

After the Wallwatch, Roberto talked with [[Sister Priya Dawnwell|Priya]]. She had already pledged the chapel's voice to him for the forum and the Magocracy meeting. This time he convinced her that [[Sir Verenthal|Quill]] shows favouritism in the shift locations he hands out to the Wall workers.

## Where Things Stand

The meeting with Vestra and [[Zarantha Verenthal]] is on the 14th or 15th of Nightal. The date will be fixed at the table. Going in, the party has a Magocracy representative quietly on their side and a chapel behind Roberto.

Session 9's open items all stand. There is no price on the hoard and no food found for the den. The work gangs are meant to hear nothing, although Kili's whisper to one of them has already gone out. Keith still owes his Wall readings, and Roberto's forum is due at the end of the month.
