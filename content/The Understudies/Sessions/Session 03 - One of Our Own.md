# Session 03 — One of Our Own

## Back on the Ground

Gaslight and Fern were already gone, climbing north toward Stormberg, by the time the second bat came down. Kili's rider set her down at the edge of the Hollowreach where Keith Poe and Roberto still stood — the same goblin who'd carried her since the first landing, peeling off from the flight a second time to finish a job nobody had asked him to do twice. Three of the batch, together for the first time since the temple.

Layla found them almost immediately, and this time she stayed. No warning shouted from a distance, no misty-step out before anyone could ask a second question — she just stood there, worn thin in a way the night before hadn't shown. Another arrival was still unaccounted for, a third name from the same batch put down somewhere close but not close enough. She'd already gone looking before she ever came to find them.

## What She Couldn't Follow

She'd tracked him to a doorway standing where none should have been, cut into the middle of the Hollowreach's tree line — a portal with edges that didn't sit right, though nobody could say exactly why. A Shadow Creature had reached him first and dragged him through it. Layla went in after him and stopped dead against the far side of it: no lock, no ward she recognized. It didn't look like anything at all. It felt like a wall the instant she leaned into it.

She didn't order them to go. She laid out what she knew and told them plainly it was theirs to decide: one of their own, still on the other side, and whatever kept her out clearly hadn't kept them out when they tried the same threshold. Before she let them through, she gave them the way to Stormberg once Samson was back, in case this turned out to be the last conversation she got.

## The Hexad

The space past the doorway ran on rules of its own — six rooms turning around a wheel, each built to test something particular: protection, generosity, freedom, predation, acquisition, domination. They worked it out through trial as much as talk, more than one wrong turn sending them back to start over. However long "hours" means somewhere without a sun, that's what it cost them.

Samson Flint turned up exactly where the shadow had taken him: unconscious, hurt no worse than the drag through the door had already left him, still not waking up.

## Out the Other Side

The way out gave when it decided they'd earned it. Real morning light waited on the far side, thirtieth of Uktar now, and the three of them stepped through carrying a fourth body between them. Four of the seven accounted for, together in one place for the first time since the rift took any of them. Stormberg was still ahead, and Samson still hadn't opened his eyes.
