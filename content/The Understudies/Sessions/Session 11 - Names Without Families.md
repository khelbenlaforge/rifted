# Session 11—Names Without Families

*Session 11 was not recorded. This note is rebuilt from the recap the DM and players gave at the start of Session 12. Anything that recap did not carry is left out.*

## Morning at the Wallwatch

The party came out of their pocket dimension on the morning of 14 Nightal and sat down with [[Rhona Voss]]. She asked them to see, if they could, that nothing spread outside. Nobody wanted a panic, and nobody wanted the people at the coming meeting to walk in already told.

## Quill's Rosters

[[Roberto]] and [[Kili]] went to [[Sir Verenthal|Quill]]'s dock office, which was full of roses. They went through the rosters. Laborers sent to the inner columns had their families written beside them. Laborers sent to the outer columns mostly had a blank.

[[Oskar Brell]] was on the outer list. He has a wife, and the roster says nothing of her. They asked a clerk named [[Ambrose Tallweather|Ambrose]] why. Ambrose said Oskar had never told them. The party figured it might be because nobody cared to ask him. He said he would look into it and hunt down any more cases like it.

Roberto suggested a data cleanup. Ambrose liked the idea, but the cleanup covered the company ledgers for the Wall labor and the harbor. Household staff and household documents were not his department.

## The Household Books

Once they had access to the household records, the financial ledgers looked balanced. One line was not. Fifteen gold pieces a week went to an entry with no label, only the words "Crystal City" written beside it. Ambrose said [[Zarantha Verenthal|Lady Verenthal]] enters it herself, for the family's holdings.

The family office alone cost 213 gold, for cleaning, flowers, servant salaries and upkeep. The whole family's expenses came to 640, so the office took about a third. The recap did not keep whether those were weekly or monthly figures. Roberto noted about thirty or forty servants in the office, all faces he had not seen. The first visit had shown another thirty or forty, different faces.

The players retconned in a question about how many people the household had on its payroll. The DM's answer was that it was not his jurisdiction, though not for lack of caring. The DM recalled him telling one of them, no, he loved his job.

## The Names on the Wall

In the afternoon Kili noted that the contracts favored seniority, and she memorized names. The first few, the people who built the Wall, had not increased in decades; the recap did not say what had not increased.

The party went to the Roll, where the names of the dead are cut into the Wall near the sea gates. The DM recalled that Kili skipped the first generations and aimed for the recent names.

## Noodles and News

Kili went on to [[Yenna Marrow]]'s stall and ordered food in quantities that grew until Yenna was confused by it. Kili let it stand without a further reason, and Yenna said she would do her best.

Roberto went to the chapel of Lathander, where [[Sister Priya Dawnwell|Priya]] had news. Rifts were said to be opening in the middle of the continent, and nobody knew where they led. The guess was the Feywild, the shadow plane or the Ethereal Plane. They were destabilizing the continent's energy. Animals in the north were migrating south far too early, the plant-eaters first and the predators following them. Worshippers of Sylvanus said it was not normal, and there was a lead the party could chase in the field. Then Priya dropped the other news. She had fifty people coming the next day: widows, mothers and children, the families of Wall workers who had died or whose breadwinners were no longer around.

## Names That Matched

In the late afternoon Roberto and Kili compared notes. They compared the names of the women they remembered with names on the Roll and in Quill's ledgers. They were not sure of all of them, but at least a couple of surnames matched workers with no dependents on the roster. The dependent had been noted only in the registration made when the worker first joined.

Oskar came to see them that night. He was glad to hear that writing his wife's name down and swearing he had family in the city might make things better. He also told them that he and his wife had been offered a deal: better working positions, in return for dropping the talk. The recap did not keep who had made the offer.
