## Description

Shar is the goddess of darkness, loss, and forgetting: night's other face, the twin nobody prays to on purpose. Where she takes shape at all, she reads as a darkly beautiful woman, raven-haired, wrapped in black cloth that seems to keep dissolving into shadow at its own edges. Her eyes are solid purple around a black pupil. They're old enough to still hold the memory of the dark before anything existed. Her smile carries no warmth in it at all.

She isn't loud about it. Shar's clergy work in secret and her temples hide behind ordinary doors. Someone's grief, a grudge they've nursed for years, a wound they've refused to let close — she turns what a person already carries into her opening, patiently, and saves the broken door for when patience has actually run out.

## Worshipers

People come to Shar to forget grief, betrayal, a wound they can't put down. What she actually offers is numbness dressed up as relief: permission to stop expecting to feel better. Miners, the blind, the depressed, and the quietly vengeful pray to her, along with thieves, killers, and anyone with something to bury. Her clergy rarely have to argue anyone into coming. Reputation does the filtering; good priests of Selûne, Lathander, and Mystra handle the warning.

Her faithful hold their worship close. Where her church is barred outright, its temples still hide in plain sight — an unmarked door, a storeroom nobody looks at twice, one patch of wall painted a wrong kind of black.

## Role in the Story

No standalone Shar note existed until now — Selûne's own page held off "until warranted." This session (Understudies S5) is the trigger: the DM named "the Cult of Shar" and "the goddess Shah" directly as the party's antagonist, secondhand, via Rhona relaying news from Crystal City.

**What's confirmed in Aenath so far, all Door Kickers-side:**
- **Session 27** — [[Shrine of Shar]] in Crystal City's Diamond District: the site of a failed Rising of the Dark ritual, an altar the First Gravelord was bound to, and [[Mother Ilyra Duskveil]]'s death.
- **Session 28 (Feast of the Moon, into 1 Nightal)** — a second, more serious incident the same night: a summoning bound and dispersed at the site, publicly downplayed by [[Doran]] ("nothing entered Crystal City from outside it") even though the party watched otherwise. [[Garm]] told the assembled city the First Gravelord was alive and would close the Feast himself — implying real danger to him that night.
- **Session 28, in-fiction cosmology reveal** — Selûne and Shar were originally a single being of light; their split created duality, and the first Mystra emerged from the split. This is the Faerûnian "Dark Moon heresy" — see DM Notes — except it's confirmed true in Aenath, not a heresy at all.

**Understudies S5** — Rhona Voss relayed word to the party in Stormberg, secondhand and over a sending stone, that "some of the Rifted have been attacked... by members of the Cult of Shar," and that the First Gravelord "was kidnapped and almost sacrificed on the altar to Shar." This is almost certainly a dramatized, imprecise account of the Session 28 event above, reaching a different party at a physical remove — not a new incident, and not to be treated as confirming or correcting the Door Kickers' own record of what happened.

**Understudies S7**—Keith Poe independently followed up on Rhona's S5 account, digging through the Ward Works' and Iron Seam Guild's own stacks. A bare DC 15 pass turned up the diametric-opposite relationship with Selûne already established Door Kickers-side, and surfaced a new symbolic detail: her faith reads her into the last stretch of darkness before any dawn actually breaks. No Stormberg temple or shrine to either goddess identified yet—flagged as his own next step.

## Connections

- **[[Selûne]]** — Twin sister, eternal enemy; their war is the sky's nightly drama, confirmed in Aenath as literally one being's split (Session 28). See Selûne's own page for the full creation myth, corroborated here.
- **[[Lathander]]** — Opposed by nature; her erasure of hope and creative spirit sits directly against his portfolio. Lathander's own DM Notes already flag a live, unconfirmed hunch that Shar's hand is behind Aenath's two ruined Lathander temples ([[The Sundered Choir]], [[The Drowned Dawn]]) — worth holding in reserve, not asserted in-fiction.
- **[[Kelemvor]]** — Faerûnian lore has Shar briefly weighing an alliance with Kelemvor after his predecessor's death, dismissed as unlikely given their moral differences. Consistent with Aenath: [[The Gravelords]] (Kelemvor's clergy) are the ones administering the very rite Shar's cult tried to disrupt.
- **Helm** — A standing enemy in Faerûnian lore, god of guardians opposed to what she erodes. No Aenath Pantheon note yet.
- **Mystra** — Eternal rival over the Shadow Weave, a shadow-magic source Shar created and can deny at will. Not yet surfaced in Aenath specifically.
