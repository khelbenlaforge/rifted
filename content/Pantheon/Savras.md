## Description

Savras is the god of divination and fate — a man with a crystalline face, clear to anyone who looks at him, who says exactly what is true and rarely what anyone wants to hear. Few worship him outright. Investigators, diviners, judges, and anyone with a professional need to uncover the truth pray to him instead, for small rituals of foresight. His only famous named servant in the wider Realms was Alaundo of Candlekeep, history's one true prophet.

His defining history is a defeat: Azuth bested him in a magical contest and imprisoned him for ages inside the Scepter of Savras. Azuth eventually freed him on condition of fealty, and the staff remains a potent symbol for Savras's faithful to this day. The two gods have settled into an uneasy, gradually more amicable working relationship — Savras still resents the arrangement, but the resentment has cooled into something closer to professional tolerance.

## Role in the Story

Named in Session 26 alongside Azuth by Lea — a lesser deity of magic present in Mystra's temple complex (not the Temple of Kelemvor, a separate location). Like Azuth and Mystra, he keeps no clergy in the traditional sense — devoted magic-users worship him rather than serve as ordained clerics.

## Connections

- **[[Azuth]]** — Conqueror and current superior. Savras serves him, and through him serves Mystra indirectly, following his imprisonment and conditional release.
