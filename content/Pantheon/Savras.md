## Description

Savras is the god of divination and fate — a man with a crystalline face, clear to anyone who looks at him, who says exactly what is true and rarely what anyone wants to hear. Few worship him outright. Investigators, diviners, judges, and anyone with a professional need to uncover the truth pray to him instead, for small rituals of foresight. His only famous named servant in the wider Realms was Alaundo of Candlekeep, history's one true prophet.

Before his ascension, Savras was mortal — a wizard of considerable skill in his own right. His defining history is a defeat: Azuth bested him in a magical contest and imprisoned him for ages inside the Scepter of Savras. Azuth freed him around the Time of Troubles, on condition of fealty, and the staff remains a potent symbol for Savras's faithful to this day. The two gods have settled into an uneasy, gradually more amicable working relationship — Savras still resents the arrangement, but the resentment has cooled into something closer to professional tolerance.

## Worshipers

Savras's clergy is thin and scattered — his shrines are tucked into the corners of libraries and archives rather than raised as proper temples. Devout worshipers carry elaborately decorated staves in his honor, each one hoping their own staff might catch Savras's notice and become a temporary vessel for him, echoing his imprisonment in Azuth's.

The **Benign Order of the Third Eye** is a secretive cult of archivists — the highest concentration of archivists found in any one group — whose members share a vision Savras once impressed on them: gather magic and lore against a coming conflict with a rival, chaos-aligned power. Zeal for the mission has made the Order infamous for graverobbing.

## Role in the Story

Named in Session 26 alongside Azuth by Lea — a lesser deity of magic present in Mystra's temple complex (not the Temple of Kelemvor, a separate location). Like Azuth, he keeps only a sparse, small clergy rather than a proper priesthood — devoted magic-users worship him informally far more often than any of them serve as ordained clerics.

## Connections

- **[[Azuth]]** — Conqueror and current superior. Savras serves him, and through him serves Mystra indirectly, following his imprisonment and conditional release.
