## Description

Savras is the god of divination and fate—a man with a crystalline face, clear to anyone who looks at him, who says exactly what is true and rarely what anyone wants to hear. Few worship him outright. Investigators, diviners, judges, and anyone with a professional need to uncover the truth pray to him instead, for small rituals of foresight.

Savras serves [[Azuth]], and his faithful carry the staff as its sign.

## Worshipers

Savras's clergy is thin and scattered—his shrines are tucked into the corners of libraries and archives rather than raised as proper temples. Devout worshipers carry elaborately decorated staves in his honor, each one hoping their own staff might catch Savras's notice and become a temporary vessel for him.

The **Benign Order of the Third Eye** is a secretive cult of archivists — the highest concentration of archivists found in any one group — whose members share a vision Savras once impressed on them: gather magic and lore against a coming conflict with a rival, chaos-aligned power. Zeal for the mission has made the Order infamous for graverobbing.

## Role in the Story

Like Azuth, he keeps only a sparse, small clergy rather than a proper priesthood — devoted magic-users worship him informally far more often than any of them serve as ordained clerics.
