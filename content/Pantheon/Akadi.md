## Session 28 Update (The Door Kickers)

[[Gond]] hammered it into Aenath's own fabric at the world's making—not destroyed, but given over as a temporary sacrifice, and still able to speak from inside the world it became. Akadi is weakening now, along with [[Grumbar]], [[Kossuth]], and [[Istishia]], all four held inside the astral barrier alongside everyone else.

## Session 8 Update (The Understudies)

[[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/Fern|Fern]]'s own Session 8 vision touched [[Kossuth]]'s fire figure directly, but felt all four—Akadi among them—holding the same weight at once: *"flame needs rock to lean on, rock needs water it can't hold, water needs air to carry it anywhere at all."*
