## Description
God of thieves, shadows, and the unseen. Mask holds that anything not nailed down — and plenty that is — belongs to whoever's clever and quiet enough to take it. Worshipped in secret by thieves' guilds and criminal syndicates who leave no public shrine, only a black mask etched somewhere only the faithful would recognize it.

## Followers & Influence
Thieves, spies, smugglers, and criminal organizations that value discretion above all else. In [[Crystal City]], honored privately within the [[The Claret]]'s upper tiers rather than through any temple.
