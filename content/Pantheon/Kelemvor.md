## Description

Kelemvor is the god of death and the dead, the most recent deity to hold the office, and by most accounts the fairest. Unlike the cruel and capricious lords of death who came before him, Kelemvor's whole theology rests on one idea: death is a natural part of life, understood and met with dignity rather than feared or dreaded as punishment. He judges souls and forwards them on, chaotic evil to the Abyss, lawful evil to the Nine Hells, neutral or godless souls onward to whatever waits beyond, refusing to hoard them the way his predecessors did. He opposes undeath in every form, treating it as a fundamental violation of the order he keeps.

His church in [[Crystal City]] runs three factions under one roof: the Monks of Long Death, the general Priesthood, and the **Kelemvor Knights** — paladins tasked specifically with guarding cross-planar travel and preventing souls from being pulled across planes without sanction. The Church of Kelemvor feuds openly with the churches of [[Mystra]] and [[Oghma]] over a real philosophical disagreement: Kelemvor's clergy holds that knowledge pursued without responsibility is exactly what destabilizes the cosmos.

Initiative rolled before anything more was learned. **Note the Gravelord is a temple leadership title, not the god himself.** Keep that distinction clear in play and in future notes, given how easily "Kelemvor" already collides with itself (see DM Notes).
