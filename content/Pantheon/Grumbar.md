Grumbar is the primordial demigod of earth and one of the four elemental spirits. [[Gond]] hammered it into Aenath's own fabric at the world's making—not destroyed, but given over as a temporary sacrifice, and still able to speak from inside the world it became. Grumbar is weakening now, along with [[Akadi]], [[Kossuth]], and [[Istishia]], all four held inside the astral barrier alongside everyone else. [[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/Fern|Fern]]'s own Session 8 vision put her hand against the earth figure specifically, but felt all four holding the same weight at once: *"flame needs rock to lean on, rock needs water it can't hold, water needs air to carry it anywhere at all."*
