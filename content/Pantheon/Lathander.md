## Description
Lathander, the Morninglord, is the god of dawn, birth, renewal, and new beginnings. Every sunrise is, in a small way, his. He is honored wherever people mark a fresh start: a birth, a first harvest, a rebuilt town, the first day of spring. His faithful hold that nothing broken is beyond mending and no fall is beyond a second sunrise.

When he takes form, he appears as a handsome, slender, muscled young man in robes of red, pink, and gold, a sash at his waist, golden sandals that scorch faint marks into the ground he walks on. He doesn't simply appear so much as arrive, in a burst of dawn light, and his face is said to darken like a solar eclipse when he's genuinely angry. He's relentlessly optimistic and easy to like, quicker to focus on what's next than to dwell on what just went wrong. That same youthfulness is also his flaw—vanity mostly, a zealot's habit of charging a problem head-on without weighing what it costs. His faithful are told to forgive that in him the same way they'd forgive it in themselves.

## Worship & Clergy

Lathanderian novices are called the Awakened; full clergy are Dawnbringers, who take on a personal-use or ceremonial name once the god is said to have personally recognized them. Ceremonies run at dawn, since deals and journeys begun at sunrise are considered blessed, and funerals run the opposite way: held at dusk, with a wake lasting until the next morning arrives. His clergy prize art, self-betterment, and hope, and have little patience for undead or for people who let evil sit unchallenged out of inertia. Favored sayings: *"From death, life,"* and *"There is always another dawn."*

## Relationships

Chauntea is his long-standing companion; [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Gond|Gond]], [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Tymora|Tymora]], Tyr, Torm, [[Ilmater]], [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Sune|Sune]], [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Selûne|Selûne]], [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Oghma|Oghma]], Kelemvor, and [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Mielikki|Mielikki]] count as allies. His clearest enemy is Talos. More relevant to this campaign is **Shar**, goddess of darkness and loss, whose worshippers work directly against everything dawn and renewal stand for.

## Session 1 Update (The Understudies)

Confirmed, on 29th Uktar Year 222 PA, as the original patron of [[The Sundered Choir]]—a previously undiscovered, abandoned temple to Lathander, torn loose from the ground and left drifting for longer than living memory. No worship of Lathander otherwise surfaced in Aenath until Session 5 (below); whether the temple predates the [[The Awakening|Awakening]] or arrived some other way is an open question.

## Session 5 Update (The Understudies)

Priya's own theology, as given in play, centers entirely on beginnings. His church abhors undeath and desecration the same way Kelemvor's does. It stops there. Where a soul goes after death isn't his church's concern. She and [[Brother Silas]] ([[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Diamond District/Temple of Kelemvor|Temple of Kelemvor]], same session) confirmed the jurisdictional line from their own sides: Lathander cannot poach a soul that hasn't pledged to him first.
