## Description

Corellon is the patron deity of all elves: creator, preserver, and eternal leader of the **Seldarine**, the elven pantheon. Their portfolio covers everything elves hold highest, magic, music, art, craft, poetry, and warfare, all at once. Genuinely genderfluid ("alternatively male or female, both or neither"), Corellon conventionally takes the form of an androgynous, preternaturally beautiful elf warrior-artist, equally at home leading a battle or losing themselves for a decade in a single piece of art. **Session 26 named them explicitly as "they/them"** at the shrine array, consistent with this. Their realm, Arvandor, is a place of deep forests and fey beauty on the plane of Arborea.

Corellon is not a simple, uncomplicated hero. Their mercy has limits, their pride has bordered on arrogance, and their early history includes real cruelty toward non-elves before the wider cosmos — and their consort Sehanine — taught them otherwise. Their oldest, deepest enmity is with **Gruumsh**, the orc god whose eye they took in single combat; their newer, more personal betrayal came from their own former wife, **Araushnee** (now Lolth), whose treachery cost the Seldarine a war and cost them, for a time, everything.

## Role in the Story

Corellon appears in Session 26 only as part of the shrine array Terra finds herself drawn to — a Parthenon-styled row of elven-god statues, Corellon and Sehanine at the center, hundreds of celebrants dancing at their feet on the Feast night. They aren't yet a character in this campaign; they're context for Sehanine's much more directly relevant appearance this session.
