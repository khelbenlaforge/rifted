Kossuth is the primordial demigod of fire and one of the four elemental spirits. [[Gond]] hammered it into Aenath's own fabric at the world's making—not destroyed, but given over as a temporary sacrifice, and still able to speak from inside the world it became. Kossuth is weakening now, along with [[Akadi]], [[Grumbar]], and [[Istishia]], all four held inside the astral barrier alongside everyone else.

[[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/Fern|Fern]]'s own Session 8 vision put her hand directly against Kossuth specifically: touching the fire figure "was like touching every fire she'd ever catalogued at once: careful where it wanted to be careless, banked where it wanted to spread." All four figures spoke through the contact—*"flame needs rock to lean on, rock needs water it can't hold, water needs air to carry it anywhere at all"*—before the dream carried her to the moment of the forging itself. She woke Kossuth's own vision-bearer, and days later found the god's own temple in [[Stormberg]] on instinct alone, only to be banned from it outright after a stray sneeze snuffed one of its sacred flames.
