Nuitari is the third of the Dragonlance-derived demigods sworn to [[Mystra]] as her vassals—literal moons, not a figure of speech for them. Aenath's astral barrier holds Mystra outside the world; unable to reach it directly, she took a kneeling vow from Nuitari, [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Solinari|Solinari]], and [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Lunitari|Lunitari]] in exchange for their own, and let all three become the moons that anchor the Weave for mortal spellcasters. She wept while she knelt back to them. Visible in Aenath's sky only on the [[Feast of the Moon]], alongside [[Selûne]]'s own moon.
