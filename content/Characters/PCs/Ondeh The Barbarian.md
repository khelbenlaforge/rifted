### Features

- **Unarmored Defense** — AC = 10 + DEX mod (+2) + CON mod (+3) = 15 while unarmored.
- **Weapon Mastery** — Assign mastery to 3 weapons (= Prof Bonus); swap on Long Rest.
  - Greataxe *(Cleave)*: on hit, free extra melee attack vs a different creature within 5 ft of the original target (no ability modifier added to bonus damage unless negative); 1×/turn.
  - Handaxe *(Vex)*: on hit, adv. on your next attack roll vs the same target before end of your next turn.
  - Dwarven Thrower *(Push)*: on hit, can push target up to 10 ft straight away (Large or smaller only); no save required.
- **Reckless Attack** — Before making a STR-based attack on your turn: gain adv. on all STR-based attacks this turn; all attacks against you have adv. until start of your next turn.
- **Danger Sense** — Adv. on DEX saves vs effects you can see while not Blinded, Deafened, or Incapacitated.
- **Frenzy** — While Raging + using Reckless Attack: first hit on your turn deals extra 2d6 damage (same type as weapon); 1×/turn. *(Rage Damage at L5 = +2 → Frenzy = 2d6. Increases to 3d6 at L9.)*
- **Primal Knowledge** — Proficiency in Acrobatics. While Raging: use STR modifier for Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival checks *(Acrobatics becomes STR +6 while Raging instead of DEX +5)*.
- **Extra Attack** — 2 attacks when taking the Attack action.
- **Fast Movement** — Speed +10 ft when not wearing Heavy armor (30 ft → 40 ft).
- **Charger** (General Feat, L4) — +1 STR included in scores above. Improved Dash: Dash action grants +10 ft speed. Charge Attack: move 10+ ft straight toward target then hit with a melee attack → +1d8 to that attack's damage OR push target up to 10 ft (if ≤ one size larger); 1×/turn.
- **Strike of the Giants — Hill Strike** (Background Feat) — On hit with a melee or thrown weapon attack (1×/turn): +1d6 damage of the weapon's type; target makes **STR 14** save or gains the Prone condition. 3×/LR.
- **Halfling Lucky** — Roll a 1 on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw: reroll and must use the new result.
- **Brave** — Adv. on saving throws against the Frightened condition.
- **Halfling Nimbleness** — Can move through the space of any creature that is a larger size.
- **Naturally Stealthy** — Can take the Hide action even when only obscured by a creature at least one size larger than you.
