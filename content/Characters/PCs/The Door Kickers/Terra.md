## Description

Terra is small even for Small ancestry — four feet of compact stillness, the kind that comes not from restraint but from a body that learned to occupy as little space as possible in a world of low ceilings and narrow passages. Her skin has the particular dullness of stone that has never dried out, the shade of someone who has never been adequately sunlit. Her movements are deliberate rather than fast. There is something careful about how she takes up space in the open air, as if she is still calibrating how much of it is hers to use.

Her eyes carry the faint luminous quality of celestial heritage, though it is easy to miss — she tends not to hold extended eye contact, not from shyness but from the habit of someone whose keenest senses are not her eyes. She listens more than she looks. She orients by sound and scent in a way that registers, in crowds, as a particular kind of alertness that reads as unease. She is not unaware. She is aware of different things.

She moves in cycles that do not match the world above. The rhythm she follows — when she sleeps, when she is most present — is the rhythm of a place without a sun, and she has not fully adjusted. Probably never will.

There is an otherworldly quality to her that has nothing to do with how she was raised. Fey mirth and fey fury both live in her, though she doesn't have names for them yet — only the occasional flash of something too bright in her expression when something strikes her as funny, and something too cold when it doesn't.

## Origin

Terra has no surname. In the community, surnames were considered attachments to a world that was already dissolving — what mattered was what you were becoming, not where you came from.

She was born into the group already occupying the old Cerro Gordo Mines in the Inyo Mountains, and the mines were all she knew for the first twenty-five years of her life. The community believed that the physical world was a membrane — permeable, if you knew where to press. A spiritual realm existed alongside it, not above or below but adjacent, and those who learned to move between both could achieve something the rest of the world had forgotten was possible: genuine immortality. Not metaphorical. Not legacy. The actual continuation of the self, indefinitely, in a form the physical body was only preparation for.

The Unbound was proof this was possible. A man who appeared to be in his early forties and never seemed to age, who arrived always in a suit that looked freshly pressed regardless of where he had been, who spoke about the other realm the way you might describe a room you knew well. Terra did not know everything about him. The community was structured around not needing to know — The Unbound understood; the work was to become capable of understanding for yourself.

The work was the mines. Deep rock, particular resonances, the way sound moved differently at certain depths. The practice was contemplative, repetitive, and isolating by design, and Terra found it easier than most. She was already disposed toward stillness, toward the keener senses that came from learning to read an environment without the shorthand of good light. She was, by all internal measures, doing well.

Then the town was sold.

The businessman who bought Cerro Gordo was not interested in the community. He was interested in the property. Legal mechanisms, trespass notices, a timeline that did not accommodate appeals. The community dispersed over a matter of weeks. The Unbound, in the chaos of it, disappeared — no farewell, no instruction, no explanation. Just absence where he had been.

The five years that followed were not gentle. The ridicule that came — journalists, podcasters, the particular cruelty of people who find spiritual sincerity inherently funny — was something Terra had no framework for. She had grown up inside a coherent worldview, and that worldview was now publicly held up as evidence of stupidity or delusion or abuse. She did not entirely know which it was. She knew she had experienced something real — the resonances, the practices, what the community had built together — and she also knew that she had emerged from a hole in the ground at twenty-five with no skills for navigating a surface world that found her baffling and that she found exhausting.

She was in a shelter in California when the light took her.

## Relationships

- **The Unbound** — Cult leader. Appeared to be in his forties, suited, ageless. Disappeared during the dispersal. Terra does not know if he abandoned them, if something happened to him, or if his disappearance was itself intentional. She is here, partly, because she needs to know.
- **The community** — Dispersed. She does not know where most of them are. She was not close to many of them individually — the practice was not especially communal in the intimate sense — but their dissolution is the shape of the void she carries.
- **The world above** — Five years of trying to exist in it, badly. She understands it better now than she did. She still finds it loud in ways that are not about sound.

## Key Events

- Raised underground in the Cerro Gordo cult community in the Inyo Mountains, California
- Forced to the surface at age 25 when the mining town was sold
- The Unbound vanished; the cult dissolved and was publicly ridiculed
- Five years living on the surface; ended up in a homeless shelter in California
- Pulled into Aenath at age 30

### Features

**Aasimar**
- **Celestial Resistance** — Resistance to Necrotic and Radiant damage.
- **Darkvision** — 60 ft.
- **Healing Hands** — Magic action: touch a creature and roll a number of d4s equal to your Proficiency Bonus; target regains that many HP. 1/LR.
- **Light Bearer** — Know the *Light* cantrip. CHA is the spellcasting ability.
- **Celestial Revelation** — At character level 3, transform as a Bonus Action 1/LR (choose each time). Lasts 1 minute or dismissed. Once per turn on a hit (attack or spell), deal extra damage equal to PB (type varies by form):
  - *Heavenly Wings* — Radiant extra damage; gain Fly Speed equal to Speed.
  - *Inner Radiance* — Radiant extra damage; shed Bright Light 10 ft. / Dim Light 10 ft.; creatures within 10 ft. take PB Radiant at end of each of Terra's turns.
  - *Necrotic Shroud* — Necrotic extra damage; creatures other than allies within 10 ft. must succeed on a CHA save (DC 8 + CHA mod + PB) or be Frightened until end of Terra's next turn.

**Ranger (Levels 1–3)**
- **Favored Enemy** — *Hunter's Mark* always prepared; cast without a spell slot 3×/LR.
- **Spellcasting** — WIS-based. See spell slots below.
- **Weapon Mastery** — Mastery property with two chosen weapons; swap on Long Rest.
- **Deft Explorer** — Expertise in one Ranger skill; one additional language.
- **Fighting Style** — TBD (player's choice).

**Fey Wanderer (Level 3)**
- **Dreadful Strikes** — On a weapon hit, deal an extra 1d4 Psychic damage. A given target can take this extra damage only once per turn.
- **Fey Wanderer Spells** — *Charm Person* always prepared. Additional spells unlock at higher levels.
- **Otherworldly Glamour** — Add WIS modifier (min +1) to all CHA checks. Gain proficiency in Deception, Performance, or Persuasion (player's choice).
