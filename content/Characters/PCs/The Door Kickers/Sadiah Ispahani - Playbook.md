## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first, before anything else:**
- **22 HP and AC 15.** She is a glass cannon with no healing on her sheet at all. Getting hit twice is a crisis.
- **About to be hit →** **Shield** (Reaction, +5 AC to **20**, including against the triggering attack, 1st)
- **Needs to break contact →** **Cunning Action** (BA): Disengage, then move 35 ft. She outruns nearly everything.
- **An ally is about to be hit by something that landed →** **Silvery Barbs** (Reaction, 60 ft., force the successful roll to be rerolled, and grant advantage to another creature, 1st)
- **Rolled a 1 on anything →** **Junoon's Word** ([[Sukoon and Junoon]], 1/SR): reroll any d20 Test, use the new result

**Action:**
- **Default, with Sneak Attack live →** **Attack action, Sukoon**, M +8, 5 ft., 1d4+6. See the Sneak Attack stack below — the sickle's damage die is almost irrelevant next to what rides on it.
- **Enemy at range, want the next hit guaranteed better →** **Shortbow** *(Vex)*, R +6, 80/320 ft., 1d6+4: on a hit, her **next** attack against that target has advantage, which is Sneak Attack enabled by itself.
- **Two targets, or a thrown option →** **Dagger** *(Nick)*, M/R +6, 20/60 ft., 1d4+4. Nick folds the light-weapon extra attack into the Attack action.
- **One target has to fail badly →** **Chromatic Orb** (R +4, 90 ft., 3d8 of a type she chooses, 1st) or **Color Spray** (Self, 15-ft. cone, 6d10 HP pool of blinding, 1st)
- **Free damage, no slot →** **Fire Bolt** (R +4, 120 ft., 1d10 fire) or **Sword Burst** (DEX DC 12, 5-ft. radius, 1d6 force to everything adjacent)
- **Needs to not be recognised →** **Disguise Self** (Self, 1 hr, 1st)

**The Sneak Attack stack — this is her whole damage engine:**
1. **Sneak Attack 2d6**, once per turn, on a Finesse or Ranged hit, with advantage **or** an ally within 5 ft. of the target and no disadvantage
2. **+1d6** if she used **Steady Aim** before attacking with Sukoon or Junoon *(Still Point)*
3. **+2** flat, her Intelligence modifier *(The Named Weight)*
4. **The Named Weight** also forces a Constitution save against her **spell save DC, currently 12** or the target **cannot take Reactions** until the start of its next turn, which shuts off anything reaction-gated it might have used
5. **Debt Paid** *(1/day, Tier 2)*: immediately after Sneak Attack damage, she silently names one person she has ever stolen for. Target makes **WIS DC 12** or is **Frightened of her** until the end of its next turn.

**Bonus Action** (her hardest decision — four things want it and she gets one):
- **Needs advantage and has no ally adjacent →** **Steady Aim**. **Still Point** means it does **not** drop her Speed to 0: she still moves up to half her Speed, and adds **+1d6** to Sneak Attack if she attacks with a sickle.
- **Needs out, or needs to be hidden →** **Cunning Action**: Dash, Disengage, or Hide
- **Hand isn't deployed yet →** **Mage Hand Legerdemain** (30 ft., invisible, cast/control)
- **Hand is already out and holding a sickle →** **The Invisible Third** *(Tier 2)*: make her off-hand attack **from the Hand's location** instead of her own, at her normal attack modifier, 1d4+2. Sneak Attack applies if its conditions are met **from the Hand's position**, and the Hand can be up to 30 ft. away.

**Reaction** (three options, one Reaction):
- **Shield** (+5 AC, and no damage from *Magic Missile*)
- **Silvery Barbs** (60 ft., reroll someone's success, hand an ally advantage)
- **Junoon's Word** (1/SR, reroll her own natural 1)

**Movement (35 ft.):**
- **Fastest PC in the party.** Wood Elf 35 ft., and *Longstrider* takes it to 45 ft. for an hour.
- **Darkvision 60 ft.**, and note her **Stealth is only +4**: she has no proficiency in it despite being the rogue, for a Passive Stealth of 14. She gets into position by being fast and by being somewhere nobody was watching, which is a different skill from being quiet.

**Resource tiering, at a glance:**
- **At-will:** Sukoon / Junoon (M +8), Shortbow (R +6), Dagger (M/R +6), all six cantrips, Cunning Action, Steady Aim, Mage Hand Legerdemain. Sneak Attack, Still Point and The Named Weight all ride free on hits.
- **Spell slots: 1st ×3 only.** Chromatic Orb, Color Spray, Disguise Self, Longstrider, Shield, Silvery Barbs all draw on the same three. Shield alone can eat the whole day.
- **1/Short Rest:** Junoon's Word. **1/day:** Debt Paid. **1/LR free:** *Longstrider* (Wood Elf), plus one 1st-level Wizard spell from Magic Initiate *(the sheet doesn't record which of her listed spells is the feat pick — worth settling, since it's effectively a free 4th slot)*.

**Comparative Notes:**

- *The Bonus Action bottleneck is her defining constraint.* Steady Aim, Cunning Action, deploying Mage Hand and The Invisible Third's off-hand swing all compete for one Bonus Action, and **the sickles carry no weapon mastery** (her masteries sit on Dagger and Shortbow), so unlike Nick, the off-hand sickle swing genuinely costs the Bonus Action. The Invisible Third also only works if the Hand is *already* deployed, which cost a Bonus Action on a previous turn. **Treat the Hand as a round-one investment that pays out on rounds two onward, not something to set up mid-fight.**
- *Steady Aim vs. The Invisible Third, once the Hand is out.* Steady Aim buys advantage (which enables Sneak Attack outright) plus +1d6 via Still Point, and Still Point means she keeps half her movement. The Invisible Third buys a second attack from up to 30 ft. away, which enables Sneak Attack **from the Hand's position** — meaning an ally standing next to the Hand's target counts. **Steady Aim when she has no positional help; The Invisible Third when the Hand is somewhere her body can't safely be.**
- *Shield vs. Silvery Barbs, both Reactions, both 1st-level slots, only three slots total.* Shield is +5 AC and applies to the attack that triggered it, so she knows it might work before spending. Silvery Barbs forces a reroll of a success that has already landed and hands an ally advantage, so it can undo a critical hit or a failed save that Shield could never touch. **Shield to keep herself upright; Silvery Barbs when someone else's success is the bigger disaster.**
- *Debt Paid vs. just taking the damage, once per day.* Debt Paid costs no action at all, since it rides on a Sneak Attack she was already making. Its only real cost is that it's once per day and keyed to **WIS DC 12**, which is low and will often be saved. **Spend it early on something whose next turn genuinely matters rather than saving it for a perfect moment; a DC 12 is not a save-it-for-the-boss button.**

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Needs hands somewhere her body can't go → **Mage Hand Legerdemain** *(RAW: 30 ft., **invisible**, and she can use thieves' tools and make Sleight of Hand checks through it)*. This is the best infiltration tool in the party: picking a lock, lifting a key, or triggering a mechanism from across a room without ever entering it.
- Locks, traps, mechanisms → **Thieves' Tools** proficiency plus **Sleight of Hand +6**, deliverable at range through the Hand
- Needs to threaten someone and be believed → **Intimidation +7** *(expertise)*. Her highest social number, and it matches her temperament: she says the thing, directly, at the wrong moment.
- Climbing, tumbling, squeezing, landing → **Acrobatics +8** *(expertise)*
- Needs to be someone else → **Disguise Self** *(RAW: illusory appearance change, 1 hr, 1st-level slot)*, backed by **Costume ×2**, **Performance +5** and the Entertainer background. She ran the [[The Low Flagon]] recon disguised rather than stealthed, which is the right read of her sheet.
- Needs to cover ground → **Longstrider** *(RAW: +10 ft. Speed for 1 hour; castable 1/LR without a slot via Wood Elf lineage, and with slots after that)*, taking her to 45 ft.
- Needs to talk to another rogue, or leave a message only one will read → **Thieves' Cant** *(RAW: secret cant and cipher system)*
- Needs to rest but not be unconscious → **Trance** *(RAW: a Long Rest in 4 hours of trancelike meditation, and she stays conscious throughout)*. She can take a watch and a long rest at once — genuinely valuable and easy to forget.
- Someone tries to charm her → **Fey Ancestry** *(RAW, XPHB: advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed condition — the 2024 version grants no sleep immunity, confirmed via a live 5etools lookup)*
- Dark rooms → **Darkvision 60 ft.**, plus **Dancing Lights** *(RAW: four lights, 120 ft., movable 60 ft., Conc.)* when she wants light somewhere she isn't
- Needs to talk across a room silently → **Message** *(RAW: cantrip, 120 ft., the target may reply)*
- Small repairs, weather-reading, snuffing or lighting a flame → **Druidcraft** *(RAW: minor nature effect, predict weather, 30 ft.)*
- Wilderness or tracking work → **Survival +4** *(proficient via Keen Senses)*, which is easy to overlook on a city rogue
- Busking her way into somewhere, or covering a job with a set → **Performance +5** plus proficiency in **Drum, Flute and Viol** (Entertainer background; all three granted by DM ruling rather than the usual single pick)
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — using Mage Hand to manipulate something at a distance that isn't quite an object-carrying task: tugging a moon-thread, testing a surface, drawing attention to a far corner → **Mage Hand** *(RAW printed limit: manipulates objects, opens containers, carries up to 10 lb, 30 ft., 1 min; it cannot attack or activate magic items)*. She already used it to pull at moon-threads in Session 26, so the table has precedent.
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW:* leaning on **Debt Paid**'s naming outside combat, as a moment of recognition instead of a fear effect → **Debt Paid** *([[Sukoon and Junoon]], RAW printed trigger: only immediately after she deals **Sneak Attack damage**, WIS DC 12, Frightened until the end of the target's next turn)*. Outside a fight it has no mechanical hook at all. The fiction of it does: they see the weight of what they owe, and that is the strongest character beat on her sheet.
