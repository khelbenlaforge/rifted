# Lea - Playbook

*Companion to [[Lea]]—regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-04. Rerun `pc-playbook` after any `character-sheet-sync` update to keep it current.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first, before anything else:**
- **AC 11. No armour, robe only.** She is hit by almost everything that swings at her. 36 HP does not cover that.
- **About to be hit →** **Shield** (Reaction, +5 AC to **16**, including against the triggering attack, 1st)
- **A hostile caster is casting →** **Counterspell** (Reaction, CON DC 15, 60 ft., 3rd). Flat save against her DC; slot level is irrelevant.
- **Someone rolled a success that ruins the fight →** **Chronal Shift** (Reaction, 30 ft., 2/LR): force a reroll of an attack, check or save. **She decides after seeing whether it succeeded**, and it works on her own rolls too.
- **Hit, or forced to save →** **Elminster's Rebuke** ([[Elminster's Spellbook]], Reaction, CON DC 15, 60 ft., 3d8 force plus disadvantage on the attacker's next attack or save; half on a success, 2nd)
- **Concentration under threat →** **Anchor's Steadiness** gives her **advantage on CON saves to maintain Concentration**. She is far more reliable at holding a spell than her CON +2 suggests.

**Action:**
- **Several enemies clustered →** **Fireball** (DEX DC 15, 150 ft., 20-ft. radius, 8d6 fire, half on save, 3rd)
- **Damage that cannot miss →** **Magic Missile** (120 ft., 3 darts, 1d4+1 force each, auto-hit, 1st)
- **Single target, want a damage type →** **Chromatic Orb** (R +7, 90 ft., 3d8, she picks acid/cold/fire/lightning/poison/thunder, 1st)
- **Talk instead of fight →** **Suggestion** (WIS DC 15, 30 ft., Conc., 2nd)
- **Free damage, no slot →** **Fire Bolt** (R +7, 120 ft., 1d10 fire)

**Borrowed Focus rides on all of it:** once per turn, the **first** creature damaged by any spell or cantrip of hers takes an extra **1d6 force** ([[Elminster's Spellbook]]). It applies to Fire Bolt as readily as to Fireball, which makes her cantrip meaningfully better than it looks — and it stacks onto auto-hitting Magic Missile with no roll involved anywhere.

**Bonus Action:**
- **Wants a slot back on a spell she'd otherwise pay for →** **Elminster's Marginalia** (1/LR): cast a spell from the book of up to **2nd** level with **no slot**. At Tier 2 that reaches Rebuke, Misdirection and Inquiry.
- **Expects to be attacked this round →** **Elminster's Misdirection** (Self, the first attack against her has disadvantage and her first save has advantage, until her next turn, 2nd)
- **An ally needs a lifeline she doesn't have to be adjacent for →** **Elminster's Parting Gift** (touch, a bead lasting 1 hr; the holder spends their own Bonus Action to squeeze it for **3d6+4 HP** or advantage on one save, or throws it 20/60 ft. for **6d6 force**, DEX DC 15 for half, 3rd)
- **Needs out →** **Misty Step** (30 ft., 2nd) or **Expeditious Retreat** (Dash as a Bonus Action, Conc., 10 min, 1st)
- **Reading anything →** **Elminster's Footnote** (touch, cantrip — see Section 3; it is a Bonus Action, so it costs almost nothing mid-scene)
- **At 0 or nearly →** **Potion of Healing** (2d4+2, 1 use)

**Reaction — five options, one Reaction. This is the busiest reaction slot in the party:**

| Option | Trigger | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | She is about to be hit | 1st slot |
| **Counterspell** | A visible creature within 60 ft. is casting | 3rd slot |
| **Silvery Barbs** | Anyone within 60 ft. succeeds at something | 1st slot |
| **Chronal Shift** | Any attack/check/save within 30 ft., hers included | 2/LR, no slot |
| **Elminster's Rebuke** | She is hit or forced to save | 2nd slot |

**Movement (30 ft.):**
- **Stay behind everyone.** At AC 11 the fatal error is letting anything reach her in the first place.
- **Not Quite There** ([[Elminster's Spellbook]]): once per turn, casting a spell **from the book** lets her shift up to 5 ft. without provoking opportunity attacks, as part of the same action. Free disengagement that costs no Bonus Action — and it only triggers on book spells, which is a reason to reach for them.
- **Temporal Awareness** adds her Intelligence modifier (**+4**) to initiative, so she usually acts before the thing that wants to kill her.

**Resource tiering, at a glance:**
- **At-will:** Fire Bolt, Mage Hand, Mending, Prestidigitation, Elminster's Footnote. Borrowed Focus, Anchor's Steadiness and Not Quite There are all passive.
- **Spell slots: 1st ×4, 2nd ×3, 3rd ×2.** Shield and Silvery Barbs both eat 1st-level slots she also wants for Magic Missile.
- **Arcane Recovery (1/LR, after a Short Rest):** recover slots totalling **2 levels** — one 2nd, or two 1st.
- **2/Long Rest:** Chronal Shift. **1/Long Rest:** Elminster's Marginalia, and *Detect Magic* free via Elven Lineage.
- **Rituals cost nothing:** **Ritual Adept** lets her cast Wizard ritual spells straight from her spellbook **without preparing them** — *Comprehend Languages*, *Detect Magic*, *Identify* and *Unseen Servant* are all effectively free utility that never occupies a prepared slot.

**Comparative Notes:**

- *Chronal Shift vs. every slot-costing Reaction.* Chronal Shift is the only one of the five that costs **no spell slot**, and the only one where she decides **after seeing the result**. Shield and Counterspell both commit a slot on a prediction, since she cannot know whether the attack would have hit or what the spell was. Silvery Barbs and Rebuke at least fire on something that has already happened, but each still costs a slot where Chronal Shift costs none. **Default to Chronal Shift twice a day and spend slots on the rest**, and remember it can force a reroll of her *own* failed save, which none of the others can do.
- *Shield vs. Elminster's Rebuke, both triggered by being attacked.* Shield is preventive: +5 AC may make the triggering attack miss outright, and it lasts until her next turn against everything else. Rebuke is retaliatory: the hit lands, then the attacker takes 3d8 force and suffers disadvantage on its next attack or save. **At AC 11, prevention is almost always worth more than retaliation** — reach for Rebuke when she has already been hit anyway, or when the attacker is the one thing that must be slowed down.
- *Magic Missile vs. Chromatic Orb, single target, both 1st level.* Chromatic Orb is bigger (3d8, damage type of her choice) but it is an **attack roll at +7** that can miss outright and take the slot with it. Magic Missile is three darts of 1d4+1 that **cannot miss**, which also means it reliably triggers **Borrowed Focus**'s extra 1d6 force with no roll anywhere in the chain. **Chromatic Orb when she needs a specific damage type or a big number; Magic Missile when the damage simply has to land** — finishing something at low HP, or hitting a target she would otherwise struggle to roll against.
- *Elminster's Marginalia, once per long rest, up to 2nd level.* Its value is entirely in which book spell she spends it on. Rebuke (2nd) is a Reaction she would otherwise pay a 2nd-level slot for; Misdirection (2nd) is defensive; Inquiry (1st) is pure information. **Spending it on Inquiry converts her scarcest daily resource into a question she could have asked with a 1st-level slot instead** — save it for a 2nd-level cast unless the question is genuinely urgent.
- *Fireball vs. holding the 3rd-level slot for Counterspell.* She has exactly **two** 3rd-level slots, and Counterspell, Fireball and Parting Gift all draw on them. **Fireball ends fights; Counterspell prevents ones she'd lose.** Against anything that casts, holding one 3rd back is usually right.

## Loadout Selection

*Wizard prepares 9 spells at level 5 (confirmed against the XPHB Wizard class table) and can swap the whole list after every Long Rest. **Spellbook contents beyond her currently-prepared spells aren't tracked in this vault or in Foundry.** That is a confirmed gap in the tooling, so the suggestions below only reshuffle among spells already listed on the sheet. Expand this manually if you want broader options.*

*Two things sit outside the count and cost her nothing: her four **rituals** (Ritual Adept casts them from the book unprepared), and the **[[Elminster's Spellbook]] entries**, which she prepares from the book itself rather than her own — at Level 5 that is Footnote, Inquiry, Rebuke, Misdirection and Parting Gift.*

**Dungeon / combat-heavy:**
- Keep: *Shield*, *Magic Missile*, *Fireball*, *Counterspell*, *Chromatic Orb*
- Add: *Cloud of Daggers*, *Tasha's Mind Whip* if she wants a second control option — both are 2nd-level and neither needs Concentration to matter
- Drop: *Expeditious Retreat* once the fight is standing still

**Social / urban:**
- Keep: *Suggestion*, *Shield*, *Silvery Barbs*
- Add: *Command*, *Alter Self* (change appearance), *Charm Person* if it's in the book
- Drop: *Fireball*

**Infiltration:**
- Keep: *Misty Step*, *Expeditious Retreat*, *Shield*
- Add: *Alter Self*, *Knock* — neither is currently prepared, and both are close to mandatory for a break-in
- *Elminster's Footnote* is already the best thing she owns for this and costs no prepared slot at all

**Balanced / unknown:**
- *Shield*, *Magic Missile*, *Counterspell* and *Misty Step* are the four that are never wrong. Build the other five around the expedition.

*Her prepared list, confirmed against Foundry, is **Magic Missile · Chromatic Orb · Suggestion · Fireball · Expeditious Retreat · Misty Step · Silvery Barbs · Shield · Counterspell** — exactly 9/9. *Command*, *Sleep*, *Alter Self*, *Knock*, *Cloud of Daggers* and *Tasha's Mind Whip* are all **not prepared**, though they still appear as rows on the vault PC note: that note's action tables show more than she has prepared at any one time, so **Foundry is the authority here, not the sheet**. Her 2014-sourced picks, **Tasha's Mind Whip** (TCE) and **Silvery Barbs** (SCC), are verified real and normal for this table's mixed kit; they just won't appear in a modern-only browse.*

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Anything written, in any language, cipher or magical notation → **Elminster's Footnote** *([[Elminster's Spellbook]], RAW: Bonus Action cantrip, touch any text and instantly read and fully comprehend it regardless of language, script or magical obfuscation; **if the text is deliberately false, she knows it was written to deceive, but not what the truth is**)*. A cantrip, costing a Bonus Action, that reads any document in the world. Unlocked in Session 26.
- A specific yes/no she needs answered → **Elminster's Inquiry** *([[Elminster's Spellbook]], RAW: Action, 1st, one **yes/no** question about a creature, object or location she can see or has touched in the last minute; the Weave answers truthfully in three words or fewer, and **she cannot ask the same question about the same subject twice**)*. She has already used this to surface the Marrow Ledger's redacted client.
- Anything arcane at all → **Arcana +10** *(expertise)*, the highest single skill number in the party, plus **Investigation +7** and **Passive Investigation 17**
- Identifying an item or an active effect → **Identify** and **Detect Magic**, both **rituals she casts unprepared** via Ritual Adept, with *Detect Magic* also free 1/LR via Elven Lineage
- A language nobody speaks → **Comprehend Languages** *(ritual, free)*, and **Footnote** for anything written
- A locked door, chest, or barred gate → **Knock**, **not currently prepared** *(RAW: 60 ft., unlocks/unsticks/unbars **one** lock on the target, suppresses *Arcane Lock* for 10 min, and makes a **knock audible 300 ft. away**. This is not a quiet entry.)*
- Needs hands, labour, or a body double for an hour → **Unseen Servant** *(ritual, free: an invisible servant performing simple tasks, 60 ft., 1 hr)* and **Mage Hand** (30 ft., 10 lb)
- Needs to be someone or something else → **Alter Self** *(RAW: Conc., choose one of aquatic adaptation, change appearance, or natural weapons)* — **not currently prepared**; worth swapping in before an infiltration job
- Something is broken → **Mending** *(RAW: 1 min cast, repairs a single break or tear)*
- Needs a small, deniable physical effect → **Prestidigitation** *(RAW: 10 ft., 1 hr — a harmless sensory effect; light or snuff a candle, torch or small campfire; clean or soil an object up to 1 cubic foot; chill, warm or flavour nonliving material; leave a mark or symbol; or conjure a worthless trinket or hand-sized illusory image lasting until the end of her next turn. Up to three non-instantaneous effects at once.)*
- Needs a room read before she commits to anything → **Insight +5** with **Passive Insight 15**, and **Persuasion +5** / **Intimidation +5** to act on it, plus the **Orzhov Insignia** and Orzhov Representative background
- Religious or planar questions → **Religion +7**, and she is the one who worked out that the book holds a fragment of [[Mystra]]'s essence
- Watch duty without losing rest → **Trance** *(RAW: four hours of trance grants the benefits of a long rest)*
- Charm effects → **Fey Ancestry** *(her sheet gives the 2014 wording: advantage on saves against Charmed **plus** immunity to magical sleep. The XPHB version grants only the Charmed advantage and drops the sleep clause entirely — confirmed via a live 5etools lookup. Worth settling which edition applies, since it decides whether magical sleep works on her.)*
- Dark rooms → **Darkvision 60 ft.**
- A check that must not fail, once per rest → **Chronal Shift** *(RAW, verified via 5etools: it applies to an **ability check** as well as attacks and saves, on herself or any creature within 30 ft., and she chooses after seeing the result)*. Almost always thought of as a combat button; it is equally a "reroll the failed lockpick" button.
- Needs slots back mid-day → **Arcane Recovery** *(RAW: 1/LR following a Short Rest, recover slots totalling 2 levels)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — using **Elminster's Footnote**'s falsehood sense on a contract, a forged writ or a ledger to establish that it is deceptive without knowing how → **Elminster's Footnote** *(RAW printed limit: it tells her the text was **written to deceive**; it does not tell her the truth, identify the forger, or apply to spoken lies)*. Narrow, but it settles "is this document honest" outright.
