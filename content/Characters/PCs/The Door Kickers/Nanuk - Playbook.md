# Nanuk - Playbook

*Companion to [[Nanuk]]—regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-04. Rerun `pc-playbook` after any `character-sheet-sync` update to keep it current.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first, before anything else:**
- **Ally at 0 HP →** **Healing Word** (BA, 60 ft., 1d4+3, 1/LR)
- **Elemental damage incoming →** **Absorb Elements** (Reaction, resist it, next melee hit +1d6, 1st slot)
- **Surrounded or about to drop →** **Hidden Step** (BA, Invisible until his next turn, 1/SR), then walk. Breaks the moment he shoots.
- **Concentrating and taking hits →** CON save is only +2, so every hit is a real chance the spell drops.

**Action:**
- **Default →** **Longbow** *(Slow)*, R +10, 150/600 ft., 1d8+5. Two shots (Extra Attack). Half and three-quarters cover ignored, and no disadvantage at long range (Sharpshooter).
- **Beast already in melee with the target →** same shot, with **advantage** (Coordinated Strike)
- **Want Beast's Strike →** sacrifice one attack: +6, 5 ft., 1d8+5 (+1d6 and Prone **if the target is Large or smaller** when the beast charged 20 ft. straight first). Bonus Action stays free.
- **Deny ground →** **Spike Growth** (150 ft., 20-ft. radius, 2d4 per 5 ft. moved, hidden behind DC 14, Conc., 2nd)
- **Pin a creature →** **Entangle** (STR DC 14, 90 ft., Restrained, repeat save, Conc., 1st)
- **Line of enemies, or gas/fire in the way →** **Gust of Wind** (STR DC 14, Self, 60-ft. line, push 15 ft., Conc., 2nd)
- **Drag someone out of position, ≤30 ft. →** **Thorn Whip** (M +6, 2d6 pierce, pulls Large or smaller 10 ft.). Cantrip.
- **Stuck in melee →** **Scimitar** *(Nick)* + **Shortsword**, both M +8, 1d6+5. Three swings inside the Attack action.

**Bonus Action** (his most contested resource):
- **Opening a fight →** **Mark the Prey** ([[Ancestor's Quiver|Quiver]], 1/LR): *Hunter's Mark*, no slot, **no Concentration**. 90 ft., +1d6 force per hit, 1 hr.
- **Mark the Prey spent →** **Hunter's Mark** (Favored Enemy, 3/LR, no slot, **Concentration**) or a 1st-level slot
- **Beast needs to act and both attacks are committed →** command it here
- **Just hit at range, enemies clustered →** **Hail of Thorns** (DEX DC 14, 1d10 in 5 ft., half on save, 1st)
- **Getting out →** **Hidden Step** (see Triage)

**Reaction:**
- **Absorb Elements**, and nothing else. No decision here, just don't forget it's there.

**Movement (30 ft.):**
- **Hold range.** 150-ft. normal range plus Sharpshooter means closing is rarely necessary.
- **Move the beast, not himself.** 40 ft. walk and climb; parking it in melee turns every shot into advantage.
- **He acts first.** Alert (+5 initiative), and he cannot be surprised via Alert *and* The Bear Watches.

**Resource tiering, at a glance:**
- **At-will:** Longbow / Shortbow (R +10), Scimitar / Shortsword (M +8), Thorn Whip, Message, Unarmed Strike. Arrows are never tracked (Quiver: Inexhaustible Arrows).
- **Spell slots: 1st ×4, 2nd ×2.** These cover Entangle, Hail of Thorns, Absorb Elements (1st) and Find Traps, Gust of Wind, Spike Growth (2nd).
- **Hunter's Mark, three separate sources, not one pool:** Mark the Prey (Quiver, 1/LR, no slot, **no Concentration**), Favored Enemy (3/LR, no slot, **Concentration**), or any 1st-level slot. Four free castings a day, exactly one of which leaves his Concentration open.
- **Special Arrows: 3 charges per Long Rest, shared across all three types** (Tracking / Silence / Cold), chosen as he draws. Not 3 of each.
- **Firbolg Magic: one use of *each* spell per Short Rest, not a shared pool**—Detect Magic 1/SR and Disguise Self 1/SR independently. *(Sourced from a live 5etools lookup of the Firbolg racial trait, not the sheet: the sheet's single "1/SR" line doesn't say whether the two share a use.)*
- **1/Short Rest:** Hidden Step. **1/Long Rest:** Healing Word, Mark the Prey.

**Comparative Notes:**

- *Commanding the beast: sacrifice an attack vs. spend the Bonus Action.* Both work. Note the vault house rule ([[Beast Master - Primal Companion Initiative]]) **overrides the XPHB default here**: RAW an uncommanded beast may only Dodge, but under the house rule it takes its own turn at Nanuk's initiative count and acts at the DM's discretion — attacking, moving, dodging or disengaging on instinct. Commanding it is rewarded, never required. Sacrificing one attack costs him a longbow shot at +10 but keeps the Bonus Action for Hunter's Mark, Hail of Thorns, or Hidden Step. Spending the Bonus Action keeps both shots but locks out everything else that turn. **Round one, spend the attack** so the Bonus Action can go to Mark the Prey; after the mark is up, the Bonus Action is usually free anyway, so command it there and shoot twice.
- *Mark the Prey (1/LR) vs. Favored Enemy's Hunter's Mark (3/LR).* Identical spell, identical damage. The only difference is Concentration, and it decides the whole turn. Mark the Prey is the **only** version that lets him run Spike Growth, Entangle, or Gust of Wind at the same time as the mark. Burning it on a fight where he never intended to concentrate on anything else throws away the one thing that makes it different.
- *Spike Growth vs. Entangle for area control.* Both Concentration, both 20-ft. areas. Entangle (STR DC 14, 90 ft.) is save-or-nothing on the way in and enemies get a fresh save every turn to break out. Spike Growth (150 ft.) has no save at all: it makes the ground difficult terrain and deals 2d4 per 5 ft. moved through, and it's camouflaged, so enemies must Search and beat DC 14 to notice before walking in. **Default to Spike Growth.** Entangle only earns the slot when a specific creature has to stop exactly where it is standing.
- *Cold arrow vs. the Longbow's Slow mastery.* Both cut the target's speed by 10 ft. Slow lasts until the start of Nanuk's next turn and Cold until the end of the target's next turn, and since the target acts between Nanuk's turns, **both are active across the target's move either way** — the wording differs, the practical effect does not. Slow is free on every longbow hit. **Spending one of three daily arrow charges to duplicate it is pure waste: take Cold for the extra 1d6 cold damage, or against something resistant to piercing, never for the slow.**

## Loadout Selection

*Ranger prepares 6 spells at level 5 (confirmed against the XPHB Ranger class table). **He does not re-prepare the whole list daily.** The XPHB Ranger's Changing Your Prepared Spells reads: "Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace **one spell** on your list with another Ranger spell for which you have spell slots." So this section is not a set of loadouts to swap between; it is a queue of single swaps, one per day.*

*Three grants sit outside the 6 and cost him nothing: **Hunter's Mark** (Favored Enemy — always-prepared spells explicitly don't count against the limit), **Detect Magic / Disguise Self** (Firbolg Magic), and **Thorn Whip / Message / Healing Word** (Magic Initiate (Druid), from the Guide background, which is also why Healing Word reads 1/LR).*

**Currently prepared (6/6):** *Entangle* · *Hail of Thorns* · *Absorb Elements* · *Find Traps* · *Gust of Wind* · *Spike Growth*

**If the next swap should buy the most, in order:**
1. **Gust of Wind → Pass without Trace.** The strongest spell available to him that he doesn't have, and it fixes a party weakness rather than a personal one: +10 Stealth for everyone, stacked on his own +8. Gust of Wind's 60-ft. line rarely has room to matter indoors.
2. **Find Traps → Cure Wounds** *(only when Gill isn't coming)*. Find Traps senses that a trap exists but not where, which is thin; being the party's only healer is thinner.
3. **Entangle → Ensnaring Strike** if he wants single-target control that keys off a hit rather than a save he has to land at range.

**Heading somewhere specific — one swap per Long Rest, so plan the order across however many nights you have:**
- **Wilderness / travel:** *Longstrider*, *Goodberry*, or *Locate Animals or Plants* — one of them, not all three
- **Urban / infiltration:** *Pass without Trace* first, then *Alarm* the following day
- **Dungeon:** *Silence* or *Cure Wounds*

*Candidates pulled from the full Ranger spell list at levels 1–2 via `query_5etools.py`. **Absorb Elements** is a Ranger spell in its XGE printing with no 2024 reprint — verified, and normal for this table's mixed kit; it just won't surface in a modern-only browse, so filter for it deliberately.*

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to track something across bad ground, days old → **Survival +9** *(expertise via Deft Explorer)*. Nothing else on his sheet comes close to that number. *(Note: his **Favored Enemy** grants only the slotless Hunter's Mark castings — it gives him no tracking or recall bonus. Terra's version of the feature does; his does not.)*
- Need to find a marked quarry again once it has moved → **Hunter's Mark** *(RAW, confirmed via a live 5etools lookup: advantage on any Wisdom (Perception or Survival) check made to find the marked creature. His sheet's compressed row omits this clause — Terra's lists it — but it is the same spell.)*
- Need to find a specific creature or person again later, through walls, for an hour → **Tracking arrow** *(RAW: the target is revealed through walls and obstacles for 1 hour; one of 3 shared Quiver charges/LR)*. The best surveillance tool anyone in the party has, and it's routinely forgotten because it's filed under "arrows."
- Need someone to stop shouting, right now, without killing them → **Silence arrow** *(RAW: CON save vs. DC 14 (8 + PB + WIS mod) or Silenced until the end of its next turn)*. Stops an alarm being raised or a spell with a Verbal component.
- Scouting a route, a shaft, or a rooftop he can't reach → **Primal Companion**, and specifically **which form he picked at his last Long Rest**. Land: 40 ft. walk and 40 ft. climb, darkvision 60 ft. Sea: 60 ft. swim, amphibious, darkvision 90 ft. Sky: 60 ft. fly, Flyby (no opportunity attacks when it flies out of reach). All three understand the languages Nanuk knows.
- Know tomorrow's job involves water, height, or a climb → **reselect the beast's form on the Long Rest before it**, not after arriving. This is a planning decision that gets made by default rather than deliberately, and it's free.
- Need to get somewhere unseen → **Stealth +8** with **Passive Stealth 18**, opened or bailed out with **Hidden Step** *(RAW: Invisible until the start of his next turn or until he attacks or casts, 1/SR)*
- Need to be a different person for up to an hour → **Disguise Self** *(RAW: illusory appearance change, 1 hr, 1/SR without a slot via Firbolg Magic; the Firbolg version also lets him appear up to 3 feet shorter or taller—sourced from a live 5etools lookup of the racial trait, not on the sheet)*. He and Sadiah already ran the Low Flagon recon disguised.
- Need to talk to someone across a room, a wall, or a crowd without anyone hearing → **Message** *(RAW: cantrip, 120 ft., the target may reply; 1 round)*. At-will, unlimited, and the party's cheapest covert comms.
- Walking into a room that feels wrong → **Find Traps** *(RAW: 120 ft., senses the presence of any trap in line of sight and its general danger, but **not** its location)*. It tells him a room is trapped without telling him where; pair it with someone who can actually find the thing.
- Wondering whether an object or a room is magical at all → **Detect Magic** *(RAW: Self, 30-ft. radius, 10 min, 1/SR without a slot; identifying a specific aura's school takes a Magic action)*
- Ambush prep, or making a camp genuinely defensible overnight → **Spike Growth** *(RAW: 20-ft. radius of difficult terrain, 2d4 per 5 ft. moved through, camouflaged behind a DC 14 check, Conc.)*
- A fire needs putting out, or gas is filling a space → **Gust of Wind** *(RAW: the sheet's own text—disperses gas and may extinguish small flames)*
- Someone is running and needs to be stopped without being hurt → **Entangle** *(RAW: STR DC 14, Restrained, repeat save)*, or **Unarmed Strike** *(RAW: grapple/shove, DC 12)*, or **Thorn Whip** to drag them 10 ft. back
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW*—using Thorn Whip to snag a rope or a lever instead of a creature → **Thorn Whip** *(RAW printed trigger: a melee spell attack against a **creature**, 30 ft., pulling a Large or smaller target 10 ft. toward him; nothing about objects)*
- Mapping the route back, or selling a map afterward → **Cartographer's Tools** proficiency plus **Survival +9**. He also earned vehicle (riding) proficiency in Session 19.
- Nobody gets the drop on **him** → **Alert** *(RAW: +5 initiative, cannot be surprised)* and **The Bear Watches** *(RAW: cannot be surprised while the Quiver is worn)*. Two independent sources for the same immunity, so the Quiver coming off doesn't cost it. Neither protects the rest of the party.
- Handling, calming, or reading an animal → **Animal Handling +6**, backed by **Nature +4** for the ecology behind it
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW*—reading a room's silence, a wrong bird pattern, or weather turning, the way his Description describes him doing on ice → **Perception +6 / Passive Perception 16 / Survival +9**. Play the instinct in the fiction; the roll is what decides how much of it he actually gets, and the DM sets that.
