### Features

- **Weapon Mastery — Dagger** *(Nick)*: Light-weapon second attack folds into your Attack action, freeing the Bonus Action; can swap mastery assignments on a Long Rest
- **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
- **Amorphous:** Can squeeze through spaces as narrow as 1 inch wide without the squeezing penalty; advantage on checks to initiate or escape a grapple
- **Hold Breath:** Can hold breath indefinitely while not incapacitated
- **Natural Resilience:** Resistance to acid and poison damage; advantage on CON saves vs. the poisoned condition
- **Shape Self:** Can reshape or recolor your body at will — adjust apparent height (±half), width, coloration, and add or remove limbs and appendages
- **Tinker's Magic:** As an Action, touch a Tiny nonmagical object to imbue it with minor magic (light, recorded message, odor, or visual effect); up to INT mod (4) active objects at once
- **Replicate Magic Item:** Use infusions to replicate magic items from your known list; 2 simultaneously infused items at level 5 (increases to 3 at level 6)
- **Tools of the Trade:** Proficiency with Heavy Armor, Shields, Thieves' Tools, Tinker's Tools, Smith's Tools, and Woodcarver's Tools
- **Artillerist Spells (always prepared):** Shield, Thunderwave *(from 3rd lvl)* · Scorching Ray, Shatter *(from 5th lvl)*
- **Eldritch Cannon:** Create as an Action; cannon: AC 18, HP 9 (5 + INT mod), immune to poison and psychic. Activate with a Bonus Action on your turn — choose Flamethrower, Force Ballista, or Protector (see Bonus Actions table). Move the cannon up to 15 ft as part of the same Bonus Action. Lasts 1 hr or until destroyed; recreate by expending a 1st+ spell slot
- **Arcane Firearm:** On a Long Rest, inscribe sigils on a wand, staff, or rod to make it your arcane firearm (spellcasting focus). When casting an Artificer spell through it, roll 1d8 and add the result to one damage roll
- **Magic Initiate (Astral Drifter):** Gained additional cantrips and one 1st-level spell from another class's spell list; those spells are always prepared
