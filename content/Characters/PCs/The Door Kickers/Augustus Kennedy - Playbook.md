## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn before anything else:**
- **An ally is down or badly hurt →** **Lay on Hands** (Action, touch, spend any amount up to the 25 HP/LR pool)
- **An ally is poisoned or diseased →** **Lay on Hands — Cure** (BA, touch, 5 HP from the same pool)
- **Augie's about to take a beating and needs the AC now →** **Shield of Faith** (BA, 60 ft, +2 AC 10 min, Conc.)
- **Frightened is loose, or the fight's about to grind him down →** **Heroism** (Action, touch self, +4 temp HP each turn, immune Frightened, Conc., always prepared)

**Action:**
- **Standard turn, in melee →** Extra Attack: two swings with **Longsword** *(Sap)* M +7, 1d8+4 slash (1d10+4 versatile), or **Spear** *(Sap)* M/R +7, 1d6+4 pierce (1d8+4 versatile) — Sap: on hit, target has disadvantage on its next attack roll
- **Need reach without switching weapons →** throw **Spear** (20/60 ft) or **Javelin** *(Slow)* M/R +7, 30/120 ft, 1d6+4 pierce, target Speed -10 ft
- **Need maximum range and accuracy doesn't matter as much →** **Light Crossbow** *(Slow)*, R +2, 80/320 ft, 1d8-1 pierce, target Speed -10 ft — weakest attack bonus in the kit, reach for it only when nothing else can close the distance
- **Undead or fiend in melee reach →** **Holy Water** flask, 5 ft, 2d8 radiant — limited supply, check the flask count before relying on it
- **Single strong target, want the next attacker to land →** **Guiding Bolt** *(Oath)* R +7, 120 ft, 4d6 radiant, always prepared — this replaces the whole Action, not a bonus swing
- **Single humanoid, want it gone without swinging →** **Command**, WIS save DC 15, 60 ft, spends a slot — flee, grovel, or halt
- **Ally needs HP now and the Bonus Action is spoken for →** **Cure Wounds**, touch, 2d8+4 (scales +1d8/slot above L1), spends a slot
- **Extended fight against a specific creature type, ally needs cover →** **Protection from Evil and Good**, touch, 10 min, Conc., spends a slot
- **Before the fight, or before a long ride →** **Find Steed**, 30 ft, summons a celestial/fey/fiend mount, spends an L2 slot
- **Before a check-heavy encounter (a grapple, a shove, a contest) →** **Enhance Ability** *(Oath)* touch, Conc. 1 hr, always prepared — advantage on one ability check type
- **Need to know what's nearby before it's a surprise →** **Divine Sense**, 60 ft, senses celestials/fiends/undead until end of next turn, 5/LR
- **No weapon in hand →** **Unarmed Strike** (Warforged), M +7, 5 ft, 1d4+4 bludgeon

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **Just hit with a weapon, want the damage →** **Divine Smite**, +2d8 radiant (L1 slot, +1d8/higher slot, +1d8 more vs undead/fiends) — spends the same slot pool as Cure Wounds, Command, and the other spells above
- **About to land a big hit and want it to stick →** **Shining Smite**, next weapon hit +2d8 radiant, target sheds light and has disadvantage on Hide checks, CON save each turn to end, Conc., spends a slot
- **Damage over time, no slot to spare →** **Divine Favor**, self, +1d4 radiant on weapon attacks for 1 min, spends a slot (cheapest of the smite-adjacent options)
- **Facing something resistant to nonmagical weapons →** **Magic Weapon** *(Oath)* touch, weapon becomes magical +1, no Conc., always prepared
- **Need to Rage for a single hard swing →** **Toothpick's Rage** — requires wielding Toothpick itself (M +5, 1d8+3 slash, weaker than Longsword), 1/day, 1-in-6 chance (Ashardalon's Fragility) it fails to trigger; exact rage bonuses aren't written down anywhere yet — confirm with the DM before leaning on it
- **A physical feat is on the table (climb, jump, haul, wrestle) →** **Peerless Athlete** *(Channel Divinity)* 1 hr, advantage on Athletics/Acrobatics, carry/push/drag x2, jump x2 — same 2/LR pool as Inspiring Smite below
- **Outmatched and it's worth the risk →** **Activate Brand** *(Kelemvor's Brand)* Speed +10, Keen Smell, +1d8 slash on melee hits; 2 extinction charges/LR — locks out L2+ spells and imposes disadvantage on Persuasion while active, and a WIS DC 12 save each turn or he attacks the nearest visible creature whether that's the right target or not
- **Brand active, just charged 20+ ft straight into a melee hit →** **Brand: Bite** (Pounce only), M +7, 5 ft, 1d6+4 pierce, once the target's already knocked prone
- **Bleeding out, nothing else fast enough →** **Potion of Healing**, self, 2d4+2 HP

**Reaction / no action required:**
- **Just landed a Divine Smite →** **Inspiring Smite** (no action), 30 ft, distribute 2d8 temp HP to self and allies — same 2/LR Channel Divinity pool as Peerless Athlete above, not a second pool

**Movement:**
- Spear and Javelin both reach without abandoning melee — no need to fully commit to ranged or melee for the round.
- Kelemvor's Brand's Pounce needs a straight 20+ ft charge into the hit; positioning for it has to start the turn before.
- Activating the Brand can't be undone by choice at this tier — if the fight looks likely to end without a kill (a surrender, a knockout, a retreat), don't activate it expecting to walk it back mid-fight.

**Comparative Notes:**
- Divine Smite spends the same pool of 6 slots (4 at L1, 2 at L2) that Cure Wounds, Command, Protection from Evil and Good, Shield of Faith, Divine Favor, Find Steed, and Shining Smite all draw from — every point of Smite damage is a slot that isn't there for whoever drops two rounds later.
- Two weapon swings from Extra Attack is the default action; trading the whole Action for Guiding Bolt only pays off when the advantage it hands the next attacker actually turns a miss into a hit — it isn't a bonus on top of the martial routine, it replaces it.
- The Brand's +1d8 slash and +10 ft speed come with a WIS DC 12 save every turn and no voluntary way to switch it off at this tier. A fight that's confidently heading toward a kill is a good use of it; a fight that's likely to end in a surrender or a retreat is not.
- Wielding Toothpick instead of Longsword drops the attack bonus from +7 to +5 and the flat damage bonus from +4 to +3, purely to access a once-a-day Rage whose actual numbers the item note doesn't spell out — worth a table conversation before committing a turn's weapon choice to it, and there's a real chance (roll a 1 on the fragility d6) it does nothing at all.
- Lay on Hands and Cure Wounds both cost the Action, so the real choice is the resource, not the economy: Lay on Hands is a flat, no-roll draw from the 25 HP/LR pool, Cure Wounds spends a slot for a rolled 2d8+4 (more on a higher slot). Lean on Lay on Hands while the pool's flush — switch to Cure Wounds once the pool is running thinner than the party's remaining slots.

## Loadout Selection

*Paladin (2024) prepares from its full class spell list and can swap the entire prepared list on a Long Rest, same as Cleric. The four Oath of Glory spells (Guiding Bolt, Heroism, Enhance Ability, Magic Weapon) are always prepared and don't count against the swappable total — everything below is the other 6 (per the cap; the sheet currently shows 8, see the flag above).*

**Dungeon / combat-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Bless* — party-wide attack/save boost across a multi-fight day; *Warding Bond* — share damage and an AC/save bonus with whoever's taking the most hits
- Keep: *Divine Smite*, *Cure Wounds* — the core damage-and-healing pair

**Social / exploration-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Zone of Truth* — interrogation; *Detect Magic* or *Locate Object* — investigation utility; *Protection from Poison* — travel/wilderness safety net
- Keep: *Command* — still useful for de-escalating a mundane threat, not just combat

**Balanced / unknown expedition type:**
- Default to what's currently prepared — Command, Cure Wounds, Divine Favor, Divine Smite, Protection from Evil and Good, Shield of Faith already spans damage, healing, and utility

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to know if someone's lying or read a room before it turns → **Insight +5**
- Need to physically dominate a negotiation or force compliance → **Intimidation +10** *(expertise)*
- Need to out-muscle an obstacle — climb, break, haul → **Athletics +7**
- Need to move something (or someone) far heavier than normal, or clear a serious gap → **Peerless Athlete** *(RAW: Channel Divinity, BA, 1 hr — advantage on Athletics/Acrobatics, carry/push/drag x2, jump x2 — same 2/LR pool as Inspiring Smite)*
- Need to know if hidden undead, fiends, or celestials are nearby before it goes wrong → **Divine Sense** *(RAW: Action, 60 ft, 5/LR)*
- Need to cure a poison or disease outside a fight → **Lay on Hands — Cure** *(RAW: touch, 5 HP from the pool)*
- Need a mount for a long ride or to cross something a mount could clear → **Find Steed** *(RAW: 30 ft, summons a celestial/fey/fiend steed, spends an L2 slot)*
- Need an edge on one specific skill check before it matters — a hard climb, a puzzle tied to one ability → **Enhance Ability** *(RAW: touch, Conc. 1 hr, always prepared — advantage on one ability check type)*
- Need to hold your own at a table, in a bar, or in a con → **Gaming Set proficiency**
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — flashing it could carry weight as proof of standing with the Guild in a social scene → **Guild Brass Armlet** *(RAW: none stated, pure flavor item)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — talking down a mundane, non-hostile threat (a spooked animal, a panicking crowd) with one flat word → **Command** *(RAW printed trigger: WIS save DC 15 vs. a combat-oriented one-word command)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — warding a scene ahead of trouble, not reacting to a hit already landed → **Protection from Evil and Good** *(RAW printed trigger: touch, 10 min, Conc., wards against specified creature types)*
