## Description
One-shot character. A glamour bard of considerable social grace and fey-touched charm — the kind of person who leaves every room having been both adored and subtly manipulated.

## Origin

## Abilities

### Features

- **Darkvision** — 60 ft.; darkness as dim light, dim as bright.
- **Fey Ancestry** — Advantage on saves vs. Charmed; magic can't put you to sleep.
- **Skill Versatility** (Moon/Sun Elf variant) — Proficiency in two additional skills (baked into Skills section above).
- **Jack of All Trades** — Add +1 (half Prof.) to any ability check not already using proficiency.
- **Expertise** — Deception and Persuasion use double Prof. Bonus (+6); reflected in skill totals.
- **Weapon Mastery — Dagger ×2 *(Nick)*:** The extra Light-weapon attack can be made as part of the Attack action (not a Bonus Action). Two daggers = two attacks in one action; frees BA for Bardic Inspiration or Mantle of Inspiration.
- **Beguiling Magic** (Glamour L3) — Charm Person and Mirror Image are always prepared (don't count against prepared limit). Immediately after casting an Enchantment or Illusion spell using a slot, you can force one visible creature within 60 ft. to make a WIS 16 save or be Charmed or Frightened (your choice) for 1 min; target repeats save at end of each of its turns. 1/LR; restore by expending one Bardic Inspiration use (no action required).
- **Font of Inspiration** (Bard L5) — Regain all Bardic Inspiration uses on a Short or Long Rest.
- **Telepathic** (feat) — Speak telepathically to any visible creature within 60 ft. that shares a language with you. Cast Detect Thoughts once/LR with no spell slot (CHA, DC 16); see Actions.
- **Musician** (Entertainer background) — Tool proficiency in three instruments. Finishing a Short or Long Rest, you can play and grant Bardic Inspiration (d6) to up to 3 allies within 60 ft. who can hear you.
