# Min Ji-ah - Playbook

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Passive, no action needed—already factored into everything below:**
- **Borrowed Focus**—once per turn, the first creature damaged by any spell or cantrip of hers takes an extra 1d6 force. Applies to Fire Bolt as readily as Fireball, and stacks onto auto-hitting Magic Missile with no roll anywhere in the chain.
- **Not Quite There**—once per turn, casting a spell *from Elminster's Spellbook* (Inquiry, Rebuke, Misdirection, Parting Gift, Footnote) lets her shift up to 5 ft. without provoking, as part of the same action.
- **Anchor's Steadiness**—advantage on CON saves to maintain Concentration.
- **Fey Ancestry**—advantage on saves against being Charmed; immune to magical Sleep.
- **Temporal Awareness**—adds her INT modifier (+4) to initiative; she usually acts early.
- **Chronurgy Magic**—her subclass has no 2024 (XPHB) source tag; Chronal Shift and Temporal Awareness above are carried over from her staged 2014-era sheet as-is.

**Triage first, before anything else:**
- **AC 11, robe only, 36 HP.** The fatal mistake is letting anything reach her, not what she does once it has.
- **Ally down at 0 HP →** she has no fast reactive heal. **Potion of Healing** (Action, to another creature, 2d4+2 HP) is the only option, and there's just 1 in inventory. Otherwise: protect the body (Suggestion on the attacker, or fall back) rather than trying to heal through it.
- **She's about to be hit / already hit / forced to save →** see the Reaction table below.
- **Concentrating and under pressure →** already covered—Anchor's Steadiness gives advantage automatically.

**Action:**
- **Several enemies clustered, no allies in the blast →** **Fireball** (DEX DC 15, 150 ft., 20-ft. radius, 8d6 fire, half on save). 3rd-level slot.
- **Damage that cannot miss →** **Magic Missile** (120 ft., 3 darts, 1d4+1 force each, auto-hit). 1st-level slot.
- **Single target, wants a specific damage type or a shot at hitting two →** **Chromatic Orb** (R +7, 90 ft., 3d8, picks the type; leaps to a new target if a damage die matches). 1st-level slot.
- **Needs a target neutralized, not hurt →** **Suggestion** (WIS DC 15, 30 ft., Charmed + follows a brief suggestion, Conc.). 2nd-level slot.
- **Free damage, no slot to spend →** **Fire Bolt** (R +7, 120 ft., 2d10 fire).
- **Everything above is spent and the fight's still on →** **Dagger**, thrown (M/R +4, 20/60 ft., 1d4+1 pierce)—the one ranged option that costs nothing. (Quarterstaff and Unarmed Strike are both strictly worse here—lower attack bonus, no ranged option—so they don't get their own branch.)

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **Wants a 2nd-level cast without paying its slot →** **Elminster's Marginalia** (1/Long Rest): casts a book spell up to 2nd level free—at Tier 2 that reaches Rebuke or Misdirection.
- **Expects to be attacked this round →** **Elminster's Misdirection** (self, first attack against her has disadvantage, her first save has advantage, until her next turn). 2nd-level slot.
- **An ally needs support and can wait a beat to use it →** **Elminster's Parting Gift** (touch, conjures a bead lasting 1 hr.; the holder later spends their own Bonus Action to squeeze it for 3d6+4 HP or advantage on a save, or throws it 20/60 ft. for a 6d6 force burst, DEX DC 15 half). 3rd-level slot. Proactive, not reactive—the bead has to already be in hand before the ally needs it.
- **Needs out, more than once →** **Expeditious Retreat** (self, Dash as a Bonus Action, Conc. 10 min). 1st-level slot.
- **One burst of repositioning, no Concentration to spare →** **Misty Step** (self, teleport up to 30 ft. to a seen unoccupied space). 2nd-level slot.
- **At 0 HP or nearly →** **Potion of Healing** (self, 2d4+2 HP). Only 1 in inventory—last resort, not a free top-up.

**Reaction—five options, one Reaction each round:**

| Option | Trigger | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | She's hit by an attack roll | 1st-level slot |
| **Elminster's Rebuke** | She's hit by an attack, or forced to make a save | 2nd-level slot |
| **Counterspell** | A visible creature within 60 ft. casts (V/S/M) | 3rd-level slot |
| **Silvery Barbs** | Anyone within 60 ft. succeeds at an attack/check/save | 1st-level slot |
| **Chronal Shift** | Any attack/check/save within 30 ft., hers included | 2×/Long Rest, no slot |

**Movement (30 ft.):**
- Hold at 90-120 ft. where the fight allows it—Fire Bolt, Magic Missile, and Chromatic Orb all reach that far, and AC 11 makes melee range genuinely dangerous for her specifically.
- Misty Step or Expeditious Retreat to break contact before repositioning, not after.

**Consumables in reserve (one use each):** Scroll of Mage Armor (1st) and Scroll of Mirror Image (2nd)—cast directly from the scroll, no preparation needed. **Healer's Kit** *(sourced from a live 5etools lookup, not the sheet: XPHB, 10 uses)*—as a Utilize action, spend one use to stabilize an unconscious ally at 0 HP, no roll needed; costs an action and a use, not truly free, but a guaranteed fix once the single Potion of Healing is gone.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Shield vs. Elminster's Rebuke, both trigger off being hit or forced to save (Rebuke covers both cases; Shield only the attack-roll case—+5 AC does nothing against a save).* Shield is cast after the hit is confirmed and can still turn it into a miss retroactively—pure damage prevention. Rebuke doesn't stop that hit's damage at all; instead the attacker makes a CON save, taking 3d8 force plus disadvantage on their next attack or save on a fail, half damage and no disadvantage on a success. Reach for Shield when preventing the damage matters more; reach for Rebuke when the fight's going to run long and slowing the attacker down is worth more than blocking one hit, or whenever the trigger was a save, where Shield can't help at all.
- *Magic Missile vs. Chromatic Orb, both 1st-level, single target.* Magic Missile never misses—three separate 1d4+1 force darts land the moment it's cast, and each one reliably triggers Borrowed Focus's extra 1d6 with no roll anywhere in the chain. Chromatic Orb needs an attack roll to connect at all, but rewards a matching die with a free jump to a second target. Spend Magic Missile when the damage simply has to land; spend Chromatic Orb when a miss is affordable and the shot at a second target is worth the gamble.
- *Chronal Shift vs. the other four Reactions.* It's the only one that costs no spell slot, and the only one that can retarget onto literally any attack/check/save within 30 ft.—hers, an ally's, or an enemy's, not just an attack against her. The other four are narrower but stronger in their own lane. Reach for Chronal Shift when nothing else fits the exact trigger, or when the spell slot matters more than the immediate fight.
- *1st-level slots are the tightest resource on the sheet.* Magic Missile, Chromatic Orb, Expeditious Retreat, Shield, and Silvery Barbs are five different uses drawing on only 4 first-level slots—Fire Bolt is the only zero-cost ranged fallback once they're gone.
- *Elminster's Spellbook spells share her personal slots, not a separate pool.* Rebuke, Misdirection, and Parting Gift draw from the same 2nd/3rd-level slots as Suggestion, Misty Step, Fireball, and Counterspell—the book adds extra spell *options*, not extra casts. Elminster's Marginalia is the one exception, and spending it on a spell she could've cast with a spare slot anyway wastes its only daily use—save it for the 2nd-level casts.

**Between fights:** Arcane Recovery refills 2 spell-slot-levels once per day, on a Short Rest—spend 1st/2nd-level slots a bit more freely early in a dungeon day, knowing a partial refill is coming. Memorize Spell also lets her re-prepare one spell after a Short Rest, not just a Long Rest—same caveat as Loadout Selection below: only useful if she's holding spellbook entries beyond what's shown on the sheet.

Spellbook contents beyond what's currently prepared aren't tracked in this vault or in Foundry—expand this section manually if a broader spellbook becomes relevant.*

*With the cap already full and no bench to draw from, there's no real reshuffle to suggest here—she brings the same 9 into every expedition. The one real lever is which of them she actually casts. For a dungeon-heavy stretch: Magic Missile, Chromatic Orb, Fireball, Shield, and Counterspell do the most work. For a social or investigation-heavy stretch: Suggestion, Silvery Barbs, and the four free ritual spells (Detect Magic and Comprehend Languages especially) carry more of the load than the rest of the list.*
