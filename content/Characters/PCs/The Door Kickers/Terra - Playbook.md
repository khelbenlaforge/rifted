# Terra - Playbook

*Companion to [[Terra]]—regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-04. Rerun `pc-playbook` after any `character-sheet-sync` update to keep it current.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first, before anything else:**
- **She is the most fragile PC in the party.** HP 27, AC 15, **CON save −1**. Two hits is a real death, and every Concentration save is a roll she is favoured to lose.
- **Concentrating and about to take a hit →** consider dropping it. Spike Growth, Ensnaring Strike and Hunter's Mark are all Concentration, and she can only hold one.
- **Cornered in melee →** **Threshold** ([[Hollowstone]], BA, teleport 15 ft. to a space she can see, 1/LR), then **Misty Step** (BA, 30 ft., 2nd) if Threshold is spent
- **Ally at 0 HP →** **Healing Hands** (Action, touch, 3 HP, 1/LR)
- **Elemental damage incoming →** **Absorb Elements** (Reaction, resist it, next melee hit +1d6, 1st)

**Action:**
- **Default →** **Longbow** *(Slow)*, R +8, 150/600 ft., 1d8+3. Two shots (Extra Attack). Half and three-quarters cover ignored (The Gap).
- **Deny ground before enemies cross it →** **Spike Growth** (150 ft., 20-ft. radius sphere, difficult terrain, **2d4 piercing per 5 ft. travelled**, Conc. up to 10 min, 2nd). Anything that couldn't see the area when she cast it must take a **Search action and succeed on a Wisdom (Perception or Survival) check vs. DC 16** to recognise it as hazardous before walking in.
- **One humanoid, want the fight not to happen →** **Charm Person** (WIS DC 16, 30 ft., 1 hr, ends if harmed; always prepared)
- **Stuck in melee →** **Scimitar** *(Nick)* + **Shortsword**, both M +6, 1d6+3. Three swings inside the Attack action.

**The one-arrow stack — every longbow hit, no extra cost:**
1. **Longbow** 1d8+3 pierce
2. **Dreadful Strikes** +1d4 psychic *(1/turn)*
3. **Fey Echo** → that psychic damage leaves the target **Rattled**: disadvantage on its next attack roll *(1/turn, [[Hollowstone]])*
4. **Fault Finding** → target makes **STR DC 16** or is pushed 5 ft. in a direction she chooses *(1/turn, [[Hollowstone]])*
5. **Hunter's Mark** +1d6 force, if it's up

**Bonus Action** (three of these trigger *off a hit she just landed* — they compete directly):
- **Just hit something that must not move →** **Ensnaring Strike** (BA immediately after a weapon hit, STR save vs. DC 16, **Large or larger has advantage on it**; on a failure Restrained and taking **1d6 piercing at the start of each of its turns**, Conc. 1 min, 1st). It or an ally can spend an action on **Athletics vs. DC 16** to break free.
- **Just hit at range and enemies are clustered →** **Hail of Thorns** (DEX DC 16, 1d10 in 5 ft. of the target, half on save, 1st)
- **Opening a fight →** **Hunter's Mark** (90 ft., +1d6 force per hit, adv. to track, Conc., 1 hr; always prepared)
- **Repositioning →** **Threshold** (15 ft., 1/LR, no slot) before **Misty Step** (30 ft., 2nd slot; always prepared)
- **The fight has turned →** **Celestial Revelation** (1 min, 1/LR). While it lasts, once per turn she adds **+3 damage** to one target she damages. Choose the form each time:
  - **Heavenly Wings** → Fly Speed 30 ft.; extra damage is radiant
  - **Inner Radiance** → bright light 10 ft. and dim 10 more; every creature within 10 ft. takes 3 radiant at the end of each of her turns; extra damage is radiant
  - **Necrotic Shroud** → non-allies within 10 ft. make **CHA DC 12** or are Frightened until the end of her next turn; extra damage is necrotic

  *(The three forms' effects come from a live 5etools lookup of the XPHB Aasimar trait — the sheet lists only their names and the DC.)*

**Reaction:**
- **Absorb Elements**, and nothing else. No decision, just don't forget it's there.

**Movement (30 ft.):**
- **Stay far away.** At 27 HP, distance is the only defence she has that always works, and The Gap removes the usual reason an archer closes.
- **Darkvision 60 ft.** lets her hold a firing line in rooms where the rest of the party needs light.
- **Cannot be surprised** while the stone is worn (Listening).

**Resource tiering, at a glance:**
- **At-will:** Longbow (R +8), Scimitar / Shortsword (M +6), Light. Dreadful Strikes, Fey Echo and Fault Finding all ride free on hits, each 1/turn.
- **Spell slots: 1st ×4, 2nd ×2.** Charm Person, Hail of Thorns, Ensnaring Strike, Absorb Elements (1st); Spike Growth, Misty Step (2nd). **Two 2nd-level slots is the whole day**, and Spike Growth and Misty Step both want them.
- **1/Long Rest:** Healing Hands, Celestial Revelation, Threshold. **1/day:** First Signal.
- **Free, always prepared:** *Charm Person* and *Misty Step* (Fey Wanderer), *Hunter's Mark* (Favored Enemy).

**Comparative Notes:**

- *Ensnaring Strike vs. Hail of Thorns vs. Hunter's Mark — the real Bonus Action fight.* All three are 1st-level, and Ensnaring Strike and Hail of Thorns both trigger off a weapon hit she has just landed, so she cannot use both on the same hit. Hail of Thorns is one burst of 1d10 to everything near the target and ends there. Ensnaring Strike costs Concentration but Restrains one creature and grinds 1d6 into it every turn until it wins an Athletics check. **Hail of Thorns when enemies are packed and the fight is short; Ensnaring Strike when one creature must not reach the party. Hunter's Mark when she expects a long fight against one target and doesn't need either.**
- *Ensnaring Strike vs. Spike Growth, both Concentration, and she gets one.* Spike Growth costs an Action and one of her two 2nd-level slots, needs no attack roll and no save, and punishes an entire area for up to 10 minutes. Ensnaring Strike costs a Bonus Action off a hit she already made, and shuts down exactly one creature. Note **Large or larger creatures get advantage on the Ensnaring Strike save**, so it is least reliable against exactly the things she most wants stopped. **Spike Growth handles ground she wants nobody crossing.** Save Ensnaring Strike for a Medium-or-smaller runner, where the save is likeliest to stick.
- *Threshold vs. Misty Step, when she needs to be elsewhere.* Threshold (15 ft., 1/LR) costs no slot and cannot pass through obstacles, so it needs line of sight and a clear path. Misty Step (30 ft.) doubles the range but spends one of only two 2nd-level slots, which Spike Growth also wants. **Spend Threshold first whenever the path is open**, and save Misty Step for what Threshold cannot solve: a door, a wall, or anything else it refuses to cross.
- *Which Celestial Revelation form, given it's 1/LR.* All three grant the same +3 once-per-turn rider, so the body of the transformation is the entire decision. Heavenly Wings buys a 30-ft. Fly Speed. Inner Radiance and Necrotic Shroud both require standing within 10 ft. of enemies — the exact position 27 HP cannot afford. **Wings by default; Radiance only when she is already surrounded and wants the area damage anyway.**

## Loadout Selection

*Ranger prepares 6 spells at level 5 (confirmed against the XPHB Ranger class table). **She does not re-prepare the whole list daily.** The XPHB Ranger's Changing Your Prepared Spells reads: "Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace **one spell** on your list with another Ranger spell for which you have spell slots." Treat what follows as a queue of single swaps, one per day, not as loadouts to switch between.*

*Also confirmed against that same feature: **always-prepared spells don't count against the limit.** *Charm Person* and *Misty Step* (Fey Wanderer) and *Hunter's Mark* (Favored Enemy) are therefore free, and all **six** slots are hers to spend — so if Foundry is listing Charm Person and Misty Step inside her six, she is carrying **two fewer real picks than she is entitled to**. Worth checking before the next session; it is two free spells.*

**Currently prepared, per Foundry:** *Misty Step* · *Spike Growth* · *Hail of Thorns* · *Ensnaring Strike* · *Charm Person* · *Absorb Elements*

**If two slots really are free, fill them first — no swap needed, and this is the biggest single upgrade available to her:**
1. **Pass without Trace.** +10 Stealth for the whole party on top of her own +6.
2. **Cure Wounds.** With CON −1 and 27 HP she is both the likeliest to go down and, besides one use of Healing Hands, the party's only ranger-side healing.

**Otherwise, the next single swap, in order of value:**
1. **Hail of Thorns → Pass without Trace** — Hail of Thorns and Ensnaring Strike compete for the same Bonus Action off the same hit, so one of them is close to redundant on most turns.
2. **Spike Growth → Cure Wounds** on a day she expects to be hit more than she expects to hold a corridor.
3. **Absorb Elements → Longstrider or Goodberry** only on a travel day where nothing is likely to breathe fire at her.

*Candidates pulled from the full Ranger spell list at levels 1–2 via `query_5etools.py`. **Absorb Elements** is a Ranger spell in its XGE printing with no 2024 reprint — verified, and normal for this table's mixed kit; it just won't surface in a modern-only browse.*

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to notice anything at all → **Perception +11** *(expertise via Deft Explorer)* with **Passive Perception 21**. That passive alone clears most hidden doors, tails and ambushes before anyone asks her to roll.
- Need to hear through a door, a floor, or a wall → **Listening** *([[Hollowstone]], RAW: advantage on Perception checks relying on hearing, and she cannot be surprised while the stone is worn)*
- **Any** social check, of any kind → **Otherworldly Glamour** *(RAW, per the sheet: adds her Wisdom modifier, minimum +1, to **any** Charisma check, not only the three social skills)*. This is why Deception, Intimidation and Persuasion sit at +6 on a CHA of 12. **She is a far better face than her Charisma score suggests.**
- Holding a room's attention while somebody else works → **Performance +9**. Otherworldly Glamour's proficiency pick is **Performance**, so that skill alone gets CHA +1, Wisdom +5, and proficiency +3 — her best social number by a clear margin, and better than several dedicated faces in the party.
- Need to read a person rather than a room → **Insight +8**, **Passive Insight 18**
- Need to identify a creature or find it again days later → **Favored Enemy** *(RAW: advantage on Survival checks to track creatures and Intelligence checks to recall information about them)*
- Need to get up, over or across → **Threshold** *([[Hollowstone]], RAW: BA, 15 ft. to an unoccupied space she can see; it does **not** pass through obstacles, so line of sight and a clear path are both required)*, **Misty Step** (30 ft.), or **Heavenly Wings** for a full minute of 30-ft. flight
- Dark, unlit, underground → **Darkvision 60 ft.** plus the **Light** cantrip *(RAW: touch an object, 20 ft. bright / 20 ft. dim, 1 hr)*. She was raised underground; this is her home terrain, not a hazard.
- Needs an answer nobody else can reach → **First Signal** *([[Hollowstone]], RAW: 1/day, one question answered in [[The Unbound]]'s voice, or something wearing his pattern; it always knows more than it says)*. A story tool, not a lookup: expect a true answer that isn't a complete one.
- Undead, necrotic traps, radiant hazards → **Celestial Resistance** *(RAW: resistance to both necrotic and radiant)*. She can walk into things that would badly hurt anyone else here.
- Something that only speaks Celestial → **Celestial** proficiency, backed by **Religion +4**
- Anything that has to look officially written → **Calligrapher's Supplies** proficiency, backed by the Acolyte background
- Making an approach safe overnight, or covering a retreat → **Spike Growth** *(RAW: Conc. up to 10 minutes, so it long outlasts a fight; the camouflage means anything that didn't watch her cast it needs a Search action and WIS DC 16 to spot the hazard)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — running Charm Person as an interrogation or infiltration opener rather than a combat spell → **Charm Person** *(RAW printed trigger: WIS DC 16, one humanoid, Charmed 1 hour, and it **ends the moment she or an ally harms the target**; the target also knows it was charmed once the hour is up, so anyone she uses it on is someone she cannot use it on twice)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — Fault Finding's shove landing on a ledge, a pit lip or a rope bridge rather than open floor → **Fault Finding** *([[Hollowstone]], RAW printed trigger: on a ranged weapon hit, STR DC 16 or pushed 5 ft. in a direction Terra chooses; the direction is hers, what the terrain does with it is the DM's)*
