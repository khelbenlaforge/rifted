# Ember - Playbook

*Companion to [[Ember]] — regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-09. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current.*

**Sheet flag, confirmed with the DM this run:** the tables list 12 leveled (non-cantrip) spells, but a level 5 Druid's prepared cap is WIS mod (+5) + level (5) = 10 — 2 over. Treated as-is below per the DM's call, but this needs a character-sheet-sync re-check.

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn before anything else:**
- **Ally down at 0 HP, Action's the only thing free →** **Spare the Dying** (cantrip), 15 ft, stabilizes
- **Ally down or badly hurt, Bonus Action free →** **Healing Word**, 60 ft, 2d4+4, 1st-level slot
- **Ally hurt but not urgent, Action free →** **Cure Wounds**, touch, 2d8+4, 1st-level slot
- **Bleeding out herself, no slot worth spending →** **Potion of Healing**, self, 2d4+2 — 2 uses total; giving it to someone else costs an Action instead of a Bonus Action

**Action:**
- **At-will melee damage →** **Primal Savagery** (cantrip), M +6, 5 ft, 1d10 acid
- **At-will ranged damage →** **Starry Wisp** (cantrip), R +6, 60 ft, 1d8 radiant, target can't turn Invisible — or **Shortbow**, R +5, 80/320 ft, 1d6+3 pierce
- **Need a target pulled into melee or off an ally →** **Thorn Whip** (cantrip), M +6, 30 ft, 1d6 pierce, pull Large or smaller 10 ft
- **Enemies grouped together, opening the fight →** **Thunderwave**, CON DC 14, self (15-ft cube), 2d8 thunder + push 10 ft, 1st-level slot
- **Sustained damage across several turns →** **Moonbeam**, CON DC 14, 120 ft, 2d10 radiant, move it as an Action each turn, Conc., 2nd-level slot
- **Set up the whole party's next hits →** **Faerie Fire**, DEX DC 14, 60 ft, 20-ft cube, targets outlined (no Invisible) and every attack against them has advantage, Conc., 1st-level slot
- **A target needs to be bigger, smaller, or hit harder/softer →** **Enlarge/Reduce**, CON DC 14 (unwilling only), 30 ft, size change + 1d4 damage mod, Conc., 2nd-level slot
- **Need another body on the field beyond Vesper →** **Summon Beast**, 90 ft, summons a beast spirit, Conc., 2nd-level slot
- **Blights or undead in range →** **Blight's Call**, WIS DC 15, 60 ft, frightens them — 1/day, and blowing it also forces Ember to make a save of her own against an effect the sheet keeps vague on purpose
- **Before a stealthy approach, not mid-fight →** **Pass without Trace**, self, 30-ft emanation, +10 Stealth + no tracks, Conc., 2nd-level slot
- **Nothing else fits, needs to grapple or shove →** **Unarmed Strike**, M +0, 1 bludgeon, or a grapple/shove at DC 10 — Sickle (M +0, 1d4-2 slash) is on the sheet too but skipped here; it's strictly worse than both Primal Savagery and even a bare Unarmed Strike at these stats

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **Vesper needs to be on the field →** **Summon/Dismiss Vesper** — adjacent, no Action cost (Fey Recall)
- **This fight calls for a body that can take hits her AC 16 can't →** **Wild Shape**, CR 1 beast form, 2x/Short Rest — drops spellcasting entirely while shifted
- **Melee weapon damage instead of a cantrip →** **Flame Blade**, M +6, 5 ft, 3d6 fire, Conc., 2nd-level slot — can be re-summoned as a Bonus Action if it drops
- **Need extra ground covered right now →** **Jump**, touch, 30-ft jump for 10 ft of movement, 1st-level slot

**Reaction / no action required:**
- **Just cast a spell at a single target, once this Long Rest →** **The Hunt as One** (no action) — Vesper gets one free attack against that same target: Bite +5, 1d10+3 pierce + 1d6 force, target Prone if Large or smaller *(Vesper's own numbers, sourced from the amulet's item note, not the character sheet)*
- **Vesper just landed a hit, resets every round →** **In Harmony** (no action) — Ember may cast a cantrip at that same target for free

**Movement:**
- Fly speed 30 ft opens vertical positioning for Moonbeam and Starry Wisp that ground movement alone doesn't.
- War Caster lets a spell with the right components replace a weapon swing on an opportunity attack — don't forget it's available when something breaks contact.

**Comparative Notes:**
- Cure Wounds spends the Action for a flat, touch-range 2d8+4; Healing Word only manages 2d4+4 but costs the Bonus Action instead and reaches 60 ft. The real difference is which resource is free that turn, not the die size — Ember rarely has both spare on the same ally at once.
- Faerie Fire spends a slot and the whole Action to hand the party advantage against everything in a 20-ft cube for a full minute; Thorn Whip is a free at-will cantrip that only pulls and hits one target for 1d6. Faerie Fire earns its cost against a cluster worth a turn's investment, and Thorn Whip is what's left when there isn't one yet.
- A CR 1 beast form trades spellcasting away entirely for extra HP and a new attack routine, and the same 2/Short Rest pool also pays for casting Find Familiar via Wild Companion — shapeshifting twice and summoning the familiar in the same rest period aren't both on the table.
- Landing Faerie Fire or Moonbeam on a target, then letting Vesper's free Hunt as One bite connect, then spending In Harmony's free cantrip on the same target chains three effects off one turn — but Hunt as One only resets on a Long Rest, so the chain only opens once per rest.
- Blight's Call frightens every blight and undead within 60 ft in one action, once a day, but what the failed curse save actually costs isn't written down anywhere accessible — treat it as a real risk, not a free action, until that's confirmed at the table.

## Loadout Selection

*Druid (2024) prepares from its full class spell list and can swap the entire prepared list on a Long Rest, same as Cleric. Everything below is drawn from the 12 currently listed (per the cap, the actual number should be 10 — see the flag above).*

**Dungeon / combat-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Hold Person* — a save-or-lose control spell nothing currently prepared covers; *Barkskin* — a flat AC floor for whichever melee ally is taking the most hits
- Keep: *Moonbeam*, *Faerie Fire*, *Thunderwave* — the core damage-and-control spread

**Social / exploration-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Locate Object* or *Locate Animals or Plants* — finding what a search alone won't; *Longstrider* — extra ground covered on a long overland leg; *Fog Cloud* — an escape or cover option, already proven useful (the Pantry Heist)
- Keep: *Pass without Trace*, *Speak with Animals* — already exploration-shaped

**Balanced / unknown expedition type:**
- Default to what's currently prepared — Cure Wounds, Faerie Fire, Thunderwave, Moonbeam, and Healing Word already cover healing, control, and damage

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to calm, direct, or read an animal (Vesper included) → **Animal Handling +8**
- Need to track, forage, navigate, or read the weather → **Survival +8**
- Need to catch a hidden threat or a subtle detail → **Perception +8** *(Passive 18)*
- Need to move unseen or shadow someone → **Stealth +6** *(Passive 16)*
- Need the whole party silent and untraceable, not just herself → **Pass without Trace** *(RAW: self 30-ft emanation, +10 Stealth + no tracks, Conc. 1 hr, 2nd-level slot)*
- Need an accurate map of unfamiliar ground → **Cartographer's Tools proficiency**
- Need to identify a plant, treat a wound, or work out what something organic actually is → **Herbalism Kit proficiency**
- Need information only an animal witnessed → **Speak with Animals** *(RAW: ritual, 10 min, self)*
- Need a low-profile scout without spending a fight's worth of Wild Shape → **Find Familiar** *(RAW: 1-hr ritual via Wild Companion — costs one of the 2 Wild Shape/Short Rest uses, not a spell slot)*
- Need to sense magic in something organic or produce a small nature effect → **Druidcraft** *(RAW: minor effects — weather, plants, sounds, a small fire; already demonstrated in-fiction examining the Goblin Fruit)*
- Need to pass a message in front of people who can't understand it → **Druidic**
- Need an ally (or herself) to have a real shot at a specific check that's coming up → **Guidance** *(RAW: cantrip, touch, target +1d4 to one ability check, Conc. 1 min)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — dragging a fleeing witness or a dropped item back within reach outside a fight → **Thorn Whip** *(RAW printed trigger: melee spell attack, 1d6 pierce + pull 10 ft, written for combat)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — a non-combat CR 1 form (a bird for an aerial look, a fish underwater) solving a problem no spell reaches → **Wild Shape** *(RAW printed trigger: Bonus Action, CR 1 max, 2x/Short Rest — a combat resource by default)*
