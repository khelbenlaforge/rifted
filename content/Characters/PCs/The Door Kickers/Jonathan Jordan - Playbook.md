# Jonathan Jordan - Playbook

*Companion to [[Jonathan Jordan]] — regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-09. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current.*

*Rogue (Soulknife) has no prepared-spell mechanic — Psionic Energy Dice and Psychic Blade are fixed subclass features, not spells. There's no Long Rest reshuffle to plan around, so this note has no Loadout Selection section.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn before anything else:**
- **An attack roll is about to be made against JJ and it matters →** **Luck Point — Intercept** *(no action, Pool 2/LR shared with Fortune)* — impose Disadvantage on that roll before it's made
- **A hit connects that exactly matches AC 15 →** **Coin's Edge** *(Reaction, 1/SR)* — flip it to a miss
- **Any other hit connects, attacker visible →** **Uncanny Dodge** *(Reaction, at-will)* — halve the damage
- **A d20 test of JJ's absolutely has to land →** **Luck Point — Fortune** *(no action, same 2/LR pool)* — grant himself Advantage, even through Disadvantage
- **Failed an ability check that matters →** **Psi-Bolstered Knack** *(no action, Psionic Energy Die, Pool 3/LR shared with Psychic Whispers spends)* — add the die to the check; if it now passes, it succeeds

**Action:**
- **Melee range, any target →** **Dagger** *(Nick)* M +7, 1d4+4 pierce — triggers a free extra attack with **Shortsword** *(Vex)* M +7, 1d6+4 pierce + target Vexed, no Bonus Action spent
- **Can't or don't want to close the gap →** **Psychic Blade** *(Vex)* M/R +7, 60 ft, 1d6 psychic + Vexed — manifests free as part of the Attack action
- **A hit landed with Advantage, OR an ally is adjacent to the target →** stack on **Sneak Attack**, +3d6, once per turn — pick whichever qualifying hit this turn to load it onto
- **The lockdown matters more than the extra damage →** **Cunning Strike** — trade 1d6 of that Sneak Attack for Poison (CON DC 14 or Poisoned 1 min), Trip (DEX DC 14 or Prone), or Withdraw (move 5 ft without provoking)
- **No weapon in hand →** **Unarmed Strike**, M +2, 5 ft, 1 bludgeon (min 1)
- **Dark or night sky, a full minute to spend before trouble starts →** **Night Caller**, 10 ft, animates one corpse as Animate Dead — 1/7 days, not a mid-fight option

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **JJ's Action this turn was Psychic Blade, not Dagger →** **Psychic Blade (second)**, M/R +7, 1d4 psychic — requires a free hand
- **Need to close, break contact, or vanish →** **Cunning Action** — Dash, Disengage, or Hide
- **About to whiff an attack that has to land →** **Steady Aim** — Advantage on the next attack this turn; Speed drops to 0
- **Psionic Energy Dice running dry →** **Regain Psionic Die**, self, 1/day
- **Bleeding out, need HP now →** **Potion of Healing**, self, 2d4+2
- **About to split up or go loud/quiet on an op →** **Psychic Whispers** — link up to 5 willing creatures telepathically for 8 hrs; first use each Long Rest is free, further uses spend 1–5 Psionic Energy Dice from the same pool as Psi-Bolstered Knack

**Movement:**
- Dagger (20/60, thrown) and Psychic Blade (60 ft) both work at range — JJ doesn't have to close in to get his two attacks unless he specifically wants Shortsword's Vex.
- Steady Aim zeroes Speed for the round — only take it when the fight doesn't need JJ to move.

**Passive, no decision needed:** Lady Luck's Favor refunds a spent Luck Point automatically on a natural 20; Double Down rolls a d6 after any successful Luck Point spend and refunds it on a 4–6. Both just happen — nothing to choose.

**Long Rest option — weapon mastery swap:** the two assignments above (Dagger *Nick*, Shortsword *Vex*) aren't fixed. On a Long Rest, Dagger can be swapped for **Scimitar** *(Nick)* M +7, 1d6+4 slash (same Nick trigger, more damage, no thrown range), and Shortsword can be swapped for **Shortbow** *(Vex)* R +7, 80/320 ft, 1d6+4 pierce + Vexed (same Vex effect, ranged instead of melee). Worth it for an expedition that's going to be one-note ranged or melee for a while — not worth reshuffling for a single fight.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Dagger+Shortsword Nick combo vs. Psychic Blade + Psychic Blade (second):* the Nick combo gets two attacks entirely off the Action, leaving the Bonus Action free for Cunning Action or Regain Psionic Die; the Psychic Blade route also gets two attacks but spends the Bonus Action to do it and needs a free hand. Take Psychic Blade twice only when the fight actually needs both attacks at 60 ft, not 5 ft.
- *Full Sneak Attack (3d6) vs. Cunning Strike:* Cunning Strike only costs 1d6 of the 3d6, and Trip or Poison can take a dangerous attacker mostly out of the fight for a round — worth it when locking the target down matters more than the roughly one die of damage it trades away. Withdraw is the pick when the goal is leaving safely, not hurting anything.
- *Coin's Edge vs. Uncanny Dodge:* Coin's Edge only fires on an exact AC-15 hit and burns the Short Rest use; Uncanny Dodge is unlimited but only halves rather than negates. Default to Uncanny Dodge and save Coin's Edge for when the attacker calls out (or it's visibly) a roll that lands right on 15.
- *Luck Point — Intercept vs. the Reactions above:* Intercept is spent before the roll and can stop a hit from happening at all; Coin's Edge and Uncanny Dodge only kick in after a hit already lands. Reach for Intercept against an attacker JJ specifically doesn't want to connect (a save-or-die rider, a crit-fisher) — it's the only option that can prevent the hit outright, but it draws from the same 2-point pool as Fortune.

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to read a room's dangers before they turn into a fight → **Perception +9** *(expertise; Passive 19)*
- Need to talk someone into or out of something, or run an interrogation → **Persuasion +8** *(expertise)*
- Need to lift or plant something unnoticed, Pantry Heist-style → **Sleight of Hand +7**
- Need to tail someone or slip past a watch → **Stealth +7** *(Passive 17)*
- Need a cover story, a busking distraction, or a convincing act → **Performance +5**
- Reasoning has stalled and leaning on someone will actually move it → **Intimidation +5**
- Need to pass information in front of people who can't know it → **Thieves Cant**
- Need past a lock or to disarm something mechanical → **Thieves Tools proficiency**
- A big out-of-combat check (a hard negotiation, a risky climb) needs to land → **Luck Point — Fortune** *(RAW: Advantage on the d20 test — same 2/LR pool as combat use)*
- That same check just failed and it still matters → **Psi-Bolstered Knack** *(RAW: expend a Psionic Energy Die, add it to the check; passes on success — same 3/LR pool as Psychic Whispers)*
- Need the party coordinating silently mid-infiltration → **Psychic Whispers** *(RAW: telepathic link, up to 5 willing creatures, 8 hrs — first use each Long Rest is free)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — flashing it could carry weight as proof of standing with the Guild in a social scene → **Guild Brass Armlet** *(RAW: none stated, pure flavor item)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — raising a scout, a distraction, or a piece of intimidation theater in the right conditions → **Night Caller** *(RAW printed trigger: animates one corpse as Animate Dead, darkness or night sky only, 1-min cast, 1/7 days)* — worth a table conversation given what it actually is, not something to spring without checking in first
