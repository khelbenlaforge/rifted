# Regius - Playbook

*Companion to [[Regius]]—regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-04. Rerun `pc-playbook` after any `character-sheet-sync` update to keep it current.*

*No Loadout Selection section below: Regius has no spellcasting at all. Fighter (Echo Knight) is a purely martial build here, so there is no daily prepared list to advise on.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first, before anything else:**
- **Hurt and need it back now →** **Second Wind** (BA, 1d10+5, pool of 2/LR). **Tactical Shift** rides along free: he also moves half his Speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Ally about to be reached →** he is the party's lock. **Sentinel** plus **Polearm Master** means anything crossing his 10-ft. reach eats a Reaction, and a hit from an Opportunity Attack sets its **Speed to 0** for the turn.
- **Needs to be somewhere else immediately →** **Swap with Echo** (BA **and** 15 ft. of movement; teleports him to the echo's space)
- **Round needs to count double →** **Action Surge** (1/SR): one extra action, not the Magic action.

**Action:**
- **Default →** **Attack action, Glaive** *(Graze)*, M +10, 10 ft. reach, 1d10+7. Two attacks (Extra Attack). **On a miss, Graze still deals 5 damage** (his STR modifier), so a whiffed swing is never nothing.
- **Echo is out and positioned →** any attack in the Attack action **can originate from the echo's space instead of his own**, chosen per attack. Free, unlimited, no resource spent.
- **Want a third swing from the echo →** **Unleash Incarnation** (3/LR): one *extra* melee attack from the echo's space, with **Echo's Grip** adding **+1d6 force**.
- **Two things need hitting in different places →** attack normally from his own space, then Unleash Incarnation from the echo's, wherever it is standing.
- **Nothing worth a resource →** just the glaive. At +10 with reach, this is already the best attack on the sheet.
- **Target is a caster or about to run →** **Flail** *(Sap)*, M +8, 1d8+5: on a hit it has disadvantage on its next attack roll. Or **Javelin** *(Slow)*, M/R +8, 1d6+5, 30/120 ft. thrown, −10 ft. speed on a hit.

**The turn that hits hardest** — both riders land on one attack:
1. **Swap with Echo** (BA, 15 ft. of movement)
2. **Attack action → Unleash Incarnation** from the echo's position
3. That single attack takes **Split Strike +1d8** *and* **Echo's Grip +1d6**, on top of 1d10+7

*One open question for the DM: [[Le Fil]] words Echo's Grip as "when Regius makes an attack from his Manifest Echo's position (Unleash Incarnation)". Read strictly, the +1d6 applies only to the Unleash Incarnation attack; read loosely, it applies to any attack made from the echo's space — which the base subclass allows for free. Worth settling, because it changes his damage on most turns.*

**Bonus Action** (contested — pick one):
- **No echo on the field →** **Manifest Echo** (15 ft., unoccupied space he can see). **Present Absence** grants **+1 AC** while it stands within 30 ft.
- **Echo is placed and he needs to be there →** **Swap with Echo** (15 ft. of movement), which also arms Split Strike
- **Hurt →** **Second Wind** (1d10+5, pool 2/LR) plus the free Tactical Shift move
- **Already attacked with the glaive →** **Pole Strike**, M +8, 1d4+5 with the butt end

**Reaction** (four options, one Reaction — this is his real decision point):
- **Something leaves his reach →** **Opportunity Attack** (M +8). On a hit, **Sentinel — Halt** sets its Speed to 0 for the rest of the turn.
- **Something enters his reach →** **Reactive Strike** (M +8, 10 ft., Polearm Master), triggering on entry rather than on exit
- **Something within 5 ft. Disengages, or attacks someone who isn't him →** **Sentinel — Guardian** (M +8)
- **Something within 5 ft. of the echo moves away from it →** Opportunity Attack from the **echo's** space
- **He rolls a natural 1 on an attack →** **One Opening** ([[Le Fil]], 1/LR): reroll and use the new result. *(The sheet files this under Reactions; Le Fil's own note doesn't assign it an action cost. This playbook follows the sheet — worth settling at the table, because if it does consume his Reaction it competes with all four options above.)*

**Movement (30 ft.):**
- **Hold a choke point.** Reach 10 ft. plus Reactive Strike plus Sentinel means a doorway or corridor he stands in costs enemies their turn to cross.
- **The echo moves 30 ft. on its own** and is where his real mobility lives. Park it behind the enemy line, then swap when it matters.
- **AC 15, or 16 with the echo up.** Present Absence is not in the sheet's AC number — it applies whenever the echo is active within 30 ft., which should be most turns.

**Resource tiering, at a glance:**
- **At-will:** Glaive (M +10, reach 10 ft.), Flail / Javelin (M/R +8), Pole Strike, Manifest Echo, Swap with Echo, and every Reaction above. Almost his entire kit costs nothing.
- **Second Wind: one pool of 2 uses**, regaining 1 on a Short Rest and all on a Long Rest, spent on **either** the Bonus Action heal **or** **Tactical Mind**. It is not two separate resources.
- **3/Long Rest:** Unleash Incarnation. **1/Short Rest:** Action Surge. **1/Long Rest:** One Opening. **1/day:** E's Direction.
- **[[Le Fil]] is designated on the glaive.** That is why the glaive is +10 / 1d10+7 while the flail and javelins sit at +8 — the +2 only applies to the designated weapon. He can redesignate on a Short or Long Rest.

**Comparative Notes:**

- *Second Wind as a heal vs. holding it for Tactical Mind.* Same pool of 2. The heal is 1d10+5 plus a free half-Speed move that ignores opportunity attacks. Tactical Mind adds 1d10 to an ability check he has **already failed**, and here is the part that's easy to miss: **the use isn't expended if the check still fails**. That makes Tactical Mind close to free on a check that was probably lost anyway, while the heal is always spent. **Don't hoard the pool out of combat, but never dump the last use on chip damage.**
- *Reactive Strike (entering reach) vs. Opportunity Attack (leaving it).* Both M +8, both his one Reaction. Reactive Strike fires as something closes, before it attacks — pure prevention. The Opportunity Attack fires as it leaves and, with Sentinel, drops its Speed to 0, which strands it next to him. **If the choice is live, ask which direction the threat is moving.** Coming toward the squishy people behind him, Reactive Strike; heading away toward them, the Opportunity Attack and Sentinel's Speed-to-0.
- *Swap with Echo vs. Manifest Echo, when the echo is already out but badly placed.* Manifest Echo re-places it anywhere within 15 ft. of *him*. Swapping moves *him* to *it* and arms Split Strike (+1d8), but note it costs **both** a Bonus Action **and** 15 ft. of his movement (confirmed against the EGW Manifest Echo text) — it is not the cheaper option, just the better-placed one. **If the echo is where the fight is, swap. If it isn't, re-manifest and accept that this turn's damage is ordinary.**
- *Glaive vs. Flail against a single dangerous attacker.* The glaive is +10 with reach and a bigger die and carries Le Fil's bonus. The flail is +8 and 1d8+5, but *Sap* gives the target disadvantage on its next attack roll. **The flail is only correct when preventing one specific incoming attack matters more than his own damage** — protecting a downed ally, say. Otherwise the glaive's reach and to-hit make it the answer.

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Any conversation that matters → **Persuasion +7**, **Deception +7**, **Intimidation +7** on CHA 18. He is the party's best face by a clear margin, with a Noble background and years of professionally reading wealthy strangers behind it.
- A check he just failed and badly needs → **Tactical Mind** *(RAW: expend a use of Second Wind to add 1d10 to a failed ability check; **the use is not expended if the check still fails**)*. This is the most underused thing on his sheet — it turns a failed lock, climb, negotiation or recall into a second attempt at almost no cost.
- Anything requiring raw physical force → **Athletics +8** on STR 20. Doors, climbs, grapples, holding a rope with someone on the end of it.
- Needs a body somewhere he isn't → **Manifest Echo** *(BA, an unoccupied space within 15 ft., 1 HP, immune to conditions, uses his saving throw bonuses, and he can move it up to 30 ft.)*. It is a visible duplicate of him that persists. **One number to settle:** his sheet records the echo at **AC 14**, but the EGW feature reads "AC 14 + your proficiency bonus", which at PB +3 would make it **AC 17** (confirmed via a live 5etools lookup). Three points of AC on a 1 HP decoy changes how freely he can park it in the open.
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — using the echo as bait to pull a guard's attention around a corner → **Manifest Echo** *(RAW printed trigger: it exists, takes a position, and can be moved; nothing in the feature says it acts, speaks, or manipulates anything, and 1 HP means any hit dispels it)*. His echo was already appearing in the [[Garnet District]] before he arrived, so the table has precedent for it being seen.
- Needs to know whether family is near → **E's Direction** *([[Le Fil]], 1/day, RAW: he learns whether anyone of his mother's bloodline is within 1 mile (cord tightens sharply), 10 miles (tightens slightly), or farther (barely moves), plus a direction. The information is always accurate.)* Non-combat only, and it points straight at the "E" arc.
- Rolls a natural 1 on an attack roll, in or out of combat → **One Opening** *([[Le Fil]], 1/LR, RAW printed trigger: **only** a 1 on an **attack roll**)*. Worth naming because it is narrower than it feels: it does nothing for a failed save or a botched skill check, so don't count on it as a general safety net.
- Needs to survive something the party can't fight → **STR save +8**, **CON save +6**, 48 HP, and Second Wind on top. When somebody has to hold the door, it should be him.
- Watching a target for hours without being noticed → he ran exactly this for [[House Verenthal]], trailing [[Sellas Verenthal]] nightly. **Perception +3** and **Stealth +1** are both poor. He managed it by having a plausible reason to be on that street, which is the only version of surveillance his numbers support.
- Getting into somewhere that isn't locked so much as guarded → **Deception +7** and **Persuasion +7**, with **Fine Clothes**, **Perfume** and a Noble background behind them. Walking in through the front is his best entry by a distance.
- Rough entry when subtlety has already failed → **Crowbar**, **Rope**, **Caltrops**, **Tinderbox** on his gear list, backed by Athletics +8
- Reading a room of nobles or gamblers → **One Gaming Set** proficiency (Noble) and **History +2**
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — presenting [[Le Fil]] itself as a credential or a conversation opener with anyone who can sense magic, the way the [[Blackstaff Brother]] reacted to it → **[[Le Fil]]** *(RAW: it has no printed social effect at all. What happened at [[Blackstaff Mansion]] was a story beat the DM ran, with nothing on the item's card driving it.)*
