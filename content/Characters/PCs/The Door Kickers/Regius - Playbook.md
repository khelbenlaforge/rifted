# Regius - Playbook

*Companion to [[Regius]]—regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-21. Rerun `pc-playbook` after any `character-sheet-sync` update to keep it current.*

*No Loadout Selection section below: Regius has no spellcasting at all. Fighter (Echo Knight) is a purely martial build, so there's no daily prepared list to advise on—his kit only changes between rests via Weapon Mastery reassignment and [[Le Fil]]'s designated-weapon swap (see Comparative Notes).*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first—check every turn before anything else:**
- **Regius is hurting and nothing urgent is happening →** **Second Wind** (BA, self, 1d10+5, pool 2/LR)
- **An important check—in or out of combat—is about to fail →** **Tactical Mind**: expend a Second Wind use to add 1d10; the use isn't lost even if the check still fails. No action cost.

**Action** *(Attack—twice via Extra Attack, unless noted below. Great Weapon Fighting automatically treats each 1 or 2 on the Glaive's damage die as a 3—no choice needed.)*:
- **Default target, no special need →** **Glaive** *(Graze)*—M +10, 10 ft., 1d10+7 slash. On a miss, may deal 5 slashing (STR mod) instead.
- **Target about to swing back hard →** **Flail** *(Sap)*—M +8, 5 ft., 1d8+5 bludgeon; hit gives it disadvantage on its next attack roll.
- **Target trying to close in or run →** **Javelin** *(Slow)*—M +8 (5 ft.) or R +8 (30/120 ft.), 1d6+5 pierce; on hit, may cut its Speed by 10 ft.
- **Need a body held or moved, not hurt →** **Unarmed Strike**—M +8, 5 ft., 6 bludgeon, grapple or shove at DC 16.
- **Burst window—something needs to die this turn →** **Action Surge** (1/SR): a full extra Action (Attack again, twice more via Extra Attack).
- **Echo already up and angled well →** **Unleash Incarnation** (3/LR): one extra melee attack from the echo's space as part of the Attack action, +1d6 force (Echo's Grip).

**Bonus Action:**
- **Regius is badly hurt →** **Second Wind** (self, 1d10+5, pool 2/LR)
- **Just swung the Glaive this turn →** **Pole Strike**—M +10, 5 ft., 1d4+7 bludgeon, butt-end follow-up.
- **No echo out yet →** **Manifest Echo**—conjure it within 15 ft., in an unoccupied space Regius can see.
- **Echo already out, want the payoff →** **Swap with Echo**—teleport into its space (costs 15 ft. movement); the first designated-weapon attack before end of turn gets +1d8 (Split Strike).

**Reaction** *(one per round total—several of these can trigger the same round, but only the first taken actually fires):*
- **Something leaves Regius's own reach →** **Opportunity Attack**—M +10, hit drops its Speed to 0 for the turn.
- **Something enters Regius's own reach for the first time →** **Reactive Strike** (Polearm Master)—M +10.
- **Something already in reach Disengages, or attacks someone else →** **Sentinel**—M +10, same Speed-0 effect on hit.
- **Something within 5 ft. of the echo (not Regius) moves 5+ ft. away →** **Echo Opportunity Attack**, resolved as if standing in the echo's space.
- **Natural 1 on any attack roll →** **One Opening** fires by itself (reroll, use the new result, 1/LR)—doesn't compete with the four above; just don't forget it.

**Movement:**
- Keep the echo within 30 ft. whenever the fight allows it—**Present Absence** grants a passive +1 AC the whole time it's active there.
- The echo can move up to 30 ft. on its own each turn (Echo Mobility)—reposition it even on turns Regius doesn't swap into it, to keep the 30 ft. Present Absence range covered and line up next turn's Unleash Incarnation or Echo Opportunity Attack angle.
- Activating Second Wind as a Bonus Action also triggers **Tactical Shift**—move up to half Speed with no Opportunity Attacks provoked, same turn.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Glaive (Graze) vs. Flail (Sap) vs. Javelin (Slow):* [[Le Fil]]'s +2 to attack and damage currently rides on the Glaive alone—trading down to the Flail or Javelin means giving that bonus up for a control effect (disadvantage on their next swing, or ten feet of Speed gone). Worth it against something whose own attack or escape is the real problem; not worth it against something that just needs to die.
- *Action Surge vs. Unleash Incarnation:* Action Surge buys a whole second Action—two more attacks via Extra Attack—but only once per Short Rest. Unleash Incarnation is a single extra attack with a force-damage kicker, recovers slower on paper (3/Long Rest) yet costs nothing but an already-positioned echo. Spend Action Surge on the turn that actually ends the fight; lean on Unleash Incarnation for the rest.
- *Swap with Echo into Split Strike, versus Second Wind:* same Bonus Action slot, opposite jobs. The swap adds real damage (+1d8, plus +1d6 if the same attack is also an Unleash Incarnation) and does nothing for survivability; Second Wind heals off a pool that only refills one use per Short Rest. Offense when the fight is still winnable on points, Second Wind when it isn't.
- *Weapon Mastery reassignment vs. Le Fil's designated weapon—not the same swap.* Which weapon carries Graze/Sap/Slow can change freely on any Long Rest. Le Fil's bonus stays locked to whatever weapon it's currently wrapped around and only moves if that weapon is lost or destroyed (reassignable on a Short or Long Rest then). Don't assume repointing one repoints the other.

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to win someone over fast—information, a favor, a room's goodwill → **Persuasion +7** or **Deception +7** *(skill checks)*—a professional life spent reading wealthy strangers doubles as social engineering anywhere.
- A fight needs to not happen, or a threat needs backing up → **Intimidation +7**, with **Athletics +8** and STR 20 behind it, doing most of the convincing before he opens his mouth.
- A crowd needs entertaining, distracting, or working for gossip and tips → **Performance +4**, paired with the **Noble gaming-set proficiency**—years of tableside charm on Saint Barthélemy, repurposed.
- Court etiquette, a noble house's history, or the weight behind a name matters → **History +2** and the **Noble background** itself—rank and protocol read instinctively to him.
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW*—subduing rather than killing, or hauling someone bodily out of a scene → **Unarmed Strike grapple/shove** *(RAW printed trigger: a combat action, DC 16)*; nothing about the mechanic actually requires a fight already underway, worth proposing to the table rather than assuming automatic.
- Need to know whether anyone from his mother's bloodline is nearby → **E's Direction** ([[Le Fil]], 1/day, RAW)—senses range (1 mi. / 10 mi. / farther) and a direction; doesn't reveal who, only that something connected to her is out there.
