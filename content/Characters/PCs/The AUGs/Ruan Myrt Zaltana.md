## Description

She stands a head and more above most rooms she walks into, the way firbolgs do — though little else about her reads as the kind her people usually cut. Pale-skinned rather than mottled grey-green, her ears the only unmistakably giant-kin thing about her, tapering long beneath a wide, weathered witch's hat that's stopped pretending it isn't half-forest itself, small mushrooms pushing up through the brim like it forgot where it ends. Her hair is a deep, unruly red, left to curl however it wants. A heavy belt rides her hip, hung with labeled vials of reagent, remedy, whatever she's mixed that week, and she leans more than she needs to on a gnarled staff threaded through with the same faintly luminous fungus that rings her in a fight. A small, perpetually unimpressed mushroom-capped familiar keeps close at her side, the nearest thing she has to company that talks back. She moves quietly for her size. It's the same care she once brought to a workbench full of reagents, now carried into how she sets her feet on a forest floor. Her eyes are steady, a little tired — she's done her crying in rooms where no one could see, and gotten good, over time, at looking fine anyway.

She lets silence sit rather than fill it. When she speaks it's measured, precise — the same care she once gave a titration, counting drops.

## Origin

Ruan Myrt Zaltana was a chemist, in Glendale, California, on the world she left. She built that title herself, out of a childhood that gave her very little to work with — a father who was the wrong kind of difficult, money that curdled into a family debt she grew up inside of. She studied her way out of it, poured herself into the work until she was good enough at it to matter, and for a while the plan seemed to be working: the debts settled, the job paid well, and she found a husband who loved her without reservation, the kind of man who made the whole miserable climb look, in hindsight, almost worth it.

Then her father died, and something in the arrangement cracked. She turned toward her mother instead — gave her the years she'd spent buried in study, tried to pay back the time she felt she owed — and for a while it held. They called each other most nights that first year, sometimes just to sit on the line without talking. Her marriage stayed her one steady room in a house that kept losing walls. Then her mother died too, five years almost to the season after her father, at the same age he'd been when he went, and Ruan stopped being able to find the pattern in any of it. No grandchildren to hand either of them. Siblings scattered into their own separate silences. And underneath all of it, a growing, quiet certainty that whatever she was owed in this life had already been spent. She knew, professionally, exactly what was in the cabinets at her workplace, and exactly what several of them could do to a person if measured wrong on purpose. She thought about it more than once. What kept her from it, every time, was her husband's face.

It was the anniversary of her mother's death when the dream came. A flower field she'd never stood in, a calm she didn't recognize as hers, voices calling her toward a forest and a portal burning blue-green at its threshold. She has not decided, and may never decide, whether she walked into that dream or whether it walked into her. She arrived on Aenath a firbolg, which she takes as either an enormous joke or exactly the kind of second chance she'd stopped believing existed, carrying a working knowledge of what decay makes possible that she never had to learn twice — it was simply there, waiting in her hands like it had always been. She's noticed the resemblance to what she used to do, right down to the calculation — how much of something poisonous a living thing can survive, and what grows back after.

## Abilities

Her Circle of Spores magic runs on the same instincts her old career did — patience mostly, and an exact, unhurried sense of how much of something a body can take before it turns dangerous. She keeps a symbiotic culture close, a ring of drifting spores she can call up around herself in a fight, turning the same decay she's spent a lifetime being careful around into something that spreads on her own terms. Where she once knew exactly how she could stop her own pain and chose, every time, not to, she now carries that same precision into the field as something that grows. Reagent and remedy, in the same breath.

### Features

- **Druidic** — Know Druidic, the secret language of Druids; can leave/spot hidden messages in it. Always have *Speak with Animals* prepared (does not count against prepared spells).
- **Primal Order — Warden** (chosen 2026-08-26). Level-1 Druid feature: proficiency with Martial weapons, training with Medium armor. The Foundry item was previously an empty stub with no sub-choice applied — update it to reflect Warden if this hasn't been synced yet.
- **Circle Spells** — *Chill Touch* is a Circle of Spores grant per the 2024 text. It is confirmed absent from her live Foundry sheet (checked 2026-08-26) and is therefore not listed in Actions or Spellcasting below. *Blindness/Deafness* and *Gentle Repose* are always prepared and do not count against prepared spells.
- **Firbolg Magic** — *Detect Magic* and *Disguise Self* each once per Long Rest; either can also use spell slots. The additional prepared *Detect Magic* is the normal Druid spell.
- **Speech of Beast and Leaf** — communicate limited meaning to Beasts, Plants, and vegetation; advantage on Charisma checks to influence them. This is not the *Speak with Animals* spell.
- **Powerful Build** — count as one size larger for carrying capacity and push, drag, and lift limits.
- **Wild Companion** — cast *Find Familiar* without material components by expending a spell slot or Wild Shape; familiar is Fey and disappears after a Long Rest.
- **Witherbloom Student** — *Cure Wounds* and *Inflict Wounds* are added to the Druid spell list.
- **Witherbloom Student / Strixhaven Initiate** — *Poison Spray* and *Starry Wisp* are confirmed always-prepared on the live character sheet (one per source); precise attribution between the background and the feat is unresolved, but both are on the standard 2024 Druid spell list, so no house-rule or homebrew grant is needed to explain them.
- **Grown In** — The fungal thread has worked its way into whatever Ruan wears, stiffening it where it needs to hold. Her worn armor functions as a Breastplate (AC 14 + Dexterity modifier, maximum 2) instead of its normal AC. This is not a magical enhancement — mundane-equivalent protection.
- **Reagent and Remedy** — The first time each turn Ruan deals necrotic damage with a spell, or restores hit points with a spell, that effect is increased by 1d6.
- **What It Does To a Body** — Ruan has advantage on Medicine or Nature checks made to determine what a toxin, fungus, or plant will do to a living thing.
- **The Measure of It** — 1/Short Rest, no action. Ruan can touch the ring and know exactly how long a wound, poison, or affliction has before it turns irreversible.
