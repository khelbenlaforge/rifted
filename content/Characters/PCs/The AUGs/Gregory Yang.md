## Description

Gregory keeps a braid at his temple threaded with worked beads, the one piece of himself he never lets the tailoring commit to symmetry. Everything else about him reads composed on purpose — a high collared coat stitched in the quilted greens and worn brass of a house that measures a man by how well his seams hold, a cracked cross pendant that has clearly been touched more than it's been looked at. He smiles easily and holds eye contact a beat longer than strangers expect — a smile with report-card discipline to it, like charm was just another subject he'd learned to test well in.

Watch him work and the polish drops. His sleeves shoved back and his hands quick with the tools, his easy face goes completely still while he's fitting a housing or calibrating a rune-line — the one posture nobody drilled into him.

## Origin

Gregory Yang did everything right, which was the problem. An OCS recruit and government-bonded scholar, home from an overseas degree straight into a desk at MAS — two years in now, contract locked until twenty-nine, at which point the plan already has him sliding sideways into fraud detection like a train changing tracks it was built to change. A girlfriend. A BTO with both their names on the loan. Twenty-six years old and every decision between now and forty already made by a version of him that filled out the forms correctly the first time.

None of it failed him. That's what made it unbearable in a way he couldn't explain at dinner. He wasn't unhappy, exactly — he was provisioned for a life he'd never once been asked whether he wanted, and somewhere along the way the wanting itself had quietly gone out of business.

The ground came up wrong first: solid where it should have given, tilted where it should have held. Only after did he register that the body catching itself against it wasn't quite the one he'd fallen asleep in. The life waiting for him this time came with its own conditions built in before he'd said a word. A banking house doesn't hand its mark to strangers for free — an heir owed the vault before the vault owed him anything, and whatever agreement put Gregory's name in that ledger had already been signed, by hands that weren't his, before he ever opened his eyes in this body. The shape of the arrangement is instantly familiar — he's been living inside one exactly like it his whole life. The only thing that's changed is that this one came with a turret bolted to his back and a reason to point it at something.

## Abilities

Artillerist training runs through Gregory like a second, better-behaved instinct — an arcane cannon he assembles and strikes true with, infusions worked into gear until the equipment starts doing more of the surviving than he does. It reads, on the surface, as the same pattern as everywhere else in his life: something built to run without asking him much. Nobody's pointed out to him yet that this is the first system he's ever built with his own hands. Inherited doctrine, maybe — the calibration's still his, at least.

### Features

- **Artillerist Spells** — *Shield* and *Thunderwave* are always prepared and do not count against prepared spells.
- **Mark of Warding** — *Alarm* and *Mage Armor* are always prepared and each cast free 1/LR; at character level 3, *Arcane Lock* is also always prepared and free 1/LR. These spells can also use slots; *Armor of Agathys* is added to the Artificer spell list.
- **Magic Initiate: Wizard** — *Minor Illusion* and *True Strike* are the two Wizard cantrips (confirmed: Minor Illusion isn't on the Artificer spell list at all per 5etools, so despite Foundry tagging both `sourceItem: class:artificer`, that tag is a live-data artifact — Magic Initiate is the only rules-consistent source for Minor Illusion). The chosen L1 Wizard spell (cast once/day without a slot) isn't identifiable from the live data — none of the other prepared L1 spells is Wizard-exclusive; confirm with the player.
- **Tinker's Magic** — Know *Mending*. As a Magic action while holding Tinker's Tools, create one item from the PHB tinker's-tools list (rope, caltrops, torch, etc.) within 5 ft; lasts until next Long Rest. Uses = INT mod (5) per Long Rest.
- **Replicate Magic Item** — knows four plans; may create up to two different planned magic items at the end of a Long Rest with Tinker's Tools.
- **Eldritch Cannon** — the cannon's firing mode/stat block was not present in the supplied actor snapshot; choose/use it from the live Foundry feature.
- **Resourceful** — gain Heroic Inspiration after each Long Rest.
- **Skillful** — gain proficiency in one chosen skill.
- **Alert** — add proficiency bonus to Initiative; may swap Initiative with a willing, non-Incapacitated ally after rolling.
- **Steady Hands** — The first time each turn Gregory hits with a spell attack, or lands an attack made as part of casting a spell (such as True Strike), he deals an extra 1d6 force damage.
- **Held Ground** — Gregory has advantage on Concentration saving throws while concentrating on a spell.
- **Practiced Composure** — Gregory has advantage on Insight checks made to tell when someone is being performative rather than genuine.
- **Thumb to the Crack** — 1/Short Rest, Reaction. When Gregory is about to make a saving throw, he can touch the pendant as a Reaction and gain advantage on that save. *(Reactive trigger — needs a manual Foundry macro to automate.)*
