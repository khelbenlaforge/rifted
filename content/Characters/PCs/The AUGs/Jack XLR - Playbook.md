# Jack XLR - Playbook

*Companion to [[Jack XLR]] — regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-08-26. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current.*

*No Loadout Selection section: 2024 Bard Spellcasting is a fixed personal list, not a daily-prep list. Jack's six prepared spells change only when he gains a Bard level (swap one, plus fill any new slots) — there's no long-rest re-prep step to build loadouts around.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn before anything else:**
- Ally at 0 HP or about to drop → Bonus Action **Healing Word** (60 ft, no target in melee range required, 2d4+5 heal)
- An enemy is about to swing on Jack or an ally he can see → **Vicious Mockery** (WIS DC 15, 60 ft, cantrip) — on a fail the target also has disadvantage on its next attack roll, which can blunt the very attack Jack is worried about
- Single dangerous enemy needs to be pulled out of the fight entirely → **Dissonant Whispers** (WIS DC 15, 60 ft, L1 slot) — on a fail it takes 3d6 psychic and must immediately use its reaction, if it has one, to move as far away as it can by the safest route (ordinary movement rules apply along the way, including any opportunity attacks allies are positioned to make)

**Action:**
- Multiple enemies grouped in a 15-ft cone from Jack → **Burning Hands** (DEX DC 15, self, 3d6 fire; ½ on success) — this is the Flamekin racial version, not a Bard known-spell slot, so it costs nothing and can be thrown every turn
- Multiple enemies grouped and pushing matters (off a ledge, out of a doorway, away from an ally) → **Thunderwave** (CON DC 15, self 15-ft cube, 2d8 thunder + 10-ft push; ½ on success, L1 slot)
- Multiple enemies grouped at range, past Burning Hands' reach → **Shatter** (CON DC 15, 60 ft, 10-ft radius, 3d8 thunder; ½ on success, L2 slot)
- Single target, want damage plus control → **Vicious Mockery** (see Triage) — a failed save also cuts the target's speed by 10 ft until Jack's next turn
- Single tough target, want it gone for a round → **Dissonant Whispers** (see Triage)
- Group needs their attacks and saves worsened for a few rounds → **Bane** (CHA DC 15, 30 ft, up to 3 targets subtract 1d4 from attack rolls and saves; Concentration, 1 min, L1 slot)
- Ranged poke, nothing better available → **Produce Flame** (spell attack +7, 60 ft, 1d8 fire, cantrip) or **Dagger** (+3, 20/60 ft, 1d4+1 pierce)
- Nothing else applies, in melee → **Unarmed Strike** (+1, 5 ft, 1 bludgeon) — worst option on the sheet, last resort only

**Bonus Action** (independent of the Action choice above):
- Ally about to make an important roll → **Bardic Inspiration** (60 ft, ally adds 1d6 to one d20 test within 1 hour, 5/Long Rest)
- An enemy's save just needs to fail *right now* and Jack doesn't want to wait on a d20 → **Unsettling Words** (60 ft, target subtracts 1d6 from its next save) — spends the same die as Bardic Inspiration above; see Comparative Notes
- Ally down → **Healing Word** (see Triage)
- Need advantage on Perception/Insight to catch something invisible, illusory, or disguised → **Clear the Mix** (self, 1 min, 1/Short Rest)

**Reaction:**
- **Gift of Gab** (self, 5 ft) — triggers whenever Jack speaks to another creature; chosen creatures within 5 ft forget his last 6 seconds of speech and instead remember him speaking a spell's verbal component. Mostly a non-combat/infiltration tool (see Non-Combat & Creative Uses) rather than something with a natural combat trigger, but nothing stops using it mid-fight if Jack needs to say something out loud without a nearby enemy actually registering it.

**Movement:**
- No feature grants extra speed or a special movement type — 30 ft, ground only. Favor positioning at 60 ft (Vicious Mockery/Dissonant Whispers/Produce Flame/Bardic Inspiration/Healing Word/Unsettling Words/Clear the Mix's listening range) over closing to melee, where only the Dagger and Unarmed Strike are available.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Burning Hands vs. Thunderwave, grouped enemies in range of both:* Burning Hands is free (racial, no slot spent) and deals more raw fire damage (3d6 vs. 2d8); Thunderwave costs an L1 slot but adds a 10-ft forced push. Default to Burning Hands when damage is all that matters; spend the Thunderwave slot specifically when the push itself solves a problem (off a ledge, out of a doorway, buying distance).
- *Vicious Mockery/Dissonant Whispers vs. the other save spells, for the extra psychic damage:* **One Frequency** only procs on a spell-attack hit or a failed save against Vicious Mockery or Dissonant Whispers specifically — Bane, Thunderwave, Burning Hands, and Shatter don't trigger it, even though they're also save-based. That extra 1d6 psychic is a real reason to reach for VM/DW on a single target over the AoE options when only one enemy is in range of both.
- *Dissonant Whispers vs. Bane, single L1 slot, one enemy the party wants dealt with now:* Dissonant Whispers is a one-shot burst (3d6 psychic plus forced repositioning) with no upkeep; Bane is a sustained -1d4 debuff on up to 3 targets that requires Concentration to hold. Reach for Dissonant Whispers to remove one problem immediately; reach for Bane when multiple enemies are grouped and Jack can afford to protect Concentration for a minute.
- *Bardic Inspiration vs. Unsettling Words, same 5/Long Rest die pool:* Bardic Inspiration is proactive — hand it to an ally before they roll, for any d20 test, within the next hour. Unsettling Words is reactive and narrower — spend the die immediately to subtract from one enemy's save this instant. Give the die away when there's time to wait for the right roll; burn it on Unsettling Words when an enemy's save needs to fail right now and there's no time to set anything up in advance.

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — retroactively erasing what a suspicious NPC actually just heard Jack say, replacing it with the impression he only muttered an arcane component, could let the party talk freely in front of someone dangerous → **Gift of Gab** *(RAW printed trigger: when Jack speaks to another creature, chosen creatures within 5 ft forget his last 6 seconds of speech and instead remember him speaking a spell's verbal component)* — the printed effect already does most of this; the stretch is trusting it to hold up as a full cover story rather than a momentary trick
- Need to confirm a voice, message, or scene is disguised or fake, fast → **Clear the Mix** *(RAW: 1/Short Rest, Bonus Action — advantage on Perception/Insight to detect invisible creatures, illusions, or disguises for 1 minute while actively listening)* — this is the direct mechanical answer to Jack's own audio-forensics background
- Need to catch a whispered detail across a noisy room → **Pulled From the Noise** *(RAW: advantage on Perception checks made to hear)*
- Need to pass a message to an ally that a listening enemy or NPC won't understand → **Thieves' Cant** *(Harper Agent feat, sourced from a 5etools lookup since the sheet's "Harper faction feature" line doesn't spell it out — RAW: knowledge of the secret argot Thieves' Cant)*
- Need to help an ally's attack land from farther back than usual, out of easy melee reach → **Distracting Melody** *(Harper Agent feat, same lookup — RAW: when taking the Help action to assist an ally's attack roll, the enemy being distracted can be within 30 ft rather than 5 ft, provided it can see or hear Jack)*
- Need to pass as someone else at a checkpoint or event → **Disguise Kit proficiency** paired with a **Costume** from Inventory, backed by **Deception +9** *(expertise)* and **Silver Tongue** *(RAW: treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10 on Deception and Persuasion checks)* — an unusually reliable disguise-and-lie combination once all three stack on the same check
- Need to act or see in total darkness → **Flamekin: Darkvision** *(RAW: 60 ft, dim light as bright, darkness as dim)*
- Need to walk through fire, hot coals, or a burning structure without taking damage from it → **Flamekin: Fire Resistance** *(RAW: resistance to fire damage — resistance, not immunity; a big enough fire source still hurts, just half as much)*
- Need a boost on literally any ability check that doesn't already use his proficiency bonus → **Jack of All Trades** *(RAW: +1 to such checks)*
