# Rismawati Bayu - Playbook

*Companion to [[Rismawati Bayu]], regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-23. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

*Two passives fire automatically and never need a branch of their own: **Line Item** adds 1d6 psychic the first time each turn a spell of hers damages a creature, and **Doesn't Balance** gives a creature disadvantage on its next attack the first time each turn it fails a save against her spell. **Reconciled** effectively hands her one extra 1st-level slot per Long Rest (recovered automatically, no action). Note: the Sickle's *(Nick)* tag is inert; nothing on her sheet grants Weapon Mastery.*

**Triage first, check every turn before anything else:**
- **Ally about to drop or already at 0 HP →** **Healing Word** (BA, 60 ft., 2d4+3, L1 slot)
- **Risma herself in danger, wants raw durability →** **Wild Shape** (BA, +9 temp HP, AC 16 if higher; 2 uses, 1/SR, all/LR)
- **Needs to disengage or get above the fight →** **Owlin Flight** (30 ft. fly, equal to walk; blocked only in medium/heavy armor, she's in Leather so it's live)

**Action:**
- **Cluster of enemies at range, worth the slot →** **Moonbeam** (CON 13, 120 ft., 2d10 radiant, half on success, Conc., L2 slot; also forces a shape-shifter to revert on a fail)
- **Cluster close around her, wants a push →** **Thunderwave** (CON 13, self 15-ft cube, 2d8 thunder + 10-ft push on fail, half/no push on success, L1 slot)
- **Single target at range, default poke →** **Starry Wisp** (R +5, 60 ft., 1d8 radiant, at-will cantrip)
- **Single target where the AoE splash matters →** **Ice Knife** (R +5, 60 ft., 1d10 pierce on hit; target + others w/in 5 ft.: DEX 13 or 2d6 cold, L1 slot)
- **Fighting an Aberration/Celestial/Elemental/Fey/Fiend/Undead →** **Protection from Evil and Good** on the ally taking the hits (touch, Conc. 10 min, L1 slot)
- **Wants a battlefield ally →** **Summon Beast** (90 ft., Conc. 1 hr., L2 slot; shares her Initiative, acts right after her)
- **Nothing else worth spending →** **Sickle** (M +2, 5 ft., 1d4 slash) or **Unarmed Strike** (M +2, 1 bludgeon; or STR/DEX 10 grapple/shove)

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **Ally down →** Healing Word (see Triage)
- **Wants durability before a big turn →** Wild Shape (see Triage); while shaped she can still cast *Cure Wounds*, *Moonbeam*, and *Starry Wisp*, nothing else

**Reaction:** none at this level.

**Movement:**
- Starry Wisp and Moonbeam both work fine from a distance; hold range where the fight allows it rather than closing for the Sickle.
- Flight lets her reposition vertically without spending an action.

### Comparative Notes

- *Starry Wisp vs. Ice Knife, single target:* Starry Wisp is a repeatable at-will 1d8, no resource cost. Ice Knife spends a slot for 1d10 plus a 2d6 cold splash on everyone within 5 feet of the target, so it earns its slot when a second creature is standing close enough to catch the explosion, not against a lone target.
- *Moonbeam vs. Thunderwave, a cluster of enemies:* Moonbeam is a sustained, movable ranged AoE and the only one of the two that also reverts a shape-shifter, but it costs concentration and a slot every round she keeps it up. Thunderwave is a one-shot centered on herself with no concentration cost, and it pushes the cluster away from her rather than just damaging it. Reach for Thunderwave when she needs breathing room or can't spare concentration; reach for Moonbeam when the fight will last and the enemies aren't going anywhere.
- *Wild Shape vs. staying in caster form:* Wild Shape trades her full spell list for raw durability, +9 temp HP and AC 16 if higher, but locks her prepared list down to just the three Circle spells while shaped. Shift when the fight calls for a damage sponge; stay caster form when she needs the rest of her kit.
- *Healing Word vs. Cure Wounds:* Healing Word is a Bonus Action for 2d4+3, leaving her Action free to attack or cast something else the same turn. Cure Wounds is a full Action for a bigger 2d8+3. Reach for Healing Word when she still wants to act that turn; reach for Cure Wounds when the extra HP matters more than the tempo.

## Loadout Selection

*Druid is a full-re-prep class (XPHB Spellcasting: "Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change your list of prepared spells, replacing any of the spells"), same shape as Cleric or Wizard. She has 6 regular prepared picks; the three Circle of the Moon spells (Cure Wounds, Moonbeam, Starry Wisp) and the two feature-granted spells (Speak with Animals via Druidic, Find Familiar via Wild Companion) are always prepared and never count against that total.*

**Current default (balanced / unknown expedition type):**
- Goodberry, Healing Word, Ice Knife, Protection from Evil and Good, Thunderwave (1st), Summon Beast (2nd): already a reasonable spread across healing, single-target, AoE, and a battlefield ally.

**Dungeon / combat-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Entangle* (L1, restrains a cluster in a 20-ft square) or *Spike Growth* (L2, turns a corridor into difficult terrain that punishes forced movement) for a multi-room crawl against groups
- Keep: Healing Word, Thunderwave

**Social / exploration-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Charm Person* (L1, a save-based social lever) or *Detect Magic* (L1, sweeps a room for enchantments before she touches anything), fits the same investigative lane as A Second Set of Books
- Keep: Goodberry (sustains a party stretched thin on rations), Healing Word

**Wilderness / travel-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Longstrider* (L1, +10 ft. speed) or *Pass without Trace* (L2, +10 Stealth and no tracks for the whole party) for ground to cover or a route she'd rather not be followed on
- Keep: Goodberry, Healing Word

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to scout ahead or slip past a watch unseen → **Wild Shape** into a small, quiet form, backed by **Silent Feathers**' Stealth proficiency even before she shifts.
- Need to appraise goods, spot a doctored ledger, or trace a hidden financial or logistical connection → **A Second Set of Books** *(Brought Forward)*, advantage on the Intelligence check; also grants Forgery Kit proficiency if a document needs to go the other way.
- Need a private word with a beast, an informant's mount, or a spooked animal → **Speak with Animals** *(always prepared via Druidic, castable as a ritual for no spell slot)*.
- Need to pass a message across a crowded room, or through a wall to someone she knows is behind it → **Message** cantrip (120 ft., only the target hears it and can whisper back).
- Need to leave a note only another Druid could read, or notice one left for her → **Druidic**, the secret language; anyone else needs a DC 15 Investigation check just to notice it's there, and can't decipher it without magic even then.
- Need to read a magically appraised item or ledger with more rigor than the eye alone gives → **Primal Order: Magician**'s WIS-mod bonus to Arcana/Nature checks (+3), stacking with her general investigative angle.
- Need to see in an unlit ruin or tunnel without a torch giving away her position → **Owlin Darkvision**, 120 ft.
- Need a fast escape route or a vantage point without a fight → **Owlin Flight**, same speed as her walk, unless she's in medium or heavy armor (she isn't).
- Need to physically assess an unconscious ally, a sick animal, or a corpse for what happened to it → **Medicine +5**.
- Need to win over or read a wary NPC → **Persuasion +4** or **Insight +3**.
- Need a big check to actually land when it counts, in or out of combat → **Lucky**, 2 points per Long Rest, spend one for Advantage on any D20 Test.
- Need to treat a poison, brew a remedy, or make rations stretch further on the road → **Herbalism Kit +2** or **Cook's Utensils +2**, both proficient.
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW:* reading a bluffing NPC's tell mid-negotiation the way the lenses "find the exact place a stance doesn't balance" → **Doesn't Balance**'s flavor, worth proposing to the table rather than assuming it works; the printed trigger is combat-only, on a failed save against one of her spells.
