## Description

Beau Rockefeller has sandy brown hair combed straight back, with nothing in it to hold it there. His eyes are a vivid cobalt blue, a little too large for his face, and the slope of his nose is a little too smooth. His skin carries no lines at all. Strangers feel something is off before they can say what, and most of them decide not to ask.

He dresses the way the men before him dressed. The suit is dappled dark grey cashmere in a plain, old-fashioned cut, and on his wrist sits a staggeringly expensive watch. He looks like a man at a zoo, observing everyone around him and never quite one of them.

## Origin

Tobias Atticus Rockefeller was born into a very wealthy, very exclusive New York family and spent his life being its heir. He took the finance degrees his family expected and climbed to the board of a major investment bank. He was ruthless on the way up, and his gift made it easy: he never lost money on a deal, and he could not explain how he knew which ones to make. His colleagues called him Midas. He resented the gift because it made him stand out, and he kept taking riskier investments just to see if he could.

His daughter Amelia had him every weekend. The rest of the time he did the ordinary things himself, like buying groceries and watching strangers in a bar. The last thing he saw on Earth was the New York skyline from his office. He turned around, and the office was gone. Amelia's voice was still coming through the phone when the line cut off.

Beau is the name he goes by in [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Lore/Aenath|Aenath]], and it is partly an alias.

## Earth Background

*Player-authored, kept close to their wording.*

My Character's name is Tobias Atticus Rockefeller (Shortened to Tobias A. Rockefeller, T.A. Rockefeller). He goes by Beau (nickname, and also a kind of alias he goes by in this new fantasy world. He is a 10/10 nepo baby, born and raised in the Upper East Side of New York City.
Instead of joining his family's business in Chase Bank or in oil, his parents pulled some strings while he was studying Finance in Princeton (following the steps of his predecessors) and later on Wharton for his MBA. He managed to acquire a job and climb up the ranks of Goldman Sachs, earning a place on the board of directors. He was an exceptional investment banker, having an almost inhumane ability to ascertain whether the market/stock was going to do well or not. That being said, he is extremely cunning and ruthless when working his way up to the top. There was little he would not do with the right amount of cash. He spends most of his time in New York, alternating between his penthouse and his Brownstone, answering calls and emails. His tendency to be a workaholic also led to his only wife to divorce him, leaving with his only daughter (Amelia), whom he sees every weekend. One might say he loves her more than life itself.
Other than that, he stays alone, which is extremely unusual for people of his position. He does not party, but he does go to the occasional work dinner or charity gala (begrudgingly). He has always felt more himself when he's alone. He never felt like he's part of this world since he feels like a person at the zoo, always observing the other humans, but never really feeling like he's participating. He longs for a connection deeper than what he has with his coworkers but he is in denial about this fact.

Many of his issues he faces as an adult can be traced back to his childhood. His family was extremely wealthy and exclusive, meaning the people he brushed shoulders with were really snobby and out of touch. He disliked the flashy, competitive lifestyle of his peers, hence he was mostly outcasted (not that much because his name still had some pull). The one time he did meet a friend was Emilio, a homeless boy he befriended not too far from one of his family's property in Brooklyn. Sweet Emilio offered Beau some of his (limited) sweets after seeing how his mother refused to buy some for him. Beau, touched and taken aback at such an act of generosity, started to hang around the area more. After bumping into Emilio a few times, they actually formed a deep friendship. Emilio went to a nearby public school while Beau went to a private school but that didn't stop them from becoming close friends. They were essentially inseparable till Beau's parents caught him sneaking food from his pantry to Emilio and his family. Afterwards, Beau never returned to that property again.

The next time he felt genuinely moved was when his daughter, the lovely Amelia, asked him an absurd question when they were going to the fair. It was so absurd that he barked out a laugh (surprised that he could even make that sound). She inherited his blue eyes and her mother's dark hair (his was more of a sandy brown). She was his everything. And the divorce from his arranged marriage took her away from him (none of his money, because his parents insisted on a prenup). Time took more of his daughter's life from him, but he showered her with gifts and made sure to clear his schedule to see her on the weekends. He is an exceptional father, to the shock and disbelief of his coworkers and subordinates.
Despite his extreme wealth, he is surprisingly down-to-earth, enjoying quiet nights at bars, listening to people gossiping after a work day. He loves people watching and doing 'normal people things', like buying groceries, or going to Central Park to have a picnic. His career was the highlight of his life (other than the birth of his daughter). He has never once lost money on an investment he made. He was nicknamed 'Midas' for his 'touch of gold', since he makes profit on every investment he does. Many people think he's just lucky and others ask how he does it, but he can never explain that he just…knows. Deep inside, he dislikes, or even resents this gift, as it makes him stand out more (on top of his name). Because of this, he also takes unnecessary risks to test this ability (aka he keeps on taking riskier investments just to see if he can).

Other than this, he looks way younger than he actually is. There is a kind of agelessness in his smooth and unblemished epidermis. He rarely gets approached, though. People just find his appearance almost unsettling. His eyes might just be a smidge too big or the slope of his nose might just be too smooth. The colours of his eyes were a vibrant, cobalt blue that seemed too piercing to belong on any face. He is never seen without his bespoke suits (his favourite was a dappled dark grey suit, cashmere, of course) cut in a classic style reminiscent of the kind that his predecessors wore. Also, a staggeringly expensive watch on his wrist. His hair was always neatly combed back (no gel, he would never suffocate his follicles like that).

The last thing he saw before being transported into the new world was the New York skyline, in his office. He was discussing the place his ex-wife would drop off Amelia before he turned around and his office (teak table and all) disappeared. His line cut off just as his daughter's exuberant (but slightly muffled) greeting sounded out from the phone.

## Abilities

Beau's pact draws on the Lower Planes. His Fiend patron keeps four spells ready for him whether he studies them or not: Burning Hands, Command, Scorching Ray and Suggestion. When Beau reduces an enemy to 0 Hit Points, he gains temporary Hit Points equal to his Charisma modifier plus his Warlock level, minimum 1. He gets the same benefit when someone else drops an enemy that stands within 10 feet of him.

The Mark of Detection feat, the gift of his background, sharpens how he reads people and places. He adds 1d4 to Investigation and Insight checks, and he casts Detect Magic and Detect Poison and Disease once each per Long Rest without a spell slot. He can also cast them with spell slots. See Invisibility joins them at level 3, and the feat adds a table of detection spells, from Detect Evil and Good up to Legend Lore, to his Warlock spell list. As a Changeling he can use an action to change his appearance and voice, including height, weight and size between Medium and Small, and he can pass for another playable species. His game statistics do not change. While he is shape-shifted this way, he has advantage on Charisma checks.

He wears [[Time Value]], the wristwatch he arrived with. At Level 3 it grants four abilities: Compounding, Second Hand, Clock Speed and Quick Change.

### Features

- **Pact Magic**: CHA casting. 2 slots, always 2nd level, regained on a Short or Long Rest.
- **Eldritch Invocations**: Pact of the Tome, Agonizing Blast, Pact of the Chain.
- **Agonizing Blast**: add CHA (+3) to Eldritch Blast damage.
- **Pact of the Tome**: Book of Shadows conjured at the end of a Short or Long Rest: 3 cantrips and 2 level 1 Ritual spells from any class, prepared as Warlock spells; also a Spellcasting Focus. (Foundry shows Guidance, Message and Prestidigitation as the cantrips and Identify and Unseen Servant as the rituals.)
- **Pact of the Chain**: Find Familiar as a Magic action with no slot, with special forms; when you take the Attack action you can forgo one attack so the familiar attacks with its Reaction.
- **Magical Cunning**: 1 minute rite: regain expended Pact slots up to half your maximum (round up); 1/Long Rest.
- **Fiend Spells**: Burning Hands, Command, Scorching Ray and Suggestion are always prepared; Fireball and Stinking Cloud join at Warlock level 5.
- **Dark One's Blessing**: when you reduce an enemy to 0 HP, or someone else does within 10 ft of you, gain temp HP = CHA mod + Warlock level (6 now, minimum 1).
- **Mark of Detection** *(House Medani Heir)*: Deductive Intuition adds 1d4 to Investigation and Insight checks. Magical Detection: Detect Magic, Detect Poison and Disease and (from level 3) See Invisibility are always prepared and castable once each without a slot per Long Rest, or with slots. Spells of the Mark (Detect Evil and Good, Identify, Detect Thoughts, Find Traps, Clairvoyance, Nondetection, Arcane Eye, Divination, Legend Lore) join the Warlock list. The casting ability was chosen with the feat (CHA assumed).
- **House Medani Heir**: Insight and Investigation proficiency, Disguise Kit, and an ability score increase among DEX, INT and WIS.
- **Changeling Instincts**: proficiency in two of Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Performance or Persuasion.
- **Shape-Shifter**: as an action (a Bonus Action with Time Value), change appearance, voice, height, weight and size (Medium or Small); can pass as another playable species; no change to statistics, clothing or equipment; advantage on CHA checks while shifted; stays in the form until you revert (same action cost).
- **Time Value** *(growth item, Tier 1: Principal)*: **Compounding:** once per turn, when a spell you cast damages one or more creatures you already damaged earlier in this combat (including earlier on the same turn), one of them (your choice) takes an extra 1d6 psychic. **Clock Speed:** a Ritual casting takes only 1 minute longer, not 10; no slot. Quick Change is in Bonus Actions and Second Hand in Reactions.
