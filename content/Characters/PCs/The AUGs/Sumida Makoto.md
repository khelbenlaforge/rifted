## Description

Makoto keeps his hair cropped close at the sides and swept back on top. His ears are long and pointed. A furrow sits between his brows, his eyes are wide, and his lips are slightly parted, as though someone has just asked him a question he did not catch.

He wears a sleeveless wrap vest over a high-collared undershirt, closed with a knotted sash whose tail hangs to the thigh. The trousers are loose and gathered at the shin by close wrappings, and the shoes are soft slippers. In one hand he carries a short-necked lute with a flat, ridged pegbox. In the other is a staff bound in bands of cord, topped with a cluster of three crystals. He is lean for his kind, and he stands like someone who expects to be walked past.

## Origin

Sumida Makoto was born in Osaka, a quiet boy whom people kept losing track of. Teachers marked him absent while he sat in the second row. At nineteen he began carrying scenery in a theatre, and a few years later he was a kuroko, a stagehand dressed in black from hood to feet who works in full view of the audience and is not meant to be seen. Makoto was very good at it. Given any set, rig or stage trick, he could tell how it was put together and which one piece, pulled out, would bring the rest down.

At twenty-two he had his first tattoo, done by a woman named Aiko in a second-floor studio. The ink had not yet healed when he knew it was wrong. It was close to something, and it was not that thing. Over the next twelve years he kept going back, until dark curling linework covered his arms, back and chest and still did not satisfy him. He wore long sleeves in August and stayed out of bathhouses.

Makoto never settled anywhere. For nine years he lived in one small flat with the boxes still taped shut in the closet. His colleagues were people he liked, and a few friends remembered to invite him to things about half the time. He felt like a guest who had been shown to a waiting room and then politely forgotten, and he had decided long ago that this was what a life felt like. The last thing he remembers of Earth is the end of an evening performance: the dark of the wings, the hood coming off, the smell of dust and hot lights.

The older hands said he had the eye, and he took their word for it.

He got his first tattoo at twenty-two, from a woman named Aiko who worked out of a second-floor studio in Namba. He knew it was wrong before the ink had healed. He couldn't say what was wrong with it, only that it was close to something and wasn't that thing. So he went back, and he kept going back for twelve years, until his arms, back, and chest were covered in dark curling linework that never satisfied him. Aiko stopped asking what he was looking for. He wore long sleeves in August, stayed out of bathhouses, and let his mother believe what she liked about the company he kept.

He never really settled anywhere. He lived in the same small flat for nine years with boxes still taped shut in the closet. He had colleagues he liked and a few friends who remembered to invite him to things about half the time. He wasn't unhappy. He felt like a guest who had been shown to a waiting room and politely forgotten, and he had long since decided that this was simply what a life felt like.

## Abilities

Makoto's magic is mostly about not being seen. As a Firbolg he can turn invisible as a bonus action until the start of his next turn, and the cloak drops when he attacks, deals damage or forces a saving throw. He can do this a number of times equal to his proficiency bonus between Long Rests. He can also cast Detect Magic and Disguise Self once each per Long Rest, and the Disguise can make him seem up to three feet taller or shorter than he is. He can speak in a limited way with beasts and plants, and they understand him.

The Mark of Shadow came with his background. It adds a d4 to his Stealth and Performance checks, gives him the Minor Illusion cantrip, and lets him cast Invisibility once per Long Rest without a spell slot. As a Sorcerer he can enter Innate Sorcery as a bonus action for a minute, twice per Long Rest. His Sorcerer spell save DC rises by 1 and he has advantage on Sorcerer spell attack rolls while it lasts. He also draws on 2 Sorcery Points to fuel Metamagic. As a Bard he can hand a Bardic Inspiration die (a d6) to a creature within 60 feet who can see or hear him, which that creature can add to a failed d20 Test within the hour.

He carries [[黒衣の竿]]. At Level 3 it grants four abilities: Follow-Through, Step Aside, Well Rehearsed and Board Up.
