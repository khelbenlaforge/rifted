## Description

Fire doesn't hold still, and neither does Jack. What passes for skin on him is banked ember-light under a dark, ashen crust, seams of orange-white heat opening and closing along his jaw and knuckles when he's paying attention to something — which, for a man who spent a decade listening for the one true sound under a thousand false ones, is most of the time. His voice carries a low crackle underneath it, a hiss like tape hiss, like a room that was never quite silent to begin with.

Years alone in a small dark room left their mark on how he carries himself — shoulders in, elbows close, never quite trusting a chair to hold his weight before he's tested it. Loud rooms make the flame under his skin flare and spit. He listens before he plays.

## Origin

For ten years Jack lived in a thousand square feet of soundproofing in Midtown Manhattan, a senior audio engineer working nights, alone with three monitors and the sound of his own coffee going cold. He specialized in spectral editing and acoustic forensics — pulling one whispered sentence out of a wall of noise for corporations and directors who cared only that it worked. Human noise, human company, never quite resolved for him the way a waveform did. He came to think of himself as something dropped onto the wrong planet, and of the world outside his studio as an ugly smear of broken frequencies he had no reason to step into.

The only company he kept was anonymous. Somewhere in the encrypted dark web he became an asset for a handler network he never saw the shape of — whistleblowers and investigative cells, passed hand to hand through people who never gave names. No one showed him a face. Corrupted, wiretapped files simply landed on his server, and he was left alone to work. He cleaned the static off leaked corporate crimes and buried blackmail, not out of any belief in the cause but because the files followed clean mathematical logic, and clean logic was the one thing the rest of the world had stopped offering him. His studio was the only place he trusted not to lie to him. His ears did the rest.

It ended at 3:34 AM, mid-master, on a file an anonymous client had no business sending. It bypassed acoustic rendering entirely — crystalline, wrong. He pushed the master fader up to isolate a sub-bass hum underneath it that shouldn't have existed, and the air in the room went solid, then violent. Sound tore the physical space open around him. He remembers pitch black, and then Aenath — waking into a body wreathed in embers, his breath a curl of smoke.

## Abilities

Jack fights the way he used to mix — find the one frequency that matters, cut everything competing with it. His Eloquent bardic voice doesn't waver under pressure or argument the way an ordinary voice does; words land de-essed, scrubbed of hiss, nothing left in them for a listener to grab onto. The fire under his skin answers to the same instinct that used to ride a fader, flaring hotter the instant he locks onto whatever in a room is worth paying attention to.

### Features

- **Bardic Inspiration** — You have five uses per Long Rest; the die is d6.
- **Expertise** — Expertise in Deception and Performance.
- **Jack of All Trades** — Add +1 to ability checks that do not already include your Proficiency Bonus.
- **Silver Tongue** — Treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10 on Deception and Persuasion checks.
- **Flamekin: Darkvision** — See in dim light within 60 ft. as bright light and darkness as dim light.
- **Flamekin: Fire Resistance** — Resistance to fire damage.
- **Flamekin: Reach to the Blaze** — Innate magic allows creation of burning flames in a multitude of forms.
- **Harper Agent** — Harper faction feature.
- **One Frequency** — The first time each turn Jack hits with a spell attack, or a creature fails its saving throw against his Vicious Mockery or Dissonant Whispers, that creature takes an extra 1d6 psychic damage.
- **Nothing Competing** — When a creature fails its saving throw against Jack's Vicious Mockery, its speed is also reduced by 10 feet until the start of Jack's next turn.
- **Pulled From the Noise** — Jack has advantage on Perception checks made to hear.
- **Clear the Mix** — 1/Short Rest, Bonus Action. For the next minute, Jack has advantage on Perception and Insight checks made to detect invisible creatures, see through illusions, or spot a disguise, as long as he's actively listening for it.
