## Description

His scalp is bald and scored with old scars — three pale lines that could be old wounds or something self-inflicted; nobody's asked. The elf in him barely shows at the ears; the points are moderate, nothing dramatic. It's the skin that tells the real story: a grey-white pallor, drow blood surfacing quietly under a half-elf's frame. Sharp cheekbones. A jaw that stays set even when nothing's wrong. One dark tassel earring, worn on the same side as the ink that climbs his right shoulder and down the bicep — black tribal lines coiled tight around a single kanji character he's never explained.

Nothing about what he wears is ornamental: dark, sleeveless wraps, built for movement over comfort. He doesn't fill silences. When he talks, it's short and flat, the same no he used to give clients who wanted the impossible done by Friday. Twenty years he spent perfecting a craft, and somewhere in his forties his knees started reminding him of every one of them. The market stopped caring anyway. His hands are still good at work nobody's buying anymore.

## Origin

Mike Kanbei drew things for a living in Shimokitazawa — storyboards mostly, sometimes ad layouts, once in a while the sleeve for somebody's album — back when a designer's hand still meant something in the finished product. He was good at it. That stopped mattering somewhere in his forties. Clients wanted results in half the time for half the rate, and more of them stopped wanting a person in the loop at all; a prompt did the job now, badly, for free, and badly was apparently good enough.

He lived alone. His days blurred into each other in a way that made the calendar feel decorative. He would sit in front of an open Photoshop file with nothing coming, or just look at the wall until the light outside changed color. Kyoto, his orange tabby, ignored him with a consistency that outdid the rest of the world; even his favorite treat couldn't buy the cat's attention some days. On the harder nights he'd put on the one CCR record he still owned and let "Have You Ever Seen the Rain" spin all the way through, the needle scratching in the runout groove long after the song ended, until he fell asleep at his desk. His mother, seventy and living alone herself, called most evenings to ask if he'd come to dinner. Most evenings, he let it ring.

Exactly when the rut turned into something closer to a loop, he couldn't say — the same day playing back with the serial numbers filed off. What came after is even foggier: one evening an empty Photoshop file, the next, two suns in a sky over [[Corranor]] he had no name for yet. He'd stopped opening the blinds sometime that spring, and by his own count never once noticed the morning they'd have let real daylight back in. [[Aenath]], at least, asks something of the stillness he'd already mastered on Earth. A held breath here means something is about to happen; nobody mistakes it for nothing.

## Abilities

Mike fights in short, deliberate strokes, the same economy he once brought to a storyboard panel, cutting everything that isn't essential. His training as a Warrior of Shadow lets him step through darkness itself: closing distance in an eyeblink, or vanishing from a fight the instant it turns against him. Getting caught losing isn't an option he keeps on the table. He vanishes mid-fight before anyone can watch him go down, an old habit that predates Aenath by years, back when he'd slam the laptop shut before anyone saw an unfinished draft. Whatever the last few years on Earth ground out of him, three levels of monastic training are slowly building back, one stance at a time.

## Key Events

**Session 1 — Feast of the Moon, Year 222 PA:** His mother's call caught him for once, three words in when the light off his monitor started spilling wrong. What followed was a vision shared with four strangers: a floating, edge-terminated world, city-sized rock falling from a sky on fire, an ocean gone from churning to boiling, a spike of light wound in a helix of spiraling stone. He tried to pull a panicking child clear of a falling boulder. He missed the grab. The rock took them both. He came awake a half-elf martial artist inside [[The Drowned Dawn]], cursing in Japanese, demanding to know what had happened to a kid he couldn't have named sober. His CCR record, [[針跡]], was simply there with him, no explanation needed or offered. Step of the Wind opened a path through the temple's shadow creatures; he called the others through by ear rather than sight.

## Relationships

Four strangers who owed him nothing followed a sound they couldn't see the source of, and that was apparently enough. Mike didn't say much beyond the fight itself — the same economy he once brought to a client call, minus the client. He led with his body instead of his words, and let the others decide for themselves whether that was trust or just efficiency.

### Features

- **Martial Arts / Dexterous Attacks** — Use DEX for monk weapons and unarmed strikes; Martial Arts die is d6.
- **Unarmored Defense** — AC equals 10 + DEX + WIS while unarmored and shieldless.
- **Focus Points** — 3 points; regain all on a Short or Long Rest.
- **Uncanny Metabolism** — Once per Long Rest when rolling initiative, regain all Focus Points and gain temporary HP equal to Martial Arts die + Monk level.
- **Unarmored Movement** — Speed increases by 10 ft. while unarmored and shieldless.
- **Shadow Arts** — Use Focus to channel shadow magic, including Darkness.
- **Shadowy Figments** — Can use Focus to cast Minor Illusion.
- **Darkvision** — See in dim light within 60 ft. as bright light and darkness as dim light.
- **Fey Ancestry** — Advantage on saves against being Charmed; magic cannot put you to sleep.
- **Drow Magic** — Know Dancing Lights; Faerie Fire and Darkness are listed racial magic from the live actor data.
- **Harper Agent** — Harper faction feature.
- **Second Pass** — When Flurry of Blows lands a second hit on the same target this turn, that hit deals an extra 1d6 necrotic damage.
- **Already Gone** — When Mike uses Step of the Wind, he can also move through the space of any creature his size or smaller, treating it as difficult terrain rather than an obstacle.
- **The Unfinished Panel** — Mike has advantage on Investigation checks made to notice when something in view has been deliberately altered, arranged, or tampered with.
- **Cut to Black** — 1/Short Rest, Reaction. When Mike drops below half his hit point maximum for the first time in a fight, he can use his Reaction to become Invisible until the start of his next turn. *(Reactive trigger — needs a manual Foundry macro to automate.)*
