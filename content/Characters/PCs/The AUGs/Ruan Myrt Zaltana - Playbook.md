# Ruan Myrt Zaltana - Playbook

*Companion to [[Ruan Myrt Zaltana]] — regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-08-26. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn:**
- Ally at 0 HP or about to drop → Action **Cure Wounds** (touch, 2d8+5 heal) — this also triggers **Reagent and Remedy** the first time each turn Ruan restores HP with a spell, adding another 1d6 on top
- Any creature (ally or enemy) enters or starts its turn within 10 ft of Ruan → **Halo of Spores** pulses automatically (Reaction, CON DC 15, 1d4 necrotic — rolled twice and effectively doubled to about 2d4 while Symbiotic Entity is active) — this is passive and doesn't need Ruan to choose anything, but it only fires once per round since it's still a Reaction; if she's already spent her Reaction this round, it won't trigger again until her next turn

**Action:**
- Enemy adjacent, willing to risk melee range for the ring's bonus damage → **Inflict Wounds** (touch, CON DC 15, 2d10 necrotic, ½ on success, L1 slot) — the first time each turn this deals necrotic damage, it also triggers Reagent and Remedy for another 1d6 (exact interaction with the half-on-success reduction is a DM call, not stated on the sheet)
- Ranged poke, unlimited, target likely to fail a DEX save → **Sacred Flame** (DEX DC 15, 60 ft, 1d8 radiant)
- Ranged poke, unlimited, target has a strong DEX save or heavy armor Ruan wants to bypass with an attack roll instead → **Poison Spray** (spell attack +7, 30 ft, 1d12 poison) or **Starry Wisp** (spell attack +7, 60 ft, 1d8 radiant, target also sheds dim light and can't be Invisible until the end of Ruan's next turn)
- Multiple enemies in a line or funnel, and Ruan can spare Concentration → **Moonbeam** (CON DC 15, 120 ft, 5-ft-radius cylinder, 2d10 radiant on entry or start of turn there, Conc. 1 min) — repeatable each of Ruan's turns by moving the beam; doesn't trigger Reagent and Remedy (radiant, not necrotic)
- Wants sustained combat necrotic damage and 12 temporary HP, and is willing to spend a Wild Shape charge → **Symbiotic Entity** (Action, spend 1 of 2 Wild Shape uses, 10 min) — melee **weapon** attacks (Sickle, not Unarmed Strike — see Comparative Notes) deal an extra 1d6 necrotic on a hit, and Halo of Spores' die is doubled for the duration; see Comparative Notes before spending the charge
- Single humanoid, before or outside active combat → **Charm Person** (WIS DC 15, 30 ft, charmed 1 hr) — the target gets advantage on this save if it's already fighting Ruan or her allies, so this works best as an opener, not a mid-fight fix
- Single tough enemy needs repeated disruption → **Blindness/Deafness** (always prepared via Circle Spells, but still costs an L2 slot to cast — CON DC 15, 30 ft, 1 min, target repeats the save each of its turns)
- Nothing else fits, in melee → **Sickle** (+1, 5 ft, 1d4-1 slash, min. 1) — weak, but the only weapon on the sheet, so it's the one option that can benefit from Symbiotic Entity's melee-weapon bonus; **Unarmed Strike** (+1, 1 bludgeon) is the true last resort and does not get that bonus

**Bonus Action** (independent of the Action above):
- Need to vanish for a turn — escape, reposition, or set up a surprise — without touching the Wild Shape pool → **Hidden Step** (2×/Long Rest, self, Invisible until the start of Ruan's next turn or until she attacks, deals damage, or forces a save)
- Need to actually become a beast (scouting, swimming, escaping in a small form) rather than fight as herself → **Wild Shape** (spend 1 of the same 2 Wild Shape uses as Symbiotic Entity, assume a known CR ¼ Beast for up to 1 hour) — she can't cast spells at all while transformed; see Comparative Notes

**Reaction:** Halo of Spores (see Triage) is the only one on the sheet.

**Movement:** 30 ft base, no special movement type. Powerful Build affects carrying/push/drag/lift, not speed.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Symbiotic Entity vs. an actual Wild Shape into a Beast, same 2/Short Rest pool:* Symbiotic Entity keeps Ruan as herself — full spellcasting, 12 temporary HP, the melee-weapon necrotic bonus, and a doubled Halo of Spores die. An actual Wild Shape replaces her with the Beast's stat block, grants only 3 temporary HP (equal to Druid level), and she can't cast spells at all while transformed (though shapeshifting doesn't break an already-running Concentration spell). Default to Symbiotic Entity in a fight where she still wants to cast; spend an actual Wild Shape charge on exploration/utility instead, where losing spellcasting costs nothing.
- *Symbiotic Entity's melee-weapon bonus — Sickle vs. Unarmed Strike:* the +1d6 necrotic only applies to melee **weapon** attacks per the feature's own text. Sickle qualifies; Unarmed Strike, not being a weapon attack, does not — easy to assume both do since the sheet's compressed cell just says "melee hits."
- *Find Familiar's cost — a spell slot vs. a Wild Shape charge:* both are real options on the sheet. A spell slot competes with Inflict Wounds/Charm Person/Faerie Fire/Detect Magic/Cure Wounds for her four L1 slots; a Wild Shape charge competes with Symbiotic Entity and actual Wild Shape for the same 2/Short Rest pool. Cast it with whichever resource is more likely to go unused that day — commonly a Wild Shape charge after a Short Rest with no fight expected before the next one.
- *Inflict Wounds vs. the three ranged cantrips:* Inflict Wounds is the only necrotic-and-spell combination anywhere on this sheet that benefits from Reagent and Remedy's bonus die, but it costs an L1 slot and requires touch range. The cantrips are unlimited and safe at range, but none of them qualify for the ring's bonus (Sacred Flame and Starry Wisp are radiant, Poison Spray is poison). Reach for Inflict Wounds specifically when she's already in melee and the extra die matters; stay on cantrips otherwise.
- *Reagent and Remedy — what actually triggers it:* only necrotic damage or healing dealt **by a spell**. It does not trigger on Halo of Spores (a Reaction, not a spell) or on Symbiotic Entity's melee-weapon necrotic bonus (a feature riding on a weapon attack, not a spell being cast) — both were deliberately excluded when this item was designed. Among everything on the sheet, only Inflict Wounds' damage and Cure Wounds' healing actually qualify.

## Loadout Selection

*Druid prepares from its full class spell list and can swap the whole list after every Long Rest — pick the set below that matches the next expedition. Candidates below are pulled from the full modern Druid L1–L2 spell list, plus Inflict Wounds (added to Ruan's spell list specifically by Witherbloom Student, not a standard Druid-list entry) — not just what's currently prepared.*

**Dungeon / combat-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Spike Growth* (L2) — turns a corridor or room into difficult, damaging terrain, good for a party that can funnel enemies through it
- Swap in: *Flaming Sphere* (L2) — a second sustained, movable damage option alongside or instead of Moonbeam
- Keep: *Inflict Wounds*, *Moonbeam* — already strong single-target and area damage

**Social / exploration-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Animal Friendship* (L1) — pairs naturally with Speech of Beast and Leaf for wilderness diplomacy
- Swap in: *Pass without Trace* (L2) — group stealth for scouting or slipping past a guarded area
- Keep: *Detect Magic*, *Charm Person* — already useful outside a fight

**Wilderness / travel:**
- Swap in: *Longstrider* (L1) — extra travel speed for the party
- Swap in: *Locate Animals or Plants* or *Locate Object* (L2) — fast information-gathering before committing to a route
- Keep: *Faerie Fire* — doubles as a way to strip an invisible creature's edge even outside a fight

**Balanced / unknown expedition type:**
- Default to what's currently prepared — *Charm Person, Cure Wounds, Detect Magic, Faerie Fire, Inflict Wounds* (L1), *Moonbeam* (L2) — already a reasonable spread across control, healing, utility, and damage

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to leave a hidden message only a fellow druid would recognize, or spot one someone else left → **Druidic** *(RAW: the secret language of Druids, including leaving/spotting hidden messages)*
- Need to get a general read on, calm, or influence an animal or hostile-seeming plant without full conversation → **Speech of Beast and Leaf** *(RAW: limited meaning plus advantage on Charisma checks to influence Beasts and Plants — distinct from the Speak with Animals spell below, which gives full communication but only with Beasts)*
- Need to actually hold a conversation with an animal → **Speak with Animals** *(always prepared, ritual-castable, 10 min)*
- Need to identify what a toxin, fungus, or plant will do to a living thing → **What It Does To a Body** *(RAW: advantage on Medicine or Nature checks for this)*
- Need to know how much time is left before a wound, poison, or affliction turns irreversible → **The Measure of It** *(1/Short Rest, no action — touch the ring)*
- Need to pass as someone else, or look noticeably taller or shorter than she is → **Disguise Self** *(Firbolg Magic, 1/Long Rest, alters appearance for 1 hr, up to ±3 ft in height)*
- Need to scan a room for magic without spending a spell slot → **Detect Magic** *(Firbolg Magic grants one free cast per Long Rest, separate from casting it as her prepared Druid spell)*
- Need to preserve a body against decay, or buy more time before it's too late to raise someone → **Gentle Repose** *(always prepared, ritual-castable — extends the resurrection window by 10 days)*
- Need to carry, drag, or force open something well past her own size → **Powerful Build** *(RAW: counts as one size larger for carrying capacity and push/drag/lift limits)*
- Need to brew a remedy, identify a plant on sight, or otherwise work like the chemist she used to be → **Herbalism Kit proficiency**, paired with the **Book About Plant Identification** from Inventory
- Need to mark a spot or object as a light source without burning fuel → **Light** *(cantrip, touch, 20 ft bright / 20 ft dim, 1 hr)*
