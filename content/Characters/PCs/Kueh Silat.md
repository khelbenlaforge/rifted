### Features

- **Weapon Mastery** — Quarterstaff *(Topple)*: on hit, target makes CON 14 save or gains Prone (optional, your choice per hit); can swap assignment on Long Rest
- **Extra Attack** — Attack action makes 2 attacks
- **Dexterous Attacks** — use DEX for attack and damage rolls with Monk weapons and Unarmed Strikes
- **Unarmored Defense** — AC = 10 + DEX + WIS (16 base) + Bracers of Defense (+2) = 18 while wearing no armor
- **Monk's Focus** — 5 Focus Points/LR; fuel Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind, Hand of Harm/Healing, Stunning Strike, Deflect Attacks redirect
- **Uncanny Metabolism** — on Initiative roll: regain all Focus Points + regain Monk level (5) + 1d8 HP; 1/LR
- **Implements of Mercy** — Proficiency: Insight, Medicine, Herbalism Kit; includes Healer feat; grants Mercy Mask
- **Healer (Healing Rerolls)** — when rolling a die for healing, reroll 1s and use new result
- **Clockwork Amulet** — when making an attack roll, may forgo rolling to treat the d20 as 10; 1×/dawn
- **Elven Lineage (Wood Elf)** — base speed 35 ft; Druidcraft cantrip; Longstrider 1/LR; Pass without Trace 1/LR; spellcasting ability WIS
- **Fey Ancestry** — Adv. on saves vs. Charmed; immune to magical sleep
- **Trance** — don't sleep; 4 hrs Trance = Long Rest
