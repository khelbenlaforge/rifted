## Description

Small, sharp-eared, and creased at the eyes from decades of squinting through a viewfinder in bad light, K wears skin an off-shade of green he's never had a real name for — he's settled for "goblin" as close enough, and nobody in this world has questioned it twice. His fingers rarely sit still, always finding something to tap or adjust, a habit left over from years of gear that needed constant fussing to keep working. He dresses for a border crossing that might happen any morning, nothing on him he wouldn't leave behind without a second thought.

What used to be a camera bag has become something stranger since Aenath got its hands on him: a rig of plating and tools slung across his back and shoulders that reads less like armor than an instrument he hasn't finished tuning, still smelling faintly of solder. He hasn't fully decided if it's protection or just the next iteration of the same old need to document what's in front of him before it disappears.

## Origin

Keith Poe, who goes by K or Kay, was a nomad long before the rift swallowed him that day. A mixed-heritage Eurasian independent journalist — half British, half Singaporean — he spent his adult life drifting between borders with a camera and an obsession.

While the mainstream press covered inane stories and geopolitical grandstanding, Keith chased the quiet horror nobody else wanted to see: the people who vanished without a trace. Funded by an ultra-wealthy patron who paid his bail and flight tickets, Keith never belonged in a normal 9-to-5 world, cursed by an obsession with proving that our reality was leaking.

And one day, he was proven right.

## Abilities

K's investigator instincts didn't retire when the by-line did. The same eye that once tracked a disappearance across three countries now steers a tinkerer's hands, building and rebuilding a suit of arcane armor around himself, plates going on the way notes used to go into a file. He fights the way he used to chase a story: patient, unwilling to call it finished until he's seen the whole thing through.

He stepped away to take it, and the walk toward the riverbank stopped being a walk at all.

- 29th Uktar, Year 222 PA: came to in [[The Hollowreach]], misrouted there by [[Layla]] when he should have joined the Stormberg route. First contact with Roberto went badly—Keith lunged at him in a panic that read as an attack, then broke for the treeline shouting at him to run.
- 29th Uktar, Year 222 PA: survived the Shadow Creature ambush that followed with Roberto, [[!D'bleh|Gaslight]] and [[Fern]], running until the ground stopped being wrong. Whatever the boundary was, the things chasing them would not put a foot over it. When the bats went up he was still on the ground; they could not carry five.

## Session 3 Update

- 29th Uktar, Year 222 PA: [[Kili]] arrived, which made three of them, which made the thing [[Layla]] wanted possible.
- 29th Uktar, Year 222 PA: the doorway was the part he could not let go of. A door standing in a tree line with nothing holding it up, edges that refused to sit right in a way nobody could put words to, and a barrier across it that had stopped Layla cold and would not stop them. She could not explain why. He wrote that down.

- 30th Uktar, Year 222 PA: out the far side into daylight, thirtieth of Uktar, with a fourth body between them who had still not opened his eyes.

- Feast of the Moon, Year 222 PA: at the outer gate a watchman's hand went toward his belt and came off it again the moment torchlight caught brass. The town was mid-Feast, names of the dead being read in doorways. Three streets in, straight to the Wallwatch, no wandering.

The vision took his soul with it when it ended — the same thing that happened to Roberto's, nothing like whatever happened to Kili's. None of it made sense the next morning either.
