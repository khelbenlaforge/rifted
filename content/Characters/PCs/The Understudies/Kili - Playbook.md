# Kili - Playbook

*Pronouns not set on the PC note — using they/them by default.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn before anything else:**
- **Ally down or dying →** **Healing Word** (BA, 60 ft., 2d4+4 HP).
- **Ally about to roll something important →** give **Bardic Inspiration** (BA, 60 ft., 1d6 die, 4/LR) *before* the roll so they can add it if they fail.
- **Enemy about to succeed an attack, check, or save you can see, within 60 ft. →** **Silvery Barbs** (Reaction, forces a reroll using the lower result, then grants adv. on the next atk/check/save to a creature of your choice, self allowed).
- **An enemy's about to make a saving throw and you have a Bardic Inspiration use to spare →** **Unsettling Words** (BA, 60 ft., expends 1 BI use, target subtracts the die from that save).

**Action:**
- **Enemies clustered or funneling through one spot →** **Cloud of Daggers** (60 ft. placement, 5-ft. cube, 4d4 slash once/turn per creature, Conc., movable 30 ft. as a Magic action).
- **One strong melee threat you want disabled for several turns →** **Tasha's Hideous Laughter** (WIS 14, 30 ft., fail: Prone + Incapacitated, new save each turn or on damage, Conc.).
- **One target you want gone from melee right now →** **Dissonant Whispers** (WIS 14, 60 ft., 3d6 psychic; fail forces it to flee via its reaction).
- **Need someone Charmed into a specific, harmless-sounding act →** **Suggestion** (WIS 14, 30 ft., Conc., up to 8 hrs).
- **Cheap single-target damage with a built-in debuff →** **Vicious Mockery** (WIS 14, 60 ft., 1d6 psychic; fail also gives disadv. on its next attack).
- **No spell worth spending →** **Dagger** (M/R +5, 1d4+3 pierce) or **Unarmed Strike**.

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **Ally about to roll →** **Bardic Inspiration** (see Triage).
- **Enemy about to save →** **Unsettling Words** (see Triage).
- **Need another Bardic Inspiration die out without touching your own 4/LR pool →** **Chop Chop: Rider Clause** (1/SR) — grants an ally the die for free.
- **Ally down →** **Healing Word** (see Triage).

**Reaction:**
- **Any attack, check, or save you can see about to succeed →** **Silvery Barbs** (see Triage).

**Movement:** No special mobility this level — 30 ft. Speed. Cloud of Daggers, once placed, can be dragged 30 ft. per turn as a Magic action if enemies try to walk out of it.

**Resource note:** **Chop Chop: Standing Ovation** *(passive)* refunds one spent Bardic Inspiration use, up to your normal max, whenever any creature you can see drops an enemy to 0 HP — don't hoard Bardic Inspiration out of scarcity mid-fight if kills are landing around you; it tends to come back.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Bardic Inspiration vs. Chop Chop: Rider Clause, topping up an ally's die:* both grant the same 1d6 die, but Bardic Inspiration draws from the same 4/LR pool Unsettling Words also spends from, while Rider Clause is a separate 1/SR use that doesn't touch that pool at all. If Unsettling Words or another round of Bardic Inspiration looks likely later, spend Rider Clause first and keep the pool intact for that.
- *Dissonant Whispers vs. Tasha's Hideous Laughter, one strong melee threat:* Dissonant Whispers deals real damage immediately and, on a fail, forces the target away via its own reaction — no ongoing commitment from you. Tasha's Hideous Laughter deals no damage but can lock the target down turn after turn as long as your concentration holds, with a new save each turn or whenever it takes damage. Dissonant Whispers gets the threat gone this turn. Tasha's Hideous Laughter trades that for a lock lasting several turns instead, provided concentration survives.
- *Vicious Mockery vs. Cloud of Daggers against a cluster:* Vicious Mockery is a cheap, repeatable single-target cantrip with a built-in debuff on a fail. Cloud of Daggers spends a 2nd-level slot and concentration but hits every creature that enters or starts its turn in the 5-ft. cube, once per creature per turn. Cloud of Daggers earns its slot once enemies start stacking in one spot — against a lone target, or with concentration already spent elsewhere, Vicious Mockery does the same job for free.
- *Silvery Barbs vs. Lucky, defending against an incoming hit:* Silvery Barbs forces the attacker to reroll and take the worse result and also hands advantage to whoever you choose next, but — like Dissonant Whispers on this same sheet — its dash in the Recharge column just means no special tag applies, not that it's free; it spends a 1st-level slot from the same 4×1st/2×2nd pool as her other leveled spells. Lucky imposes disadvantage on the same attack instead, from a separate, always-available pool of only 2 points that also covers your own failed checks and costs no slot at all. When slots are the scarcer resource, Lucky is free where Silvery Barbs isn't; when the wider scope — any creature's success within 60 ft., not just an attack against her — or the extra advantage rider matters more than the slot, Silvery Barbs earns its cost.

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Becoming someone else entirely for an infiltration, disguise, or cover job → **Shape-Shifter** (Action, at will — change appearance, voice, height, and size Medium↔Small; adv. on CHA checks while shape-shifted)
- Need the con to survive real scrutiny while shape-shifted → **Closer's Instinct** *(Chop Chop passive, RAW: advantage on Persuasion/Deception checks while shape-shifted)*, stacking with **Silver Tongue** *(RAW: treats a 9-or-lower on a Persuasion/Deception roll as a 10)*
- A contract, promise, or handshake deal might secretly favor someone other than who agreed to it → **Second Face** *(Chop Chop, 1/Long Rest — press the chop against an agreement stand-in to sense who it really favors, in broad strokes)*
- Need a whispered aside across a crowded room with no one else hearing → **Message** (120 ft.; hears a whispered reply)
- Talking her way past someone who should say no → **Persuasion +8 (expertise)**, boosted further by Silver Tongue
- Need to catch a lie or read what a room's really feeling → **Insight +5 (expertise)**
- Intimidating someone without a fight → **Intimidation +6**
- Need a check in something you were never actually trained in → **Jack of All Trades** *(RAW: adds half proficiency bonus, rounded down, to any ability check that doesn't already add proficiency)*
- One more shot at a check that just failed → **Lucky** (2 points; spend 1 for advantage on your own d20 Test)
- Need to pick a lock or lift something unnoticed → **Thieves' Tools proficiency** + **Sleight of Hand +5**
- A busker's cover, a game of cards to work a mark, or genuine music → **Horn** and **Playing Cards** tool proficiencies
- Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW — Second Face's read on an agreement's true beneficiary could plausibly extend to a spoken promise or verbal deal Kili just witnessed, not only one she physically stamps → **Second Face**'s printed trigger only covers a physical stand-in, so this would need the table's sign-off first, not an automatic ruling
