## Description

Burly and brawny, built for hauling and hard labour, Samson wears his early thirties like a man who's spent every one of them working with his hands. His hair is long, tied up in a man bun; his beard is neatly trimmed against an otherwise rough-edged look. Tattoos run up his neck and down both arms in full sleeves, ink laid over muscle earned on scaffolding rather than in a gym. His features carry the mixed cast of Birmingham's Digbeth streets, English by upbringing and Asian by blood, and when the light catches wrong his eyes throw back a faint silver sheen that nobody in his crew has ever asked about.

## Origin

Dropping out of school at fifteen felt less like failure than escape. Classrooms always felt suffocating, walls built to constrain something that didn't fit inside them. Birmingham's soot-choked skies and the cold scaffolding of Digbeth made more sense to him than any lesson ever had. Hard labour was honest work, and he came to depend on his wits and his hands. To the rest of the crew, he was just Samson, another bloke in a high-vis jacket dragging bags of cement up four flights of unfinished stairwell.

To him, the world was shaped by an underlying order no one else could see. When he picked up a carpenter's pencil, layout drafting became effortless, load distributions arriving whole in his mind before he'd finished the sketch. Blueprints scratched onto scrap drywall held exact, flawless proportions, geometric lines that read more like ancient runes than modern engineering. He laughed off the crew's astonishment as blind luck. Deep down, he knew luck didn't explain that kind of precision.

He lived with a quiet, persistent displacement long before the rift ever took him. Standing on high joists, he'd feel a phantom weight beside him, a subtle spatial flicker, as though another version of himself stood ready to catch the load if he slipped. In dark basements his eyes threw back that faint silver sheen, and when a trench wall caved or scaffolding lurched, his body moved on instinct, throwing itself into the gap to shield a mate before his mind had caught up. Samson Flint stayed grounded in Birmingham's mud even as something about the world kept feeling wrong in a way he couldn't name.

## Abilities

Samson fights like he builds, reading the structure of a fight the way he once read load-bearing walls and placing himself exactly where the weight will fall. As an Echo Knight, that old phantom-weight sensation has become literal: he can call up a spectral echo of himself to flank, cover ground, or take a hit meant for someone else, the same instinct that used to throw him into collapsing trenches now given a name and a shape. His aasimar blood surfaces under pressure, the faint silver eye-sheen no trick of nerves.

- **Weapon Mastery** — Greatsword *(Graze)*, Flail *(Sap)*, Javelin *(Slow)*: see Actions table for each property; can swap one weapon assignment on a Long Rest.
- **Fighting Style: Defense** — +1 AC while wearing armor (already included in AC above).
- **Tactical Mind** — can expend a Second Wind use on a failed ability check instead of healing: roll 1d10, add to the check (may turn it into a success); if it still fails, the use isn't spent.
- **Unleash Incarnation** — when taking the Attack action, make one additional melee attack from the echo's position. Usable a number of times equal to CON mod (3/day); all uses recharge on a Long Rest.
- **Rune Shaper** (Rune Carver background feat) — knows 1 rune at this level (half proficiency bonus, rounded down), chosen from the Rune Spells table (Cloud/Fog Cloud, Death/Inflict Wounds, Dragon/Chromatic Orb, Enemy/Disguise Self, Fire/Burning Hands, Friend/Speak with Animals, Frost/Armor of Agathys, Hill/Goodberry, Journey/Longstrider, King/Command, Mountain/Entangle, Stone/Sanctuary, Storm/Thunderwave). Inscribe the known rune on a nonmagical object each Long Rest to temporarily learn its 1st-level spell until the next Long Rest; invoke it once free per Long Rest, or cast with a spell slot (Samson has none as a non-caster). Spellcasting ability is INT, WIS, or CHA (chosen when the feat was taken). **Which rune Samson knows is not yet set in Foundry — pending player choice.**
- **Celestial Resistance** — resistance to Necrotic and Radiant damage.
- **Darkvision** — see in dim light within 60 ft as if bright light, and in darkness as if dim light (no color in darkness).
- **Light Bearer** — knows the Light cantrip; Charisma is the spellcasting ability for it.
