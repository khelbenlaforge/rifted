## Description

Fur the color of banked ash, a mane that never sits still, windblown even indoors as if it remembers being caught mid-transit between worlds. !D'bleh's eyes have gone pale, near-luminous blue, the kind of glow that makes people forget to finish their sentences when the stare lands on them. He favors a low, coiled stance, weight forward on the balls of the feet, claws always half-bared. It reads less as aggression and more as habit: the readiness of someone who learned that new places rarely announce their dangers in advance.

Ornate dark armor, all silver filigree and deep-set jewels, sits over him like it was tailored by someone who understood ceremony as much as combat. A long blue cloak trails and billows around him even at rest, threaded through with something that catches the light like starfield, maybe a leftover of whatever crossing brought him here. When !D'bleh draws his blades, he doesn't reach for a sheath: twin edges of raw psychic light gather into his hands from nothing, humming faint and cold, gone again the instant the fight ends.

## Origin

Back on Earth, !D'bleh's other life was a lawyer's life — Stellenbosch, South Africa, a 9-to-5 that filed itself into years without ever quite becoming a life. The passport gathered dust. The wish, when it came, was simple and childish: to snap his fingers and simply *be* somewhere else, anywhere else, on any given day.

People assumed the travel was about food, culture, postcards. Underneath that was a habit formed at five years old, in an alley, at the hands of a gang of ruthless six-year-olds who took a child's wallet and left behind something harder to name. Since then, every city !D'bleh has passed through has gotten the same quiet audit: who here preys on the innocent, and what can be done about it before I leave. Growing up, he'd learned how safe a city could feel when it was looked after properly — and he never stopped chasing that feeling for other people, one street at a time.

Whatever pulled him across into Aenath didn't ask permission any more than the wish did. !D'bleh woke up leonin — claws that were already his in some truer sense, a mane, a body built for the kind of quiet, decisive violence his Earth self never had the leverage to deliver. The purpose held. It just finally has teeth.

## Abilities

!D'bleh is a Soulknife rogue in the truest sense: his blades are as much mind as metal, psychic energy given a killing edge. Sneak Attack turns a moment's distraction into a decisive strike. Cunning Action keeps him a half-step ahead of anyone trying to pin him down, and Psychic Blades let him draw a weapon out of nothing but focus when steel isn't at hand or isn't subtle enough. Expertise sharpens what he's already good at into something closer to certainty. The whole style is built for the kind of work he came looking for: get close, solve the problem, disappear before anyone's sure what happened.

### Features

- **Expertise** — Intimidation and Stealth use double proficiency.
- **Sneak Attack** — Once per turn, add 2d6 damage on a hit with a Finesse or Ranged weapon if you have adv., or if an ally is within 5 ft of the target and you don't have disadv.
- **Thieves' Cant** — Secret rogue language and cipher.
- **Weapon Mastery** *(approximate — verify current assignments)* — Rogue can use mastery properties of 2 chosen weapon kinds and swap them on a Long Rest. Dagger *(Nick)*: extra Light-weapon attack as part of the Attack action, not a Bonus Action. Shortsword/Shortbow *(Vex)*: on hit + damage, adv. on your next atk vs the target before end of your next turn.
- **Psionic Energy Dice** — 4d6 pool for Soulknife powers; regain 1 die on a Short Rest and all dice on a Long Rest. The first use of each Soulknife power after each Long Rest is free.
- **Psi-Bolstered Knack** — If you fail a proficient skill or tool check, roll 1 Psionic Energy Die and add it; the die is spent only if the roll then succeeds.
- **Darkvision** — See in dim light as bright light and darkness as dim light within 60 ft.
- **Claws** — Unarmed strikes can deal 1d4+3 slash instead of normal bludgeon.
- **Hunter's Instincts** — Leonin skill choice is Athletics proficiency.
- **Astral Drifter** — Insight and Religion proficiency; Deep Speech; 20d6 years older than you look from time in the Astral Sea.
- **Magic Initiate** — True Strike and Vicious Mockery are learned cantrips (always available, no preparation needed); Comprehend Languages is the chosen 1st-level spell, always prepared (spellcasting ability: CHA), castable 1/LR without a slot.
