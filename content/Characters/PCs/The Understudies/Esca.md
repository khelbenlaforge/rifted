## Description

Dark hair swept back off a widow's peak, one long fringe cutting loose across the brow to half-shadow his right eye. It looks careless; it isn't. Pointed ears, the fine bone structure Corellon left on all his children, a face too smooth and too symmetrical to be read for age the way a human's would be, which makes the grin that splits it look all the more wrong. It's wide, fast, and shows too many teeth, the expression of a man who has just spotted the exploit in the system and can't quite help himself. His eyes are sharp, narrow, canted, an elf's keen sight behind a stare that never quite stops calculating angles, exits, tells.

Mid-spell, mid-argument, half a second from doing something reckless: that's when arcing threads of pale blue-white energy crawl up from his jaw to flicker at his temple, the same magic he uses to turn aside a blow gone visibly restless under his skin. He wears a high-collared white coat over dark olive at the shoulder, plain and durable, chosen for a man who expects to need to move fast in it.

Elf features aside, nothing about him reads as timeless the way a 750-year lifespan should. Fey Ancestry and a body that will barely age make no difference to a mind that's already spent one whole life burning out early — Esca wears his new immortality like it's wasted on him.

## Origin

Esca was born poor, in Mokpo, a port city on the southern coast of South Jeolla Province, the sort of place people tend to leave rather than boast of being from. His family had nothing to give him except long hours and short tempers, and by the time he was old enough to notice, he'd already noticed that nothing in his life was ever hard enough. School bored him. Chores bored him. Even the other kids' cruelty bored him, too easy to route around. Starcraft was the first thing that pushed back.

He was good almost immediately, and then he was frightening, and by his teens he'd done what no one from a background like his was supposed to do: fight his way into the GSL as the league's first Royal Roader, a nobody who'd clawed up through nothing but ladder points and obsession. For a while, Esca was the story, the poor kid from Mokpo who out-built and out-thought players with a decade of academy polish on him. His parents didn't see it as a miracle. They saw a son who'd thrown away a future for a keyboard, and when he chose the pro circuit over anything resembling their idea of a life, they disowned him. He didn't grieve it much at the time. He had a stage now, and the stage was the only thing that had ever felt like enough.

It didn't last. The compulsive need for a challenge sharp enough to feel real, the same appetite that made him great, found its way to match-fixing, and the fall was as public as the rise had been. Conviction, years inside, and a release into a world that had already moved on to the next prodigy. He came out with no team, no sponsors, no parents, and no idea what to do with a mind built entirely around outplaying someone. Legitimate work felt like the same old boredom in a cheaper suit. Crime, at least, had stakes. Heists had the shape of a match: scout the opponent, find the exploit, execute under pressure, get out clean. It was the first thing since the GSL finals that made him feel anything.

Now, thirty and past whatever prime he had, Esca is a man who has already lived and lost one entire life's worth of glory. He isn't sure the second one, whatever this is, is worth building. Aenath didn't ask him if he wanted a third. It just handed him pointed ears, an elf's sharpened senses, and centuries he never asked for on top of a life he'd already half-wasted once.

The hunched shoulders, the thousand-yard stare at a monitor, reflexes tuned to a screen that no longer exists: whatever softness the game once left on him has hardened into something leaner and warier in this new body. He moves like a man used to being watched, and used to it going badly, shoulders squared for a fight, eyes flicking to exits before he's finished a sentence. There's a stillness to his hands that unsettles people who don't know why, the same stillness a sniper or a safecracker learns, minus the excuse of a legitimate trade. He doesn't talk about Korea unless pushed, and even then only in fragments. When he does talk, it's fast and clipped, oddly formal, the cadence of someone who spent more years narrating his own reflexes to a livestream than making conversation with people in a room.

## Abilities

Whatever Aenath handed him slotted into reflexes that were already inhuman. The hand-eye coordination and split-second decision-making of a former pro gamer translate disturbingly well into a fight, or a heist, or anything that rewards reading a situation half a second before everyone else does. He treats combat and crime alike as a solved system waiting to be exploited: probe for the weakness, commit the exploit, don't flinch when the read is wrong. ### Features

- **Darkvision** — see in dim light 60 ft. as bright, darkness as dim (no color).
- **Fey Ancestry** — advantage on saves vs Charmed; immune to magical Sleep.
- **Keen Senses** — proficiency in Perception (High Elf Lineage choice).
- **Trance** — 4-hr meditative trance satisfies a Long Rest instead of 8-hr sleep.
- **Elven Lineage (High Elf)** — knows *Prestidigitation* cantrip (INT-based); +1 language of choice.
- **Spellcasting** — INT-based; spellbook holds all known spells; can prepare INT mod + Wizard level spells (8 at level 3) daily, swappable after a Long Rest. Ritual-tagged spells in the book can be cast unprepared via Ritual Adept.
- **Ritual Adept** — can cast any Ritual-tagged spell from the spellbook without preparing it (must read from the book).
- **Arcane Recovery** — 1/day after a Short Rest, recover expended spell slots totaling ≤ half Wizard level (round up, 2 levels' worth), no slot of 6th level+.
- **Scholar** — expertise (double proficiency) in Investigation.
- **Arcane Deflection** *(War tradition)* — Reaction, when hit by an attack or fail a save: +2 AC vs that attack or +4 to that save. Can't cast spells above cantrip until end of next turn after use.
- **Tactical Wit** *(War tradition)* — add INT modifier (+5) to initiative rolls.
- **Alert** *(Criminal background feat)* — add proficiency bonus to Initiative; can swap Initiative with a willing, adjacent ally at the start of combat; can't be Surprised while conscious.
