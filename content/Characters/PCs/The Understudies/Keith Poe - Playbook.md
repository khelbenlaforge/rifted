## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Only one Armor Model is active at a time** (Dreadnaught, Guardian, or Infiltrator—swappable with Smith's Tools on a Short or Long Rest, not mid-fight), so the model-specific attacks below are a pre-fight decision, not a turn-by-turn one.

**Caught in the Flash & Already in Frame (both passive, [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Items/Understudies/The Last Exposure|The Last Exposure]], always on):** the first attack that connects each turn adds +1d6 radiant, and any creature Kay has already hit this fight gets no benefit from hiding, Invisibility, or cover against them. Both trigger on "hits with an attack"—clearly true for anything with an attack roll (the model attacks, Dagger, Unarmed Strike). Thunderwave and Catapult are saving-throw effects with no attack roll at all, so neither triggers either rider. Magic Missile is the one real edge case: its darts auto-hit with no attack roll, but the spell's own text says they "hit"—whether that counts for these riders is a DM call, not settled here.

**Triage first—check every turn before anything else:**
- **Ally down or badly hurt →** **Cure Wounds** (Action, touch, 2d8+5 HP—now genuinely prepared, not a gap anymore).
- **Just rolled a natural 1 on any d20 Test →** **Warping Flesh** triggers automatically: CON save (DC 15) or Stunned until the end of your next turn. Not a choice, just track it.
- **Bloodied and Guardian model is active →** **Defensive Field** (BA, gain temp HP = 4, lost if armor is doffed).

**Action:**
- **Dreadnaught model active, want reach and to shove/pull something off you or an ally →** **Force Demolisher** (M +7, 10 ft. reach, 1d10+5 force; push or pull 10 ft. if the target is ≤1 size smaller).
- **Guardian model active, want to peel attention off an ally →** **Thunder Pulse** (M +7, 5 ft., 1d8+5 thunder; target has disadv. on attacks against anyone but you until the start of your next turn).
- **Infiltrator model active, want range →** **Lightning Launcher** (R +7, 90/300 ft., 1d6+5 lightning; once/turn on hit, +1d6 lightning bonus die).
- **Single target, guaranteed damage regardless of AC →** **Magic Missile** *(always prepared, Armorer)* (120 ft., 3 darts, 1d4+1 force each, auto-hit, splittable across targets).
- **Enemies grouped close to you →** **Thunderwave** *(always prepared, Armorer)* (self, 15-ft. cube, CON 15, 2d8 thunder + pushed 10 ft. on fail; ½ dmg no push on success).
- **An unattended 1–5 lb. object is within 60 ft. and worth throwing →** **Catapult** (DEX 15, 60 ft.; 3d8 bludgeoning to the struck creature and the object on a fail).
- **Need intel instead of damage—a seen creature is hiding something →** **Last Frame** *(The Last Exposure, 1/Short Rest)* (30 ft.; learn one true concealed thing—DM's call on specificity).
- **Nothing else worth spending →** **Dagger** (M/R +3, 1d4+1 pierce, thrown) or **Unarmed Strike** (M +1, 5 ft., 0 bludg. or Grapple/Shove).

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **Dreadnaught model active, need reach or size right now →** **Giant Stature** (self, 1 min: reach +5 ft., becomes Large if room allows, 5/LR).
- **Guardian model active and Bloodied →** **Defensive Field** (see Triage).
- **Need a burst of repositioning →** **Misty Step** *(always prepared, Fey-Touched)* (self, teleport up to 30 ft. to an unoccupied space you can see; 1/LR free—no 2nd-level slot to recast it with yet).

**Reaction** (only one per round):
- **Self or a seen creature starts falling →** **Feather Fall** (60 ft., up to 5 creatures, no fall damage if landing before the effect ends, 1 min).
- **A creature you can see within 60 ft. just succeeded on an attack roll, ability check, or save →** **Silvery Barbs** *(always prepared, Fey-Touched)* (force it to reroll and take the lower result, then hand a creature of your choice—including yourself—advantage on its next attack roll, check, or save within 1 minute; 1/LR free, then a normal 1st-level slot).

**Movement:** Dreadnaught and Guardian both want to be in melee (5–10 ft.); Infiltrator wants range (90/300 ft.) and also grants **Powered Steps** (+5 ft. Speed while active) and **Dampening Field** (advantage on Stealth while active)—neither is captured in any table, but both apply automatically whenever Infiltrator is the active model. Base Speed is 30 ft., but Chain Mail currently caps it at 20 ft. unless the Arcane Armor conversion gets confirmed. Since the model can't be swapped mid-fight, plan the model choice around the expected fight before it starts, not around this turn's positioning; Misty Step is available regardless of model for an extra 30 ft. of positioning once per Long Rest.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Magic Missile vs. a model weapon attack, single target:* Magic Missile auto-hits for a flat 1d4+1 per dart across up to 3 darts, splittable, with no attack roll to miss. A model attack (Force Demolisher/Thunder Pulse/Lightning Launcher) rolls to hit at +7 for a bigger single swing (1d10+5 / 1d8+5 / 1d6+5) that also carries Caught in the Flash's radiant rider on a hit, guaranteed—the rider only turns ambiguous for Magic Missile itself, per the "hit" ruling above. Reach for Magic Missile when a guaranteed result matters more than the bigger, riskier swing.
- *Choosing an Armor Model before the fight:* Force Demolisher (Dreadnaught) trades range for reach and a forced-movement rider; Thunder Pulse (Guardian) trades damage for a control debuff that protects allies rather than Kay; Lightning Launcher (Infiltrator) trades melee presence for range and repeatable bonus damage on a hit. Set on the last Short or Long Rest, not mid-turn—pick based on what the next fight is expected to need.
- *Thunderwave vs. Magic Missile against multiple enemies:* Thunderwave hits everyone in a 15-ft. cube from you at no risk of missing entirely (save for half, no push on success), but needs enemies close. Magic Missile's darts split across separate targets at 120 ft. with guaranteed damage on each, though each dart alone is small. A cluster nearby is Thunderwave's moment; scattered targets at range belong to Magic Missile.
- *Silvery Barbs vs. Feather Fall, same Reaction slot:* only one Reaction fires per round. Feather Fall needs nothing but a falling trigger within 60 ft. and stops the damage outright; Silvery Barbs needs someone to have already succeeded on a roll and only flips that one result plus a future advantage, nothing guaranteed. If both trigger the same round, whichever is actually costing the party something right now wins the Reaction.
- *Cure Wounds vs. letting the party's other casters carry healing:* Cure Wounds costs Kay's Action and a 1st-level slot for a flat 2d8+5 at touch range—solid in a pinch, but every round spent healing isn't a round spent on Magic Missile, Thunderwave, or a model attack. Reach for it when nobody else can reach the downed ally in time; otherwise let it sit prepared as insurance.

- Swap in: *Disguise Self* (alter appearance for 1 hr.) and *Detect Magic* (self, 30 ft., sense magic present and its school)
- Keep: *Identify*—never a bad slot to hold

**Balanced / unknown expedition type:**
- Default to what's currently prepared—it's already a reasonable spread

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Sensing whether magic is present nearby, and what school → **Detect Magic** *(not currently prepared—see Loadout Selection)*, or in the meantime, **Identify** (touch, ritual, 1-min. cast—properties, attunement requirement, charges)
- Setting camp somewhere that isn't safe → **Alarm** (ritual, 30 ft. cast range, wards a 20-ft. cube, alerts you audibly or mentally when a creature you didn't exempt enters, 8 hr.)
- Need to fix a broken tool, lock, or object on the fly → **Mending** (touch, 1-min. cast, repairs a single break/tear ≤1 ft.; free, doesn't count against prepared spells)
- Crafting a one-off magical gadget for a specific problem → **Tinker's Magic** (Magic action with Tinker's Tools in hand, create one Tinker's Magic item from the XPHB list within 5 ft.; usable 5×/day, lasts until Long Rest)
- Need to grab or manipulate something at a distance without getting close → **Mage Hand** (30 ft., carries ≤10 lb., can move up to 30 ft. as a Magic action)
- Expecting a stealth- or speed-heavy stretch coming up (scouting, infiltration, covering ground fast) → swap into **Infiltrator model** at your next Short or Long Rest for **Dampening Field** (adv. on Stealth) and **Powered Steps** (+5 ft. Speed), both automatic while active—not a mid-scene switch, so plan ahead
- Need a minor magical trick—a spark, a clean-up, a mark, a flavor change → **Prestidigitation** (10 ft., 1 of 6 minor effects)
- Someone's story or a check against the party just landed suspiciously well → **Silvery Barbs** *(always prepared, Fey-Touched—RAW: reaction, force a reroll-lower on their success, then hand a creature of your choice advantage on its next attack roll, check, or save within 1 minute)*
- Need to know what someone in front of you is actively hiding—a lie, a motive, a fact—without a fight → **Last Frame** *(The Last Exposure, 1/Short Rest, 30 ft.—one true concealed thing; the DM decides how specific)*
- Reading a scene for what's off, or telling whether someone's lying → **Old Instincts** *(The Last Exposure, passive—advantage on Investigation and Insight checks made for exactly this)*
- Speaking to an ally silently across a room without a shared language → **Limited Telepathy** *(Verdan, RAW: 30 ft., no shared language needed, but the target must understand at least one language; simple ideas only)*
- Need an edge resisting a Charm or Fear effect → **Telepathic Insight** *(Verdan, RAW: advantage on all WIS and CHA saving throws)*
- Holding their breath through gas, smoke, or submersion → **Breathless** *(Aberrant Anatomy, RAW: hold breath for 1 hour)*
- Need to spot something everyone else missed → **Perception +8 (expertise)** paired with **Blindsight 15 ft.**
- Investigating a scene, chasing a lead, or piecing together what happened → **Investigation +7**, **History +7**, **Arcana +7**—the old journalist's instincts didn't retire
- Need to talk your way past someone → **Persuasion +2** *(Verdan)*
- Building, repairing, forging, or faking an ID → **Smith's Tools**, **Tinker's Tools**, **Disguise Kit**, **Thieves' Tools** proficiencies
- Need a better night's recovery after a rough Short Rest → **Black Blood Healing** *(Verdan, RAW: reroll a 1 or 2 on any Hit Die spent at the end of a Short Rest, must use the new roll)*
- Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW—Aberrant Anatomy's Warping Flesh is written as an involuntary liability (a natural 1 risks Stunning them), but the same unstable biology could plausibly be provoked deliberately in the right narrative moment rather than only triggering by accident → the sheet only describes the accidental version, so a deliberate trigger would need the table to agree it works that way
