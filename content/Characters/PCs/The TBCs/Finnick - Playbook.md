# Finnick - Playbook

*Companion to [[Finnick]] — regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-08-30. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current. Finnick is a guest for The TBCs' first session only — this note still covers his full kit, same as any other PC.*

*No Loadout Selection section — Sorcerer knows a fixed list of spells that doesn't change until level-up; there's no daily prep to swap.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn before anything else:**
- Elemental damage (acid/cold/fire/lightning/thunder) about to land → Reaction **Absorb Elements** (resist the triggering damage; next melee hit adds +1d6 of that same type)
- Finnick or an ally about to fail (or just failed) a big attack/check/save → Reaction **Silvery Barbs** — reroll it, then grant another creature advantage on their own next roll; costs a spell slot
- Frightened save incoming → **Brave** gives advantage automatically
- Rolled a natural 1 on a d20 test → **Luck** forces a reroll automatically (must use the new result) — a passive safety net, separate from Tides of Chaos below
- One roll this turn absolutely has to land → **Tides of Chaos**: banked advantage on one d20 Test, free to use, no action required — but spending it means the *next* slotted Sorcerer spell he casts (or a Long Rest) resets it, and that resetting cast automatically triggers a Wild Magic Surge (see Comparative Notes)

**Action:**
- Reliable single-target damage, no resource cost → **Fire Bolt** (R +6, 120 ft, 1d10 fire)
- Bigger, unpredictable damage, willing to spend a slot → **Chaos Bolt** (R +6, 120 ft, 2d8+1d6 of a random damage type; if the two damage dice match, it leaps to a new target and rerolls)
- Zone denial against something chasing the party → **Caltrops** (covers a 5-ft. square; a creature entering takes 1 piercing and its Speed drops to 0 until treated)
- Expecting a fight and Mage Armor isn't already up → **Mage Armor** (touch, AC becomes 13 + DEX for 8 hours) — his listed AC 14 assumes this *isn't* active yet
- Melee backup → **Dagger** (M/R +6, 5 ft./20/60 ft., 1d4+4 pierce)
- Mid-scene social lever → **Friends** (10 ft, Concentration, advantage on CHA checks vs. one creature — it knows it was charmed once the spell ends, so only use this with an exit already planned) or **Suggestion** (30 ft, WIS DC 14, Concentration, compels a reasonable course of action for up to 8 hours)
- Need to know what a target is actually thinking → **Detect Thoughts** (self, WIS DC 14, Concentration)
- Need a small distraction, not real damage → **Minor Illusion** (30 ft, sound or image; an Investigation check reveals it)
- Need to manipulate something at range → **Mage Hand** (30 ft, 10 lb. object)
- Fire-damage setup with an ally → **Oil** (DEX save DC 14, coats target, next fire hit within 1 min. does +5 fire) then **Torch** (M +1, 1 fire)

*Footnote: the Spear is on the sheet but strictly worse than the Dagger for this build — lower attack bonus (+1 vs. +6, since it's not Finesse and STR is -1) and lower average damage even two-handed. Reach for the Dagger.*

**Any casting of a spell that has V/S/M components** — pay 1 Sorcery Point (of 2 total, Long Rest pool) for **Subtle Spell**: removes Verbal/Somatic/Material components except anything costly or consumed. Good for casting Friends or Suggestion without anyone seeing it happen.

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above — pick whichever the situation calls for):
- About to lean hard on spellcasting this fight → **Innate Sorcery** (self, 1 min. — Sorcerer spell save DC +1, spell attacks have advantage) — 2×/Long Rest
- Need to cast a normally Action-cost spell without giving up the Action → **Quickened Spell** (spend 2 Sorcery Points — the entire pool) to cast it as a Bonus Action instead

**Reaction:**
- Elemental damage or a bad roll → Absorb Elements or Silvery Barbs (see Triage)

**Movement:**
- **Halfling Nimbleness** lets him move through a larger creature's space (can't stop there) — useful for slipping past Melvin or an enemy without provoking a squeeze check.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Tides of Chaos vs. Innate Sorcery, both about getting an edge:* Tides of Chaos costs no action at all — it's a standing benefit, already banked, that applies to any one roll for free — but spending it locks in a guaranteed Wild Magic Surge the next time he casts a slotted spell (or it resets on a Long Rest, unused). Innate Sorcery costs a Bonus Action and one of only 2 uses/Long Rest, but boosts every spell attack and the save DC for a full minute rather than a single roll. Reach for Tides of Chaos on one specific make-or-break roll; reach for Innate Sorcery when the whole turn (or several turns) will be spell-heavy.
- *Quickened Spell vs. Subtle Spell for the 2 Sorcery Points:* Quickened Spell spends the entire pool on one cast, buying back the Action that turn. Subtle Spell costs only 1 point per use — twice from the same pool, or once with a point spare — but doesn't change the spell's action cost, only whether anyone can tell he's casting. He can't do a full Quickened cast and a Subtle cast in the same Long Rest; decide which the moment actually needs before spending either.
- *Wild Magic Surge, passive vs. forced:* after any slotted spell, he can optionally roll a d20 — only a natural 20 triggers a random surge-table effect, and skipping the roll costs nothing. The surge Tides of Chaos forces on its reset cast isn't optional — it happens automatically, whatever the table gives him.
- *Fire Bolt vs. Chaos Bolt:* Fire Bolt is free, at-will, reliable 1d10 fire. Chaos Bolt spends a spell slot for a bigger, unpredictable 2d8+1d6 of a random type with a chance to leap to a second target on a matching roll — worth it when the fight can use the bigger number or the possible extra hit, less so against a target that might resist whatever type comes up.

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to forge a document or fake credentials → **Forgery Kit** proficiency
- Need to lift something without being noticed → **Sleight of Hand +6**
- Need to disappear behind someone bigger → **Naturally Stealthy** *(RAW: can Hide while obscured only by a creature at least one size larger — an easy pairing if Melvin's nearby)*
- Need to slip through a crowded or larger-creature-occupied space → **Halfling Nimbleness** *(RAW: move through a larger creature's space, can't stop there)*
- Need to know what someone's actually thinking, or dig for a specific memory → **Detect Thoughts** (self, WIS DC 14, Concentration)
- Need someone talked into a specific, reasonable-sounding course of action for hours, not just one exchange → **Suggestion** (30 ft, WIS DC 14, Concentration, up to 8 hours)
- Need a quick edge on a con, and can live with the mark realizing afterward → **Friends** *(RAW: the target knows it was magically influenced once the spell ends — plan the exit first)*
- Need a small distraction — a sound from the wrong room, a flicker that isn't real → **Minor Illusion** (30 ft; RAW: an Investigation check reveals it as illusory)
- Need to manipulate an object at range — a lever, a latch, a small item out of reach → **Mage Hand** (30 ft, 10 lb.)
- Force open a locked chest, door, or grate the mundane way → **Crowbar**
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — pairing Subtle Spell with Friends or Suggestion so nobody nearby notices him casting anything at all, not just that the spell's effect went unnoticed → **Subtle Spell** *(RAW printed effect: removes Verbal/Somatic/non-costly-non-consumed-Material components, 1 Sorcery Point)*
