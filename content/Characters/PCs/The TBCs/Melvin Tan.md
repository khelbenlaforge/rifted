## Description

He fills a doorway the way a sack of grain fills a cart — all shoulders and gut under a padded leather harness, fists gloved in matted fur that could crack a skull without trying. A dark hood swallows most of his face, ears poking through two slits at the top, and a green wrap is pulled up over his nose and mouth like a man who'd rather not be recognized. What spoils the effect is the eyes above the wrap, creased soft at the corners, and the little scrap of cloth he keeps folded in one huge hand — worried at constantly, the way a nervous man works a coin. A flask and a row of pouches ride his belt, packed for a longer walk than most bruisers bother with.

## Origin

Before Aenath, Melvin Tan Jun Jie ran with a Singapore gang, the kind of outfit where being the biggest, scariest guy in the room was a full-time job description. He got out the way smart criminals do — sideways, not up — trading muscle-for-hire for a "legitimate" import-export operation dealing in "relaxing puffs," a business built on the same instincts that used to get him paid to break kneecaps: know your supplier, know your buyer, and never let either one see you sweat.

What nobody in that old crew ever knew, and what he still goes to absurd lengths to hide, is that his actual soft underbelly has four legs and answers to Chichi and Huahua — two chihuahuas so small he could lose one in a coat pocket, and does, on purpose, when he can't bear to leave them behind. He built a reputation on being the last person anyone should hand a puppy to. He built his whole life since around making sure that reputation never gets tested.

Then the world he knew fell away entirely, and he came through the tear on the other side changed down to the bone — a hulking, fanged Bugbear where a merely large man used to stand, some new and primal strength coiled under the surface that he'd never had to reach for back home. The dogs came through with him, somehow, small and shaking and very much his responsibility. The wrap over his face still hides how often he's smiling at nothing, thinking about two dogs he isn't supposed to have — same secret, new world.

## Abilities

Melvin fights the way his old crew trained him to — close, heavy, and built to end things fast, but the Rage itself is new, born the same day the tear remade his body: a beast's resilience or its speed, called up from a well he never had on Earth. What he never mentions on a job is that the same primal current running through Path of the Wild Heart lets him speak with animals as easily as breathing, a gift he keeps almost exclusively for two very small dogs and nobody else.

### Features

- **Unarmored Defense** — While unarmored, AC = 10 + DEX mod + CON mod (16).
- **Weapon Mastery** — Greataxe *(Cleave)*: on hit, may make an attack roll (not automatic) against a second creature within 5 ft. of the first and within reach; on a hit, deals weapon damage without adding ability mod (unless negative); once/turn. Handaxe *(Vex)*: on hit, gain adv. on your next attack against that creature before end of next turn; may change a mastery choice on a Long Rest.
- **Criminal Contact** — Reliable contact who relays messages to/from a network of criminals across long distances (local messengers, corrupt caravan masters, seedy sailors).
- **Creature Type** — Humanoid; also considered a goblinoid for prerequisites and effects.
- **Fey Ancestry** — Adv. on saves to avoid or end Charmed.
- **Long-Limbed** — Melee attacks on your turn have 5 ft. extra reach.
- **Powerful Build** — Count as one size larger for carrying capacity and push/drag/lift.
- **Sneaky** — Proficiency in Stealth; may move through and stop in a Small-creature-sized space without squeezing.
- **Surprise Attack** — A hit against a creature that has not taken a turn this combat deals +2d6 damage.
- **Danger Sense** — Adv. on DEX saves unless Incapacitated.
- **Reckless Attack** — First attack on your turn may use adv. with STR; attacks against you have adv. until your next turn.
- **Animal Speaker** — You can cast Beast Sense and Speak with Animals only as Rituals; WIS is the spellcasting ability for them.
- **Rage of the Wilds** — On Rage choose Bear (resistance except Force, Necrotic, Psychic, Radiant), Eagle, or Wolf (allies have adv. on attacks against enemies within 5 ft. of you).
- **Primal Knowledge** — Proficiency in Animal Handling; while Raging, may use STR for Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival checks.
- **Bad Batch** *(Steady Flame)* — First hit each turn: +1d6 poison damage.
- **Close It Out** *(Steady Flame)* — Once per turn, when you reduce a creature to 0 HP: gain temp HP equal to your Barbarian level until your next turn.
- **Never Flinch** *(Steady Flame)* — Adv. on Intimidation checks when you hold the upper hand or are calling a bluff.
