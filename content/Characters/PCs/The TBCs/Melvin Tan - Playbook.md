# Melvin Tan - Playbook

*Companion to [[Melvin Tan]] — regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-08-30. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current.*

*No Loadout Selection section — Barbarian has no spell-preparation mechanic. Animal Speaker grants Beast Sense and Speak with Animals as fixed ritual-only casts (no spell slots, nothing to swap after a Long Rest).*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn before anything else:**
- Not yet Raging and expecting a real fight → Bonus Action **Rage** before the first exchange, not after taking damage — the B/P/S resistance only helps for hits taken *while* raging
- Already Raging and about to take a beating → the resistance is already active; nothing more to spend
- DEX save incoming (area effect, trap) → **Danger Sense** gives advantage automatically, unless Incapacitated — not a choice, just don't forget it's live
- A Charm attempt lands on him → **Fey Ancestry** gives advantage on the save to avoid or end it — same, automatic
- Reduced a creature to 0 HP this turn → **Close It Out** *(Steady Flame, passive)* grants temp HP equal to Barbarian level automatically, once/turn

**Action:**
- Target hasn't taken a turn yet this combat → **Greataxe** *(Cleave, M +6, 10 ft., 1d12+4 slash)* + **Surprise Attack** (+2d6, automatic on that hit)
- Multiple enemies within reach → Greataxe *(Cleave)* — on hit, may make an additional attack roll (not automatic) against a second creature within 5 ft. of the first and within reach; that second hit deals weapon damage without the STR bonus
- Single tough target, want the next hit to land clean → **Handaxe** *(Vex, M/R +6, 10 ft./20/60 ft., 1d6+4 slash)* — on hit, gain advantage on the next attack against that same creature before the end of next turn
- Opening the fight or need the highest hit chance → **Reckless Attack**: first attack this turn can use advantage (STR-based) — but every attack against Melvin gets advantage in return until his next turn (see Comparative Notes)
- No weapon drawn, or want the shove → **Unarmed Strike** *(Tavern Brawler, M +6, 10 ft., 1d4+4 bludgeoning, reroll 1s)* — once/turn, may push the target 5 ft. on a hit
- Fire-damage setup with an ally → **Oil** (DEX save DC 13, coats target, next fire hit within 1 min. does +5 fire) then **Torch** (M +6, 1 fire) or an ally's fire spell
- First hit each turn, any weapon → **Bad Batch** *(Steady Flame, passive)* adds +1d6 poison automatically — no separate choice, just land the hit

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above — pick whichever the situation calls for):
- Not yet Raging → **Rage** (3 uses/Long Rest, 1 back on a Short Rest) — choose an aspect on activation: **Bear** (resistance to nearly all damage types while raging, not just B/P/S), **Eagle** (Dash and Disengage become Bonus Actions while raging), or **Wolf** (allies get advantage on attacks against enemies within 5 ft. of Melvin) — see Comparative Notes
- Already Raging, chose Eagle, need to reposition fast → Dash or Disengage as a Bonus Action (Rage of the Wilds)
- One shot to make something back off → **One Warning** *(Steady Flame, WIS DC 13, self/sight, 1/Short Rest)* — Frightened until the end of Melvin's next turn on a failed save

**Reaction:**
*None at this level.*

**Movement:**
- Long-Limbed gives 5 ft. extra melee reach (already folded into the 10 ft. ranges above) — Melvin doesn't need to close as tight as most melee to stay in range.

*Footnote: no armor worn — AC 16 comes from Unarmored Defense (10 + DEX mod + CON mod), not a suit that needs managing. Melvin's Creature Type is Humanoid, but he's also considered a goblinoid for prerequisites and effects — relevant if an item, spell, or hazard specifically keys off goblinoid.*

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Greataxe (Cleave) vs. Handaxe (Vex):* Cleave adds a second, non-automatic attack roll against a nearby target (no STR bonus on that second hit) — strong when enemies are clustered. Vex doesn't hit harder itself, but guarantees advantage on the *next* attack against the same target — better when one target needs to go down and staying accurate matters more than spreading damage.
- *Reckless Attack:* the advantage on Melvin's own attack is real, but so is the cost — every attack against him gets advantage back until his next turn. Worth it early against a single target or while he can afford to eat a few hits; risky when multiple enemies are already free to pile on.
- *Rage of the Wilds aspect (Bear/Eagle/Wolf), chosen fresh every time Rage activates:* Bear is the pick against non-physical damage (fire, cold, acid, poison, etc. — Rage's base resistance only covers B/P/S); Eagle is the pick when the fight needs mobility (chasing, repositioning, covering an ally's retreat); Wolf is the pick when other melee allies are also going to be adjacent to the same enemies and can use the advantage.
- *One Warning vs. Eagle's Dash/Disengage:* both want the same Bonus Action on a turn already Raging — decide between the fear effect and the extra movement before the turn starts, they can't both happen the same turn.

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need eyes or ears somewhere he can't physically go → **Beast Sense** *(RAW: touch a willing Beast, ritual, Concentration, perceive through its senses for 1 hour)*
- Need to actually talk something out with an animal — including Chichi and Huahua → **Speak with Animals** *(RAW: ritual, self, 10 min., communicate with Beasts and use Influence options)*
- Need to force a door, chest, or portcullis → **Athletics +6**, backed by a **Crowbar** for real leverage
- Need to move quietly or fit through a gap most people can't → **Sneaky** *(RAW: proficiency in Stealth; move through and stop in a Small-creature space without squeezing)* + **Stealth +5**
- Need to intimidate without swinging first, or call someone's bluff → **Intimidation +4**, boosted by **Never Flinch** *(Steady Flame, RAW: advantage on Intimidation checks when holding the upper hand or calling a bluff)*
- Need to move or carry something oversized — a barricade, an unconscious ally, heavy loot → **Powerful Build** *(RAW: counts as one size larger for carrying capacity and push/drag/lift)*
- Need underworld information or a message sent quietly across a long distance → **Criminal Contact** *(RAW: a reliable contact relays messages through local messengers, corrupt caravan masters, and seedy sailors)*
- Need to pick a lock or disarm a mundane trap → **Thieves' Tools** proficiency
- A scene calls for gambling, a con, or a distraction → **Playing Card Set** proficiency
- While Raging, a Stealth, Perception, Survival, Intimidation, or Acrobatics check calls for muscle over finesse → **Primal Knowledge** *(RAW: proficiency in Animal Handling; while Raging, use STR in place of the normal ability for Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival checks)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — letting Reckless Attack's drawback work in Melvin's favor, deliberately eating the incoming advantage to hold enemy attention while a stealthier ally slips past → not an automatic effect, just a tactic worth proposing to the table
