## Description

Her skin carries the pale blue-grey of high cloud, hair bound up in a loose knot that never quite holds against the wind she brings with her. What she wears started as a uniform: a dark cabin-crew jacket and patterned neckerchief. A gold name badge still sits pinned above her heart, tucked now beneath a paladin's silvered breastplate and pearl-set pauldrons. She carries a tall staff crowned with a storm-lit pearl, its length propped against the round shield stamped with a bird in flight. Mist gathers low around her boots, echoing the spires at her back. She still nods to doors before pushing through them, and mutters an apology to any chair she happens to bump.

## Origin

Before Aenath, Xiaoxiao spent her days thirty thousand feet above it, working the aisles of a Singapore Airlines cabin and privately cataloguing every failure of manners she witnessed along the way. Earth, in her considered opinion, had forgotten how to say please and thank you without being reminded. She said both anyway, to everyone and everything — to doors she walked into, to chairs she knocked over, to her own suitcase when she kicked it in the dark, to the aircraft itself when the turbulence got bad enough to rattle the overhead bins. The door and the chair had little to do with it; the habit itself was about keeping care alive in a world she'd decided had stopped extending any.

Then the world she knew fell away entirely, and she came through the tear on the other side changed down to the bone: air moved with her now the way it once only moved around her, an Air Genasi with no memory of asking to become one. Whatever pulled her to Aenath left her body altered, but the reflex it built its home in — the small, relentless courtesy — came through intact.

Swearing the Oath to the noble genies felt like a formal recognition of something she'd already been practicing her whole working life: that grace under pressure, offered freely and without needing to be asked for, is its own kind of power.

## Abilities

Xiaoxiao fights as a Paladin sworn under the Oath of the Noble Genies, staff and shield in hand, drawing on wind and elemental air to back up her word. Her class features read as service rendered with impeccable form — a shove of wind to clear a path, a ward extended to someone who didn't think to ask for it. She apologizes on the way in and doesn't stop moving once she's committed.

### Features

- **Weapon Mastery** — Longsword *(Sap)*: on hit, target has disadv. on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn. Javelin *(Slow)*: on hit that deals damage, you can reduce its Speed by 10 ft. until the start of your next turn (maximum -10 ft.); can change chosen weapons on a Long Rest.
- **Channel Divinity** — 2 uses; regain 1 on a Short Rest and all on a Long Rest. Its save DC is 14.
- **Elemental Smite** — Immediately after casting *Divine Smite*, expend 1 Channel Divinity for one effect: **Dao's Crush** (target Grappled and Restrained; escape DC 14); **Djinni's Escape** (teleport 30 ft.; until end of next turn resist B/P/S and immune Grappled, Prone, Restrained); **Efreeti's Fury** (target and another creature within 30 ft. each take 2d4 fire); or **Marid's Surge** (target and chosen creatures in your 10-ft. emanation make STR DC 14 save or are pushed 15 ft. and Prone).
- **Genie's Splendor** — While not wearing armor, base AC = 10 + DEX mod + CHA mod; a Shield is allowed. You have proficiency in Persuasion. This AC formula doesn't apply while wearing Chain Mail.
- **Genie Spells** — *Chromatic Orb*, *Elementalism*, and *Thunderous Smite* are always prepared and don't count against prepared spells.
- **Paladin's Smite** — Divine Smite is always prepared; you can cast it once per Long Rest without a spell slot.
- **Fighting Style: Blessed Warrior** — You learn two Cleric cantrips of your choice as Paladin spells; Charisma is their spellcasting ability.
- **Mingle with the Wind** — You know *Shocking Grasp*. You can cast *Feather Fall* once per Long Rest without material components; Charisma is the chosen spellcasting ability.
- **Unending Breath** — You can hold your breath indefinitely while not Incapacitated.
- **Lightning Resistance** — Resistance to Lightning damage.
- **Channeled Prowess (Echoing Soul)** — Proficiency in two chosen skills and Expertise in one skill you have proficiency in (currently Insight); choose again after each Long Rest.
- **Inherent Tongues (Echoing Soul)** — You know one additional chosen language.
- **Intrusive Echoes (Echoing Soul)** — Immediately after a natural 1 on a D20 Test, make a CON save (DC 15); on a failure, you are Incapacitated and your Speed is halved until the end of your next turn.
- **By the Book** *(Cabin Ready)* — First hit each turn on a creature within 5 ft. of an ally: +1d6 radiant damage.
- **Between You and the Aisle** *(Cabin Ready)* — First time each turn a creature within 5 ft. of Xiaoxiao is hit, the attacker has disadv. on its next attack against that same creature before her next turn.
- **The Right Tone** *(Cabin Ready)* — Adv. on Persuasion checks to calm, reassure, or de-escalate a hostile or frightened creature.
