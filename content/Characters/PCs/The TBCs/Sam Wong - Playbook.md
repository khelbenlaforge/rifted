## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first — check every turn before anything else:**
- Ally at 0 HP or dying → Bonus Action **Lay on Hands (heal)** (touch, restore up to remaining HP from the 15-HP pool)
- Ally Poisoned → Bonus Action **Lay on Hands (remove Poisoned)** (touch, costs 5 HP from the same pool)
- Multiple allies below half HP after a bad exchange → Bonus Action **Turn the Tide** (30 ft, chosen creatures at ≤½ HP heal 1d6+4 each) — costs a Channel Divinity charge, no roll required
- A Charm attempt lands on Sam → **Fey Ancestry** gives advantage on the save automatically

**Action:**
- Undead or Fiend in range → **Holy Water** (thrown, DEX save DC 11, 2d8 radiant on a failed save)
- Melee, setting up a Bonus Action smite → **Longsword** *(Sap, M +4, 5 ft, 1d8+2 slash — on hit, target has disadvantage on its next attack roll)*
- Ranged, want to slow a target → **Javelin** *(Slow, M/R +4, 5 ft./30/120 ft., 1d6+2 pierce — on a damaging hit, reduce its Speed by 10 ft. until the start of your next turn)*
- Need to stop healing on a tough single target → **Chill Touch** (M +6, touch, 1d10 necrotic; target can't regain HP until the end of Sam's next turn)
- Crowd control with a single word, in or out of combat → **Command** (WIS DC 14, 60 ft, one-word order the target obeys next turn on a failed save — always prepared)
- Need a defensive ward against a specific creature category → **Protection from Evil and Good** (touch, Concentration, 10 min — wards against Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, Undead)
- Ally needs emergency healing at range instead of touch → **Cure Wounds** (touch, 2d8+4 HP) — costs a slot, shorter range than Lay on Hands but doesn't touch the 15-HP pool
- Nothing better to do with the Action → **Unarmed Strike** (M +4, 5 ft, 1+2 bludgeoning)
- First hit each turn, any weapon → **Power Chord** *(Encore, passive)* adds +1d6 thunder automatically

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above — pick whichever the situation calls for):
- Ally down/Poisoned/badly hurt → Lay on Hands or Turn the Tide (see Triage)
- Need intel before committing → **Divine Sense** (self, 60 ft, detect Celestials/Fiends/Undead/consecrated-desecrated ground for 10 min) — costs a Channel Divinity charge, shares the pool with Champion Challenge and Turn the Tide
- One dangerous target needs to stop moving away → **Champion Challenge** (WIS DC 14, 30 ft — failed save, can't willingly move more than 30 ft. from Sam) — costs a Channel Divinity charge
- Same goal, but also want to stop it attacking anyone else → **Compelled Duel** (WIS DC 14, 30 ft, Concentration — failed save, disadvantage attacking others *and* can't willingly move more than 30 ft. away — always prepared, costs a slot instead of Channel Divinity, see Comparative Notes)
- Just hit with a melee/unarmed attack, want guaranteed extra damage → **Divine Smite** (+2d8 radiant, +1d8 more vs. Fiend/Undead) — first use each Long Rest is free, after that it costs a spell slot
- Just hit, want damage that lingers → **Searing Smite** (+1d6 fire, guaranteed; CON DC 14 save or 1d6 fire again at the start of each of its turns until it saves) — costs a slot
- Need to reposition instantly → **Starlight Step** (self, teleport up to 30 ft. to a seen unoccupied space) — 2×/Long Rest
- Need a light source that can move with the party → **Dancing Lights** (120 ft, up to four 10-ft. dim lights, Concentration 1 min) to start, then **Dancing Lights (move)** each following turn to reposition them up to 60 ft.
- One shot to prep the party for incoming saves → **One More Song** *(Encore, 30 ft, 1/Short Rest)* — up to 3 allies who can hear Sam get advantage on their next saving throw before his next turn

**Reaction:**
*None at this level.*

**Movement:** nothing beyond normal 30 ft. — Starlight Step (Bonus Action) is the mobility tool.

*Footnote: a creature already under Champion Challenge or Compelled Duel that attacks someone other than Sam automatically takes 1d6 thunder the first time each turn — **Feedback** *(Encore, passive)*, no action spent, just a reason the lock keeps paying off even when the target doesn't come after Sam directly.*

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Divine Sense vs. Champion Challenge vs. Turn the Tide, all drawing on the same 2-charge Channel Divinity pool (1 back on a Short Rest):* Divine Sense spends a charge on a 10-minute detection window; Champion Challenge spends one to lock down a single dangerous target's movement (no repeat save, just a one-time check); Turn the Tide spends one on guaranteed field healing for everyone at half HP or below, no roll involved. With only 2 charges, using one on Divine Sense out of habit can leave nothing for a fight that actually needs the lock or the heal.
- *Champion Challenge vs. Compelled Duel, the two single-target locks:* Champion Challenge only stops the target from moving away — no restriction on what it attacks — and costs a Channel Divinity charge, not Concentration. Compelled Duel adds disadvantage on attacking anyone but Sam, but requires Concentration (breaks if Sam takes a solid hit, and competes with Protection from Evil and Good for the same Concentration slot) and spends a spell slot instead. Reach for Champion Challenge when Concentration is already committed elsewhere or Channel Divinity is flush; reach for Compelled Duel when the target attacking an ally is the actual danger.
- *Divine Smite vs. Searing Smite:* Divine Smite is the bigger single burst (2d8, +1d8 more vs. Fiend/Undead) and the first use each Long Rest is free. Searing Smite deals less up front (1d6, guaranteed) but adds a recurring 1d6 fire each turn until the target makes a CON save — better against a target likely to keep failing that save, worse against one with a strong CON that shakes it off turn one.

## Loadout Selection

*Paladin prepares from its full class spell list and can swap the whole list after every Long Rest — pick the set below that matches the next expedition. Command, Compelled Duel, and Divine Smite are always prepared via Oath Spells/Paladin's Smite and don't count against the swappable slots below. Candidates pulled from the 2014 Paladin spell list (query_5etools.py, `edition = 'legacy'`) since Oath of the Crown is a 2014-only subclass — not the 2024 list.*

**Dungeon / combat-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Bless* — party-wide attack/save buff for a sustained fight; *Heroism* — temp HP plus immunity to Frightened for one ally going in first
- Keep: *Searing Smite* — recurring damage is worth more the longer a fight runs

**Social / exploration-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Detect Evil and Good* — a first-pass read on a room or NPC; *Detect Magic* — scout for wards/enchantments before triggering them
- Keep: *Protection from Evil and Good* — already a broad ward for investigating a dangerous site

**Balanced / unknown expedition type:**
- Default to what's currently prepared (*Command*, *Cure Wounds*, *Protection from Evil and Good*, *Searing Smite*) — it's already a reasonable spread

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need a hands-free, movable light source in a dark space → **Dancing Lights** (RAW: up to four lights, 10-ft. dim light each, movable up to 60 ft./turn while Concentration holds)
- Need to know if a location or creature is Aberrant/Celestial/Elemental/Fey/Fiend/Undead-touched before committing → **Protection from Evil and Good** (as a ward) or **Divine Sense** (as detection — costs a Channel Divinity charge)
- Need a blunt one-word order an NPC obeys — open, drop, flee, kneel → **Command** (WIS DC 14, 60 ft, save-or-nothing) — as useful for defusing a standoff or buying an escape as it is in combat
- An overnight watch or stakeout with no chance to actually sleep, or resisting sleep-based magic → **Astral Trance** *(RAW: no sleep, magic can't put Sam to sleep; after a 4-hour conscious trance, gain one PHB skill and one weapon/tool proficiency until the next Long Rest)*
- Addressing a crowd, or negotiating with several NPCs at once → **Play to the Back Row** *(Encore, RAW: advantage on Performance checks, and on Persuasion checks made while addressing two or more creatures at once)*
- Resisting a Charm attempt outside combat → **Fey Ancestry** (advantage on the save)
- Arcane, historical, religious, or natural-lore knowledge check → **Arcana +4** / **History +2** / **Religion +2** / **Nature +2** — an unusually even knowledge spread for a Paladin
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW* — using Chill Touch's "can't regain HP" clause as a visible marker on a fleeing target, so allies can track who was tagged → **Chill Touch** *(RAW printed trigger: 1d10 necrotic on hit, target can't regain HP until the end of Sam's next turn)*
