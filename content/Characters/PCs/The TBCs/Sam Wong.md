## Description

Something about him doesn't sit still to the eye. Skin a shade too pale to read as merely fair, catching light like it's reflecting off something farther away than the room he's standing in. His eyes carry a faint depth, the kind that makes people glance twice without knowing why, and his ears taper long enough that he's stopped bothering to hide them under his hair. He still moves like a project manager: measured, a little too calm in a crisis, the sort of person taking notes in his head before he ever takes them on paper. The plate and tabard sit oddly formal over what used to be tour-worn stage posture.

## Origin

Before Aenath, Sam Wong ran project timelines for a living, Malaysian-born, Singapore-based, the guy in the meeting keeping four departments from missing the same deadline for four different reasons. Off the clock he was something else entirely: a lapsed-not-quite-lapsed otaku who traded spreadsheets for a guitar and fronted a J-rock cover band that played anime conventions up and down the region, chasing the version of himself that felt most alive under stage lights doing note-for-note covers of songs written for characters who never existed. He never fully worked out how those two lives sat inside the same person. He just lived both of them.

What he did work out, somewhere between deadlines and encores, was a philosophy simple enough to survive being tested: everyone's stuck living in the same shared reality, so the only sane plan is to make that reality a little more bearable for whoever's standing next to you. He never turned it into a pitch or a caption. It was just the thing he actually believed, quietly, the way people believe things they've never had to defend out loud.

Then something took him mid-commute, and he came out the other side an Astral Elf, skin gone pale as starlight and a faint hum of somewhere-else settling under his pulse. The paladin's oath came with it, fitting itself to him like it had been waiting for a body that finally believed the right thing. He still isn't sure whether Aenath handed him a calling or just recognized the one he already had, but the philosophy came through the crossing intact, sword and all.

## Abilities

Paladin conviction runs practical on him the same way his old project management did, steady under pressure, quick to put himself between a problem and whoever it's about to hit. His Oath of the Crown magic reads like an extension of that same instinct: with a word he can lock enemies onto him mid-fight, so a creature he's singled out can't put real distance between itself and him while the compulsion holds, and when someone near him starts flagging he can call out a quick Channel Divinity blessing that pulls them back from the brink without breaking stride. Guarding the innocent and answering the guilty were never separate skills for him, just the same project run on different terrain.

### Features

- **Weapon Mastery** — Longsword *(Sap)*: on hit, target has disadv. on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn. Javelin *(Slow)*: on hit that deals damage, you can reduce its Speed by 10 ft. until the start of your next turn (maximum -10 ft.); can change chosen weapons on a Long Rest.
- **Channel Divinity** — 2 uses; regain 1 on a Short Rest and all on a Long Rest. Its save DC is 14.
- **Crown Channel Divinity** — **Champion Challenge** and **Turn the Tide** are your Oath of the Crown options.
- **Paladin's Smite** — Divine Smite is always prepared; you can cast it once per Long Rest without a spell slot.
- **Fighting Style: Blessed Warrior** — You learn two Cleric cantrips of your choice as Paladin spells; Charisma is their spellcasting ability.
- **Scion of the Outer Planes (Evil)** — Resistance to Necrotic damage; grants *Chill Touch* cantrip (Charisma is the spellcasting ability, chosen when selecting this feat).
- **Astral Fire** — *Dancing Lights* cantrip; Charisma is the chosen spellcasting ability.
- **Fey Ancestry** — Adv. on saves to avoid or end Charmed.
- **Keen Senses** — Proficiency in Perception.
- **Astral Trance** — No sleep; magic can't put you to sleep; 4-hour conscious Long Rest. After it, gain one PHB skill and one weapon or tool proficiency until your next Long Rest.
- **Creature Type** — Humanoid; also considered an elf for prerequisites and effects.
- **Oath Spells** — *Command* and *Compelled Duel* are always prepared and don't count against prepared spells.
- **Tenets of the Crown** — Law, Loyalty, Courage, Responsibility.
- **Power Chord** *(Encore)* — First hit each turn: +1d6 thunder damage.
- **Feedback** *(Encore)* — First time each turn a creature under your Champion Challenge or Compelled Duel hits someone other than you: it takes 1d6 thunder damage.
- **Play to the Back Row** *(Encore)* — Adv. on Performance checks, and on Persuasion checks made while addressing two or more creatures at once.
