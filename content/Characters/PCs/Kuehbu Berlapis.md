### Features

- **Weapon Mastery** — Sun Blade *(Sap)* · Javelin *(Slow)*; can swap assignments on Long Rest
  - *Sap:* On hit, target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
  - *Slow:* On hit with damage, reduce target's Speed by 10 ft until the start of your next turn (multiple hits don't stack).
- **Powerful Build** — count as one size larger for carrying capacity, push, drag, and lift limits
- **Dueling** (Fighting Style) — +2 to damage rolls when wielding a melee weapon in one hand with no other weapons (shield in other hand qualifies)
- **Paladin's Smite** — always have Divine Smite prepared; on a melee weapon hit, expend a Paladin spell slot as a Bonus Action to deal smite damage
- **Oath of Devotion** — subclass listed in Foundry; see DM Notes regarding Channel Divinity
- **Sentinel Shield** — Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks while holding this shield (passive, equipped)
- **Font of Magic / Sorcery Points** — 3 SP/LR; create spell slots (1st = 2 SP, 2nd = 3 SP) or convert slots to SP (1 SP per slot level)
- **Metamagic: Heightened Spell** — spend 3 SP: one target has Disadvantage on its first saving throw against the spell
- **Metamagic: Quickened Spell** — spend 2 SP: change a spell's casting time from Action to Bonus Action
- **Wild Magic Surge** — once per turn, after casting a Sorcerer spell with a slot, roll d20; on 20, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table
- **Tides of Chaos** — 1×/LR: Advantage on one D20 Test before rolling; recharges when you cast a Sorcerer spell with a slot (also auto-triggers a Wild Magic Surge)
- **Lucky** — 3 Luck Points/LR: spend 1 to gain Advantage on a D20 Test, OR impose Disadvantage on an attack roll against you
