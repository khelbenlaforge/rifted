# Odette Langford - Selûne Primer

*Companion to [[Odette Langford]]. A player-facing primer on [[Selûne]] and her church: what a Twilight cleric of the Moonmaiden would know, and what Odette has already seen in Coldford.*

**How to read the tags:** **Played** = said or seen at the table. **Lore** = published Forgotten Realms lore; it holds for Aenath unless the DM rules otherwise.

## The short version

- Selûne is the goddess of the moon and of everyone who lives and works by its light. She is Chaotic Good, forgiving, and asks little of her followers.
- She is locked in a war with her twin sister [[Shar]], goddess of darkness. Her faithful take that war literally, as the sky's actual weather.
- Her church prizes self-reliance over obedience. It draws wanderers, outcasts, night-workers and people who don't fit elsewhere.
- Her domains are Knowledge, Life and Twilight, so Odette's Twilight Domain is one of hers. [[Sister Margaret]] also called [[Sehanine Moonbow]], Selûne's close moon-goddess ally, "a sister of twilight."

## Who she is (Lore)

She goes by Our Lady of Silver, the Moonmaiden and the Night White Lady. She is ancient, older than most of the gods.

**Her moods follow the moon**
- **Full:** joyful, dancing, vivacious.
- **Waning:** remote and grieving, still sad over tragedies from long ago.
- **Roused against real evil:** cold and merciless.
- **In between:** motherly, tranquil, almost poetic.

She is slow to anger. She won't fight if she can avoid it, but she won't hold back when evil forces the issue. When her clergy call on her, she answers.

**Her forms**
- A dusky-skinned woman with green eyes and hair to her knees.
- A dark-haired girl trailing moondust.
- A gray-streaked, matronly woman who sometimes walks among mortals unrecognized.
- In darkness she always carries a faint blue-white glow, whatever shape she wears.

**How she shows up**
- Usually as drifting motes of light called **moondust** or **moon motes**. They guide lost travelers, and they can shine for her faithful when they need light for delicate work.
- The motes sometimes drip pearly droplets, her holy essence, which the faithful prize.
- Her moon is her watching eye. She poured her spirit into it, and it is said she can sense any deed done in its open light. Many believers say the faint light trailing the moon is her tears.

**Her things**
- **Weapon:** the Rod of Four Moons, a four-flanged heavy mace.
- **Armor:** scale mail that glows faintly silver. She will lend it to mortals questing for her without losing its protection herself.
- **Realm:** the Gates of the Moon. Its heart is Argentil, a silver hall of quiet and moonlight.

## The war with Shar

**Lore:** The sisters share an origin and have fought since before the other gods existed. Their war shows every night: Shar devours the moon's light at the new moon, and Selûne returns and renews. Even they set it aside to fight beside the gods against the primordial powers when the worlds were young. That is why her faithful say the balance between them matters more than the fight.

**Played:** Sister Margaret told Odette that in the beginning there were only Selûne and Shar, two sides of the moon. She also said Shar's worshippers in [[Crystal City]] once kidnapped a few priests at the [[Feast of the Moon]] to try to stop Selûne's return.

**Lore:** Shar's followers keep a rite of their own on the Feast night, called the Rising of the Dark. Most of Corranor treats it as a story told to frighten children. A Selûnite should treat Shar's cult as a standing enemy, not an abstraction.

## What she cares about (Lore)

- **Travelers and the lost.** Wanderers, sailors and navigators (who pray to her against Umberlee's temper), and anyone caught outdoors in the dark.
- **People who work at night,** honestly.
- **Seers.** Diviners, astrologers, fortune-tellers and mystics. She is also a goddess of magic.
- **Birth and love.** Couples hoping for children pray for good timing. Children born under a full moon are said to turn toward magic, and those conceived in moonlight to "have the moon alive within them."
- **Lycanthropes of good or neutral heart,** who honor her as mistress of their nature. Lycanthropy is politely called "Selûne's Kiss."
- **Women.** The church's message of courage, guidance and self-determination has made it popular with alewives, laundresses, seamstresses and servants.
- **Outsiders.** Adventurers, outcasts and mavericks. Even people who aren't devoted give her an offering before a night raid or a night journey.

## Customs you can use at the table (Lore)

- **When to pray:** under moonlight, when she is said to gain real power. Most goodly folk pay homage at the full moon. She will guide anyone who meditates in moonlight, even if they don't pray to her.
- **Offering:** milk in a silver cup on full-moon nights.
- **Sacred things:** silver (her hardened tears), moonstones, and the colors blue and silver.
- **Holy symbol:** a pair of eyes ringed by seven silver stars.
- **Signs of favor:** moondust trails, owls, moon-touched shapechangers, and her shining celestial servants, the Shards. A moonstone showing up when you need it counts too.
- **Superstition:** sailors say that mocking Selûne aboard a ship means you never finish the voyage.

> Let your moon be my light, and I shall let my sword be your shining symbol.
> A basic prayer to Selûne

## Friends and enemies (Lore)

**Allies**
- [[Sehanine Moonbow]]: the elven moon goddess, whom she works with most closely.
- [[Mystra]]: her greatest ally against Shar.
- [[Lathander]]: she respects his passion and hopes to work with him against Shar's dark deeds.
- [[Tymora]], [[Corellon]], and the travel and sea gods Shaundakul and Valkur.

**Enemies**
- [[Shar]], above all.
- [[Mask]], for the mischief he makes in the shadows her moonlight casts.
- Umberlee, over ships at sea.
- Moander, god of rot.

## What you know in Aenath (Played)

**The Feast of the Moon**
- Margaret said Selûne waxes full on the [[Feast of the Moon]], and that Sehanine sends strength to guard her as she does.
- Every faith keeps its own vigil that night: [[Kelemvor]]'s Deeds of the Dead, Sehanine's Mystic Rites of the Luminous Cloud, and Shar's Rising of the Dark.

**The Temple of Selûne in Coldford**
- The [[Temple of Selûne]] stands in the faith district, next door to [[The Tallhouse]].
- Its clergy are clerics and one sorcerer. It has no paladins, because Selûne is also a goddess of magic.
- A statue of the goddess stands inside. Odette's body relaxed when she walked in with bread.

**Sister Margaret**

A very tall aasimar of elven and celestial blood, well over a century old. She runs the temple and is Odette's friend and ally.
- Odette told her about the tiefling and aasimar blood trade, in confidence.
- Margaret cast Commune, then said, "This is not good." She expects the tiefling community to be hit first.
- She set a Silence over the entrance and windows against eavesdroppers, and later lifted it from the door.
- She will warn her tiefling and aasimar worshippers and spread the word. She told Odette to watch for [[The Claret]], whom she felt were starting to move fast.
- Margaret works from the temple's side while [[Petra Hale]] works from the Guild's. Once Petra agrees, Margaret will also speak to Kelemvor's paladins and the [[Monks of the Long Death]].

**Around the temple**
- Petra Hale prays to Selûne.
- Coldford also has a Temple of Kelemvor (two paladins) and a Temple of Lathander. Margaret said Tyr's main temple is in Crystal City, and Coldford has none.
- Odette's habit so far: bread for the temple, and a conversation with Margaret.

## Open questions for you and the DM

The campaign hasn't set these yet. Odette's player can propose answers.
- Clergy ranks and titles, and what a Selûnite rite looks like in Aenath.
- Whether the church recognizes Twilight clerics as a distinct calling.
- Odette's own relationship to Selûne: called, inherited, or newly found.
- Whether a Selûnite prayer has a practical effect, such as light or guidance, in moonlight.
- How the moon behaves in Aenath outside the Feast night.

## Two ways this connects to Odette

- **The hush before dark.** Selûne is the goddess of the stretch between light and dark, and Odette's faith is built on that same hush. Her faith protects first and harms second, which matches how the church frames Selûne's mercy.
- **Choosing for herself.** Odette's life was planned for her. A church that preaches finding your own path fits that, and it is a plausible reason she took to the temple so easily.
