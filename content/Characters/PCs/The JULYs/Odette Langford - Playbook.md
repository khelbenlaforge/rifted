## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Two things to hold in your head before anything else:**
- **Concentration:** Guidance, Detect Magic, Faerie Fire, Sleep, Moonbeam, and Spiritual Weapon are all Concentration—only one runs at a time. Cure Wounds, Inflict Wounds, Healing Word, Sanctuary, See Invisibility, Augury, and Misty Step don't touch it.
- **Channel Divinity is one shared pool of 2 uses, not four separate ones.** Divine Spark (heal), Divine Spark (harm), Turn Undead, and Twilight Sanctuary all draw from the same 2 uses (regain 1 on a Short Rest, both on a Long Rest)—picking one spends toward the same total as picking any other.

**Passives, always on:**
- **Standing Between** (The Hush Before Dark)—the first time each turn a creature within 5 ft. of Odette is hit by an attack, her next healing spell before the end of her next turn restores +1d6 HP.
- **Step In Front** (The Hush Before Dark)—the first attack roll each round against a creature within 5 ft. of her, by an attacker also within 5 ft. of her, has disadvantage.
- **Lords' Alliance Agent**—once/turn on a critical hit, an eligible ally within 30 ft. gets Heroic Inspiration; when a visible enemy damages an ally within 5 ft. of Odette, her next attack against that enemy has advantage.

**Triage first—check every turn before anything else:**
- **Ally about to drop, or already at 0 HP →** **Healing Word** (BA, 60 ft., 2d4+4 HP)
- **Odette or an ally about to get focused down or surrounded →** **Sanctuary** (BA, WIS DC 14, 30 ft.)—attackers and damaging casters targeting the warded creature must save or retarget/lose the effect, 1 min.
- **An ally is Charmed or Frightened and it's costing the party →** **Twilight Sanctuary** (Action, Channel Divinity, self 30-ft. dim-light sphere, 1 min)—whenever a creature (including Odette herself) ends its own turn inside it, she can grant it 1d6+3 temp HP or end one Charmed/Frightened effect on it; her choice, once per creature per turn.

**Action:**
- **Undead in the fight →** **Turn Undead** (Channel Divinity, WIS DC 14, 30 ft.)—Frightened and Incapacitated 1 min, ends early if damaged.
- **One hard target, heal-or-harm swing →** **Divine Spark** (Channel Divinity, 30 ft.)—heal: 1d8+4 HP, no save; harm: CON DC 14, 1d8+4 necrotic or radiant, half on success.
- **Fiend or Undead specifically, worth the last use →** **Holy Water** (DEX DC 10, 20 ft., 2d8 radiant on failed save, 1 use)
- **Enemies clustered in melee around her, at-will damage wanted →** **Word of Radiance** (cantrip, self 5-ft. emanation, CON DC 14, 1d6 radiant to chosen creatures in range, no dmg on success)—she picks which creatures in the emanation are affected, so allies standing in range aren't forced into it.
- **A spot enemies keep entering or starting their turn in →** **Moonbeam** (2nd, always prepared, CON DC 14, 120 ft., 5-ft.-radius cylinder, 2d10 radiant, half on success, Conc.)—damages again automatically each time a creature enters it or starts its turn there.
- **Setting up the party's hits on a cluster →** **Faerie Fire** (1st, always prepared, DEX DC 14, 60 ft., 20-ft. cube, Conc.)—failed save: outlined, attacks against them have advantage, 1 min.
- **A handful of weak enemies worth taking out of the fight entirely →** **Sleep** (1st, always prepared, WIS DC 14, 60 ft., 5-ft. radius, Conc.)—failed save: Incapacitated, repeats each turn, up to 1 min.
- **Single target in melee reach, nothing else pressing →** **Inflict Wounds** (1st, touch, CON DC 14, 2d10 necrotic, half on success)
- **Weapon swing only, no spell worth spending →** **Mace** (M +1, 5 ft., 1d6-1 bludgeon)—weakest option on the sheet; Word of Radiance is almost always better when a target's already in melee range.

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **Ally down (see Triage) →** Healing Word
- **Odette or an ally in danger (see Triage) →** Sanctuary
- **Melee ally needs sustained extra damage →** **Spiritual Weapon** (2nd, BA, 60 ft., M +6, 1d8+4 force, Conc.)—attacks immediately on cast, then again with a BA on later turns.
- **Need to close 30 ft. instantly →** **Misty Step** (2nd, always prepared, self teleport up to 30 ft. to an unoccupied space she can see, 1/LR free)

**Reaction:**
- *None on the sheet.*

**Movement:**
- Speed 30 ft. flat—no movement-boosting features on the sheet. Position to keep allies within Step In Front/Standing Between's 5-ft. range when possible; both are free and passive.

*Grapple/Shove isn't in the tree above—DC 9 off her -1 STR makes it a weak option; a control spell (Sleep, Faerie Fire) or an ally with better STR is almost always the better call. Unarmed Strike also isn't listed—Mace outclasses it outright.*

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Divine Spark (harm) vs. Inflict Wounds:* Divine Spark draws from the same 2-use Channel Divinity pool as Turn Undead and Twilight Sanctuary, so spending it here leaves less pool for whichever of those comes up next; Inflict Wounds spends a renewable 1st-level slot instead (4 available) and never touches that pool. Reach for Inflict Wounds when Channel Divinity is better held in reserve; reach for Divine Spark when the pool's fresh or the range matters (30 ft. vs. touch).
- *Turn Undead vs. Divine Spark (harm), against undead specifically:* Turn Undead removes the threat outright on a failed WIS save—Frightened and Incapacitated, no damage; Divine Spark (harm) trades that removal for guaranteed-if-hit necrotic damage on a CON save instead. Both spend the same shared pool. Reach for Turn Undead when the undead needs to stop acting right now, Divine Spark when the party needs the damage more.
- *Sleep vs. Faerie Fire, a cluster of enemies:* Sleep can take several weak enemies out of the fight entirely on a failed WIS save, but a strong save or high current HP gets nothing for the slot; Faerie Fire never removes anyone, but grants advantage on every attack against the outlined targets for a full minute over a bigger area (20-ft. cube vs. 5-ft. radius). Sleep is the gamble against low-HP enemies; Faerie Fire is the safer pick when the party's melee just needs the edge.
- *Healing Word vs. Cure Wounds:* Healing Word is a Bonus Action at 60 ft. for 2d4+4; Cure Wounds costs the Action and requires touch, but heals more (2d8+4). The Action is the deciding factor—hold onto Healing Word when it's needed for something else that turn, spend Cure Wounds when the target's already adjacent and the bigger number is worth it.

Currently prepared: Cure Wounds, Inflict Wounds, Healing Word, Sanctuary, Spiritual Weapon, Prayer of Healing—exactly at the cap.*

**Dungeon / combat-heavy:**
- Swap in: *Guiding Bolt* (1st, R +6, 120 ft., 4d6 radiant, next attack against the target has advantage), *Hold Person* (2nd, WIS DC 14, 60 ft., Paralyzed on failed save, repeats each turn, Conc.)

- Swap out: *Inflict Wounds*, *Spiritual Weapon*

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to know if someone's actually sincere, not performing → **The Hush Before Dark: Reading the Room** *(1/Short Rest, RAW: study one creature she can see and knows, learn with certainty whether it's sincere or performing right now—nothing more specific)*
- Need to check whether an item, door, or room is warded before touching it → **Detect Magic** *(High Elf lineage, always prepared—RAW: sense magic within 30 ft. for 10 min, Magic action to see auras)*. One free 1-action cast per Long Rest (no slot); beyond that, it's a ritual, castable any number of times without a slot but taking 10 extra minutes.
- Before committing the party to a risky, planned course of action → **Augury** *(always prepared, 1/Long Rest free, ritual—RAW: an omen for a course of action planned within the next 30 minutes)*
- Suspect an invisible threat nearby → **See Invisibility** *(2nd, self, 1 hr.—RAW: see invisible creatures/objects and into the Ethereal Plane)*
- Party's heading somewhere pitch-dark with no light source to spare → **Eyes of Night** *(RAW/TCE: her own Darkvision is permanently 300 ft., no action needed; sharing it with up to 4 willing creatures within 10 ft. for 1 hr. is the 1/Long-Rest action—reusable by spending any spell slot if needed again before the next Long Rest)*
- An ally—or Odette herself—needs to act first in a fight everyone can see coming → **Vigilant Blessing** *(touch, can target herself—RAW: target has advantage on their next initiative roll, ends after the roll or if used again)*
- Group needs a Short Rest's worth of healing outside a fight → **Prayer of Healing** *(2nd, 10-min cast—RAW: up to five creatures gain the benefit of a Short Rest and regain 2d8 HP)*
- An ally is about to lean on one skill repeatedly (a lock, a climb, a tricky negotiation) → **Guidance** *(cantrip, touch, Conc.—RAW: +1d4 to checks using one chosen skill, for 1 min)*
- Needs light without burning a torch or a hand → **Light** *(cantrip, touch, 1 hr.—RAW: a Large or smaller object sheds bright light 20 ft./dim light 20 ft. beyond that)*
- A high-society door won't open on Persuasion alone → **Signet Ring / Scroll of Pedigree** *(Langford Group credentials—no RAW mechanic, pure flavor proof of standing)*, backed by **Persuasion +6** or **Deception +4**
- Reading a room full of nobles, clergy, or scholars for what they actually want → **Insight +4**, **History +5**, **Religion +5**—all Noble-background territory
- A negotiation turns hostile and someone tries to Charm or manipulate her into a bad call → **Fey Ancestry** *(RAW: advantage on saves to avoid or end being Charmed)*
- Ally needs stabilizing or an odd affliction needs diagnosing outside combat → **Medicine +6** *(Keen Senses proficiency)*
- A hostile spellcaster tries to put her to sleep, or the party needs a watch who can't be knocked out that way → **Trance** *(RAW: magic can't put Odette to sleep; also means she needs no sleep of her own and completes a Long Rest via 4-hr meditation)*

*Coral's Thread isn't a live option above—it's a passive, narrative-only cue (the bracelet warms when Odette makes a choice that's genuinely her own). Nothing to trigger; worth remembering when a scene calls for that beat. Elven Lineage also lets her swap her High Elf lineage cantrip (one of Guidance/Light/Word of Radiance—the sheet doesn't say which is the swappable one; separate from Detect Magic, which is a 1st-level spell, not a cantrip) for a different cantrip after a Long Rest—a level-up/build decision, not a field one, so it's not in the tree above.*
