*Companion to [[Masamune Kento]], regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-30. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current.*

**DM rulings (2026-09-30):**
- Searing Smite has no concentration (2024 text); the sheet's Conc. tag is wrong and the note shows it without.
- Detect Magic is Conc. for 10 minutes and a ritual (2024). The sheet doesn't tag it.
- Hunter's Mark: moving the mark to a new target takes a Bonus Action.
- Booming Blade is allowed (a TCE cantrip, not a 2024 one).
- Kento has Smith's Tools, which he is already proficient with, so Fast Crafting works.
- A thrown Javelin hit can carry a smite: Divine Smite and Searing Smite trigger on a hit with a Melee weapon or Unarmed Strike (XPHB), and the Javelin is a Melee weapon with the Thrown property.
- Weapons and Holy Water appear only in the Actions table by sheet convention, not in the Inventory list.
- 5etools' rules data has no entry for Concentration or for how many spells a turn allows, so this note doesn't say which spells combine. Check the Rules Glossary before stacking Bane, Hunter's Mark, Bless or Shield of Faith, or an Action spell with a Bonus Action spell.

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Triage first, every turn:**
- **An ally at 0 HP, or Kento about to drop →** **Lay on Hands** (BA, touch, can target himself, up to 15 HP from the pool; spend 5 to cure Poisoned; pool 15/LR)

**Action:**
- **Undead or Fiend in the fight →** **Holy Water** (replaces one attack of the Attack action, which is his only attack until Extra Attack at Paladin level 5; 20 ft, DEX DC 10, 2d8 radiant on a failed save vs Fiend/Undead; 1 flask on the sheet, which Protection from Evil and Good also uses if prepared)
- **Target already damaged this combat →** hit it first for **[[目利き]] The Flaw** (first hit each turn: +1d6 radiant)
- **Target you've already hit this combat →** **目利き Second Offense** (from your second hit on it, it has disadvantage on attacks against anyone but Kento until the end of its next turn)
- **Adjacent target →** **Longsword** (M +7, 5 ft, 1d8+5 slashing; 1d10+5 two-handed)
- **Target likely to run or reposition →** **Booming Blade** (cantrip, M +7, 5 ft, weapon hit; +1d8 thunder if it willingly moves 5+ ft before Kento's next turn)
- **Target out of reach →** **Javelin** (M/R +7, 30/120 ft, 1d6+5 piercing; a thrown hit can carry a smite)
- **Several enemies grouped →** **Bane** (CHA DC 13, 30 ft, up to 3 targets, -1d4 to attack rolls and saves on a failed save, Conc. 1 min, L1 slot)
- **One hard target you'll keep hitting →** **Vow of Enmity** (Channel Divinity, 30 ft, declared when he takes the Attack action, so not on a Booming Blade turn: advantage against one target for 1 min; can shift to another creature within 30 ft when it drops, no action)

**Bonus Action** (one per turn, so pick one):
- **Just hit, want the big hit →** **Divine Smite** (+2d8 radiant, +1d8 vs Fiend/Undead; 1 free cast/LR, then a slot)
- **Just hit, want it shoved and flat →** **Thunderous Smite** (+2d6 thunder; STR DC 13 or pushed 10 ft and Prone)
- **Just hit, want it burning →** **Searing Smite** (+1d6 fire, 1d6 fire at the start of each of its turns until CON DC 13 ends it, 1 min)
- **Just hit, want it scared →** **Wrathful Smite** (+1d6 necrotic; WIS DC 13 or Frightened 1 min, repeats the save each turn)
- **Fight starting, weapon hits should hurt more →** **Divine Favor** (+1d4 radiant on weapon hits, 1 min)
- **Fight starting, one target to focus →** **Hunter's Mark** (90 ft, +1d6 force on hits, Conc. 1 hr; a Bonus Action moves it to a new target when the first drops)

**Reaction:** Opportunity Attack with the Longsword (a Reaction every creature has; M +7, 1d8+5); no class reactions at this level.

**Movement:** speed 30 ft. Javelin covers a target you can't reach this turn.

**Weapon Mastery** *(which two are assigned isn't recorded on the sheet; these are the sheet's two weapons):*
- **Longsword →** **Sap:** on a hit, the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of Kento's next turn.
- **Javelin →** **Slow:** on a hit that deals damage, reduce its Speed by 10 ft. until the start of Kento's next turn; it doesn't stack past 10 ft.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Two-handed Longsword vs. Longsword and Shield:* only the two-handed grip gets the 1d10+5 die and Great Weapon Fighting (1s and 2s on the damage dice count as 3s), and two hands on the sword leaves none for the Shield. Chain Mail (16) plus Shield (+2) is the sheet's AC 18, so dropping the Shield means AC 16.
- *Divine Smite vs. a smite spell:* Divine Smite adds the largest dice (2d8 radiant, +1d8 vs Fiend/Undead) and has one free cast per Long Rest; each smite spell spends one of the three 1st-level slots to add a smaller die plus a rider (push and Prone, ongoing fire, or Frightened). Only one Bonus Action fits per turn, so the choice is the rider against the bigger hit.
- *Vow of Enmity vs. Bane:* Vow of Enmity has no saving throw, but it draws on the two-use Channel Divinity pool that Divine Sense also uses (1 back on a Short Rest, all on a Long Rest, not 2 uses of each); Bane spends a slot and Conc. and can fail against every target.
- *Divine Favor vs. Hunter's Mark:* both are Bonus Action damage riders for the whole fight, so one Bonus Action gets you only one of them that turn. Hunter's Mark is the bigger die and the longer duration but carries Conc.; Divine Favor isn't Conc. (2024 spell text).

## Loadout Selection

*Paladin replaces only one prepared spell per Long Rest (2024 Paladin Spellcasting), so this is a queue of single swaps, not loadout sets, and rebuilding all four non-always-prepared spells takes four Long Rests. Currently prepared beyond the always-prepared four: Divine Favor, Searing Smite, Thunderous Smite, Wrathful Smite. Only 1st-level spells fit his slots (1st: 3).*

Swap in this order:
1. **Divine Favor → Bless** *(Action, 30 ft, up to 3 creatures add 1d4 to attack rolls and saves, Conc. 1 min; component: a Holy Symbol worth 5+ GP, and his Holy Symbol is also his spellcasting focus for Paladin spells)*. Bless costs the Action instead of the Bonus Action, which the smites, Hunter's Mark and Lay on Hands all compete for. Worth it when the party fights together.
2. **Wrathful Smite → Cure Wounds** *(Action, touch, 2d8 plus spellcasting modifier; CHA +3 on the sheet)*. For a long stretch after the 15-HP Lay on Hands pool is spent.
3. **Searing Smite → Shield of Faith** *(Bonus Action, 60 ft, +2 AC, Conc. 10 min)*. When the fight is about keeping allies up more than adding damage.
4. **Thunderous Smite → Protection from Evil and Good** *(Action, touch, Conc. 10 min; Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends and Undead have disadvantage on attack rolls against the target, and it can't gain the Charmed or Frightened conditions from them; consumes a 25 GP flask of Holy Water per cast (20 GP with the Crafter discount); he has one on the sheet, the same flask the Holy Water branch throws, so buy spares first)*. For a site full of those creature types.

*Other Paladin 1st-level options if none of these fit: Command (Action, 60 ft, WIS save, one-word order), Compelled Duel (Bonus Action, 30 ft, Conc. 1 min), Heroism (Action, touch, Conc. 1 min, immunity to Frightened plus temporary HP equal to the spellcasting modifier each turn), Detect Evil and Good (Action, 30 ft, Conc. 10 min; senses Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends and Undead). Candidates pulled from the Paladin spell list (5etools, 2024 sources), not just what's on the sheet.*

*Cantrip: on a Long Rest, High Elf Lineage lets him replace its cantrip with a different cantrip from the Wizard spell list (2024 Elf, looked up). That is a separate swap from the spell queue above, and its options aren't researched here.*
