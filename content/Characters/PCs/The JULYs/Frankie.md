## Description

Pale skin, white hair, eyes that linger a beat too long before she answers. She holds herself quietly and keeps her distance in a crowd. Put her in a stable or a clearing instead and her shoulders drop; animals get a version of her that people rarely see.

## Origin

She sold veterinary pharmaceuticals for a living. Clinic waiting rooms, sales targets, vets she needed to charm into ordering the next round of vaccines. The job put her steps away from animals all day, and she still came home exhausted, because the actual work was managing people: reading a room, holding a smile a beat longer than she felt it, performing warmth on a schedule. Animals never asked that of her. Their fear and their trust were both plain, and she trusted that plainness more than she trusted most people she worked with.

She stopped eating meat at some point, mostly to avoid being the reason something died. She carried spiders outside instead of killing them. Sometimes she wondered, half-seriously, if there was a place where the gap between person and animal wasn't so wide — where a wolf could actually understand her.

The Rift answered that wondering directly. Whatever pulled her out of her old life dropped her somewhere wild enough that the gap she used to imagine barely exists here. She hasn't decided yet if that makes this world a gift or just a different kind of strange.

## Abilities

She fights by giving up her own shape. Wolf, bear, whatever the moment calls for — she's more comfortable in a beast's body than she often was in meeting rooms back home, and she brings that into combat as claw and weight rather than spellwork. Outside a fight, the same instinct shows up quieter: animals settle around her without being asked to, in a way that looks a little like magic and mostly isn't.

### Features

- **Primal Order: Magician** — knows one extra cantrip from the Druid spell list (Druidcraft or Thorn Whip); Intelligence (Arcana/Nature) checks gain a bonus equal to WIS mod (min +1)
- **Druidic** — knows the secret Druid language; always has *Speak with Animals* prepared
- **Elven Lineage (Drow)** — Darkvision to 120 ft.; grants *Dancing Lights* (cantrip) and, at level 3, *Faerie Fire*, each castable free once per Long Rest, using WIS as the spellcasting ability (confirmed directly on the Faerie Fire spell item's own data, not assumed). RAW has both always prepared; Foundry only flags *Faerie Fire* as always-prepared — *Dancing Lights* is grouped among her regular chosen cantrips instead (same practical result either way)
- **Fey Ancestry** — advantage on saves vs. being charmed; immune to magical sleep
- **Keen Senses** — grants proficiency in one of Insight/Perception/Investigation (not reflected among her current skill proficiencies below — worth checking in Foundry)
- **Trance** — doesn't sleep; instead meditates 4 hrs/day for the benefit of a Long Rest
- **Spellcasting** — Wisdom-based; can use a druidic focus; can cast known ritual spells without preparing them
- **Wild Shape (2 uses/Long Rest, 1 regained on Short Rest)** — Bonus Action shape-shift into a known Beast form
- **Circle Forms** — while Wild Shaped: max form CR = Druid level ÷ 3 rounded down (currently 1); AC becomes 13 + WIS mod if higher than the Beast's own; gain temp HP = 3× Druid level (9) on shifting
- **Circle of the Moon Spells** — *Cure Wounds*, *Moonbeam*, and *Starry Wisp* always prepared; castable while Wild Shaped
- **Wild Companion** — can cast *Find Familiar* (as a Fey spirit, no material cost) by expending a spell slot or a use of Wild Shape
- **Weapon Mastery — Sickle *(Nick)*:** when making the Attack action with a Light weapon, can make one extra attack with a second Light weapon as part of the same action instead of a Bonus Action (no ability mod to the extra hit's damage unless negative); assignment can swap on Long Rest
- **Lucky (Origin feat, Merchant background):** 2 Luck Points (= Prof Bonus), regained on Long Rest — spend 1 to give yourself advantage on a d20 Test, or to impose disadvantage on an attack roll against you
- **[[The Calm Read]] (Tier 1, homebrew item, attunement required, functions through Wild Shape):** **Reading the Pulse** *(passive)* — first hit each turn deals an extra 1d6 damage of the same type. **Calm the Animal** *(passive)* — beasts within 10 ft (including Frankie wildshaped) have advantage on saves vs. frightened. **Vitals Check** *(1/Short Rest, no action)* — sense a creature's exact current HP fraction and whether it's diseased/poisoned. **Bedside Manner** *(passive, narrative only)* — wild beasts instinctively read her as safe
