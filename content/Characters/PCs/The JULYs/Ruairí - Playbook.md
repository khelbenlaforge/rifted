# Ruairí - Playbook

*Companion to [[Ruairí]]—regenerated from the synced Character Sheet on 2026-09-19. Rerun this skill after any character-sheet-sync update to keep it current.*

## Combat Decision Flowchart

**Two things to hold in your head before anything else:**
- **Concentration:** Guidance, Protection from Evil and Good, Witch Bolt, Mind Spike, Calm Emotions, Faerie Fire, Phantasmal Force, Sleep, and Hex are all Concentration spells—only one can be active at a time. Casting any of these drops whichever one is already running. Eldritch Blast, Sword Burst, Misty Step, and Called In don't touch Concentration at all.
- **Passives, always on:** **Second Helping** (Rounds) adds 1d6 of the same damage type the first time each turn a spell attack hits—no action spent. **Steady Hands** (Rounds) gives advantage on Constitution saves to maintain Concentration, which matters given how much of the list above lives on that one Concentration slot.

**Triage first—check every turn before anything else:**
- **Any creature within 30 ft. (himself included) just failed a saving throw →** **Called In** *(Rounds, 1/SR, no action cost)*—forces a reroll, new result stands even if worse.
- **Ally about to drop, or already down →** nothing on the sheet heals directly; closest is **Misty Step's Refreshing Step** (BA, 1d10 temp HP to himself or an ally within 10 ft.).
- **Surrounded or about to get focused down →** **Misty Step** (BA, self, teleport 30 ft. to a seen unoccupied space, free 4/day)—triggers **Taunting Step**: creatures within 5 ft. of the space he left need WIS DC 14 or fight at disadvantage against anyone but him until his next turn.

**Action:**
- **Single target, nothing else going on →** **Eldritch Blast** (R +6, 210 ft., 1d10 force, at-will, no Concentration)
- **Multiple enemies already adjacent to him →** **Sword Burst** *(2014)* (DEX DC 14, self 5 ft., 1d6 force each, no half on save)
- **Setting up the party's hits, or revealing an invisible enemy →** **Faerie Fire** (DEX DC 14, 60 ft., 20-ft. cube, no half—advantage on all attacks vs. anyone who fails, 1st-level slot, always prepared, Conc.)
- **One tough target he wants gone, or tracked if it flees →** **Mind Spike** (WIS DC 14, 120 ft., 3d8 psychic, ½ on save, learns target's location on a fail, 2nd-level slot, Conc.)
- **One target worth committing a slot to, likely to stay close →** **Witch Bolt** (R +6, 60 ft., 2d12 lightning on hit, then BA 1d12/turn while sustained, 1st-level slot, Conc.)
- **A weaker single target he wants out of the fight entirely →** **Sleep** (WIS DC 14, 60-ft. range, 5-ft. sphere—Incapacitated, then Unconscious on a 2nd failed save, 1st-level slot, always prepared, Conc.)
- **A Hostile Humanoid (or group) he'd rather not fight →** **Calm Emotions** (CHA DC 14, 60 ft., 20-ft. sphere—Hostile to Indifferent, or immune Charmed/Frightened, 2nd-level slot, always prepared, Conc.)
- **Aberration / Celestial / Elemental / Fey / Fiend / Undead targeting an ally →** **Protection from Evil and Good** (touch, disadvantage on its attacks vs. the target, can't Charm/Frighten it, 1st-level slot, needs Holy Water, Conc.)
- **No Verbal/Somatic available (silenced, hands bound, antimagic) →** **Dagger** (M/R +2, 5 ft. / 20–60 ft., 1d4 pierce)—needs neither.

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **Wants sustained extra damage on a target he's about to keep hitting →** **Hex** (90 ft., +1d6 necrotic on hit, one ability check at disadvantage, 1 hr., 1st-level slot, Conc.)
- **Needs to reposition, escape, or grab temp HP →** **Misty Step** (see Triage)

**Reaction:**
- *None on the sheet.* Called In fills the "something just went wrong" role instead, without spending the Reaction itself.

**Movement:**
- **Eldritch Blast** range: 210 ft. total (base 120 ft. + Eldritch Spear's +90 ft. at level 3).
- **Alert** (Origin feat): +proficiency bonus to Initiative; can swap his rolled Initiative with a willing ally's immediately after rolling.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Hex vs. Witch Bolt:* both are 1st-level Concentration, so they can never run together—casting one drops the other outright. Hex only costs the bonus action to cast, not to sustain, so once it's up your bonus action is free again for Misty Step next turn; Witch Bolt needs that same bonus action every turn just to keep dealing damage, and breaks if the target leaves 60 ft. Pick Hex when you want the bonus action back fast, Witch Bolt when you're committing to sit on one target.
- *Sleep vs. Mind Spike, single hard target:* Sleep can take the target out of the fight entirely on a failed save, but a strong Wisdom save or high current HP shrugs it off with nothing to show for the slot; Mind Spike guarantees at least half damage even on a success, and adds tracking if the target tries to run. Reach for Sleep against something you can afford to whiff on, Mind Spike when you need the slot to do something regardless of the roll.
- *Faerie Fire vs. Calm Emotions:* same range and area, opposite jobs. Faerie Fire escalates a fight the party's already in—outline the target, let everyone else's attacks land easier. Calm Emotions is the one that can end the fight before it starts, turning a Hostile Humanoid crowd Indifferent instead. Cast Faerie Fire once swords are already out; reach for Calm Emotions before they are.
- *Sword Burst vs. Eldritch Blast:* Sword Burst only pays off against a cluster of enemies already adjacent to him, and it puts a squishy caster in melee range to get it—Eldritch Blast is the safer default at range against a single target. Only step into Sword Burst range when retreating isn't an option and there's more than one target to hit.

## Loadout Selection

*Warlock spells known change only on level-up, not after a Long Rest, so there's no daily reshuffle to offer here. His four personally-chosen spells are Protection from Evil and Good, Witch Bolt, Hex, and Mind Spike—the other five leveled/utility options (Calm Emotions, Faerie Fire, Misty Step, Phantasmal Force, Sleep) come free from the Archfey Spells feature and don't count against that four.*

*Resource note: both Pact Magic slots are 2nd-level and recharge on a Short Rest, not just a Long Rest—safe to spend both fully in nearly every fight, since they'll be back well before the next one. Magical Cunning also lets him claw back one slot mid-adventuring-day (1/Long Rest, 1-minute meditation) if no short rest is available.*

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Need to pass as someone else entirely, or shake a tail → **Shape-Shifter** *(RAW: Action to change appearance, voice, height/weight, size Medium/Small; advantage on CHA checks while shape-shifted)*—reverting also costs an action, so don't shift right before he needs his own hands back in a hurry.
- Wants a room to warm to him fast, shape-shifted or not → **Deception +6** / **Persuasion +4**, with Shape-Shifter's advantage on CHA checks stacking on top while disguised.
- Someone needs to back down without a fight, especially shape-shifted into something that reads as more threatening than his true form → **Intimidation +6** *(Changeling Instincts proficiency)*.
- Needs a fast read on whether an NPC is lying or hiding something → **Insight +4** *(Changeling Instincts proficiency)*.
- A tense crowd or frightened mob needs defusing without violence → **Calm Emotions** *(2nd-level slot, always prepared—same spell as the combat entry, RAW Hostile-to-Indifferent clause reads just as well on a crowd)*.
- A found or wary animal won't let anyone near it → **Animal Handling +2**, boosted further by **A Nose For It** *(Rounds—advantage on Animal Handling checks)*.
- Wants to know if a door, item, or room is warded before touching it → **Detect Magic** *(Eldritch Sight invocation, at will, no slot; RAW: sense magic within 30 ft., Action to see aura + school)*.
- Needs eyes somewhere he can't safely go, or a message carried quietly → **Find Familiar** *(ritual, no slot; RAW: summons a CR 0 Beast-form spirit)*—worth pre-casting ahead of a scouting-heavy day.
- Needs to talk to an actual animal, not just his familiar → **Speak with Animals** *(ritual; RAW: comprehend and talk with Beasts, 10 minutes)*.
- Needs a whispered message to cross a room or through a wall unheard → **Message** *(120 ft.; RAW: whisper to one target, they can whisper back)*.
- Needs a small on-the-spot effect to sell a bluff or set a mood—a gutting candle, a curl of mist, a kicked-up patch of dust → **Elementalism** *(RAW: minor elemental effect at 30 ft., no combat application)*.
- A campfire scene, a busker's cover story, or a distraction needs real music → **Flute +2** proficiency, stacked with **Performance +4**.
- Lost or in danger while actually in the Feywild → **Feywild Connection** *(Feylost background feature—RAW: friendly Fey are inclined to help him)*—narrow trigger, only fires on that specific plane.
- *Sickle and Unarmed Strike aren't in the tree above*—Dagger beats both on damage without giving up range, so it's the fallback of choice; they're on the sheet for completeness, not as live options.
- *Archfey Patron isn't in the tree above either*—it's a fluff/lore feature with no independent mechanical effect of its own; its actual grants are Archfey Spells and Steps of the Fey, both already covered.
