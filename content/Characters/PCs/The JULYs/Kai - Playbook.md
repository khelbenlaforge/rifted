## Combat Decision Flowchart

**One thing to hold in your head before anything else:**
- **Sneak Attack (2d6, once/turn) needs either Advantage on the attack roll, or a non-Incapacitated ally within 5 ft. of the target while Kai himself doesn't have Disadvantage.** It only applies to a Finesse or Ranged weapon hit—Dagger, Shortsword, and Shortbow all qualify; Unarmed Strike and Grapple/Shove don't. **First Move** (畅通无阻) stacks an extra +1d6 of the same damage type on top, but only fires on the first *Advantage*-rolled hit each turn—the ally-adjacent path to Sneak Attack doesn't also trigger it.

**Triage first—check every turn before anything else:**
- **About to walk the party into an unscouted route, room, or plan →** **Second-Guess** (1/Short Rest, 畅通无阻)—advantage on the Investigation, Perception, or Survival check made to judge whether it's safe.
- **Failed a save against Charmed or Frightened →** **Steel Yourself** (Reaction, 1/LR)—add +2 to the roll; only once per Long Rest, so save it for a save that actually matters.
- **A foe ends its turn within 5 ft. of Kai →** **Skirmisher** (Reaction)—move up to half Speed with no opportunity attack; free repositioning, not something to plan a turn around.

**Action:**
- **Attack roll would have Advantage (hidden, target distracted, or Steady Aim already used this turn) →** **Dagger** (M/R +6, 5 ft. or 20/60 ft., 1d4+4 pierce) / **Shortsword** (M +6, 5 ft., 1d6+4 pierce) / **Shortbow** (R +6, 80/320 ft., 1d6+4 pierce, 20 arrows) + **Sneak Attack** (2d6) + **First Move** (+1d6 same type)—the full damage stack.
- **No Advantage, but a non-Incapacitated ally is within 5 ft. of the target →** same weapon choice + **Sneak Attack** only (2d6)—First Move needs Advantage specifically and won't trigger here.
- **Neither condition met, and Steady Aim isn't worth giving up movement for →** plain weapon attack, no Sneak Attack.
- **Several weak enemies grouped up →** **Oil (flask)** (DEX DC 14, 20 ft., 5 fire on failed save, splashes target/space, 7 uses)—a saving throw, not an attack roll, so it doesn't trigger Sneak Attack.
- **Need someone Grappled or knocked back/Prone, no better option available →** **Grapple** or **Shove** (STR/DEX DC 10, 5 ft.)

**Bonus Action** (independent of the above):
- **No Advantage available any other way →** **Steady Aim**—guarantees Advantage on the next attack this turn, but Speed drops to 0. Worth it when Kai's already in position; costly when he needs to move too.
- **Needs to vanish, break away, or reset for next turn's Advantage →** **Cunning Action** (Dash, Disengage, or Hide)

**Reaction:**
- **A foe ends its turn within 5 ft. →** **Skirmisher** (see Triage)
- **Failed save vs. Charmed/Frightened →** **Steel Yourself** (see Triage)

**Movement:**
- **Reading the Ground** (畅通无阻, passive)—difficult terrain is normal terrain for Kai, and he automatically senses traps/natural hazards within 30 ft. (still needs to search to disarm or avoid them). Lean on this to scout ahead at full speed without slowing the party down.

**Comparative Notes:**
- *Steady Aim vs. hiding for Advantage:* Steady Aim guarantees Advantage this turn but sets Speed to 0—the right call when Kai's already where he needs to be. Hiding (via a prior turn's Cunning Action) keeps full movement instead, but depends on cover or concealment actually being available, which isn't always there when it's needed.
- *Dagger vs. Shortbow, both Sneak Attack-eligible:* Dagger doubles as melee (1d4+4) or a 20/60-ft. throw, so Kai never has to swap weapons between ranges; Shortbow's flat 80/320-ft. range wins when the fight is spread out further than a thrown dagger reaches. Default to Shortbow at range, Dagger when the distance keeps changing.
- *Cunning Action: Disengage vs. Skirmisher:* Disengage is a proactive Bonus Action that guarantees no opportunity attacks that turn—spend it when leaving is the whole point. Skirmisher is a Reaction that fires whenever a foe ends its turn within 5 ft.—RAW (XGE): "when an enemy ends its turn within 5 feet of you." It's a safety net that costs nothing when it happens to trigger, not something to plan around.

## Non-Combat & Creative Uses

- Judging whether a route, plan, or position is actually safe before committing → **Second-Guess** *(1/Short Rest, 畅通无阻—RAW: advantage on an Investigation, Perception, or Survival check made for that purpose)*
- Navigating rough or hazardous terrain without slowing the party → **Reading the Ground** *(畅通无阻, passive—RAW: difficult terrain is normal terrain; senses traps and natural hazards within 30 ft. without a check, though still needs to search to disarm or avoid)*
- Picking a lock, disarming a trap, or bypassing a mundane mechanism → **Thieves' Tools +2**
- A card trick or a rigged game → **Playing Card Set +2**; a sleight-of-hand cover for something else happening at the same time → **Sleight of Hand +6**
- Needs to move, hide, or search without being noticed → **Stealth +6**, **Acrobatics +6**
- Tracking something, reading weather or terrain, or living rough for a stretch → **Survival +5** *(expertise; base proficiency from Skill Versatility)*
- Identifying a plant, animal, or natural hazard by sight → **Nature +4** *(expertise)*
- Spotting an ambush, a tail, or a detail everyone else missed → **Perception +5** *(expertise; base proficiency from Skill Versatility)*
- Passing a coded message to, or getting a read from, another criminal contact → **Thieves' Cant**
- Needs to speak with or read something in Draconic or Elvish → **Languages** *(Common, Draconic, Elvish, Thieves' Cant)*
- Operating in total darkness with no light source → **Darkvision 60 ft.**
- Rolling into a fight that started as an ambush → **Survivor: Hypervigilance** *(RAW: on initiative, reroll a d20 result of 9 or lower; the new roll stands)*
- A negotiation or interrogation tries to Charm him into a bad answer, or someone tries a sleep spell → **Fey Ancestry** *(RAW: advantage on saves against Charmed; magic can't put Kai to sleep)*
- *Possible DM-adjudicated stretch, not RAW*—needing an underworld contact or safehouse in a city that isn't Baldur's Gate → **Criminal Contact** *(RAW as printed only works in Baldur's Gate, per Kai's own DM Notes; would need reskinning to a network Kai actually has ties to here to function as written elsewhere)*
