## Description

A face that never quite settles is the most honest thing about him. Nanaya can wear an ordinary man well enough when the job calls for it: unremarkable height, unremarkable features, a face a room forgets within the hour. But left to his own devices he settles into something sharper. Shaggy black hair falls past a pale, angular face. His ears taper to a fey point. His eyes are a violet no human iris carries — the kind of small, unconscious tell that gives a changeling away if you know to look for it. He dresses for the edges of a room, not the middle of it: a battered leather coat, plated at the shoulder, buckled across the chest, a dagger slung where his hand can find it without looking down. Years of being watched have left their mark too. His movements are economical. He clocks exits without meaning to anymore. His laugh lands half a second late, like it had to clear a checkpoint first.

## Origin

Before he had a file or a handler, Nanaya was a street kid running errands for the yakuza. A changeling child learns fast that survival means becoming whatever the moment needs; he learned faster than most. He picked locks before he could read. He talked his way out of more knives than he can count. By the time he was a teenager he'd built and burned through a dozen small cons against the very government that would eventually come looking for him. When they caught him, it wasn't through force. He simply ran into someone better at the game than he was, and instead of burying him, they made an offer.

What followed was a career spent as someone else. A changeling makes exactly the asset an intelligence service wants: a man who can be anyone and is remembered as no one. He spent years working the slow, patient business of Japan-U.S. relations on paper, while the real work sat underneath it — watching Russia, watching the other old powers, learning the shape of threats before anyone had put a name to them. Moscow was meant to be routine. Whatever pulled him out of that city and into the Rift gave him no warning, and no time to burn the file.

## Abilities

Nanaya fights the way he lives: from the edges, and never head-on if he can help it. His ranger training gives him a hunter's patience and a woodsman's eye. What suits him best, though, is the Fey Wanderer's touch of something otherworldly, which lets him read a room's true intentions the same way he once read marks before a con. Add a changeling's gift for wearing any face, and you get someone built for a different job than a fair fight: making sure the fight never starts, or if it does, making sure nobody afterward can say for certain who was there.
