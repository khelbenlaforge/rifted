## Description

The face he wears is a black cat's, and he's in no hurry to swap it for anything else. Slit-pupilled eyes the grey-green of a Feywild dusk sit above a nose stud, whiskers fanning out stark white against fur so dark it drinks the light. He drapes himself in layered black cloth, buckled and strapped like it might need to come off in a hurry, and carries himself with the loose, watchful ease of something that knows exactly how far it can push before a room stops finding it charming. Talk to him and he might not bother answering—not because he can't, but because a cat that ignores you is somehow less rude than a man who does the same thing, and Ruairí has learned which version gets him left alone faster.

## Earth Background

*Player-authored, kept close to their wording.*

**Ruairí (ror-ee), he/him**

A mischievous, guarded care worker who, after getting past the initial shock of arriving in Aenath, is enjoying his newfound freedom and abuses his changeling ability for fun and convenience.

*From*—Greater Manchester, England, though his family is Irish. His paternal grandparents emigrated from Ireland before he was born, and he was raised primarily by them. He is of mixed Irish-Asian heritage. Although he grew up in England, much of his home life and sense of family came from his Irish grandparents, Nanna and Granda.

*Job? Life?*—Ruairí was a bit of a quietly impish and stubborn child. He was free-spirited and occasionally snuck out of classes, got into fights, and disliked being told what to do. Despite that, he was deeply affectionate toward his grandparents, even if he hated admitting it.

In his early teens, Granda began involving him in Nanna's day-to-day care—partly because help was genuinely needed, and partly because responsibility gave Ruairí somewhere useful to direct his restless energy. He discovered that he was patient and attentive when somebody genuinely depended on him.

After Nanna died, Ruairí found himself quite uninterested in most pursuits and did the odd job here and there. Further education especially seemed both financially unrealistic and unappealing. He and Granda fought the most over this for a time. Eventually, however, memories of Nanna proudly telling people how caring and dependable her grandson was ended up nudging him towards a vocation in healthcare.

He secured financial assistance for nursing school and, to Granda's surprise, committed to it. When Granda later needed residential care, Ruairí took leave from his studies and began working as a care assistant at the same home so he could stay close while earning money.

After Granda's eventual passing as well, Ruairí remained at the home and never returned to nursing school, and the days blurred together and went by.

*Earth is not Home*—Ruairí does not hate Earth. There simply isn't much for him there that feels like **home**.

Life has taught him that people still move on without you. After both his grandparents passed on, Ruairí was ultimately left without the people around whom his life had been built. He had acquaintances, coworkers and people who cared about him, but very few relationships in which he allowed himself to truly depend on anyone.

After Granda's death, he largely continued living through habit: work, sleep, errands, repeat. He had no particular ambition pulling him forward and nobody he felt he needed to return home to, save for the neighbourhood strays he fed. He was somewhat content with where he was in life—unbothered, independent, and with a roof over his head. And also maybe terribly bored.

Arriving in Aenath all pale and wraith-like initially creeps him out, but once the shock passes, there is also an unexpected sense of freedom. For the first time in his life, Ruairí has no responsibilities, no predetermined future and nobody expecting anything from him.

## Origin

Whatever pulled Ruairí out of that care home didn't drop him straight into Aenath. Something in the crossing snagged him first—a detour through the Feywild he still can't fully account for, long enough for its rules to leave a mark on him before it let him go. He came out the other side a changeling: a body that isn't fixed to one face anymore, and instincts that read a room the way Nanna used to read his moods before he'd said a word.

He'd spent years being the reliable one, the steady hand at a bedside, and here he arrived with none of that expected of him and a stranger willing to make him an offer besides. He hasn't settled on how much of himself to give an archfey he barely understands—the bargain was struck somewhere hazy and half-remembered, favors owed to something Titania-adjacent, or perhaps a whole court's worth of smaller debts instead of one grand patron. He isn't telling, possibly because he genuinely doesn't know which is true.

What he does know is that a face is just another thing he can put down and pick back up, same as scrubs and a lanyard used to be. So he wears one that isn't his—sharp-eared, black-furred, impossible to read—and finds that people forgive a cat things they'd never forgive a man.

## Abilities

Ruairí's pact runs on the same trick as his body: nothing about him stays where you last saw it. His patron lets him step sideways through the world itself, closing distance or vanishing from it in an eyeblink, sometimes leaving a companion steadier for the encounter and sometimes leaving pursuers stumbling over each other instead. Layered under that is a changeling's native gift—any face, any voice, held as long as he likes, dropped the moment it stops being useful—so between the fey bargain and the shapeshifting, very little about a fight or a negotiation goes the way anyone expects who's only ever met the mask.

### Features

- **Pact Magic**—Charisma is his spellcasting ability (save DC 14; spell attack +6). 2 Pact Magic slots, both 2nd-level; regain all on a Short or Long Rest.
- **Eldritch Invocations (3 known)**—*Pact of the Tome*: a Book of Shadows grants 3 cantrips (Guidance, Elementalism, Message) and 2 ritual 1st-level spells (Find Familiar, Speak with Animals) from any class's list, always prepared as Warlock spells while the book is on hand, thematically the same book/ink/parchment kit sitting in his Inventory. *Eldritch Sight*: cast Detect Magic at will, no slot. *Eldritch Spear*: extends Eldritch Blast's range by 30 ft. per Warlock level (+90 ft. at level 3, for 210 ft. total).
- **Magical Cunning**—Once per Long Rest, meditate for 1 minute to regain expended Pact Magic slots up to half his maximum, rounded up (1 slot at this level).
- **Archfey Patron**—Bonded to an archfey (or a web of fey favors and debts) whose identity he hasn't settled on, or hasn't been told.
- **Archfey Spells**—Calm Emotions, Faerie Fire, Misty Step, Phantasmal Force, and Sleep are always prepared; they don't count against his 4 personally-chosen spells.
- **Steps of the Fey**—Casts Misty Step free 4 times per day (equal to his CHA modifier), recharging on a Long Rest; still castable via a normal Pact Magic slot once free uses run out, since it's always prepared. Each free cast also triggers either Refreshing Step (he or an ally within 10 ft. gains 1d10 temporary hit points) or Taunting Step (creatures within 5 ft. of the space he left must succeed on a WIS save vs. his spell save DC or have disadvantage on attacks against anyone but him until his next turn).
- **Changeling Instincts**—Proficient in Insight and Intimidation.
- **Shape-Shifter**—Action to change appearance, voice, height/weight, and size (Medium/Small); advantage on Charisma checks while shape-shifted; reverts only by taking an action.
- **Feywild Connection** (Feylost)—Friendly Fey are inclined to help him if he's lost or in need within the Feywild.
- **Alert** (Origin Feat)—Add proficiency bonus to Initiative rolls; can swap his rolled Initiative with a willing ally's immediately after rolling.
- **Second Helping** (Rounds, Tier 1, passive)—First time each turn he hits a creature with a spell attack, deals an extra 1d6 damage of the same type.
- **Steady Hands** (Rounds, Tier 1, passive)—Advantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain Concentration.
- **A Nose For It** (Rounds, Tier 1, passive)—Advantage on Animal Handling checks.
- **Called In** (Rounds, Tier 1, 1/Short Rest, no action required)—When Ruairí or a creature within 30 ft. of him fails a saving throw, he can force a reroll of it; the new result stands even if worse.
