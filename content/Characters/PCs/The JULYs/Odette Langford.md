## Description

She still stands like a photograph is being taken. A decade of finishing school doesn't unlearn itself just because the cameras are gone, so her chin stays set and her weight stays balanced for a lens that isn't there. Her hair falls loose now, copper-brown, no longer pinned for a shoot, and her elven ears are a detail no stylist on Earth ever had to plan around. She still dresses like money. The fabric is soft, the stitching fine, the jewelry worth more than most people's houses, and she hasn't found a way to take it off yet.

The performance cracks in her eyes. They linger too long on things that aren't scripted, searching in a way that gives away how new this all is. Nobody ever asked what she wanted before. She hasn't worked out yet how to answer now that someone might.

## Origin

Odette Langford was born to be looked at. The Langford Group is a British empire of luxury healthcare, hospitality, and philanthropy, and Odette was raised to be its face. Her parents made that decision early, and made sure she never forgot it wasn't hers to change. Every photograph was arranged. Every school was chosen. Every romance ran on a schedule someone else set. Mixed-blood, Chinese-British, photogenic in exactly the way the brand consultants wanted, she was built into a princess before she had a vote in the matter.

The sheltering worked. She's charming and poised in front of a camera and untested at almost everything else, because nobody ever needed her to be. Coral, her longtime caretaker, was the one exception. Coral raised the actual girl instead of the image, and let her be scared or curious or unkind sometimes, without a publicist in the room measuring the damage.

Take away the family name and the practiced smile, and Odette isn't sure what's left of her. She doesn't know if she has any strength that's actually hers. She doesn't know if she could matter to anyone on terms she picked herself.

Earth had already decided all of it. Whatever pulled her into Aenath, she hasn't fought hard to get back — this is the first place in her life where nothing has been decided yet.

## Abilities

Odette's faith belongs to the hush between sunset and true dark, the last stretch of safety before night closes in. She fights from a place of protection. Her instinct in a fight is to plant herself between danger and whoever needs her, and healing comes easier to her than harm does. For once, it's a skill nobody marketed for her.

### Features

- **Spellcasting** — Wisdom is the spellcasting ability (save DC 14; spell attack +6). You prepare Cleric spells and regain spell slots on a Long Rest.
- **Channel Divinity** — You have 2 uses; regain 1 on a Short Rest and all on a Long Rest. Choose Divine Spark, Turn Undead, or Twilight Sanctuary when using it.
- **Divine Order** — The export does not record whether you chose Protector or Thaumaturge.
- **Lords' Alliance Agent** — Once/turn on a critical hit, grant Heroic Inspiration to an eligible ally within 30 ft.; when a visible enemy damages an ally within 5 ft. of you, your next attack against it has adv.
- **Darkvision** — You have Darkvision 60 ft.; **Eyes of Night** increases your own range to 300 ft.
- **Elven Lineage (High Elf)** — You can replace your High Elf cantrip after a Long Rest; *Detect Magic* is always prepared and can be cast once per Long Rest without a slot.
- **Fey Ancestry** — You have adv. on saves to avoid or end Charmed.
- **Keen Senses** — Proficient in Medicine.
- **Trance** — You do not need sleep; magic cannot put you to sleep; a conscious 4-hour meditation completes a Long Rest.
- **Twilight Domain Spells** — *Faerie Fire*, *Sleep*, *Moonbeam*, and *See Invisibility* are always prepared.
- **Twilight Sanctuary** — Your Channel Divinity creates the twilight sphere shown in Actions.
- **Divinely Favored** — *Augury* is always prepared and may be cast once per Long Rest without a slot; the feat's chosen 1st-level spell is not recorded in the export.
- **Fey-Touched** — *Misty Step* is always prepared and may be cast once per Long Rest without a slot; its chosen 1st-level Divination/Enchantment spell is not recorded in the export.
- **The Hush Before Dark: Standing Between** — First time each turn a creature within 5 ft. is hit: your next healing spell before the end of your next turn restores +1d6 HP.
- **The Hush Before Dark: Step In Front** — The first attack each round against a creature within 5 ft. of you by an attacker also within 5 ft. of you has disadvantage.
- **The Hush Before Dark: Coral's Thread** — The bracelet warms when you make a choice genuinely your own rather than one expected of you.
