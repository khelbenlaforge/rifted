## Appearance

Half-elven, which in practice means he reads as human until you notice his eyes, the quality of his stillness, the way he always seems to know which direction is north. Medium build, practical clothing, no armor that isn't made of something that once grew. Carries a staff. There is a crown of twisted blackened metal at his belt — it doesn't look like much until it's in use.

## Personality

Sage. Patient. Quiet in company, listening rather than withdrawn. He is the member of the Knights most likely to say nothing for an hour and then, when he speaks, to be exactly right. Occasionally grim in a way that has nothing to do with cruelty; more the acceptance of someone who has watched natural processes play out, people dying among them, until he made his peace with it.

The land speaks to him. He takes it seriously.

## Background

Half-elven druid, Circle of the Land (temperate forest). A founding member of the [[Knights of Myth Drannor]]. His Harper affiliation is loose — he has worked with them, he shares their values, but he operates primarily from his own sense of what the natural world requires.

He was positioned at Shadow Ridge before the battle began, watching for movement from the north. He knows the terrain of the Dalelands the way most people know their own houses.

## Role in the Battle

His Harper unit was deployed along Shadow Ridge before the Knights arrived. He comes in with them at Round 12.

Once in the battle, he positions for control: Call Lightning if the sky cooperates (it usually does for him), Conjure Animals to hold a flank, Entangle on massed enemies. He will identify the wounded and move to them if the moment allows.

His Fire Crown goes once per day. He doesn't waste it.
