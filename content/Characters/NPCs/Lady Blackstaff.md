> # Lady Blackstaff

Lady Blackstaff is the stern-faced matriarch of the Crystal City branch of the Blackstaff family, a woman in her thirties or forties who carries old authority without needing to raise her voice.

She received [[Min Ji-ah]], [[Nanuk]], [[Terra]], and [[Regius]] at the family's mansion after [[Garm]] escorted them in, standing in for a household whose formal head was absent despite sitting on the Council of Seven. Controlled and severe at first, she let real emotion show when Min Ji-ah described her vision of [[Corran Blackstaff]]'s landing and death in Corranor; she confessed she had never known what he looked like, and Min Ji-ah's account matched the history her family preserves.

She was candid about the limits of her knowledge. On the [[Sunless Citadel]], she knows nothing beyond what [[Elmindaria L'Thorian|Daria]] and [[Garm]] have already told her, and said the family will speak to the Hucreles when the island next descends. But once she examined [[Elminster's Spellbook]], her caution shifted into urgency: recognizing spells written in a script only very advanced wizards would know, she judged the book older than the Blackstaff family's own knowledge and concluded that a Blackstaff expedition to the citadel can no longer wait. After the Feast of the Moon, two days after the party's meeting with the Claret, the family intends to travel there with the Director of Antiquities.
