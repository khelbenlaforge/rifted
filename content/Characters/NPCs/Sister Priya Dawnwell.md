## Description

- Young, unguarded cleric tending Stormberg's chapel of Lathander. Told Roberto that his vision also called him to [[Helm]] and [[Sune]]. Explained her god's theology: focused on beginnings, indifferent to what happens to a soul once it's gone. She also asked Rhona, in Roberto's hearing, whether there was word of "the ancient floating temple of Lathander recently discovered." She never identified it as any specific place.
