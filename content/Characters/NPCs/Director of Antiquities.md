> # Director of Antiquities

The Director of Antiquities runs the antiquities department at the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]]. He is approximately seven to eight feet tall, bald, with gray skin and glasses - he can reach the top shelves without a ladder. He lit up when [[Shen]] described the murals in the [[Sunless Citadel]], and immediately made Shen a department collaborator. His department's central question: why does no one in Aenath remember anything from before two hundred years ago?
