## Appearance

Elven — meaning he looks younger than he is by centuries. Lean, precise, moves with the particular economy of someone who has learned to conserve effort in a fight because fights are sometimes very long. Carries a silver-bladed longsword whose edge catches the light differently from ordinary steel. His eyes are always cataloguing the room.

## Personality

Academic. Curious. Comfortable with complexity in a way that comes from having had a long time to sit with it. He does not underestimate anyone, which is a quality that took decades to develop and is now automatic. Warm with [[Jhessail Silvertree]], dry with everyone else. He will engage earnestly with an interesting question in the middle of a fight if the question is genuinely interesting.

He takes the craft of fighting seriously and the craft of magic seriously and sees no contradiction between them. The sword knows where the spell can't reach. The spell knows where the sword can't reach. Together: there is no gap.

## Background

An elf, which means he has been doing this longer than most of the Knights have been alive. A Fighter and a Wizard in the proper sense — not an archetype that gestures at one while being fundamentally the other, but genuinely excellent at both. He married [[Jhessail Silvertree]] in front of the Elven Court, where he proposed to her.

His silver-bladed longsword was commissioned specifically. Silver because some things can only be killed with it, and he had learned early that the time to find that out is not mid-fight.

## Role in the Battle

Arrives at Round 12 with the Knights. Immediately assesses the most technically dangerous enemy on the field — a spellcaster, a named officer, anything with meaningful magic resistance — and moves toward it. He is the one who will Counterspell the enemy cleric's Spirit Guardians before it becomes everyone's problem.

He fights in melee and casts simultaneously. Watching him work is instructive and slightly alarming.
