## Appearance

Slim, strikingly beautiful. Fiery orange hair. Greenish-gray eyes that have the quality of watching more than they reveal. She dresses for someone who won't be trading blows in melee, but whose spells will decide the fight anyway.

## Personality

Passionate. Strong-willed. Extremely skilled, and she carries it without arrogance or false modesty. She knows what she can do. She has been the most dangerous person in the room before and has learned to calibrate accordingly. Warm with people she trusts. Precise with everyone else.

Married to [[Merith Strongbow]]. She taught [[Illistyl Elventree]] the Art. She is protective of Illistyl the way a master is protective of a student who has exceeded expectations — with pride, and with the specific worry of someone who knows exactly what the work requires.

## Background

One of the founding members of the [[Knights of Myth Drannor]]. Her mastery of evocation magic is not academic — it was forged in the field, running operations in dungeons and cities where the consequences of a Fireball aimed wrong were immediate and permanent. This is why Sculpt Spells is not a technical curiosity for her; it is the thing that lets her fight without killing her friends.

## Role in the Battle

Arrives at Round 12 with the Knights. Positions 60ft from the heaviest concentration of enemy soldiers. Does not need to be told where to stand. Begins identifying priority targets within two rounds.

Her Wand of Magic Missiles handles anything that needs to die quietly and specifically. Her Fireball handles everything else — and with Sculpt Spells, she can center it on the party's position.
