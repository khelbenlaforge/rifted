## Appearance

Young-looking even for an elf — she is genuinely one of the less experienced members of the Knights, which puts her in the company of veterans old enough to be her great-grandparents. Slight. Alert. Has the quality of someone who is always paying attention to three things at once, and is generally right about all three.

## Personality

Sharp. Quick-moving, quick-thinking. She was tutored by [[Jhessail Silvertree]] and took to the Art with an aptitude that surprised everyone except Jhessail, who had seen it coming. She also has an intuitive talent for what the Realms would call psionics — a sensitivity to thought and emotion that goes beyond what her spells technically allow. She notices things people are feeling before they say them.

She is [[Torm of the Dales]]'s girlfriend. She has been worried about him.

## Background

Member of the [[Knights of Myth Drannor]]. Elventree by name — she has ties to the community near Myth Drannor, which gives her a stake in the Knights' longer purpose. Trained directly by Jhessail. Lower level than the rest of the Knights but growing fast, and her Portent ability makes her disproportionately useful in critical moments.

When the Knights arrived at Shadowdale, Illistyl saw "Torm" already fighting and felt something she can't name — relief that he's alive, and something else underneath it that she's filing away for later.

## Role in the Battle

She arrives at Round 12 with the Knights. Stays near Jhessail when possible — both for protection and because they work well together, Jhessail providing area effects while Illistyl provides precision and Portent.

Her Wand of Magic Missiles handles anything that needs targeted, reliable damage. Her Portent dice are the tactical asset — she will save them for a moment that matters.

**The Torm Complication:** At the first moment the battle eases, she will try to reach "Torm." She's going to touch his arm, say something specific, expect recognition. JJ doesn't know who she is. This is a problem he will have to navigate, mid-battle, in character, with no warning.

## Session 15

She arrived at Round 12 with the other Knights and went straight to work. Between phases at the Ashaba Bridge, she found "Torm." *"You're alive."* Launched him back into the fray with a force spell. Kissed him on the cheek as he flew.

[[Jonathan Jordan]] had no idea who she was. He played it as well as he could.

She filed the specific quality of his confusion away. She doesn't have a name for it yet. She will.
