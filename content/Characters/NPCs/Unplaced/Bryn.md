> # Bryn

Bryn is the cart driver [[Layla]] hired to ferry new arrivals from the Hexad on to [[Westward]].

Matter-of-fact, and unbothered by anything about a new body's shape or a stranger's bloodline—infernal, celestial, it made nobody good or bad by itself, just interesting to ask about. She fixed a broken wheel herself mid-road, without waiting on anyone. She dropped the party at the Cliffwatch's door without asking for anything past her fee. She travels with a quiet, armed companion.

## Session 3 (AUGs)

Met the party with [[Jack XLR|Jack]] still unconscious and fire-wreathed from the Hexad, unfit for the road; her companion carried him toward the nearest druid circle instead, with [[Gregory Yang]] going along rather than leave him behind. [[Mike Kanbei|Mike]], [[Ruan Myrt Zaltana|Ruan]], [[Rachel Tan|Rachel]] and [[Rismawati Bayu|Risma]] went on with her alone. The road gave them a broken wheel, which she reseated and drove home with a mallet herself, and a shared wineskin that hit some of them harder than others. She talked freely the whole way—world, calendar, currency, the Guild's own crest—and let a few of them talk back about home. At the Cliffwatch's door she waved off any coin past her fee and left them with [[Dessa Kestrel]].
