> # Oskar Brell

Far-ground rigger on Stormberg's Wall crews, twenty years in, no family in the city. Broad through the shoulders in the way twenty years of rope work builds, hands scarred and calloused past feeling much anymore. Weathered face, older than his years, wind-burned red across the cheeks and nose from full days on exposed Wall stretches. A bandage on his forearm gone brown at the edges, from an injury he walked off and went back out on the next morning; everything else about him is old wear, patched gear, a coat gone thin at the elbows because new gear goes where the nobles can see it. Talks in short bursts and stops when he's said enough. Stoic and terse by long habit, not bitterness, a workhorse who stopped expecting better a long time ago and doesn't perform his suffering for anyone. What he wants isn't sympathy but comprehension: he'll stand up and speak at the forum, but he wants a noble to hear "a man hurt his arm" and understand why it matters, not just log it as an incident.
