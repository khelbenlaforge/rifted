> # Daria

Daria is a tall elven wizard associated with the [[Adamantine Guild]] in [[Crystal City]]. She holds some authority over [[Istar Silverlock]] — as of Session 21, Istar was serving a punishment under her direction (cleaning duties, wearing an apron). She was present at the guild's arena testing of the new arrivals, standing alongside [[Erky]].

The nature and extent of her role in the guild is unclear.
