## Description

An ancient red wyrm whose name the cult that built this place spoke in reverence and in fear. They called him *the Devourer* as doctrine, never as mockery. Long before the [[Sunless Citadel]] sank into the earth, Ashardalon commanded a power that was not purely draconic: he had merged with something demonic, and the merger marked him visibly. Those who saw him described a scar across his chest in the shape of a great winged figure — a wound that had become a part of him.

Whether Ashardalon still lives, sleeps, or has long since passed from the world is unknown to most. The cult's enduring inscription answers for itself: *He Who Devours Returns.*

## Legacy

### The Citadel and the Cult

The [[Sunless Citadel]] was built by Ashardalon's followers — a dragon cult devoted to the great wyrm's glory and, eventually, to the promise of his return. The cult's most significant construction is the Sanctum hidden behind the Sealed Door on the Grove Level — though the Sanctum's true contents predate the cult, suggesting that Ashardalon's followers built atop something far older than their faith.

An illusory statue of the great dragon guards the Sanctum's entrance, dissolving only for those who pass the door's divine trial. The relationship between the cult and the chamber's cosmological significance — statuary of [[Mystra]], [[Gond]], [[Bahamut]], [[Takhisis]], and the four primordial elemental gods arranged around a model of [[Aenath]] — raises profound questions about what drew the cult here in the first place.

### Gulthias and the Tree

The chain that connects Ashardalon to the Grove Level runs through a single name: Gulthias.

Gulthias was the cult's high priest — the intermediary between the dragon and his followers. When Ashardalon fell to a mortal wound, Gulthias preserved what remained of the dragon's power and dedicated his life to preparing for his lord's return. In pursuit of that charge, Gulthias came to the [[Sunless Citadel]] — and here he ended. He was killed and staked to the ground. Vampire lore holds that a stake drives the undead back into death; what happened to Gulthias was different. The stake became a tree.

The [[Gulthias Tree]] on the Grove Level is what remains of the cult's most devoted servant. The red and white fruits it bears, the blights it spawns, the thralldom it imposes on those who eat of it — all of it is the dragon's influence, still reaching, centuries after the cult that planted his worship is dust.

## Relationships

- Worshipped by a dragon cult that built and inhabited the [[Sunless Citadel]]
- **[[Gulthias Tree]]** — the tree on the Grove Level is the transformed remains of Gulthias, Ashardalon's high priest; the dragon's influence persists through it
- Connected to [[Bahamut]] and [[Takhisis]] through the Sanctum's statuary, though the nature of this connection is unclear
