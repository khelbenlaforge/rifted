> # Sellas Verenthal

Sellas Verenthal is the wayward young lady of House Verenthal, slipping out of the estate at inappropriate hours and becoming difficult for her own household to track.

She has not been introduced to the party and does not yet know she is being watched. What the household will say about her, carefully, is that she is loud in the way old money can afford to be loud — opinionated, performative, allergic to being ignored, the kind of presence that turns a quiet room into a production. Whether that's the whole truth or a story the family prefers to the alternative is exactly the sort of thing ten nights of surveillance might answer.

Her family has not yet moved to open confrontation. Instead, through steward [[Ostrin Feld]] and on [[Soral Vayne]]'s introduction, they quietly hired [[Regius|Reggie]] to trail her at night without making contact unless the situation turns dangerous. As a last resort, the house provided him ten crystal glyphs to break if Sellas gets into real trouble, summoning help without forcing the family to admit publicly that something is wrong.
