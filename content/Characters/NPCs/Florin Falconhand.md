## Appearance

Tall, broad-shouldered, curly hair. A scar runs across his right hand from a childhood encounter with a leashed baby red dragon — he tried to pet it. He wears this like most people wear their worst ideas: plainly, without apology. Not a handsome man in any formal sense, but the kind of face that reads as trustworthy at a hundred yards.

## Personality

Direct. Honest. Polite in the way someone is polite when they genuinely mean it, not as performance. He leads the Knights the way a river leads — not by declaring itself, but by consistently being where it's going. Protective to his core. Will charge the heaviest thing on the field because someone has to.

## Background

Senior member and de facto leader of the [[Knights of Myth Drannor]]. Husband of [[Dove Falconhand]]. A Harper operating out of Cormyr and the Dalelands. He has run enough operations to know that the plan falls apart on contact and the people around you are the only plan that lasts.

He knows Elminster personally. He is here because the dale is in danger and because Elminster sent word. Those two reasons are, to Florin, the same reason.

## Role in the Battle

Arrives at Game Round 12 from the Mill Bridge road, Dove at his side, the rest of the Knights behind them. Finds [[Torm of the Dales]] already fighting. Pulls his horse up for exactly one breath — a look he doesn't have time for, a grip on Torm's shoulder as he passes — and then he's through the enemy line and into whatever needs hitting most.

He will not have time to ask questions until the battle is over. That moment is coming. He will want answers.
