## Appearance

Tattered leather armor. Tear-streaked face. A bloody blade still in her hand when the party sees her; the blood came from fighting through the rubble of the Temple to reach them, not from an enemy. She does not look like she has been crying. She looks like someone who was crying and stopped because there was still work to do.

## Personality

Grim. Fierce, in the way that comes from devotion rather than cruelty, and right now that devotion has just watched its object die. She will have answers. She will have them *now*. She's reasonable enough in principle, but nobody's going to convince her of anything quickly in this state.

## Background

A Knight of Myth Drannor and close companion of [[Elminster]]. She was fighting in the battle outside the Temple of Lathander when the explosion occurred. She came through the rubble with drawn sword because she found strangers standing in the wreckage where Elminster had been. The math looked simple. She was wrong, but she doesn't know that yet.

## Role in the Prequel Vision

She delivers the accusation that ends the prequel vision:

> *"I am Sharantyr. Lay down your weapons — now — or perish. You will stand trial on the morrow for the murder of Elminster, Sage of Shadowdale."*

The moment she finishes speaking, the vision fractures. The PCs never see what happens next.
