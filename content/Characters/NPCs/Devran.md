## Description
A stocky, weathered dwarf who's spent enough years on Stormberg's docks that the brisk coastal wind barely registers anymore — thick oilskin coat, a logbook he never seems to be without, and the kind of squint that comes from reading manifests in bad light. He's known primarily through the words of his brother, the [[Dwarven Bartender]] of the [[Garnet District]], who references him when speaking of the wilds beyond the city walls, but in Stormberg itself he's a familiar fixture: less a guild official than the person everyone eventually gets pointed to when they need to know what's actually going on.

## Background
[[Devran]] resides in [[Stormberg]], a settlement on the northern coast. His brother, a bartender in [[Crystal City]]'s [[Garnet District]], often shares secondhand knowledge of the [[Corranor]] main island's wilderness -- knowledge that likely originates from [[Devran]]'s experiences in the more remote reaches of the land. He was mentioned in conversation with [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Augustus Kennedy]], suggesting he may serve as a useful contact for those venturing northward.
