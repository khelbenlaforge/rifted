## Appearance

Hollis wears his plate the way some men wear a good suit: polished, correctly fitted, never a strap out of place. He's built for the shield wall, not speed — broad through the shoulders, a jaw that looks perpetually set for an argument he hasn't had yet. Hair cropped short under the helm. His holy symbol, Tyr's balanced scales resting on a warhammer, stays worn openly over his breastplate, never tucked away. He salutes before he speaks to anyone with rank, out of old habit.

## Personality

Hollis takes his oaths at face value, which the Hall of Justice trained out of most of its paladins years ago and never quite managed to train out of him. He says what he means and expects the same courtesy back — arguing with him is exhausting precisely because he only concedes a point when he's actually convinced. He's seen enough of the world to know how badly it can go. He just never decided that knowing it required becoming cynical.

*(Left loose deliberately — the guest player should feel free to run him however they want to at the table.)*

## Background

Hollis came up through the Hall of Justice's training courtyards the standard way: an Acolyte's postulancy, calligrapher's ink still under his nails from years of copying case law before he ever drew a blade in anger. [[Sir Gareth Ironhand]] signed off on his ordination personally, which around the Hall counts for more than most people realize. He was partnered with [[The Exodus/The Door Kickers/PCs/Augustus Kennedy|Augustus Kennedy]] not long after Augustus's own conversion path settled — the two of them work the same rounds now, backing each other on whatever the Church of Tyr's judiciary hands down that week.

## Role in the Story

Augustus's partner on the beat. Wherever Tyr's law-enforcement duties take Augustus, Hollis is usually a step behind or beside him — which means he's a plausible reason for a guest character to be standing next to the party when something goes wrong.

### Features

- **Weapon Mastery** — Longsword *(Sap)*: on hit, target has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn. Javelin *(Slow)*: on hit and damage, reduce the target's Speed by 10 ft. until the start of your next turn (doesn't stack beyond 10 ft.). Swap either assignment on a Long Rest.
- **Fighting Style — Dueling**: +2 to damage rolls when wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapon.
- **Lay on Hands** — Pool of 25 HP that replenishes on a Long Rest; spend it (Bonus Action, touch) to heal, or spend 5 HP from the pool to cure the Poisoned condition instead of healing.
- **Paladin's Smite** — Always has Divine Smite prepared; can cast it once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot.
- **Channel Divinity** — 2 uses; regains 1 on a Short Rest, all on a Long Rest. Choose Sacred Weapon or Divine Sense each time.
- **Sacred Weapon** *(Channel Divinity)* — As part of the Attack action, expend a use to imbue a held melee weapon: add your Charisma modifier (+3) to attack rolls with it (minimum +1) and its damage type can become Radiant, for 10 minutes or until used again. The weapon sheds Bright Light 20 ft. and Dim Light 20 ft. further. Ends early for free, or if you stop carrying the weapon.
- **Oath of Devotion Spells** *(always prepared, don't count against the 6 prepared spells)* — Protection from Evil and Good, Shield of Faith (3rd level); Aid, Zone of Truth (5th level).
- **Faithful Steed** — Always has Find Steed prepared; can cast it once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot.
- **Extra Attack** — Attacks twice when taking the Attack action.
- **No Ritual Casting** — 2024 Paladins don't have a Ritual Casting feature; none of Hollis's spells can be cast as rituals.
