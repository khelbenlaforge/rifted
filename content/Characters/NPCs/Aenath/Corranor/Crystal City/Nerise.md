Nerise is the atypical, sword-carrying mage Keeper of Exploration, rarely in the city and dressed in practical robes, who oversees the returning expedition sent to discover other lands.

## Appearance

A sword at the hip where the other Keepers carry nothing but their office, practical robes instead of ceremonial ones, none of the usual bearing a city mage puts on for a crowd. She looks like someone who spends more time away from Crystal City than in it, because she does.

## Personality

Nerise doesn't perform for the Council's audience the way the others do. Council business she doesn't care about, she doesn't pretend to care about. What holds her attention is the expedition she sent out to find other lands, now on its way home, and she watches the rest of the proceedings the way someone watches a meeting they'd rather not be in. She isn't cold, exactly. She's just somewhere else.

## Background

Keeper of Exploration on the [[Corranor Council]], responsible for what lies beyond Corranor, not what happens inside it. She's rarely in the city because the job doesn't keep her there. Her expedition, sent to find other lands, is the only Council matter she's shown genuine investment in.

## Role in the Story

At the Council's appearance before the city in Session 28, Nerise stood apart from the others, her attention on the crowd, her mind already on her expedition and not on the fiend or the Temple. She flinched, slightly, when Oriel turned his inquiry on the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Diamond District/Temple of Kelemvor|Temple of Kelemvor]]. Doran, beside her, did not.

She holds one of the Council's two confirmed votes against the party in the Session 29 status hearing, alongside [[Doran]]. The hearing never reached the party, so nothing has actually tested that vote yet.
