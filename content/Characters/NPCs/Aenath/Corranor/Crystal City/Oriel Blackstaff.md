Oriel Blackstaff is the tall, lanky, icy, extremely poised Keeper of the Writ on the Corranor Council and head of House Blackstaff, with jet-black hair and beard and black robes with silver lace.

## Appearance

Tall and lanky, jet-black hair and beard, black robes lined in silver. Everything about him drops the temperature of a room a few degrees before he's said a word.

## Personality

Oriel is exacting, unhurried, and entirely unbothered by the discomfort he causes. He doesn't raise his voice because he's never needed to; his authority is assumed, never argued for. He reaches into matters outside his formal jurisdiction without apology when he judges the situation warrants it, and doesn't explain himself further than a single sentence unless pressed.

## Background

Keeper of the Writ, the Council's own legal authority, and head of House Blackstaff, the oldest family on the continent. His office makes and interprets the city's laws; his family name carries weight the office alone wouldn't.

## Role in the Story

Oriel declared the Cistern Street shrine unlawful assembly and its property forfeit to the city, then opened an inquiry into the Temple of Kelemvor and the Knights of the Eternal Order's own admissions during a vigil, reaching into religious law he has no formal standing in and daring anyone to say so aloud. He remanded [[Ser Tamsin Rook]] to his own Order for judgment, keeping it off the public square.

He holds the Council's deciding vote in the Session 29 status hearing on the party, split 50-50 between [[Doran]] and [[Nerise]] against and [[Garm]], [[Wystan]], and an unnamed gnome Keeper for. The hearing never reached the party, so his vote remains uncast.
