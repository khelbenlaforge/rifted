> # Director of Antiquities

The Director of Antiquities runs the antiquities department at the [[Ether Scryer's Academy]]. He is approximately seven to eight feet tall, bald, with gray skin and glasses - he can reach the top shelves without a ladder. He lit up when [[Shen]] described the murals in the [[Sunless Citadel]], and immediately made Shen a department collaborator. Through him, Shen also became a paid Academy employee: thirty-two gold a week, sixty-four earned over the two weeks he spent tracking a hunting party through the wilds.

His department's central question hasn't changed: why does no one in Aenath remember anything from before roughly two hundred years ago? Ask the Director and he'll give you a harder number—two hundred and twenty-two years, precise enough to argue over. Ask anyone else at the Academy and the figure softens back to a round estimate. Nobody there agrees on why the gap exists at all; the Antiquities department has one theory, every other faculty has its own, and the politics between them run hot.
