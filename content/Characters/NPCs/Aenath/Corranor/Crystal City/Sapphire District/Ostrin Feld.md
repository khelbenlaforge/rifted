> # Ostrin Feld

Ostrin Feld is House Verenthal's steward, a precise and visibly tired man handling delicate problems himself because he trusts very few people to do it quietly.

He watches doors more than faces, measures every word before he spends it, and has the air of someone trying to keep a household standing while listening for the next blow. When he is about to say less than he knows, two fingers tap lightly against his signet ring. He does not offer easy warmth, only competence, and even his request for discretion carries the edge of fear. Whatever threat hangs over House Verenthal, Ostrin is not merely managing around it; he is frightened of it too.
