> # Sellas Verenthal

Sellas Verenthal is the wayward young lady of House Verenthal, slipping out of the estate at inappropriate hours and becoming difficult for her own household to track.

What the household will say about her, carefully, is that she is loud in the way old money can afford to be loud—opinionated, performative, allergic to being ignored, the kind of presence that turns a quiet room into a production.

Her family has not yet moved to open confrontation.
