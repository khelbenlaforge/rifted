## Appearance

A fox-like creature with midnight blue velvety fur and multiple tails — the kind of creature that belongs in a fae glade rather than a goblin dungeon. She moves with quiet, watchful grace and responds to address in Sylvan.

## Background

Her name was given rather than chosen — whether by the goblins or by circumstance is unclear. She answers to it regardless.

## Session 10 Update (The Door Kickers)

Found caged in [[Durbuluk Goblins]] territory within the [[Sunless Citadel]], held alongside the gnome acolyte [[Erky]]. The party persuaded [[Grenl]] to release both.

A companion who joined the party in Session 10 and has since become quietly integral to it.

## Session 12 Update (The Door Kickers)

Cautious and calculating at first, Little Sister spent her early time with the party observing rather than committing. Over time—particularly after descending to the Grove Level—something shifted. She began choosing the party rather than simply tolerating them, and by the time of the Sanctum she was in the fight properly, engaged and present in a way that felt deliberate.

Like [[Balsag]] and [[Erky]], she cannot perceive the [[Sanctum of Ashardalon|Sanctum]]'s true contents—only the Ashardalon illusion and the ancient elven architecture. Why the Sanctum reveals itself only to the five from Earth remains an open question.
