Alys Rook is [[Ser Tamsin Rook]]'s sister. She died in [[Crystal City]] five years ago, and her name never reached the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Diamond District/Temple of Kelemvor|Temple of Kelemvor]]—her soul has been stranded there, uncounted, ever since.

## Background

Nothing about the death itself is documented. No cause, no circumstances—only that it happened five years ago, and that her brother never stopped believing the temple had failed her. He never pursued it through the Order; doubting his own institution wasn't a thought he let himself finish. So he served it anyway, faithfully, for five years, until her name came due and he broke the one rule he'd never bent for anyone else.

## Role in the Story

In Session 28, First Gravelord [[Cadmus Tallow]] named her publicly during the temple's overnight recitation for the city's dead—the one name on the roll he could not read, because the soul behind it had never arrived to be counted at all. Her brother had known the whole time, and the night before the recitation he opened a door for people who promised him they could give her rest—the same unsanctioned rite he'd already confessed to the party in Session 27, letting [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Shar|Shar]] cultists past his post in exchange for that promise. Rather than let the city's anger land on Tamsin, Cadmus took the failure onto his own office in front of the crowd: *"I am expected to say that he betrayed us. I have read the book. The book says we failed her first."*
