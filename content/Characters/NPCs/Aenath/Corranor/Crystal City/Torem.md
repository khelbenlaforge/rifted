> # Torem

Torem is a birdfolk ranger and senior member of the [[Azure Tamers]] who oversees the communications network across Corranor cities and keeps eyes on movement of people and goods.

---

Race:: Birdfolk
Location:: Azure Tamers Tower, [[Crystal City]]
Affiliation:: [[Azure Tamers]]

- Took [[Nanuk]] on aerial scouting aboard Little Bella during approximately 21st Uktar downtime
- Showed Nanuk the supply chain: magical creature blood flows east to [[Crystal City]] via the alabaster highway and then to the Academy and Alchemist's Guild
- Does not track who buys the blood after it arrives
- Aware of the [[Azure Tamers]] patrol network covering the eastern road and frontier communities
- [[Stormberg]] mines the northern region; [[Coldford]] on the east coast has an Azure Tamers presence

## Session 29

- Gave [[Nanuk]] a Stormberg travel-time table.
- Flagged herds migrating south early and heavier than the season should—cause unknown.
- Offered Bella the griffin as a rider-only mount, transferable, max 2 riders.
- Confirmed via an advantage Nature check that the grove will be sensed rather than sighted from the air.
- Hired [[Nanuk]] for a paid job: report back on why the herds are running early and heavy, if the road takes him near them.
- Road intel: stay on the road; heavy forest between Crystal City and [[Stormberg]]; floating islands are a sky hazard, not a ground one; predators trail the herds south, and anything bigger than a house means run, not fight; bandits likelier holed up against the cold than active, but any watching the road may still come looking, with thinner winter patrols to stop them; roadside taverns and campsites break up the trip.
