## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- First met on screen at the Wash Court in the Old Streets, in the uniform of the Landgate with rank cords at her belt, drinking tea and counting jerkins aloud while a gate guard washed leather armour.
- Told [[Odette Langford]] not to look so rich in Coldford and sent the gate guard running to Emery's for plain wool clothes, with the bill to go to Petra.
- Said [[House Colwyn|Lord Colwyn]] pays her but has little power and every Manifest House holds more; said the Houses grow rich stockpiling food and are not above smuggling spell reagents and rare ores, including flight stones that by law belong to the Council or one great house.
- Warned Odette she was asking sensitive questions in a very open place, and to stay away from anyone with a ruby earring: two studs, run; three, leave the city. Pointed her to the [[Temple of Selûne]] and [[Sister Margaret]].
- Later came to the Tallhouse, sat down with Petra and the party and named [[The Claret]]: red garnet studs, one for a low-level member, two for a sergeant, three for a lieutenant; said Coldford is where the Claret makes its money, so its members leave the city's people alone. Argued with Petra over smuggling and trafficking, as old friends do.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- [[Odette Langford]] brought leftover bread for her guardsmen and asked about a man in a fancy hat.
- Tamsin said he sticks out and moves like a shadow; he is a regular around the Tallyrows, the back roads and the Manifest House side near the [[Old Bearded Man]] pub, and she saw him the previous morning talking to [[Marl Dunning]].
- She described him as slender, clean-shaven and tanned, with deep bright violet eyes, always walking alone.
- She said she did not find him suspicious and thought he would tire of Coldford in three or four days.
- Odette's Detect Magic found a residual enchantment on Tamsin, not aimed at her, that encourages people to feel all is well.
