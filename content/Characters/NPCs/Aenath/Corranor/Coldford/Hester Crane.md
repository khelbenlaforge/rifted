## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- A baker in her forties with flour in the creases of her knuckles, working a bakery one lane below the Lamp Stair in the Old Streets. She keeps time by the tally-bells at the docks, since Coldford has no clocks.
- Took [[Odette Langford]] on as an apprentice. Odette rose ten minutes before the dawn bell ("You're cutting it close, my dear"), learned to fold butter into dough for the city's version of the croissant, and was paid about two silver a day if the loaves were out on time, one if she came late, and nothing if the bread was already out.
- Was behind on the morning loaves, and the Wash Court notices when bread runs late. By the end of the stretch Odette could shape, fold and time a loaf, but how much butter to use, Hester's real craft, still escaped her.
- The work put Odette within reach of the steward of [[House Colwyn]], and bakers know the people at the Widow's Orchard, where apples come from.

## Session 5 Update (The JULYs)
- Odette handed out bread from the bakery she works at, on the street, as cover when she and [[Kai]] were nearly made.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- On 8 Nightal [[Odette Langford]] asked to carry bread to [[House Colwyn]]; Hester said no, kindly, saying Odette was doing good work at the ovens and did not need to be a delivery girl.
