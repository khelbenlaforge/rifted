## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- Met [[Kai]] at the Aerie after Kai climbed eleven stories of ladders and platforms to the top, and explained the harbour bells: each pier has one, and when a ship is coming in only that pier's bell may ring, so ships can steer by sound through the fog.
- Pointed out the three districts (the harbour, the Tallyrows and the Old Streets) and a walled, well-built castle with no name on it among the Old Streets' poor lanes.
- Said the Aerie keeps one bird in the sky at a time in case a hungry sea monster comes in at landfall, that the fishing boats are the city's lifeline, and that the [[The Manifest Houses|Manifest Houses]] run Coldford. Called [[House Colwyn|Lord Colwyn]] a nice man who is almost ineffectual and commands fewer soldiers than some of the Houses.
- Introduced the greater griffin Tiffy, the size of a horse, and Leartha, an old, partly blind eagle brooding her eggs on the top landing.
- Offered to take Kai on if Petra sent word.
