## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- A very tall woman with elven and celestial blood, at least a hundred and thirty years old, met by [[Odette Langford]] near the goddess's statue in the [[Temple of Selûne]] when Odette brought bread. She noticed Odette's bracer and asked after [[Petra Hale]], whom she had not seen in weeks.
- Told Odette that [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Selûne|Selûne]] waxes full at the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Lore/Feast of the Moon|Feast of the Moon]], that her ally [[Sehanine Moonbow]] sends strength to guard her as she waxes, and that in the beginning there were only Selûne and her sister [[Shar]], two sides of the moon. Worshippers of Shar in [[Crystal City]] once kidnapped a few priests at the Feast to try to stop it.
- Said the temple has no paladins, only clerics and a sorcerer, because Selûne is also a goddess of magic. Said Tyr's main temple is in Crystal City and [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Coldford|Coldford]] has none; that [[Kelemvor]]'s temple is the most important of the others, with two of his paladins in Coldford; and that [[Lathander]] has a temple here too.
- Said a small community of aasimar lives in Coldford, one working for the [[Azure Tamers]] and one as a dock worker, and that there are a few tieflings as well.
- Told in confidence by Odette about the trade in tiefling and aasimar blood, she made a sign and cast Commune, then said "This is not good" and sensed that the tiefling community would be hit first. She asked whether Odette had seen ruby studs, which Odette had not, set a Silence over the temple's entrance and windows against eavesdroppers, and later lifted it from the door.
- Said she would warn Selûne's worshippers who are tieflings or aasimar and spread the word. Told Odette to watch for [[The Claret]], who she felt were starting to move fast, and said Petra would work from the Guild's side and she from the temple's. She would speak to Kelemvor's paladins and the [[Monks of the Long Death]] too, once Petra agreed.
