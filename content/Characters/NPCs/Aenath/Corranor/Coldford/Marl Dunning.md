## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- [[Ruairí]], watching the east end of the Tallyrows from the shadow of an awning, saw a man in a good hat with two studs stop at Marl's stall, buy and sell nothing, touch his hat brim, say something pleasant and move on. It happened three times that day.
- Each time, Marl looked at his brass scales, put a weight on one, and counted something off his ledger.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- On 8 Nightal he was wary of the brass on [[Ruairí]]'s wrist and stiffened when asked about the fancy gentleman who kept stopping by; he said only that this is a port town and only merchants come by, and Ruairí saw he was lying.
- After Ruairí rolled a natural 20 on him, Marl said his wife had died and had taught their daughter to read.
- He had met the man in the good hat only in the past week, never learned his name, and did not like how the man looked at his daughter.
- The man's papers were in order, and he sometimes came with goods and sometimes without.
- Red chalk check marks keep returning to one leg of his counter after each visit, though he rubs them off.
- He says he has been losing pieces of his memory. His daughter says he sometimes forgets to call the count; he denies it.
- He said he does what his House tells him and would pay any price to get his daughter out of this.
- [[Ruairí]]'s Detect Magic found memory alteration and suggestion cast more than once in that place; Ruairí has not told him.
