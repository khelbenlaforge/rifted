## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- [[Ruairí]] met her on the second stool at [[Marl Dunning]]'s stall on 8 Nightal.
- Her mother, who has died, taught her to read.
- She remembers what her father forgets: when the man in the good hat hands over a paper and tells Marl to mark off the goods, Marl marks them without checking, and he sometimes forgets to call the count.
- [[Ruairí]] offered her a place at [[The Tallhouse]]; it was settled that she stays at the stall through the busy morning and goes to the Tallhouse in the afternoon.
- [[Petra Hale]] showed her the papers she goes through all the time and called it important work for a branch leader; the girl was starstruck.
- [[Odette Langford]] offered to tutor her.
