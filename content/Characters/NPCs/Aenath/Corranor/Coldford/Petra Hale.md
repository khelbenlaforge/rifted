## Description

Built for the work, not the desk—a javelin-thrower's stance even standing still, a longsword worn like it's never once been ceremonial, and a way of angling herself in a room that always leaves her a clear line to the door. Reads a fight, or a person, half a beat before it actually starts. Keeps the Tallhouse's front room in better order than its staffing numbers have any right to allow.

## Personality

Direct without being cold. She doesn't posture, doesn't oversell the branch's thin resources, and doesn't pretend a problem is smaller than it is—she's the kind of honest that costs her something and she pays it anyway. Registers new members like she means every word of the induction, because she does; tests them the same way, plainly, with a reason stated up front rather than sprung on them. Wins or loses a fight without changing who she is afterward—no gloating, no sulking, just an honest read of what she actually saw.

## Background

Escort and freight-security work before the desk, the same trade [[Rhona Voss]] made at the Stormberg Wallwatch: field work first, an office second, never fully letting go of the field. Runs the Tallhouse—Coldford's Adamantine Guild branch—alone in every practical sense, thinner-staffed than the Wallwatch up in [[Stormberg]] and further from anyone who could spare her backup. Started quietly tracking a pattern of "insurance" shakedowns hitting small Manifest House stalls on her own time, off the clock, because nobody else currently can.

## Role in the Story

First real point of contact for The JULYs' Adamantine Guild standing in [[Coldford]] (Session 3)—registers their already-worn brass bracers rather than issuing fresh ones, then tests them personally in the Tallhouse's pocket-dimension yard before backing them without reservation. Same function as [[Rhona Voss]]'s Guild evaluation of the Understudies at the Stormberg Wallwatch, played in Petra's own register: not retiring-and-nostalgic like Rhona, but stretched thin and unusually careful right now specifically because of the shakedown pattern she's already tracking.

## D&D 5e Stat Block

*Encounter stat block—Petra is an intended Guild-evaluation combat encounter, same function as [[Rhona Voss]]'s build. Mixed from two real, mundane, non-magical CR-appropriate sources, the same construction method as Rhona's: the **Guard Captain (XMM, CR 4)** supplies the martial chassis (AC, attacks, ability scores, skills)—a direct thematic match for escort/freight-security work, distinct from Rhona's arena-fighter Gladiator base. The **Ambitious Assassin (BMT, CR 5, zero spellcasting)** supplies a partial legendary layer: Legendary Resistance, an Uncanny Dodge reaction, and two of its three legendary actions (renamed, re-pointed at Petra's own weapons; its invisibility-granting "Vanishing Escape" option was dropped as too far from a mundane security captain). See DM Notes above for full build rationale, sourcing, and encounter-math calibration.*
