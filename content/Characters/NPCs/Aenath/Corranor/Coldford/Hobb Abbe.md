## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- Recommended by [[Petra Hale]] to [[Ruairí]] as a job for a caster. About eighty, deaf in one ear, with a right arm bigger than Ruairí's thigh, he rings the tally-bells for a whole run of desks from a raised platform in the Tallyrows.
- Counts tallies under his breath and writes them down with his left hand, and loses his place when someone calls from the far end of the row. Ruairí cast Faerie Fire along the row so Hobb could see through the fog, and used Message to pass count changes down the line.
- Would not hand over the mallet. Paid about a silver a day, made chowder at the end of it, and gave Ruairí a seat on the platform with one of the best views of the Tallyrows.
- The [[The Manifest Houses|Manifest Houses]] noticed and wondered who the black cat was and why Hobb had let him help.
- Petra said she had heard good things about the three from [[Dagny Rusk|Dagny]], from the bakery and from Hobb.
