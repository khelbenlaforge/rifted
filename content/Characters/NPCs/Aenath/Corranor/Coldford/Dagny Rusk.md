## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- A lean woman in her sixties with a leather guard strapped on, found working down the row of bells at Fogbell Pier, tightening them and hammering out dents. She is a bell pilot, one of the few who know every code that guides a ship to its pier.
- Thinks the odd night bell is a prank by some kid or punk and does not believe the stories about the lost boat, though she admitted it rings only once every few weeks.
- Offered to teach [[Kai]] to be a bell pilot. Kai set about learning the codes and had not learned them all; it took him so long that he earned nothing and left him worn through.
- Told [[Petra Hale]] good things about the three.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- On the morning of 8 Nightal she was practising her bell codes along the row of bells.
- She taught [[Kai]] the hold-off warning for bad tide or water and the night codes for the first sixty feet. She described the signal a pier sends to the others when a ship is coming in, each pier's own come-in code, the emergency codes for a ship that ignores orders, and a further night set beyond the first sixty feet.
- Kai learned that a pilot has to know about seven or eight come-in codes, and that day bells are simpler, since a boat can be seen for the first sixty feet even in thick fog, while at night boats are guided in from about two hundred feet out.
- If a ship comes in that does not know the pier codes at all, she said, Kai's job is the most important one: signal every pier at once.
- Kai left with about ten to fifteen percent more of the codes.
- She asked whether Kai was very sure the night bell was not a prank. When he said he did not think so, she said they would need evidence, or one of them would have to hear it.
