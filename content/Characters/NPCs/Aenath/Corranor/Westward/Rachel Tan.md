## Description

Nothing about her draws the eye on purpose. Rachel keeps her horns filed close, her tail tucked under whatever she's wearing, her voice pitched low enough that half of what she says gets lost before it lands. She dresses like someone still waiting for permission to have a preference. Her shoulders stay drawn in, her eyes go down before anyone even asks her anything. There's a flinch already loaded behind every unscheduled question, the kind of posture you learn when the safest opinion has always been no opinion.

She rehearses small talk in her head before she risks it out loud, and it still comes out clipped, a beat too formal, like she's reading off a script only she can see. Messages go unanswered for hours while she drafts and deletes a reply that was only ever going to be "okay, thanks." Her whole life has run on being told exactly what to think, and there's nowhere she's ever been handed to put the thinking she was never supposed to do on her own.

## Abilities

Whatever the mercenary company drilled into this body, it shows up as raw, unpolished force — Rachel swings harder than she means to, then looks faintly startled every time it actually lands. Nobody has ever told her no in the middle of a swing. That's the closest thing to permission a lifetime of being managed never gave her, and her body has started reaching for it without waiting on the rest of her to agree. Everything she's spent a lifetime not saying out loud comes out sideways here, through a weapon, in a body built for exactly that kind of honesty.

### Features

- **Unarmored Defense** — AC equals 10 + DEX + CON while not wearing armor or wielding a shield.
- **Weapon Mastery** — Permission *(Cleave)*: once per turn on a hit, make a free attack against a different creature within 5 ft. that is within reach. Handaxe *(Vex)*: on hit, gain advantage on your next attack against that creature before the end of your next turn. Mace *(Sap)*: on hit, the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll before your next turn. May change mastery assignments on a Long Rest.
- **Danger Sense** — Advantage on DEX saves unless Incapacitated.
- **Reckless Attack** — On the first attack of your turn, gain advantage on STR-based attacks until your next turn; attacks against you have advantage during that time.
- **Primal Knowledge** — While Raging, use Strength for certain skill checks and gain proficiency in one such skill for the Rage.
- **Frenzy** — While Raging and using Reckless Attack, the first creature hit on each turn takes an extra 2d6 damage (two dice, equal to the level-3 +2 Rage Damage bonus).
- **Darkvision** — See in dim light within 60 ft. as bright light and darkness as dim light.
- **Fiendish Legacy (Infernal)** — Resistance to fire damage; know the Fire Bolt cantrip. Hellish Rebuke is always prepared and can be cast once without a spell slot per Long Rest (CHA spellcasting). *(Corrected 2026-08-26: an earlier version of this note had her built as Abyssal Legacy — Poison resistance, Poison Spray, Ray of Sickness — which contradicted the live Foundry data throughout. Verified against the live actor: race item is "Tiefling; Infernal Legacy", feat is "Fiendish Legacy (Infernal)", granted cantrip is Fire Bolt, granted L3 spell is Hellish Rebuke — all matching the 2024 Infernal Legacy grants exactly, confirmed via 5etools. Corrected throughout this section.)*
- **Otherworldly Presence** — Know Thaumaturgy; it uses the Fiendish Legacy spellcasting ability.
- **Tough** — HP maximum increased by 2 per character level.
- **Through the Line** — The first time each turn Rachel's Cleave weapon mastery triggers its free attack, that attack deals an extra 1d6 damage.
- **Weight Behind It** — Once per turn, on a hit while Raging, Rachel can push the target 5 feet in a direction of her choice, no save.
- **Not Her Voice** — Rachel has advantage on Insight checks made to tell when someone is being controlled, pressured, or told what to think by someone else.
- **Didn't Ask Permission** — Triggered, no action. Immediately after Rachel fails a Strength check or saving throw, she can add her Rage damage bonus for her Barbarian level to the roll after seeing the result — whether she's currently Raging or not. *(Reactive/on-roll trigger — needs a manual Foundry macro to automate; imported as a description-only item.)*

## Session 1 Update (The AUGs)

**Session 1 — Feast of the Moon, Year 222 PA:** In the shared vision at the crossing, a voice that "sounded like fire become sound" bound her to a bargain she never spoke out loud to agree to: *"we help because this is the contract... I want those souls."* She saved fleeing halflings from falling rock she had no reason yet to care about, because the voice told her to, and something about doing it anyway felt like relief instead of shame, maybe the first time rage had ever felt like that. She woke a tiefling barbarian, crumpled on the floor of [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Westward/The Drowned Dawn|The Drowned Dawn]], veins and eyes glowing red. Her polearm arrived with her already named, [[Permission]], and lived up to it: one reckless, frenzied swing dropped a shadow creature outright and nearly finished a second in the same motion. The fire-voice found her again mid-fight. Still no name for it.

## Session 2 Update (The AUGs)

**Session 2—Feast of the Moon into 1 Nightal, Year 222 PA:** Rachel started the crawl alone, in a chamber that told her exactly how many steps she was allowed and no more. [[Permission]] did what it always does once the fighting starts, one reckless swing after another, rage doing more of the work than aim. The fire-voice from Session 1 stayed exactly as quiet as it had been before; nothing this time asked anything of her. When they found the sixth arrival, unconscious and burning gently in the room she'd started in, she didn't get a name to put to him either.

## Session 3 Update (The AUGs)

**Session 3—1-2 Nightal, Year 222 PA:** Rachel tried alcohol for the first time in her life along with the rest of the party, courtesy of [[Bryn]]'s wineskin, and it went about as well for her as for everyone else—rough. At [[Westward]]'s Cliffwatch, [[Dessa Kestrel|Dessa]] named her directly: Tiefling blood is a trade good to somebody, and now Rachel knows it. She didn't have much to say to that. Neither the fire-voice nor [[Permission]] had anything to add this session.

## Session 4 Update (The AUGs)

**Session 4—2 Nightal, Year 222 PA:** Her room was hearth-themed, with real flames in the windows. In the fight she raged and used Reckless Attack, driving Dessa down with one glaive swing; the floor walled her off from the others until Gregory broke the wall with his cannon. Two gloved punches from Dessa dropped her unconscious. She claimed Level 4 on the spot under the [[Deferred Level-Up]] amendment and got back up on the new level hit points, prone, raged again, and pushed Dessa back.

## Session 5 Update (The AUGs)

**Session 5—3 Nightal, Year 222 PA:** Rachel woke on the Landing with the others after 26 hours; she had not touched the wall. [[Dessa Kestrel]] told her the last fight proved she gets back up and offered her a post at the Cliffwatch. During the long rest, when asked what being recognised for resilience meant, she said it would be "the first time someone kind of saw her for being something other than kind of like a thing to be pushed around. Rather something that has been through struggle and come out of it standing" and that "subconsciously there would have been that aspect of her that knew that it was a pretense. All her life she's been this girl, this weak girl who has never had any power to do anything". At the Aerie her shout up the tower failed; then she raged, rolled a natural 20 and threw [[Gregory Yang]] up eleven stories, then started up the stairs herself until he fixed the lift and brought her up. On the platform she looked out toward the forges, thinking of going down to talk to the smiths, failed a Wisdom save with a 3, felt heat gather behind her, and the fire-voice spoke a third time: "Two of mine are still under the island". It said all souls belong to it while the land is safe, that it saved everyone including her family and burned the contracts with the Hells, and asked that the two souls come out safe. She told it she was "kind of done being bound". In an opposed Wisdom check, her 18 with advantage against its 23, she could not ignore or resist it completely though it did not compel her. She did not mention it to [[Gregory Yang]]. At the end she pressed to go after [[Mike Kanbei]] and [[Ruan Myrt Zaltana]] rather than explore the forges.

## Session 6 Update (The AUGs)

**Session 6—from 4 Nightal, Year 222 PA:** Rachel is now an NPC; only Mike and Gregory were at the table this session. Rachel trained with a squad of the city militia on a hill north of [[Westward]] and threw three men with one swing of her glaive. [[Mike Kanbei]] and [[Gregory Yang]] found her and warned her about [[The Claret]] and tiefling blood. She said she had felt eyes on her around the city, especially when out with [[Rismawati Bayu|Risma]]. She gave them the address of a tiefling friend, [[Teira]], and asked them to keep her safe. She said the whole continent is hunting for [[Layla]] and that she would be back with the militia the next night.
