## Session 8 Update (The AUGs)
- Asked [[Gregory Yang|Gregory]] whether he had told [[Dessa Kestrel|Dessa]] he said hello; Gregory had.
- His forge burns without fuel (red and blue glow, too hot); the shaft behind it is a metal ring a foot off the floor, five to ten feet across, one person at a time, with a column of heat; he keeps it so no child falls in and has never been down.
- Lifted a maul twice his height with one hand.
- Said Guild expeditions of about ten people take a week of food and are paid from Crystal; Dessa lost some trainees below and it is still raw for her; probably six or seven rooms have been excavated, with guardians he does not think are evil, and at least three rooms are avoided because the guardians kill; materials and schematics have come up, mostly unreadable, half at the [[The Deep Index|Deep Index]] and more important ones in Crystal; nobody knows what makes the heat; the first rooms are relatively safe and he thought mostly empty.
- Said he felt another expedition was almost due, especially once the continent lands.
- Said a little birdie told him the party had found a ruin, and said he hoped for more interest there and here.
- After the group's Persuasion check (median 16), showed a scroll map of the first seven chambers, passages, unexcavated wall sections and thin walls; he keeps it because Dessa told him to. Pulled Gregory back to work.
