## Description

A young clerk at the [[The Deep Index|Deep Index]] in [[Westward]], with very pale skin, pink eyes, white hair, glasses, and ink to his elbows. He is stooped. He carries the crates in from the line that runs out the door to the numbered drawers, and his body shakes a little as he does.

## Personality

Courteous and hurried, and swamped by a backlog of crates. He welcomes visitors from the Guild who want to do research and asks for two things in return: tell him what leaves the room, and do not shuffle his papers. His filing system is about eight piles of paper, each kept in a different part of the room, and he finds a thing by remembering where he put its pile. Ask him how it works and he says he wishes he could tell you. He writes down the name of whoever handles each item, so that any of them can be traced back.

## Role in the Story

The clerk of the Academy's westernmost branch. Reports for the [[Director of Antiquities]] go through him. He records who handles each item that reaches the Deep Index and sends a report to the Director every ten days.

## Session 6 Update (The AUGs)

- Hired [[Mike Kanbei|Mike]] to lift crates, read bad handwriting and help catalogue: a silver a day, doubled if Mike did more, with the run of the library. He was swamped and took off his glasses to stare at Mike's bracer.
- Said he sends a report to the [[Director of Antiquities]] every ten days and writes down the name of whoever touches each item. He named the Director as Yilde.
- Offered to write up Mike's account of what the party saw near the Ribs and of the friend who had not come out, and to bring the party to meet the academians when they arrive.
- Opened his drawers: dragon figurines and parchments of deity symbols copied from a door at the [[Sunless Citadel]]; a door fragment whose carving shifts for Mike and Gregory but not for him, with [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Kelemvor|Kelemvor]]'s symbol returning most often; and a drawer from the temple of [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Lathander|Lathander]] where the party arrived.
- Guessed that the muscled figure with the hammer in the Sunless Citadel's mural is [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Gond|Gond]]. Said that people who had lived long enough remembered the gods speaking to their clerics, and that since the Awakening none had.
- Recited ten to fifteen deity names while Mike and [[Gregory Yang|Gregory]] described the moving symbols, paid them a gold and said the Director must hear it.
