> # Pell

A lean woman in a harness who keeps the ledger at the [[Azure Tamers]]' Aerie in [[Westward]], with tribal markings on her face, pointed ears and a long bow on her back.

## Description

Lean, with the straight back of someone who has worked for years on a platform eleven stories up. A working harness crosses her chest. Its straps have gone pale at the buckles. Her long bow rides across her back, though the quill is nearly always in her hand before the bow is. Tribal markings run over her face, and her ears come to points. She carries a thick, ink-smudged ledger against one hip. On the boards she moves easily, one hand never far from a rail she doesn't bother to look at.

## Personality

Pell is kind and practical, and the ledger has made her a little overworked. Somebody has to keep the Aerie's books, so she does, and any excuse to put the quill down gets her full attention. She teaches a newcomer by putting them in the wind itself and talking them through it, one instruction at a time. Pell tells you what a thing costs before you ask. She vouches slowly, and she takes [[Dessa Kestrel]]'s word seriously. Pell's eye still drifts toward the paperwork in the middle of a conversation. The work never ends, and she knows it.
