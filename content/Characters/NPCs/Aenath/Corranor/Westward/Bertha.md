> # Bertha

A giant eagle the size of a house, the only creature on the [[Westward]] Aerie's second landing.

## Description

Bertha is the size of a house. Her feathers are the brown-gold of old rope, and a pale crest lifts when the wind turns. Each eye is wider than a shield and seldom blinks. She sits low on the second landing with two or three huge eggs tucked under her. Settling her wings turns the wind across the platform. No other creature shares this landing.

## Personality

Bertha teaches. New hatchlings learn to glide on the second landing, and she watches every attempt. A hatchling gets room to fail. She steps in only when more failing would teach nothing. She respects effort and has no patience for fuss. Her eggs keep her close to the landing and sharp with anything that crowds them.
