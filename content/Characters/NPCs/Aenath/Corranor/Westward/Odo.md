> # Odo

A Tamers ranger at the Aerie in [[Westward]] who keeps its lift winch working though he is no engineer.

## Description

He has broad shoulders and forearms streaked grey and orange from rust and grease, and a hammer hangs on his belt. The knees of his clothes are worn shiny from kneeling on the platform boards. His scowl has set into his face like weather into a cliff. The scowl looks the same whether he is angry or tired. Visitors meet his back first, because he is often bent over the lift mechanism.

## Personality

Odo's temper is aimed at situations. The Aerie gave him a broken lift and no engineer, and he says so before anyone can accuse him of botching the job. He distrusts wizards on principle and judges each one on results; a repair that holds turns his suspicion into loyalty. Magic itself does not interest him: it helps the lift or it doesn't. Whatever he has, he shares, and he pays fairly for work. He asks for help on a schedule and has no use for speeches of thanks.
