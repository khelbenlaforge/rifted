> # Oskar Brell

Green-tinged orc, late thirties or early forties, far-ground rigger on Stormberg's Wall crews, twenty years in, with a wife who helps at the mills and no children. Broad through the shoulders in the way twenty years of rope work builds, hands scarred and calloused past feeling much anymore. Weathered face, brown hair streaked white, older than his years, wind-burned red across the cheeks and nose from full days on exposed Wall stretches. A bandage on his forearm gone brown at the edges, from an injury he walked off and went back out on the next morning; everything else about him is old wear, patched gear, a coat gone thin at the elbows because new gear goes where the nobles can see it. Talks in short bursts and stops when he's said enough. Stoic and terse by long habit, not bitterness, a workhorse who stopped expecting better a long time ago and doesn't perform his suffering for anyone. What he wants isn't sympathy but comprehension: he'll stand up and speak at the forum, but he wants a noble to hear "a man hurt his arm" and understand why it matters, not just log it as an incident.

## Session 09 Update (The Understudies)

- 12 Nightal, Year 222 PA: [[Kili]] found him on a break at [[The Salt Line]], eating a meal she had brought from [[Yenna Marrow]]'s stall. She gave him a coupon for her speakeasy; he said he would come by after seeing his wife.
- He has been a far rigger for twenty years, with a wife who helps at the mills and no children. He hurt his arm slipping on the rope coming down the Wall. The far crews go out about four days, work in pairs, sleep in small settlements the supervisors have workers build, and come home for a week out of every month or so.
- He said he would love to work nearer the city, but there are rules. Kili's Insight (17) read a spark in that: something might be in the works for him, and he would never say so in front of the man beside him.
- Kili told him one stretch of the Wall is safe now and something is guarding it.
