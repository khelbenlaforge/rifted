Magister Vestra Coln, the Magocracy's representative in Stormberg; Rhona Voss expects her to be hands-off by policy and reserved at the 13 Nightal meeting.

## Personality

Hands-off by policy and reserved in her opinions. Magocracy administrators do not want to be seen overstepping the noble houses, so Vestra opposes nothing that does not cut across her responsibilities, chiefly the safety of the people.

## Background

The council's representative in Stormberg, standing in for the [[Magocracy of Corranor]] much as a king's representative would. The noble houses govern most cities as fiefs leased from the Magocracy, so her office is oversight rather than rule, as [[Rhona Voss]] described it. She can still make executive decisions in a crisis.

## Role in the Story

Sits across from the party at the 13 Nightal meeting beside [[Zarantha Verenthal]] of [[House Verenthal]]: the council's eyes on the Wall.
