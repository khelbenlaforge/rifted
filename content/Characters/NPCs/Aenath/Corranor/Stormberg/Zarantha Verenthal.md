Head of House Verenthal's Stormberg branch, who runs the mills and forges and funds the Wall's upkeep; Rhona Voss says she is warm in company with a lot of ice under it.

## Personality

Warm in company, with "a lot of ice" under the warmth, as [[Rhona Voss]] warns, and she knows how to work a room. She has held her post for fifteen years or more without being ousted or publicly badmouthed.

## Background

Heads [[House Verenthal]]'s Stormberg branch, a post she has held for at least fifteen years. Stormberg is the family's fief, granted by the [[Magocracy of Corranor]], and she works it like one: forges, mills and kilns, with wages paid to workers who are free to leave. Her house funds the upkeep of the [[Stormwall]]. Her nephew Quill ([[Sir Verenthal]]) runs Wall labor rostering and pay and takes much of the public blame. Like every noble house, the Verenthals keep a manor in Crystal City.

## Role in the Story

The nobles' voice across the table at the 13 Nightal meeting, and the one whose coin keeps the Wall standing.
