Head of House Verenthal's Stormberg branch, who runs the mills and forges and funds the Wall's upkeep; Rhona Voss says she is warm in company with a lot of ice under it.

## Personality

Warm in company, with "a lot of ice" under the warmth, as [[Rhona Voss]] warns, and she knows how to work a room. She has held her post for fifteen years or more without being ousted or publicly badmouthed.

## Background

Heads [[House Verenthal]]'s Stormberg branch, a post she has held for at least fifteen years. Stormberg is the family's fief, granted by the [[Magocracy of Corranor]], and she works it like one: forges, mills and kilns, with wages paid to workers who are free to leave. Her house funds the upkeep of the [[Stormwall]]. Her nephew Quill ([[Sir Verenthal]]) runs Wall labor rostering and pay and takes much of the public blame. Like every noble house, the Verenthals keep a manor in Crystal City.

## Role in the Story

The nobles' voice across the table at the meeting (held on 15 Nightal), and the one whose coin keeps the Wall standing.

## Session 12 Update (The Understudies)

- 14 Nightal, evening: she came into the party's [[Wallwatch]] tavern with [[Terendar]], [[Jarr]] and two armed guards. She lowered her hood: long silver hair loose around a human face, about forty-five, her right eyebrow studded along its whole length, and a plain, costly necklace. Nobody gave her name; everyone recognized her. [[Kili]] and [[Roberto]] took her to be Zarantha and chose to pretend they had not realized.
- She asked for [[Rhona Voss]], said she had heard good things about the place and was sorry it had taken her so long to come, and took the two for new recruits. She said she was grateful for the Guild's work, and that without it many people would be lost with no champion. Kili rolled a 22, with Roberto's Guidance. Roberto rolled a 25 on Insight and found her very hard to read.
- She noticed the Morning Lord's symbol on Roberto's chest and asked after [[Sister Priya Dawnwell|Sister Priya]].
- She greeted Rhona as "my old friend." She had only known of the Rifted in Crystal, and called Kili and Roberto the rumored second round; it was said to be just as well that they had landed in Stormberg instead.
- Kili rolled a natural 20 on Insight on the small talk and read it as Zarantha gauging Rhona's state of mind. When Roberto brought up [[Sir Verenthal|Quill]], her face did not change at all, as if she already knew. She said she left the administration to her nephew, an amazing administrator.
- She told Rhona that Quill had come to her that very day and somehow knew about tomorrow's meeting, which she had thought was kept inside the council. She said all these little misunderstandings could be worked out.
- She said anything to do with the [[Stormwall]] was her jurisdiction. She had heard talk of sending an extermination team, though she now understood the matter had been settled and the stretch was a cordoned safe zone. Her own men were already on the way to inspect it. Roberto used [[Neat]]'s One Real Pull on her: she was acting surprised, and in her own mind she knew everything, though whether she truly did was another matter.
- She agreed that the witnesses should speak first and that [[Vestra Coln]] should attend. Her soldiers could be back no earlier than 48 hours after her visit to the tavern.
- For about forty-five minutes she sat with Kili and Roberto. She said the [[House Verenthal|House of Verenthal]] was grateful to Roberto and in his debt. Every worker on the Wall mattered to her, from the patchers to the squad leaders, gang leaders and section leaders; she knew the hierarchy thoroughly, though not every worker in it. Handling so many people was a risk, and no one could please them all. She suggested that Rhona and Quill sit down together, praised Roberto's mead and said [[The Gilded Loom]] might do well to stock some. The two checks Kili and Roberto rolled came to 11 and 23. She was confident on partial knowledge, and she did not hear everything from Quill.
- On herself: her uncle had been the esteemed overseer of the city. She visited him often to learn and came to love Stormberg. When he stepped down and went back to the main house in Crystal, she was handed enormous shoes to fill. She thanked Rhona, Vestra and the work gangs, naming every group in the city and missing none.
- On plans: now that the Wall had stood the test of time, the city was looking outward, toward the Arctic and the elemental stones the city earns so much from. They were thinking of maybe building lighthouses on separate islands and clearing some of the islands. Some of the Guild's own people were out there still, unable to get back until Stormberg's island landed. She spoke of a rest stop beyond the Arctic gates for ships. Roberto's roll was a 21.
- 15 Nightal, noon: she came into the long room in the [[Hall of Gray Stone]] smiling, in the gray and silver of her house, which are also the city's colors, with Terendar and Jarr in livery. She held a hand out to each of the party: "Good morning. I hope the kitchen forgives me for last night." She is one of four voters (Zarantha, Vestra, Rhona, [[Jeezindra]]); a decision needs three of the four. The session ended before the party had presented its facts.
