## Session 09 Update (The Understudies)

- 11 Nightal, Year 222 PA: Keith found him at the Iron Seam, broad and soot to the elbows, embarrassed to be collecting a debt from a friend. The favor came to a copy of the Wall readings Keith had taken on an earlier survey, after which they would be square. He asked Keith to send it soon.
- He told Keith the archives were his to go through and rated the plans Keith asked about by difficulty: an Alchemy Jug easy, a Bag of Holding and a +1 shield harder, Sending Stones hardest. Keith rolled a 25 and left with the plans for the Alchemy Jug and the Bag of Holding.
- He said the Iron Seam is paid by the Magocracy together with the Academy, by output. The noble houses drop in to act noble and are sent "upstairs," the guildmaster is usually away on research, and he is grateful to the nobles for investing in the city and giving people work.
