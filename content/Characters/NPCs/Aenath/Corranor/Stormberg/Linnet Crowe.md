## Description

Runs the booking desk at the foot of the [[Stormberg Aerie]]. She is thin and sharp-faced, with a squint from watching the sky and chalk on her sleeves. A slate board hangs behind her, covered in times, weights, wind and names, and she rubs out a line as soon as it stops being true.

## Personality

Linnet is brisk and exact, and she dislikes surprises. She quotes the price of a flight first and the schedule second. She reads the sky and the Wall every morning and writes what she sees on the board, so the handlers get the day's weather from her chalk. She is slow to commit a bird to a flight and quick to cancel one.
