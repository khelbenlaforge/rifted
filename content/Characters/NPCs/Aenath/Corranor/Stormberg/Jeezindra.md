## Session 12 Update (The Understudies)

- 15 Nightal, noon: arrived at the long room in the Hall of Gray Stone holding a mug, after [[Zarantha Verenthal]]. He made an entrance that [[Vestra Coln]] seemed to find vulgar, and he waved off her complaint: life was a little too short, my love. He sat in the last chair and said his name was Jeezindra, the [[Merchant Guild]]'s representative.
- Vestra winced whenever he opened his mouth. When [[Rhona Voss|Rhona]] introduced each of the party with their record, he said "In the flesh." He told them the Merchant Guild would love to pick their brains about the worlds they came from.
- He is one of four voters at the meeting (Zarantha, Vestra, Rhona and Jeezindra), and a decision needs three of the four, so either he could be won or Zarantha embarrassed into voting with the party.
