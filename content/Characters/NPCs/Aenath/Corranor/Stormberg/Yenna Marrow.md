## Background

- Before the noodle stall, ran with **[[Rhona Voss]]** on cull teams pulling undesirables off the Wall—the same work Rhona did before she traded it for the Wallwatch desk. Retired from that entirely once the stall could support her; now cooks cheap, filling meals for wall-gang laborers and Wallwatch members alike, the same crowd she used to work alongside from the other side of the ledger.

## Relationships

- **Partner** (she/her, unnamed) — more of a crafter than a cook; the two of them are getting on in years together.
- **[[Rhona Voss]]** — old cull-team colleague, now on opposite sides of a desk-and-stall divide; the two never really stopped looking out for the same people.
- **[[Kili]]** — worked her morning line in Session 5; a longer conversation in Session 7's downtime went deep enough that Yenna opened up about her own past.

## Session 7 Update (The Understudies)

- 1–10 Nightal (downtime): [[Kili]] stayed long past the usual shift talk. Yenna opened up about the partner she comes home to, still working with their hands despite what the years have started asking of both of them, and about a version of herself from before the stall: cull-team work up on the Wall, done alongside a much younger [[Rhona Voss]], left behind for good the day the noodles started paying their own way.
