## Background

- Before the noodle stall, ran with **[[Rhona Voss]]** on cull teams pulling undesirables off the Wall—the same work Rhona did before she traded it for the Wallwatch desk. Retired from that entirely once the stall could support her; now cooks cheap, filling meals for wall-gang laborers and Wallwatch members alike, the same crowd she used to work alongside from the other side of the ledger.

## Relationships

- **Late husband** (he/him, unnamed)—more of a crafter than a cook, now deceased; Yenna has children. [[Terendar]] called the two of them legends in the Guild who had saved many workers on the Wall (Session 12).
- **[[Rhona Voss]]** — old cull-team colleague, now on opposite sides of a desk-and-stall divide; the two never really stopped looking out for the same people.

## Session 5 Update (The Understudies)

Kili worked a shift at her stall and stirred a small tax-fairness conversation among line cooks and laborers.

## Session 7 Update (The Understudies)

- 1–10 Nightal (downtime): [[Kili]] stayed long past the usual shift talk. Yenna opened up about her late husband, more of a crafter than a cook, and about a version of herself from before the stall: cull-team work up on the Wall, done alongside a much younger [[Rhona Voss]], left behind for good the day the noodles started paying their own way.
- **[[Kili]]**—worked her morning line in Session 5; a longer conversation in Session 7's downtime went deep enough that Yenna opened up about her own past.

## Session 09 Update (The Understudies)

- 11 Nightal, evening: [[Kili]] asked how Stormberg feeds itself. Yenna said hunting had stopped for winter, the fields were fallow, and the city was living on its stores. She buys lesser cuts cheaply from trappers for her laborers and will ask around among them. When [[Kili]] said she was buying for the speakeasy, Yenna said the price had to be good enough because sellers had a living to make.

## Session 11 Update (The Understudies)

- Session 11, 14 Nightal: [[Kili]] went to her stall and ordered food in quantities that grew until Yenna was confused by it. Kili let it stand without a further reason, and Yenna said she would do her best.

## Session 12 Update (The Understudies)

- Session 12, 14 Nightal, evening: [[Terendar]] said she was retired now, and that she and her late husband were legends in the Guild who had saved many workers on the Wall. He said he would like to taste her broth if time allowed.
- 15 Nightal, morning: arrived in the city square with a small army of cooks and set up a booth of her own. She called [[Rhona Voss]] a freeloader and patted her metal shoulder as Rhona stress-ate. [[Kili]] teamed up with her noodles, which sat side by side with [[Roberto]]'s mead in big kegs.
