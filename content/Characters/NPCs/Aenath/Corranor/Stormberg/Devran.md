## Description
A stocky, weathered dwarf who's tended bar in Stormberg long enough that the brisk coastal wind barely registers anymore. Where his brother's Garnet District tavern in Crystal City draws a settled regular crowd, Devran's place turns over faster — dockhands off shift, or the odd [[Adamantine Guild]] culler nursing a drink after a bad day underground. He stays out of Guild business entirely; people talk more over a full mug than they realize, and he hears plenty of it.

## Background
[[Devran]] resides in [[Stormberg]], a settlement on the northern coast. His brother, the [[Dwarven Bartender]] of [[Crystal City]]'s [[Garnet District]], often shares secondhand knowledge of the [[Corranor]] main island's wilderness.
