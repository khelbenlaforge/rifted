## Description

A young handler on the mid landings of the [[Stormberg Aerie]]. Wren is wiry and quick, in a harness patched at both shoulders, with feathers in their hair they have stopped noticing. They go up and down the ladders without using their hands.

## Personality

Wren is fearless and talkative. They like the birds more than the work and the work more than the pay, and they say so. They collect gossip from the far crews who drink at [[The Salt Line]], and they repeat it before they have checked it. They will tell a stranger nearly anything, and they have not yet learned which things they should not.
