- Iron Seam Guild's senior artificer, pushing six feet, forearms scarred pale from decades of work on the Wall's siege engines. Talks like every conversation is a maintenance report, even the ones that aren't.

## Personality

Clipped and technical by default, but the jargon comes out unprompted the moment something genuinely interests her—tension ratios, load limits, numbers that lose most of the room. Cordial but guarded with anyone new: the Guild's had outside "interest" before that turned out to be someone angling for its blueprints, so what a newcomer gets first is the polite, surface-level version of her, nothing more, until they've earned otherwise.

## Session 7 Update (The Understudies)

- 1–10 Nightal (downtime): Keith showed up again with colleagues in tow this time, not blueprints. A round of new Iron Seam faces at [[Kili]]'s speakeasy, and a standing discount for anyone wearing the Guild's mark by the end of the night. Varnick watched him work the room and came away with a read she hadn't expected: someone who won't flinch at height or heat.
- 1–10 Nightal (downtime): asked him up onto the Wall to help run the siege engines once the continent makes landfall and the storms settle. He took it as a date. It took her a beat to realize why he'd gone quiet, and by then the whole workshop had heard—*"Awwwwww, you're such a sweetie... but no."*
