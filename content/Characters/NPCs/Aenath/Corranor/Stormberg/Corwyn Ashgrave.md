## Description

- Tavern master of **[[The Gilded Loom]]**, Stormberg's upscale mezzanine tavern. Silver-haired and unhurried, the kind of calm that reads as authority rather than age. Remembers every regular's drink order and every regular's debt, in that order.

## Personality

Soft-spoken and precise, never raises his voice even mid-sentence to cut someone off. Treats the Loom's private mezzanine as neutral ground and guards that reputation carefully—it's good for business when nobles and Guild officers both trust the room enough to talk in front of each other.
