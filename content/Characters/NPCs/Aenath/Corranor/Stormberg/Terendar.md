> # Terendar

Tall elf who serves as seneschal to [[Zarantha Verenthal]]. He looks about thirty, though he is at least two hundred years old, and his long right ear is pierced from tip to base with twisted wool and gold ornaments. He speaks politely and without warmth.

## Session 12 Update (The Understudies)

- 14 Nightal, evening: the tall elf of the five hooded guests who came into the party's [[Wallwatch]] tavern. He bowed to [[Kili]] and asked for the best the house had, and said he appreciated the care when the plates arrived. He said the place was amazing and the fare was not what they were used to, but it was well made.
- He gave his name as Terendar. He is Lady [[Zarantha Verenthal|Zarantha]]'s seneschal, her right hand, answering only to her. Anyone who wanted an appointment went through him, and he ran a team of manservants under him. He said he had learned from the best, the head seneschal to a high lord in Crystal.
- The twisted shapes in his ear were wool as well as gold, grown into ornaments, reminders of his family: wood elves, some of them druids.
- His telling (a claim, not confirmed): the family was among the oldest, though without the accolades of the Blackstaffs. Corranor is named for Blackstaff, who saved them all in the Awakening. The Verenthals were the first to see that the storms were a danger no society could live with, so they sent their best sorcerers, not wizards. In his telling the Verenthals spearheaded the building of the [[Stormwall]], with the Magocracy's backing.
- He said the soldiers and investigators had left about two hours ago, and that their lady wanted to know what had happened. It was only an inspection, since the House's soldiers were good in a fight but were not sorcerers. Workers had been hurt, and then word came that the stretch was safe.
- Asked about [[Yenna Marrow]], he said she was retired now, and that she and her late husband were legends in the Guild who had saved many workers on the Wall. He turned to [[Rhona Voss]] and said she had been in the same party as Yenna, and Rhona agreed. His eyes sparkled when he talked about Yenna, and he said that if time allowed he would like to taste her broth.
- 15 Nightal, noon: he and [[Jarr]] followed Zarantha into the [[Hall of Gray Stone]] in livery.
