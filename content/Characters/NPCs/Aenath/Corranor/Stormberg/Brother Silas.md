## Description

- Exhausted monk on skeleton-crew duty at Stormberg's Temple of Kelemvor while most clergy rest after the Feast. Like the rest of Kelemvor's clergy, he teaches that the Wailing Wall is gone and that every soul goes somewhere, worshipper or not.
