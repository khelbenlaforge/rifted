## Description

Keeper of the [[Stormberg Aerie]] and its senior ranger. He is broad through the shoulders and grey at the temples, with forearms scarred pale and ridged from years of talon and beak. A heavy glove hangs from his belt and he is rarely without it. He climbs the tower's ladders slowly, because he knows each one.

## Personality

Jorund is patient and quiet, and he speaks to the birds before he speaks to anyone else. He answers a question only after he has finished the thing his hands were doing, so a conversation with him starts late. He counts the Aerie's feed out loud at the end of every day and does not hide the number from anyone who asks. He trusts what an animal does over what a person says it will do.
