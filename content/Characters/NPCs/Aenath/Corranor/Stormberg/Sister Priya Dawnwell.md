## Description

- Young, unguarded cleric tending Stormberg's chapel of Lathander. Half-elf, early twenties by the eye, sun-worn olive skin like she stands outside for every dawn service. Dark hair cropped short and uneven, cut herself and not well. Rising-sun robes a size too big, sleeves rolled back to the elbow so they don't catch on temple work. Open, expressive face, she hasn't learned to hide anything yet, which is half of why she's unguarded. Told [[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/Roberto|Roberto]] that his vision also called him to [[Helm]] and [[Sune]]. Explained her god's theology: focused on beginnings, indifferent to what happens to a soul once it's gone. She also asked Rhona, in [[The Exodus/The Understudies/PCs/Roberto|Roberto]]'s hearing, whether there was word of "the ancient floating temple of Lathander recently discovered." She never identified it as any specific place.
