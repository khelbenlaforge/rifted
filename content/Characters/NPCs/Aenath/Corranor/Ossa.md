> # Ossa

Ossa runs [[The Straggler's Rest]], a waystation tavern on the north road out of [[Crystal City]].

Broad-shouldered and sun-cracked, she was already reaching for cups before anyone had ordered, and has clearly run the Rest for years. Talkative, motherly, and about as forthcoming as a woman who genuinely doesn't know more than she's telling can be.

## Session 30 (The Door Kickers)

Hosted a group of travelers overnight on the road north. Over food and a room she gave up what she had: a trapper who'd quit his season early three days back, worn down by a run of bad luck rather than anything he seemed to fear; a friend near Crystal City who'd lost livestock to something she couldn't name either; word of [[Coldford]]'s permanent mist further east; and, pressed on stranger things, only a shrug about "the Lord of the Hells." Someone tried the old tall tale about people arriving from other worlds on her. She turned it around on them instead, citing the [[Stormwall]]'s own wizards as proof it couldn't be done—if anyone could, it'd be them, and even they hadn't—and a Deception check kept the lie standing.
