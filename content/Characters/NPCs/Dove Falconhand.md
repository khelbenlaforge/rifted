## Appearance

Six feet tall, broad-shouldered, with long silver hair — the same silver as [[Storm Silverhand]]'s, the same silver as all the Chosen. She dresses like a working ranger: brown or green leather breeches, a jerkin, boots that have seen serious use. Nothing that announces what she is. Quiet, capable, unflappable. The most dangerous person in most rooms she walks into, and the last one to mention it.

## Personality

Economical with words. Observant in a way that feels almost impolite — she reads people faster than they know they're being read. Can maintain perfect calm in the middle of a blood-soaked fight. Occasionally wry. She has been doing this for a long time and has learned that underestimating the situation costs more than underestimating yourself.

She enjoys patient observation of wildlife. She is an accomplished singer and harpist, which surprises people who have only seen her fight.

## Background

Born Ambara Dove Silverhand — one of the Seven Sisters, all daughters of [[Mystra]] by mortal men. [[Storm Silverhand]]'s sister. A Chosen of Mystra, she carries a fragment of the Weave within her; unlike Storm, whose power expresses as Silverfire, Dove's Chosen nature shows primarily as near-immortality, regeneration, and an instinctive grasp of the Weave's state in any given location.

She is a founding member of the [[Knights of Myth Drannor]] and married to [[Florin Falconhand]]. A Harper. She follows Harper doctrine absolutely.

At the time of the Avatar Crisis: Mystra's death has already happened. Dove felt it the moment it occurred. She is carrying that in the way Chosen carry things — inward, contained, functioning. It does not impair her. It may explain why she is watching [[Midnight]] so carefully.

## Role in the Battle

Arrives at Game Round 12 with [[Florin Falconhand]] and the Knights. Immediately reads the field — she will know within one round where the pressure points are and move to them without being told.

She'll catch [[Torm of the Dales]]'s eye after Florin passes. A look that says: *We will talk about this.* Then she's gone.

She fights alongside the party if they're in her sector. She will not require explanations mid-battle.
