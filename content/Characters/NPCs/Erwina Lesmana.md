> # Erwina Lesmana

Erwina Lesmana was a tiefling bard (College of Swords, L3) from Balikpapan Bay, Indonesia — an environmental activist who arrived in Aenath as a guest during Session 21 and faded by session's end, having no soul-anchor to hold her here. Her keris, [[Nyala]], remained behind when she dissolved — a relic of old infernal craft that predates her lineage.

She introduced herself as "Erwin." She healed people she was supposed to be fighting, because it was the only mode she had.
