## Overview

The Ranger Beast Master (XPHB) Primal Companion feature lets the Ranger choose one of these three stat blocks whenever the beast is summoned; the choice can be changed each Long Rest. Its Armor Class, Hit Points, attack bonus, damage, and relevant save DC scale from the summoning Ranger's own level, Wisdom modifier, spell attack modifier, and spell save DC rather than using fixed values. The shared-initiative house rule in [[Beast Master - Primal Companion Initiative]] applies to all three forms.

## D&D 5e Stat Block

### Beast of the Land

Medium beast; AC is 13 + your Wisdom modifier, and HP is 5 + five times your Ranger level (Hit Dice are d8s equal to your Ranger level). It has 40-foot walk and climb speeds, 60-foot Darkvision, understands the languages you know, and has STR 14, DEX 14, CON 15, INT 8, WIS 14, CHA 11.



Example at Ranger Level 5 (PB +3)

AC is 13 + 3 = 16. HP is 5 + (5 × 5) = 30 (5d8). Nanuk's Spellcasting line states Attack Bonus +6 and Save DC 14 (PB +3, WIS mod +3).



### Beast of the Sea

Medium beast; AC is 13 + your Wisdom modifier, and HP is 5 + five times your Ranger level (Hit Dice are d8s equal to your Ranger level). It has a 5-foot walk speed, 60-foot swim speed, 90-foot Darkvision, and STR 14, DEX 14, CON 15, INT 8, WIS 14, CHA 11.



Example at Ranger Level 5 (PB +3)

AC is 13 + 3 = 16. HP is 5 + (5 × 5) = 30 (5d8). Nanuk's Spellcasting line states Attack Bonus +6 and Save DC 14 (PB +3, WIS mod +3).



### Beast of the Sky

Small beast; AC is 13 + your Wisdom modifier, and HP is 4 + four times your Ranger level (Hit Dice are d6s equal to your Ranger level). It has a 10-foot walk speed, 60-foot fly speed, 60-foot Darkvision, and STR 6, DEX 16, CON 13, INT 8, WIS 14, CHA 11.



Example at Ranger Level 5 (PB +3)

AC is 13 + 3 = 16. HP is 4 + (4 × 5) = 24 (5d6). Nanuk's Spellcasting line states Attack Bonus +6 and Save DC 14 (PB +3, WIS mod +3).



## Notes

Damage type for Beast of the Land and Beast of the Sea is chosen by the player at the moment of summoning; it is not fixed. [[Nanuk]] currently uses Beast of the Land, reflavored as a polar bear companion (*irinaliutiit*).

**Foundry auto-scaling (added 2026-07-30, fully verified 2026-07-31):** on Nanuk's Foundry sheet, all three forms are wired as separate Summon Activities on his Primal Companion item, so AC, HP, attack bonus, damage, and Primal Bond's proficiency-on-all-checks/saves compute live from Nanuk's own current stats (Wisdom modifier, proficiency bonus, Ranger level) instead of needing manual recomputation at future level-ups. **Confirmed working at the table for all three forms** (Land, Sea, and Sky) — every number (AC, HP, attack bonus, damage, Primal Bond) checked live and correct. The worked Level-5 examples above remain accurate as a snapshot but are no longer the source of truth — the live Foundry sheet is.
