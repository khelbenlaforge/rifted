## Description

The full expression of what the Shadow Creatures send when they want something dead. A Shadow moves with deliberate, cold patience — it does not chase, it converges. Its Draining Swipe doesn't just wound; it unmakes, bleeding off the victim's physical strength until nothing is left to fight back with. Humanoids killed this way don't stay dead.
