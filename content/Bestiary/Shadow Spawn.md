## Description

Weak manifestations of the full Shadow — patches of animate darkness that coalesce wherever the Shadow Creatures breach the boundary between their realm and the waking world. They lack the predatory STR-draining hunger of their more evolved kin, but move fast, cling to dim corners, and overwhelm through numbers and shock. Where Shadows are surgeons, Shadow Spawn are the flood.
