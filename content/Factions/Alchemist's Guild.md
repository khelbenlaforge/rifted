## Description

Crystal City's institution for alchemical research, trade, and licensing, headquartered in the Alchemy Quarter of the [[Amethyst District]]. Its Extra Planar Studies Branch keeps sealed transit logs and ledgers on planar mechanics; the guild's broader ledgers also track the city's magical-creature-blood supply, currently strained by an unexplained buyout.

Organizationally, the Guild is an autonomous department of [[Ether Scryer's Academy]] rather than a fully independent faction — it runs its own contacts, ledgers, and day-to-day business without Academy oversight, but the Academy is its parent institution. The autonomy isn't a formality: the Guild and the Academy proper are in genuine, ongoing rivalry, rooted in a real philosophical divide — the Academy's wizardry leans on rigorous, near-mathematical arcane theory, while the Guild's alchemy is empirical, experimental, and grounded in natural materials. Neither side considers the other's methods the "real" path to power over magic, and that tension is why an Academy affiliation buys no goodwill with a Guild contact.
