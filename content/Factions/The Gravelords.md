## Description

The Gravelords are the clerical body of the [[Temple of Kelemvor]] — the people who actually run the building. They process the dead, maintain the records, administer the souls, and manage the institutional relationship between the living who arrive at the door and the divine mandate that governs what happens to them. They are not morbid. They are fastidious.

The name comes from an old translation of their charge: those who keep the ground. Every death processed correctly is a grave kept in order. Every soul properly assessed is one less disruption in a system that must hold. The Gravelords are the ledger. They are also the lock.

They keep a library. It is not public. Accounts of clerics who plane-shifted to police soul trafficking — mostly into the Nine Hells, where the most egregious violations accumulate — fill the older shelves. The reading is dry. The work those accounts describe was not.

## Leadership & Members

No named high priest has made themselves known to the party. The Gravelords present a collective face: individual clerics are identifiable by their vestments and subdued manner, but names are offered slowly, if at all. The institutional preference is for the work to speak.

- **[[Augustus Kennedy]]** — Adherent. Not ordained; not a cleric. A recognized affiliate granted library access and formal standing because the divine signature on his [[Kelemvor's Brand|Brand]] was too clear to argue with. The Gravelords do not argue with clear signatures.

## Operations

The Gravelords administer the temple's day-to-day operation: incoming soul assessments, the library, the Neutral Cloister, and all records pertaining to planar transit. They process requests from the living — inquiries about the deceased, theological questions, requests for the Processing Chambers — with the efficiency of a very well-run post office that also happens to adjudicate the afterlife.

Their plane-shifting operations are historical rather than current. The library accounts describe past enforcement actions against soul traffickers — clerics who went into the Nine Hells authorized and came back changed. Whether that operational capacity still exists in [[Crystal City]] is not something they confirm publicly.

## Current Status

Stable and well-resourced within Crystal City. They hold institutional respect across the [[Diamond District]] and maintain the feud with the Churches of [[Mystra]] and [[Oghma]] as a point of principle rather than active hostility — wild magic destabilizing the Weave disrupts planar transit, and disrupted planar transit creates administrative nightmares. The Gravelords have long memories and a comprehensive filing system.

[[Augustus Kennedy]] now holds adherent status. It is not a common distinction for someone who is not a cleric. The Brand made the argument for him.

## Membership

**How to Join:** Formal ordination for clerics; divine recognition for lay adherents. Lay affiliation via a recognized sponsor (such as [[Sir Gareth Ironhand]]) is rare but accepted when the evidence is unambiguous.

- Temple recognition — the Gravelords will speak to you, and that counts for something

- Contacts within the Nine Hells documentation network
