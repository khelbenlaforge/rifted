> # House Blackstaff

Three different accounts describe how old this family is, and none of them quite agree on the wording. [[Oriel Blackstaff]] calls it the oldest family on the continent. [[Soral Vayne]] calls it Crystal City's first family—a family of wizards, full stop. [[Lady Blackstaff]] governs specifically the Crystal City branch, which implies the rest of the name goes further than one city.

The family's head-of-house seat on the [[Corranor Council]] is one of the oldest fixtures of that body—held currently by [[Oriel Blackstaff]], Keeper of the Writ, the Council's own legal authority. Council law requires every seated Keeper to renounce their outside affiliations on investiture; Oriel genuinely tries to honor that, which is why the family's actual head was conspicuously absent from the mansion meeting in Session 23. The city still calls it the Blackstaff seat regardless—a fiction it maintains out of sheer force of habit.

House Blackstaff also owns one of only two airships anywhere in the Magocracy, the other belonging to the Magocracy itself.

## Blackstaff Mansion

The family's Crystal City estate reads more like an arcane stronghold than a noble residence: towers built to evoke old wizard keeps, no visible front door anywhere on the property. A visitor stands before blank wall until someone trusted inside speaks for them, at which point a line of light traces itself down the stone and a concealed doorway opens where there was none. Even past the threshold, nothing about the place feels unguarded—its throne room alone is watched over by scores of magically armored guards, the kind of force that says this house expects enemies worth warding against, not merely locking a door on.

## Session 19 and 23

In Session 19, [[Soral Vayne]] told [[Min Ji-ah]] at [[Hucrele Manor]] that the Blackstaffs are Crystal City's first family, that their scion sits on the Council of Seven, and that they are, above all, a family of wizards. When [[Regius]] and Min Ji-ah later asked Soral, in Session 23, whether any noble house needed a discreet fixer, he ruled the Blackstaffs out immediately—too scrupulously above board for that kind of hire.

In Session 23, [[Garm]] arranged a meeting between the party and the family—not with Oriel, who as a seated Keeper doesn't receive strangers on Council business terms, but with **[[Lady Blackstaff]]**, the stern-faced matriarch standing in for the family that day, and an unnamed **[[Blackstaff Brother]]** whose magical sensitivity read instantly the moment [[Regius]] placed [[Le Fil]] in his hands. Min Ji-ah described her landing vision of [[Corran Blackstaff]]'s sacrifice and death at Corranor's founding; Lady Blackstaff, controlled and severe until that moment, let real emotion show—she had never known what Corran looked like, and Min Ji-ah's account matched what the family's own history preserves.

Once Lady Blackstaff examined [[Elminster's Spellbook]], her caution turned to urgency. She recognized script written in a hand only very advanced wizards would know, judged the book older than anything the family's own knowledge could account for, and told the party the family can no longer put off an expedition of its own to the [[Sunless Citadel]]. The plan she gave them directly: after the [[Feast of the Moon]], two days past the party's own meeting with the Claret, House Blackstaff travels there together with the [[Director of Antiquities]].
