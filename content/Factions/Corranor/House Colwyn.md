The family that holds Coldford's seat by old charter. Its crest sits over the Landgate, and locals defer to the name more out of habit than evidence. Its seat stands in the Old Streets.

## Session 4 Update (The JULYs)
- [[Torun]] called Lord Colwyn a nice man who is almost ineffectual and commands fewer soldiers than some of the Houses.
- [[Tamsin Vell]] said Lord Colwyn pays her but has little power, and that every Manifest House holds more. She added that he is lucky to hold a mandate from the council to look after the city, and is not completely useless, since he still helps with civic works and repairs the streets.
- [[Petra Hale]] said she would send word to Lord Colwyn, to pass down to the [[The Manifest Houses|Manifest Houses]].
- [[Hester Crane]]'s bakery work put [[Odette Langford]] within reach of the steward of House Colwyn.

## Session 6 Update (The JULYs)
- Asked by Petra whether she had met anyone from House Colwyn, [[Odette Langford]] said she did not think so.
- On 8 Nightal Odette asked [[Hester Crane]] if she could carry bread to House Colwyn. Hester said no, kindly.
- At the debrief, whatever Lord Colwyn is doing was counted among the open questions.
