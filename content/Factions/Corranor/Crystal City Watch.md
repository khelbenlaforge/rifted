> # Crystal City Watch

Crystal City's law-enforcement body, answering to **[[Doran]]**, Keeper of the Bulwark on the [[Corranor Council]]—the office responsible for what does and doesn't get into the city. Doran commands "the city Watch and its wardens" directly: the night the [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Diamond District/Shrine of Shar|Cistern Street shrine]] was raided, he deployed sixty of the Watch and eleven of his own personal guardians through the [[Diamond District]] and had it closed before dawn.

## District Detachments

[[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Characters/NPCs/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Diamond District/Sir Gareth Ironhand|Sir Gareth Ironhand]], training paladins out of the [[Hall of Justice - Temple of Tyr|Hall of Justice]], named four separate bodies a recruit could take work with: the **City Guard**, [[Jade Spire]] soldiers, **[[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Citrine District/Citrine District|Citrine District]] Watchmen**, and **[[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Crystal City/Sapphire District/Sapphire District|Sapphire District]] guards**. Named as four distinct options rather than one, they at least suggest district-level detachments alongside a city-wide Guard—though exactly how each relates to Doran's Bulwark office isn't stated anywhere sourced (see DM Notes).
