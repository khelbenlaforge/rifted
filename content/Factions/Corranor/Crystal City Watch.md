> # Crystal City Watch

Crystal City's law-enforcement body, answering to **[[Doran]]**, Keeper of the Bulwark on the [[Corranor Council]]—the office responsible for what does and doesn't get into the city. Doran commands "the city Watch and its wardens" directly: the night the Cistern Street shrine was raided, he deployed sixty of the Watch and eleven of his own personal guardians through the [[Diamond District]] and had it closed before dawn.

## District Detachments

Sir Gareth Ironhand, training paladins out of the [[Hall of Justice - Temple of Tyr|Hall of Justice]], named four separate bodies a recruit could take work with: the **City Guard**, [[Jade Spire]] soldiers, **Citrine District Watchmen**, and **Sapphire District guards**. Named as four distinct options rather than one, they at least suggest district-level detachments alongside a city-wide Guard—though exactly how each relates to Doran's Bulwark office isn't stated anywhere sourced (see DM Notes). [[Augustus Kennedy]] enlisted with Tyr's law-enforcement arm out of the Hall of Justice as a junior officer, taking on patrol and judicial duties.
