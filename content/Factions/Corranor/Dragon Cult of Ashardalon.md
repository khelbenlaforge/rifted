> # Dragon Cult of Ashardalon

A dragon cult built and inhabited the [[Sunless Citadel]] long before it sank into the earth, devoted to the great wyrm [[Ashardalon]]'s glory and, in time, to the promise of his return. They called him the Devourer, in reverence and in fear, and inscribed *He Who Devours Returns* into the citadel's stone as both doctrine and prophecy.

## High Priest and Doctrine

[[Gulthias]] served as the cult's high priest, the living bridge between the dragon and his followers—organizing the worship, shaping it, making it operational. He established the cult's central temple at Nightfang Spire, coordinating Ashardalon's followers and conducting arcane research in the dragon's service.

When Ashardalon fell to a mortal wound, the cult fractured. Gulthias did not: he preserved what remained of the dragon's power and devoted the rest of his life to preparing for his lord's return—preparation that, in the end, transformed him into something the cult never planned for.

## The Sanctum

The cult's most significant construction, hidden behind the Sealed Door on the [[Sunless Citadel]]'s Grove Level, predates the cult itself. Its statuary of [[Mystra]], [[Gond]], [[Bahamut]], [[Takhisis]], and the four primordial elemental gods arranged around a model of [[Aenath]] suggests Ashardalon's followers built atop something far older than their own faith. An illusory statue of the dragon guards the entrance, dissolving only for those who pass the door's divine trial.

## Legacy

Nothing sourced describes any surviving organized membership—what remains is the [[Gulthias Tree]] on the Grove Level, transformed from Gulthias's own staked remains, still spreading the dragon's influence through blights and thralled fruit two centuries after the faith that planted it turned to dust.
