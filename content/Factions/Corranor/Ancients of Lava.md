> # Ancients of Lava

The Ancients of Lava (known historically as the Lavaliths) are a wilderness-based druid circle with a focus on elementalism. They refuse to operate in cities and travel solo. They maintain loose ties with nature deity temples in [[Crystal City]]. Roughly two centuries ago, members of the circle mass-cast Shape Earth for months to raise Stormberg's Wall; its earthen core was later sheathed in Stormberg obsidian. This history was learned by [[Keith Poe]] (The Understudies) through the [[Iron Seam Guild]] in Session 7. [[Belak]] was once counted among their best before disappearing forty years ago.
