> # Ancients of Lava

## Description

The Ancients of Lava (known historically as the Lavaliths) are a wilderness-based druid circle with a focus on elementalism. They refuse to operate in cities and travel solo. They maintain loose ties with nature deity temples in [[Crystal City]]. Roughly two centuries ago, members of the circle mass-cast Shape Earth for months to raise [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Stormberg/Stormberg|Stormberg]]'s Wall's earthen core; the [[Magocracy of Corranor]] later sheathed it in [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Stormberg/Stormberg|Stormberg]] obsidian and built out the wall's magical defenses, and publicly takes the credit for the Wall—which is exactly why the wall can be burrowed through from the inside, whatever the obsidian manages on the outside. [[Belak]] was once counted among their best before disappearing forty years ago.

Elder **[[Jess]]** maintains a grove of her own and has met only a handful of the circle's members in person, since most stay deep in the woods and out of contact even with each other.
