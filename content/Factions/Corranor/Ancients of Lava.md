> # Ancients of Lava

The Ancients of Lava (known historically as the Lavaliths) are a wilderness-based druid circle with a focus on elementalism. They refuse to operate in cities and travel solo. They maintain loose ties with nature deity temples in [[Crystal City]]. Roughly two centuries ago, members of the circle mass-cast Shape Earth for months to raise [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Locations/Aenath/Corranor/Stormberg/Stormberg|Stormberg]]'s Wall's earthen core; the [[Magocracy of Corranor]] later sheathed it in Stormberg obsidian and built out the wall's magical defenses, and publicly takes the credit for the Wall. This history was learned by [[Keith Poe]] (The Understudies) through the [[Iron Seam Guild]] in Session 7. [[Belak]] was once counted among their best before disappearing forty years ago.
