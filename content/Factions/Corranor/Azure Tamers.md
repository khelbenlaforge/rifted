> # Azure Tamers

The Azure Tamers are an elite ranger collective based in the western wall towers of [[Crystal City]], responsible for transportation, long-distance communications, and the taming of wild animals including griffins and eagles. The animals in their care are wild, and the relationship they maintain with them is remarkable.

## Members

- **[[Torem]]**—Birdfolk ranger, senior member; oversees the communications network across Corranor's cities and keeps eyes on movement of people and goods.
- **[[Pell]]**—keeps the ledger at the [[Westward]] Aerie.
- **[[Odo]]**—works the lift winch at the [[Westward]] Aerie.

## Current Notes

The docks and the Tamers work closely together, and goods needing speedy delivery to other towns and cities move through a standing Azure Tamers pipeline, riding tamed griffins and eagles rather than any airship.

**Network confirmed, 2026-09-23:** the courier pipeline isn't Crystal-City-only—the Tamers keep an Aerie in every one of Corranor's cities except [[Sunhold]] (no overland or air route reaches its island at all). See [[Corranor]]'s Distances section for the resulting flight times.

## Bella

Bella is Torem's own griffin, white-winged and enormous.

## Bertha

[[Bertha]] is the house-sized giant eagle who holds the [[Westward]] Aerie's second landing, where hatchlings learn to glide.

## Session 30 Update (The Door Kickers)

Bella is Torem's own griffin, white-winged and enormous—and, by every account of the party who's ridden her, entirely comfortable with the right rider before they've finished introducing themselves. She has carried party members on reconnaissance and travel across three sessions: aerial scouting of the alabaster highway east of Crystal City (Session 21), and the trip north out of Crystal City (Sessions 29 and 30), where she sighted the early, heavy southward wildebeest migration that became Torem's own migration job.

She's carried more than riders, too: on that same Session 30 road trip, surplus deer meat the party didn't need went back down the road on Bella's back to be sold off, covering the trip's costs before it had even finished costing anything.
