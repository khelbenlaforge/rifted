> # Cult of Shar

An underground organization. No one knows its real shape or how far its leadership actually reaches, and that opacity is the point. What's confirmed comes from a single failed ritual and its aftermath, nothing more.

## The Rising of the Dark

On the night of the [[Feast of the Moon]], Year 222 PA, the cult attempted the **Rising of the Dark**: a ritual timed specifically to [[The Exodus/00_My Notes/Pantheon/Selûne|Selûne]]'s fullness, meant to strip away a portion of Kelemvor's authority over the dead and return souls to nothingness instead—Shar's own domain—rather than let Kelemvor's clergy see them off.

The ritual required a hostage. Cultists abducted **[[Cadmus Tallow]]**, First Gravelord of [[Crystal City]], from his own vigil during the Deeds of the Dead and bound him to an altar at the [[Shrine of Shar]], a hidden one-room shrine off Cistern Street in the [[Diamond District]]. **[[Mother Ilyra Duskveil]]** officiated. The operation's scale makes clear Ilyra wasn't improvising or acting alone: cultists had been planted inside the vigil itself, a decoy Gravelord waited in the crowd, and coordinated cells were working several more streets that same night. Someone above her set this in motion. She may not have been the cult's actual leader—only its most visible piece.

The party broke the ritual before midnight. [[Gill Bates|Gill]] tore the Gravelord free of the altar; [[Min Ji-ah]] killed Ilyra with Tasha's Mind Whip before she could finish. An unnamed fiend—reading, by every account, closer to a fallen celestial than any devil or demon—absorbed Ilyra's body in the instant of her death and escaped. How something like that crosses the barrier at all is an open question nobody has answered.

**How the cult recruits:** Ilyra's own method, confirmed through [[Ser Tamsin Rook]]—a Knight of the Eternal Order, five years without incident until that night—was grief. A pale, hooded elf approached him a week beforehand in the [[Citrine District]] with proof his dead sister's soul had never crossed over, and a promise the cult could fix it. He let cultists past his post in exchange. Rook's own confession named the trade explicitly: *they have my sister, said they could fix what was broken.*

## Aftermath

The shrine sat exposed through the night, its darkness spell gone since [[Ember|Ember]]'s Dispel Magic broke it. Its officiant lay dead where she fell, and nobody moved to secure or investigate the room before dawn.

The following day, at the Council's public address before the assembled city, **[[Doran]]** confirmed the shrine "beneath Cistern Street was closed and its officiant dead" and insisted publicly that nothing had entered Crystal City from outside it—a denial the party alone knew to be false, having watched the fiend leave under its own power. **[[Oriel Blackstaff]]** then declared the Cistern Street shrine unlawful assembly and its property forfeit to the city, and opened a formal inquiry into the [[Temple of Kelemvor]] and how the [[Knights of the Eternal Order]] admit people to the precinct during a vigil of obligation. [[Ser Tamsin Rook]] was remanded to his own Order for judgment rather than the public square.
