> # The Manifest Houses

The informal trade bloc that actually runs [[Coldford]]'s docks. Real inspection never developed in a city built around the Coldmist's permanent harbor fog, and what grew up in its place was speed, discretion, and a price list for both. The Manifest Houses run the intake desks side by side in the **Tallyrows**, Coldford's dockside district: open ledgers, tally slates, a bell rung for every closed count. Their actual legal specialty is the "blind manifest"—cargo and passage logged by count and fee only, no names, no papers—the reason half of Corranor sends anything through Coldford it would rather not have tracked elsewhere.

Nominal authority over Coldford sits with **House Colwyn**, whose crest sits over the Landgate and whose name locals defer to out of habit more than evidence. Whether House Colwyn actually directs anything the Manifest Houses do is a genuinely open question, left that way on purpose.

## Operations

A constant low tally-bell chime rolls out of the Tallyrows day and night, marking closed transactions. When a ship or caravan comes in on schedule, the nearest desk's queue swells fast and the district's noise doubles for a while. The Houses also run contracts for freight-security and escort work, most of it posted through the [[Adamantine Guild]]'s Coldford branch (**the Tallhouse**), whose Branch Leader [[Petra Hale]] fields the postings.

## Current Status

Petra Hale has quietly started tracking a string of "insurance" shakedowns hitting smaller Manifest House stalls—nothing citywide yet, no names, just a pattern on a corkboard only she looks at. Per [[The Claret]]'s own established pattern of moving into gaps left by organizations stretched too thin, the Claret has begun quietly testing a foothold in Coldford: new, local, low-visibility, unnamed on-screen so far. Nothing here confirms that to the party yet, and nothing connects it to any named Crystal City figure.
