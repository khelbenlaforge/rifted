> # Ancients of Lava

The Ancients of Lava (known historically as the Lavaliths) are a wilderness-based druid circle with a focus on elementalism. They refuse to operate in cities and travel solo. They maintain loose ties with nature deity temples in [[Crystal City]]. [[Belak]] was once counted among their best before disappearing forty years ago.
