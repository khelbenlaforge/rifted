## Description

[[Crystal City]]'s oldest criminal organization — not a guild, not a brotherhood, just organized crime that grew out of the [[Garnet District]] over a century and never left. The Claret does not advertise. It does not have a public name in any official sense. People in the Garnet call them the Claret because of the deep red garnet chips worn as earrings by those who are made — one stud for associate, two for full membership. The [[Magocracy of Corranor]] knows they exist. The arrangement is old and comfortable for both parties.

Their business: protection on the blocks around the [[Garnet District]]'s tavern quarter, smuggling through the [[Citrine District]] docks, and information — bought, sold, or traded depending on who's asking.

## Leadership & Members

- **Leader** — Unknown to the party. Does not appear at [[The Low Flagon]] and does not meet new contacts.
- **[[Tavia Orn]]** — Collections runner, three-block territory in the Garnet. The party's point of contact. Mid-level, trusted, not interested in climbing.
- **Various associates** — Small-time operators, dockworkers, barkeeps, and runners throughout the Garnet and into the Citrine. Most do not know each other by name.

**Internal tension:** The Claret has always been pragmatic — neutral on politics, focused on business. A faction within the organization has begun cultivating relationships with visiting merchants from the eastern islands since the [[Citrine District]] docks reopened. The old guard considers this a departure from a century of deliberate insularity. Nothing has broken yet.

## Operations

Running protection on the [[Garnet District]] tavern quarter. Maintaining smuggling channels through two sympathetic dockworkers in the [[Citrine District]]. Brokering information between factions that do not want to be seen talking to each other. Watching the [[Adamantine Guild]]'s understaffed period with interest — the Claret fills gaps the guild currently can't.

## Current Status

Stable, quiet, and content. The only unusual activity in recent months has been increased interest in a specific category of information: anything related to the five strangers who appeared on the [[Jade Spire]] during the Shadow attack. Someone is paying well for that intelligence. The Claret is collecting it without knowing who placed the order.

## Membership

**How to Join:** Vouched by two current members. No formal test — the Claret watches candidates for months before any offer is made.

### Benefits & Restrictions

**Rank 1 — Associate** *(Renown 3+)*
- *Benefit:* Safe passage through [[Garnet District]]; access to Claret-run fences and information brokers
- *Restriction:* No independent operations in the Garnet without clearance; no contact with City Watch on Claret matters

**Rank 2 — Made** *(Renown 10+)*
- *Benefit:* Access to the back room at [[The Low Flagon]]; introduction to district-level contacts; discount at [[Citrine District]] dockside fences
- *Restriction:* Active duty when called — one request per season, no refusal
